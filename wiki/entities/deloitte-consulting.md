# Deloitte & Touche Consulting

> Separates Risk Management and QA/QC; rarely solely responsible for end product; collaborative approach


## Top observations

- Deloitte separates Risk Management (corporate function with National Partner) and QA/QC (practice-level Senior Partner) into entirely separate functions — [[study-ie-appendix-lane-qa-research-e594cb]]
- Deloitte rarely engages in projects where employees are wholly responsible for end product; project teams work with client staff and are jointly managed — [[study-ie-appendix-lane-qa-research-e594cb]]
- European and Canadian practices ISO 9000 certified; some Asia/Pacific (notably Australia); US practices not ISO 9000 but evaluating SEI CMMs and PMI certification — [[study-ie-appendix-lane-qa-research-e594cb]]
- US practices formalizing QA methodologies across all practice areas under Senior Partner; also creating consistent global Risk Management review templates — [[study-ie-appendix-lane-qa-research-e594cb]]
