# IBM

> AS/400 launched June 1988; RAMP-C benchmark used to compare with HP.


## Top observations

- Not yet mature: no C compiler, 1MB program size limit, frequent OS patch tapes, OS/400 immature — [[study-1989-vidmar-as400-hp3000-and-metadata-a32d04]]
- AS/400 B60 offers ~25% less performance than HP 3000 Model 70; B70 approximates Model 70 — [[study-1989-vidmar-as400-hp3000-and-metadata-a32d04]]
- Journaling and Checksum recommended for production systems can reduce throughput by up to 60% — [[study-1989-vidmar-as400-hp3000-and-metadata-a32d04]]
- MIPS invalid for AS/400 vs. HPPA comparison; AS/400 MIPS undisclosed by IBM; multiprocessor architecture makes definition unclear — [[study-1989-vidmar-as400-hp3000-and-metadata-a32d04]]
- AS/400 lacks growth path today; multiple-AS/400 clustering not a reasonable alternative — [[study-1989-vidmar-as400-hp3000-and-metadata-a32d04]]
- AS/400 evolved through iSeries, System i to IBM i on Power Systems; still actively supported in 2026 with 100,000+ installations — [[study-1989-vidmar-as400-hp3000-and-metadata-a32d04]]
- AS/400 OS/400 single-level storage more likely to require lengthy restores after system failure than conventional midrange approach — [[study-1989-vidmar-as400-hp3000-and-metadata-a32d04]]
