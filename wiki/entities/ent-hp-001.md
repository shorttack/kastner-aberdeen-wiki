# Hewlett-Packard Company

> HP split into HP Inc. (PCs and printers) and Hewlett Packard Enterprise (servers/services) in 2015; Vectra PC line discontinued 2002 after HP-Compaq merger


## Top observations

- HP merged with Compaq in 2002 for $25 billion; Vectra line discontinued; replaced by HP Evo (based on Compaq platform); DMI competition moot `[ps=5]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP OpenView rebranded as HP Software Division products in 2007 after acquisitions of Mercury Interactive and Peregrine Systems; eventually sold to MicroFocus; now part of OpenText `[ps=5]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- NightDIRECTOR uses Magic Packet technology (co-developed with AMD) to remotely power on PCs for maintenance without disturbing end users `[ps=4]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP added approximately 300 DMI manageable attributes plus 65 unique HP DMI attribute extensions to Vectra PC line `[ps=2]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP Vectra BIOS flash capability allows IS managers to extend PC life by 2 years beyond typical 3-year major technology cycles `[ps=2]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP was the first major supplier to introduce DMI-compliant PCs starting in 1992 `[ps=1]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP will position its DMI-based desktop management environment as a major differentiator to surpass Compaq in enterprise PC sales `[ps=1]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP TopTOOLS is the only product capable of upgrading BIOS of multiple PCs simultaneously from a central console `[ps=1]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP expects to reduce desktop management costs by at least 20-25% (approximately $3,000 per PC) in the near term `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP Vectra Corporate PC features more than 350 total DMI attributes `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP OpenView Professional Suite manages small-to-medium companies with 10-2,000 nodes; OpenView Network Node Manager supports LANs and WANs with more than 250 nodes `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- TopTOOLS includes AssetTOOLS (inventory), ConfigTOOLS (configuration/troubleshoot), FlashTOOL (remote BIOS upgrade), SafeTOOLS (health monitoring), ProtectTOOLS (security) `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- Vectra PC platform prices declined significantly in 1996 due to CPU and memory price reductions `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
