# Nortel Networks Corporation (Northern Telecom)

> Subject of this profile. Filed for bankruptcy January 14, 2009. Assets sold: wireless to Ericsson ($1.13B), enterprise to Avaya ($915M), optical/Ethernet to Ciena ($774M), carrier VoIP to GENBAND ($282M), patents to Apple/Microsoft/Ericsson/RIM/EMC/Sony consortium ($4.5B 2011). Peak market cap $297-400B (~2000). At bankruptcy, ~30,000 employees; peak was 95,000.
