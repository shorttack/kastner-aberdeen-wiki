# Siebel Systems Inc.

> Founded 1993 by Thomas Siebel and Patricia House; CRM market leader by late 1990s with 45% market share; acquired by Oracle January 2006 for $5.85 billion; Siebel brand continues as Oracle's on-premises CRM
