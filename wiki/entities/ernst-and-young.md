# Ernst & Young LLP

> Formed from merger of Ernst & Whinney and Arthur Young in 1989; Quality Advisor program started 1992


## Top observations

- Quality Advisor program started 1992; outgrowth of internal restructuring triggered by unhappy client engagements from Ernst and Whinney / Arthur Young merger — [[study-ie-appendix-lane-qa-research-e594cb]]
- Quality Advisor assigned to all engagements with revenues above $1M; advisor at partner level or higher and recognized expert in practice area — [[study-ie-appendix-lane-qa-research-e594cb]]
- Quarterly reviews lasting up to 2-3 days; red/yellow/green flag system for findings; poor QA review can negatively affect engagement manager bonus — [[study-ie-appendix-lane-qa-research-e594cb]]
- Provides summary but not detailed reports of quarterly review to clients based on advice of internal counsel — [[study-ie-appendix-lane-qa-research-e594cb]]
