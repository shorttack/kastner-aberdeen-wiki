# Lawson Software, Inc.

> Named as ERP vendor alongside SAP, Oracle, Infor. Lawson later acquired by Infor. | Source: Lawson Software acquired by Golden Gate Capital and merged into Infor in 2012 for approximately $2 billion. https://www.industryweek.com/technology-and-iiot/information-technology/article/21947548/infor-acquires-lawson-software-for-2-billion


## Top observations

- Over 20 years of core-business applications experience as of 1996; strong AS/400 midrange heritage — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- 4 Process Suites incorporating 20 predefined best-practice process models (AP Invoice Approval, Requisitions, Credit Check, Employee Review) — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- Multi-tier product/market strategy combining best-of-breed workflow with integrated process-oriented GUI desktop — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- Process-oriented GUI with Lawson Toolbar and Intelligent Folder Tabs; C/C++ Win32; supports Windows 95 and NT — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- Three levels: Personal (OED agents), Departmental/Workgroup (OED + Departmental Workflow Engine), Enterprise (OED + Process Suites) — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- MAPI-compliant email with embedded active/passive application objects; future VIM support in v7.0 — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- R&D working on OED release with Java-based Internet deployment; Aberdeen expects Lawson in CSS Internet vanguard — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- Cross-domain workflow spanning Lawson and non-Lawson via plug-and-play APIs; supports Wang OpenWorkflow and FileNet Visual Workflow — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- Three deployment levels (personal/departmental/enterprise) matching diverse computing environments — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- Data capture of key process efficiency metrics; Rapid Process Development (RPD) methodology for continuous improvement — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- OED's business-process perspective 'stands unique among CSS' and represents 'distinct market advantage' — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- Lawson should be on any prospective customer's evaluation list; uniquely qualified to compete across multiple market segments — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- Lawson will escalate the client-server fray and could redefine the CSS value-added technology model; potential for significant competitive market advantage — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
- When OED with Java is delivered, Lawson could find itself in the vanguard of CSS suppliers able to fully exploit the Internet's business potential — [[study-aberdeen-1996-lawson-software-desktop-workflow]]
