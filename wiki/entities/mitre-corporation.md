# Mitre Corporation

> Federally funded research organization; gave Kastner a cold-call presentation in 1982 that initiated the NORAD contract chain.


## Top observations

- Kastner chose not to pitch fault tolerance alone; covered full architecture — communications controllers, modularity, data protocols, spares strategy — inferring complex defense integration need. — [[study-volume-1-ch05-stratus-fault-tolerant-wars-1981-1987]]
- Mitre brought in GTE Sylvania and Systems Development Corporation; extended procurement process led to landmark NORAD contract. — [[study-volume-1-ch05-stratus-fault-tolerant-wars-1981-1987]]
- Fifty silent engineers sat in the auditorium; declined to state their requirements; Kastner had one hour. — [[study-volume-1-ch05-stratus-fault-tolerant-wars-1981-1987]]
