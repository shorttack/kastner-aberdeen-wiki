# Netlink, Inc.

> Networking hardware company; acquired by Cabletron; used Intel i960 processor (matching Cabletron's ASIC philosophy); technology rapidly assimilated into Cabletron product line.


## Top observations

- Netlink acquired by Cabletron Systems for ~$158-160M in stock in September 1996 — same month as this profile; Cabletron subsequently dissolved into 4 entities by 2001; frame relay products eventually discontinued `[ps=5]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-frame-relay-access-devices]]
- Payback in 12-18 months; some customers in less than 12 months; driven by 20-25% reduction in leased-line costs plus MIS labor and network licensing savings `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-frame-relay-access-devices]]
- Aberdeen believes Netlink FRADs represent a sound investment decision with robust data services market continuing into next century; Matrix VC provides smooth transition to future capabilities `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-frame-relay-access-devices]]
- Standard 4 ports; max 64 ports; routes IP/IPX; RFC 1490; hard drive storage; 2 Ethernet or Token Ring LAN; hub for star network `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-frame-relay-access-devices]]
- Enterprises with SNA traffic >40% or performance-sensitive SNA will benefit greatly from FRADs over routers; routers far less efficient at SNA traffic handling `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-frame-relay-access-devices]]
- Single consistent FRAD product line for enterprise data interconnect; TurboFRAD (branch), OmniFRAD (hub), NetFRAD (carrier); Matrix VC switching architecture `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-frame-relay-access-devices]]
- RFC 1490 FRAD: 9 bytes/frame; TCP/IP SDLC encapsulation (router): 70 bytes; DLS (router): 50 bytes. Routers consume 50%+ of SNA frame at peak loading. `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-frame-relay-access-devices]]

- Private/hybrid frame relay edge switch with backbone switch capabilities plus multiprotocol FRAD and routing functions; SNMP-managed via OmniView on HP OpenView `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-frame-relay-access-devices]]
