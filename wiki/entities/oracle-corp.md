# Oracle Corporation

> Oracle RDBMS selected as back-end database for SAP R3 (replacing SAP's proprietary mainframe DB); HP 9000 Series 800 was Oracle's most important Unix revenue platform

## Top observations

- Oracle announced Oracle Unbreakable Linux — support for RHEL-compatible Linux at lower fees than Red Hat charges. — [[study-novell-and-microsoft-good-for-business-4-59404e]]
- Oracle did build the integrated stack: Exadata database machines, Exalogic, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, SaaS apps — realizing Kastner's prediction. — [[study-on-computers-tips-is-sun-s-sparc-archite-65b3b9]]
- Oracle laid off most of the Solaris and SPARC engineering staff in September 2017; SPARC M8 was the last new CPU; T-series entered long-term sustaining mode. — [[study-on-computers-tips-is-sun-s-sparc-archite-65b3b9]]
- Oracle granted Dell the exclusive right (through 2004 year-end) to deliver preinstalled Oracle Standard Edition One on PowerEdge 2600/2650 running Red Hat Linux or Windows; pricing starts at $4,108 for 5/10/25/unlimited user variations. — [[study-oracle-and-dell-take-aim-at-midsize-busi-770ea6]]
- Oracle-Dell also expanded partnership into China; Oracle certifying PowerEdge servers with Red Flag DC 4.0 Linux and Oracle Database 10g at Oracle China Development Center in Beijing. — [[study-oracle-and-dell-take-aim-at-midsize-busi-770ea6]]
- Oracle Apps World 2002 messaging shifted from new-product launch to 'let's get you guys to use what we already have' — focused on upgrading the installed base to 11i. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Approximately 50% of Oracle client base (~1,500 customers) still on a 10x release of the applications suite. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Initial releases of Oracle 11i had quality assurance (QA) problems that further dampened upgrade enthusiasm among existing customers. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Oracle added 'daily close' capability to 11i, enabling virtual closing for any part of the organization on a daily cadence. Lawson Software users had this feature first. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Oracle renewed its 'we'll do it for you' outsourcing pledge to users, even offering to buy back customer investment in hardware and IT staff to support the application suite. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Oracle claimed outsourcing to Oracle can save customers 31% to 84% on IT infrastructure costs (vendor-cited ranges). — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Oracle claimed product and service response time can improve by as much as 50% when Oracle manages and maintains its own software. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Senior executive Mark Barranchea moved from heading CRM to spearhead a newly created top-level customer-care and relations role. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Larry Ellison's tone at Apps World was markedly more conciliatory toward partners and customers than in prior years ('A Kinder, Gentler Larry Ellison?'). — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Aberdeen expects customers to respond positively to many components of Oracle's service and hosting propositions. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Oracle's cloud/SaaS applications business became a multi-billion dollar business. Oracle acquired PeopleSoft (2004), Siebel (2006), and numerous other enterprise app companies. Oracle Cloud Applications (Fusion Applications) launched commercially in 2012 and grew to billions in revenue. By 2015-2020, cloud ERP SaaS dominated new enterprise app deployments. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Aberdeen predicts businesses won't be rushed to upgrade due to economy, IT investment slowdown, and magnitude of effort involved. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Upgrades are key 'unlocking' events for incremental revenue opportunities within an installed base; newest version is the platform for extended functionality. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Paper concludes: 'Oracle provides such an application suite, enabled with comprehensive intra-enterprise and business-to-business best practice business processes designed to generate a sustainable benefit stream.' — [[study-oracleroiwhitepaperprod-7-bcf73f]]
- Oracle announced a $5.1 billion hostile takeover bid for PeopleSoft the prior week; rare hostile takeover in software industry; Ellison expressed little interest in PeopleSoft products, more in customers. — [[study-peoplesoft-bid-mirrors-lofty-goals-of-or-6be2ec]]
