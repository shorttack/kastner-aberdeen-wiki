# Safeway Inc.

> Plaintiff/party in major antitrust and breach-of-contract dispute centered on data-warehousing technology; one of the largest North American supermarket chains.

## Top observations

- By mid-1990s, data warehouses had become strategic infrastructure for large retailers, consolidating POS, supplier, and inventory data for analytical decision-making. `[ps=5]` — [[study-volume-1-ch09-expert-witness-technology-on-trial-1992-2015]]
- Better analytical capability translated directly into better purchasing negotiations, more effective promotions, and reduced inventory costs — all affecting margins in a thin-margin business. `[ps=5]` — [[study-volume-1-ch09-expert-witness-technology-on-trial-1992-2015]]
- Planned application rollouts delayed by the incident — opportunity cost beyond direct damages `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Risk of permanent loss of data confidence — users may not trust data warehouse outputs after corruption event `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Business interruption during recovery — grocery retail operations depend on daily/weekly data cycles `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Inability to evolve the system during recovery period — platform frozen while competitors advanced `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Used Teradata equipment had limited resale market — replacement cost significantly exceeds residual value `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Safeway was building an Operational Data Store (Class 3 applications) for real-time retail data — most advanced tier of DW deployment `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Data warehouse is a strategic asset — loss or degradation would permanently impair Safeway's competitive position in grocery retail `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Damages calculated using three-tier methodology: direct recovery costs; direct replacement costs; business interruption costs — standard approach for IT disaster claims `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Safeway's DW investment trajectory — from $10.2M initial to $16.7M total — demonstrated increasing strategic commitment to analytics-driven retail `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Risk of undetected data loss — some corrupted data may not be identified until much later `[ps=3]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Expert opinion: full replacement preferred over repair due to data integrity concerns and risk of latent hardware damage from fire suppression chemicals `[ps=3]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Case established market value of enterprise data warehousing capabilities in terms courts could understand and apply — described as the fundamental contribution of expert testimony. `[ps=3]` — [[study-volume-1-ch09-expert-witness-technology-on-trial-1992-2015]]
