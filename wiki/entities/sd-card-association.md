# SD Card Association

> Global standards body for SD Card formats; co-founded by Panasonic, SanDisk, Toshiba


## Top observations

- SD Memory Cards captured almost 25% of the sector approximately $81M in US retail sales in January and February 2003, overtaking Sony Memory Stick for the first time. — [[study-sd-cards-overtake-memory-stick-12-may-20-81d89c]]
- Consumer demand for higher capacity and faster access removable media propelled SD cards to 400 percent sales growth in 2002, per NPD Group. — [[study-sd-cards-overtake-memory-stick-12-may-20-81d89c]]
- The SD format is supported by 550+ companies through the SD Card Association; consumer demand increased more than fivefold in 2002. — [[study-sd-cards-overtake-memory-stick-12-may-20-81d89c]]
- SD memory cards lead all other formats both in terms of number of products and number of product categories, with over 1,300 SD-enabled products available worldwide. — [[study-sd-memory-cards-to-drive-market-for-digi-71a434]]
