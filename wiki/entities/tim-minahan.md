# Tim Minahan

> Aberdeen Group Category Creator: Supply Chain Management / e-Procurement / e-Sourcing — credited by Peter S. Kastner. Lead Aberdeen analyst who developed the firm's e-Procurement, e-Sourcing, e-Travel, and e-Expense practice coverage; sole lead on the 2000 e-Procurement Automation End User Survey (February) and e-Procurement Solution Functionality Survey (March), defining the canonical Aberdeen e-Procurement taxonomy (catalog management, transaction management with EDI/OBI/XML/e-mail, marketplace strategies, dynamic pricing, reverse auctions). Foundational analyst for the e-Business@Aberdeen.com practice's procurement and supply-chain coverage. Later CMO at SAP Ariba and Coupa, then EVP Strategy at Citrix — careers explicitly built on the SCM/procurement-software category Aberdeen helped frame.
