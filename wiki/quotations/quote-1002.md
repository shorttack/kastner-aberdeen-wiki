# Computerworld — 1993-03-22

**Headline**: DEC moves to VAX, Alpha mixed clusters

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> It means that those customers can plan to install mixed clusters almost immediately and that their investment in clustering has been protected.

## Rationale

Digital’s OpenVMS quickly supported mixed-architecture VAX/Alpha clusters, and by the mid-1990s DEC was explicitly documenting and promoting VAX–Alpha mixed VMScluster configurations as the standard migration path, allowing customers to add Alpha nodes into existing VAX clusters rather than replace them outright. Through roughly 1993–1996, many OpenVMS sites did exactly this, reusing their cluster infrastructure, storage, and operational practices while introducing Alpha hardware, so their original clustering investments were largely preserved even though some high-availability and layered-product scenarios took longer to be fully supported.
