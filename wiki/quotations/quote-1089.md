## Rationale

In the early 1990s, building native GUIs with X11 and Motif on VMS and UNIX was widely regarded as difficult and labor‑intensive, requiring event‑driven programming expertise and low‑level toolkit work that most traditional business developers lacked. Over roughly the next three years, DEC’s DECwindows strategy explicitly centered on X/Motif compatibility to court workstation‑style ISVs and meet enterprise RFP checkboxes, even as easier PC tools like Visual Basic and PowerBuilder began shifting new GUI development toward Windows and away from DEC platforms. The claim slightly overstates the long‑term difficulty of GUI coding as higher‑level tools spread, but for the stated timeframe and DEC/X/Motif context it captured the key technical and ecosystem constraints accurately.
