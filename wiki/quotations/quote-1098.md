## Rationale

Within about three years of its mid-1990s launch, Sybase System 11 (ASE 11.x) delivered roughly an order-of-magnitude TPC-C performance gain over its predecessors and was widely regarded as having resolved the worst performance concerns that had dogged System 10, validating the claim that it was a definite improvement and that performance had been “put to bed.” At the same time, ASE 11.x became the core of many stable production OLTP deployments through the late 1990s, supporting the characterization of it as a solid, higher-quality product, even though these technical gains did not ultimately stop Sybase’s longer-term market-share erosion against Oracle and Microsoft beyond the 3-year horizon.
