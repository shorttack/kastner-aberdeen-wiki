## Quote

> Peter S Kastner Media Quotations IDC STUDY SAYS WINDOWS COULD BE CHEAPER THAN LINUX A study funded by Microsoft and conducted by IDC shows that the total cost of ownership for Windows 2000 could be lower for many businesses than for Linux over a five-year period.

## Rationale

The IDC study did find, in 2002, that Windows 2000 appeared to have lower TCO than Linux in several traditional midmarket server scenarios, largely because many enterprises then had more in-house Windows skills and treated Linux as a specialty platform. However, even by the mid‑2000s, rival TCO analyses and real‑world deployments showed Linux to be cheaper or comparable for web, application, and infrastructure workloads, and Linux quickly became mainstream on servers, undermining the notion of a sustained 30% staffing‑cost penalty. Over the longer run, Linux’s dominance in web hosting, its central role in enterprise infrastructure, and later its position as the default server OS in cloud environments all contradicted the idea that Linux was generally more expensive to operate than Windows.
