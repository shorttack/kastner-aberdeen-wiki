## Rationale

Within three years, CA’s InterBiz, Jasmine, 3‑D visualization, and Neugents technologies failed to define a new, durable category and saw limited market traction, with InterBiz’s application portfolio sold off to SSA Global by 2002. Other vendors such as IBM Tivoli, HP OpenView, BMC Patrol, and ERP/BI players like SAP, Oracle, Tibco, and SAS were already building or soon shipped real-time, information-rich monitoring and analytics for business processes, so CA was not alone in this space even at the time. The claim that this was “totally new and different” and uniquely positioned therefore did not hold up against subsequent enterprise management and business activity monitoring trends.
