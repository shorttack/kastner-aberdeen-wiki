## Quote

> Dell is clearly serious about its expansion efforts. “Do not overlook the fact that Dell Computer Inc. changed its name to Dell Inc.,” Kastner says. “That's a direct message from the board of directors that computers are not Dell's only business anymore.” That may be good news for consumers and bad news for other consumer electronics marketers. ## Rationale

Within three years of its early-2000s name change to Dell Inc., Dell did in fact broaden beyond PCs into a range of consumer and peripheral categories, including Dell-branded printers, LCD TVs, handhelds like the Axim, and music players such as the Dell DJ, alongside a growing peripherals and services business. This expansion validated the idea that the company no longer saw itself as only a computer maker, and helped intensify price competition in several PC-adjacent consumer electronics segments, even though Dell remained heavily PC-centric and many of the new categories later underperformed or were exited.
