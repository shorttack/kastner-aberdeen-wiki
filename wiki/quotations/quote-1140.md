## Quote

> Kastner pointed out that the elected position of state attorney general is often a stepping-stone to higher political office, and that the Microsoft case offers high visibility to the attorneys general involved. "Draw your own conclusions," Kastner said.

## Rationale

Within three years of the 2004 comment, prominent Microsoft-case state attorney general Eliot Spitzer parlayed his national antitrust profile into a successful run for New York governor in 2006, illustrating the office as a springboard to higher office. More broadly, the early 2000s continued a longstanding U.S. pattern in which high-visibility state attorneys general, including those active in major tech and tobacco litigation, used the position to build name recognition for gubernatorial or senatorial campaigns. However, not all of the attorneys general involved in the Microsoft case moved up within that short window, so the observation remained more directionally accurate than specifically predictive.
