## Rationale

Kastner’s characterization that Microsoft’s security patches were largely focused on fixes rather than adding new functionality held up over the next several years, as the company formalized Patch Tuesday and delivered mostly security-only updates across Windows and Internet Explorer. However, the assertion that Microsoft fixed flaws “as quickly as possible” was only partially borne out: while Microsoft did respond relatively quickly to some issues (and had already begun the Trustworthy Computing and SDL initiatives), high-profile incidents like Blaster, Sasser, and the WMF vulnerability fueled ongoing criticism that its patch cadence and response were still too slow. Overall, his defense of Microsoft’s intent and direction was directionally right, but it understated the real and widely acknowledged lag and controversy around Microsoft’s patch responsiveness in the 2003–2006 period.
