## Rationale

Within roughly three years of the quote, server density and price/performance improved rapidly via 1U rack servers, x86/Linux web farms, and emerging blade servers from HP and IBM, letting e-commerce sites add more functionality without large footprint increases. At the same time, basic mobile web access via WAP/GPRS, NTT DoCoMo’s i-mode, and early smartphones/PDAs (e.g., BlackBerry, Palm) did make wireless devices more web-compatible, although they were still far from the rich mobile e-commerce experiences that only took off after the iPhone (2007) and app stores. The prediction about smarter, more targeted e-marketing was strongly validated in this early-2000s window by Amazon-style collaborative filtering recommendations, personalization engines, and email targeting, even if the full transformative impact of mobile on e-commerce took longer than the nominal 3-year horizon.
