## Rationale

Within roughly three years of this statement, Microsoft Passport failed to attract broad consumer enthusiasm or merchant adoption and remained largely confined to Microsoft’s own services, undermined by privacy concerns, low trust, and regulatory scrutiny. Users continued to enter information separately on most sites or rely on emerging browser-based form-fill and password tools, and Passport never became the eagerly awaited, widely adopted solution implied by the quote. Although the underlying pain point of repetitive data entry was real, it was later addressed more by alternatives like federated logins (e.g., Google/Facebook) and integrated browser and OS features than by Passport itself.
