## Rationale

This quote makes two concrete short-horizon claims: intensifying **third‑party toner/ink competition** for HP and Dell’s imminent entry into the printer business. By the early–mid 2000s, the aftermarket for compatible and remanufactured cartridges had indeed become a significant structural challenge to HP’s high‑margin supplies business, pressuring pricing and share of supplies revenue, validating the competitive concern within a few years. Dell did in fact enter the printer market in the early 2000s with Dell‑branded inkjet and laser printers manufactured by partners such as Lexmark, and maintained an extensive Dell‑branded printer line through the 2000s before finally exiting the segment around 2016.[1][3][7] The direction and substance of Kastner’s short‑term assessment therefore held up well, even though Dell ultimately failed in printers over a longer horizon than the 3‑year window being scored.
