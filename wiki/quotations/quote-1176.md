## Quote

> Five years ago, major service companies told Kastner that the TCO outcomes they saw were in the range of $700 to $3,000 per PC per year. On the low end, Intel's vPro technology has cut that down "to under $500 per PC per year, and that's not all that much more than just the device itself," Kastner sai

## Rationale

Enterprise PC total cost of ownership did decline through the 2010s and early 2020s as hardware costs fell and remote management, automation, and platform features like Intel vPro reduced desk-side support and outage costs. However, fully loaded PC TCO in large organizations remained well above bare device cost because labor, software, security, and compliance stayed dominant cost drivers, and many organizations did not fully exploit vPro’s manageability capabilities. The claim that vPro would push typical TCO to “not all that much more than just the device itself” was therefore directionally right about savings but overstated the magnitude and generality of the effect over the following few years.
