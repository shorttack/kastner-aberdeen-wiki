## Rationale

Kastner’s core idea that Intel vPro–style remote management would materially reduce per‑PC TCO and deliver meaningful savings per employee broadly held up, as later Forrester and Intel-sponsored TEI studies consistently reported significant multi-year TCO reductions, lower support labor, fewer onsite visits, and strong ROI from vPro deployments.[1][3][5] However, the specific magnitude he implies (cutting typical TCO bands to under $500 on the low end and knocking $1,000 per employee per year off costs for less efficient companies) is higher than what most documented case studies show, which tend to report percentage savings (e.g., ~17–20% and six- to seven-fold ROI) rather than such dramatic absolute per-PC annual reductions.[1][3] Enterprise adoption of vPro and continued investment in its manageability and security features through the 2010s and 2020s validate the direction of his argument, but the exact cost ranges and attribution of savings primarily to vPro were overstated.[7][9]
