## Quote

> You must be careful when making any changes or modifications to the firmware of your GPS because there is a risk that things can go wrong and render the GPS inoperable. And sorry, I won't go sailing with you and your hacked GPS.

## Rationale

The claim that modifying GPS firmware can render a device inoperable and make it unsafe to rely on for navigation aligns with how embedded and navigation systems evolved after 2005. Later GPS issues such as the 2019 GPS Week Number Rollover showed that firmware problems could cause older GPS units to malfunction, crash, or misdirect users, underscoring how fragile these devices can be when firmware is incorrect or mishandled.[2][4][7] More broadly, industry treatment of firmware and over‑the‑air updates across safety-related systems (including automotive) has consistently emphasized that update or modification errors can lead to malfunction or new vulnerabilities, validating his caution about not trusting a “hacked” navigation device for something like sailing.[6]
