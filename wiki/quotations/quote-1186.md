## Rationale

Kastner’s conclusion that Apple had maintained a current, parallel Intel port of Mac OS X and a secret Intel-focused development team was later confirmed directly by Apple. At WWDC on June 6, 2005, Steve Jobs revealed that for the previous five years every Mac OS X release had been built for both PowerPC and Intel, confirming an ongoing, up-to-date Intel port and internal project (later known as Project Marklar).[5][3] The subsequent public transition to Intel-based Macs beginning in January 2006 and the complete move of the Mac product line to Intel by August 2006 further validated that Apple had been keeping that Intel codebase current and production-ready well before announcement.[1][2]
