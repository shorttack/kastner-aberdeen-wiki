## Rationale

Within a few years of the mid‑2000s, SATA hard drives became standard in high-end consumer desktops and were widely used for midline/nearline server storage, displacing many legacy parallel SCSI disks. In the same timeframe, new enterprise servers shifted rapidly from parallel SCSI HDDs to SAS and SATA, effectively ending the era of classic SCSI disk drives in new deployments even though SCSI-derived protocols persisted.
