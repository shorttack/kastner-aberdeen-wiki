## Quote

> The price as low as $722 (qty 1,000) makes the Cranford value chips attractive for a new generation of low-cost 4- way servers. Dell immediately jumped in with its offering. These will sell by the boxcar for web processing and Tier 1 apps.

## Rationale

Intel’s low-cost 4-socket Xeon MP parts (widely associated with the Cranford codename) did underpin a wave of Dell and other OEM 4-way x86 servers that were adopted for web-serving, application, and departmental database workloads in the mid‑2000s, so the basic direction of the claim was correct. However, shipment data from 2004‑2008 show that the bulk of x86 volume and “boxcar”–scale web infrastructure growth centered on 1- and especially 2-socket rack servers, with 4-socket systems remaining a relatively small share of units, so the magnitude of demand for these specific value 4-way chips was overstated. There is limited public documentation tying the Cranford codename and this exact price point to unit volumes, so the assessment is conservative.
