## Quote

> It should improve the overall usability of Tandem products,

## Rationale

Tandem’s introduction and maturation of NonStop SQL in the late 1980s and early 1990s did in fact make its systems more approachable to mainstream enterprise developers by providing a standard relational interface over its proprietary Guardian OS. Within a few years, customers could use familiar SQL tools and skills on Tandem platforms, which reduced the visibility of the underlying proprietary environment and improved overall application development and data access usability. However, these usability gains were mostly within Tandem’s existing and niche high-availability customer base rather than transforming the broader market, so the impact was directionally correct but not game-changing.
