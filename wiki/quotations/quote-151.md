# Computerworld — 1994-06-20

**Headline**: IBM adds EISA server to PS/2 family

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-5y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> “IBM is belatedly waking up to the fact that Compaq [Computer Corp.] is walking off the playing field with the server marketplace just when it seems to be taking off,'' said Peter Kastner, vice president at Aberdeen Group in Boston.

## Rationale

Compaq became the leading vendor of Intel-based PC servers and overall PC shipments in the mid-1990s, capitalizing on the Windows NT and x86 server boom while IBM’s proprietary PS/2 and Micro Channel Architecture strategies stumbled and IBM had to retool around more standard PC Server/Netfinity lines. Over the 1994–1998 period Compaq maintained a strong lead in the volume server segment, but IBM remained a major force in higher-end Unix and mainframe servers and later regained overall server revenue leadership, so Compaq did not permanently 'walk off' with the entire server marketplace.
