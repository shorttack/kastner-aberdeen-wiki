# Computerworld — 1994-09-12

**Headline**: High-end tools gain steam

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=2, horizon=SH-5y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> Dynasty, Seer, Forte, along with many other industry players are heading upscale into enterprise client/server both to meet market demand and to avoid the bottom-clearing juggernaut that is Microsoft's tools division,'' Kastner said.

## Rationale

By the late 1990s, Microsoft’s development tools (Visual Basic, Visual C++, and then Visual Studio) had effectively swept the mass‑market Windows client/server and departmental tools space, pushing many non-Microsoft 4GL and RAD competitors out of the low end. Vendors like Forte Software and Seer Technologies indeed positioned themselves as high-end, enterprise application development and multi-tier client/server platforms, selling into large banks and telecoms rather than competing head‑on with Microsoft desktop tools. While those companies did not thrive long term as independent giants, the pattern Kastner described—niche players moving upscale to escape Microsoft’s commoditizing “juggernaut” at the bottom of the market—accurately characterized the competitive dynamics over the ensuing several years.
