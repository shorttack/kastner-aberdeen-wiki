## Quote

> We are projecting a further consolidation of hardware vendors in the industry,

## Rationale

From 1989 through the late 1990s the enterprise hardware market did in fact consolidate significantly, with major 1980s vendors like Digital Equipment Corporation, Data General, and Tandem either acquired or disappearing as independents (DEC to Compaq in 1998, Data General to EMC in 1999, Tandem to Compaq in 1997). Over roughly the 10‑year horizon, server and systems hardware clustered around a smaller set of large vendors such as IBM, HP, Compaq, and Sun, while many minicomputer and workstation players exited, matching the prediction of further consolidation. The call was directionally accurate and well-aligned with what happened, but it echoed an already-visible trend rather than a uniquely specific or bold forecast, so it is scored as largely rather than remarkably prescient.
