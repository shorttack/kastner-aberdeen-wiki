## Quote

> “Enterprise Manager will be the [database administrator] Relief Act of 1996, a boon to [the administrators] and their bosses,'' said Peter Kastner, an analyst at Aberdeen Group, Inc.

## Rationale

Oracle Enterprise Manager in the mid‑1990s did introduce a centralized GUI console, event monitoring, and job automation for distributed Oracle databases, moving DBA work toward more automated, "lights out" management and clearly easing some routine administrative burdens.[7][10] Over the late 1990s it became the standard Oracle DBA console and a genuine productivity booster, but DBAs still relied heavily on scripts and command‑line tools, and rival platforms such as Microsoft SQL Server shipped their own increasingly capable GUI management tools, so OEM was helpful rather than the transformative, uniquely unmatched "Relief Act of 1996" implied in the quote.[7][8]
