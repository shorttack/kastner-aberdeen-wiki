## Quote

> But it is bad news for Novell, Inc.'s NetWare, which is losing market share to NT Server, according to Peter Kastner, an analyst at Aberdeen Group, Inc., a market research firm in Boston.

## Rationale

In the early 1990s Novell NetWare held the dominant LAN/server OS share, often cited above 60–70%, but from the mid-1990s through the early 2000s its share steadily eroded as organizations migrated file, print, and application workloads to Microsoft Windows NT Server and then Windows 2000 Server. By roughly 1999–2001, industry reports from firms such as IDC and Gartner showed Windows NT/2000 surpassing NetWare in new server OS licenses and installed base, and Novell’s core NetWare business entered long-term decline that led it to pivot toward directory services and later Linux (e.g., the 2003 SUSE acquisition), confirming that NetWare’s market-share losses to NT not only continued but accelerated over the five-year horizon.
