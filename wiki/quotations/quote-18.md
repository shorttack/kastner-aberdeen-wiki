## Quote

> and you will see it on the software side as well.

## Rationale

Kastner’s broad prediction that consolidation among hardware vendors would be mirrored on the software side largely came true over the following decade, especially in enterprise and mainframe-oriented software. The 1990s saw major roll‑ups in systems and application software—Oracle, SAP, CA, BMC, and others acquired many smaller vendors, and database/middleware markets coalesced around a few large suppliers, reducing the number of independent “software houses” serving big‑iron corporate environments. However, the same period also saw a proliferation of new software firms in client‑server, PC, and Internet segments, so consolidation was strong in the DB2/mainframe and large‑enterprise tier but not across all software generally.
