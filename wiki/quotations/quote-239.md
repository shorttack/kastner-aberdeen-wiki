# InformationWeek — 2002-05-13

**Headline**: HP Reinvented

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=3, confidence=3, horizon=SH-5y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> The division should take on more outsourcing and systems integration work, but at the same time, it needs to be careful of competing too closely with partners such as EDS.

## Rationale

HP did exactly what the quote prescribes in the first half: over the 2002–2008 period it aggressively expanded into outsourcing and systems integration, and by acquiring EDS in 2008 for about $13.9B it roughly doubled services revenue and became the No. 2 IT services provider behind IBM.[1][2][7][10] However, this growth explicitly came by competing directly with, and ultimately absorbing, its former partner EDS—rebranding it as HP Enterprise Services in 2009—rather than by avoiding such competition, so the caution about not competing too closely with EDS did not hold.[7][9]
