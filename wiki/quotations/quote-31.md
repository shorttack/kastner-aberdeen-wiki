## Rationale

Within five years of 1990, Microsoft had effectively won the desktop GUI battle, with Windows 3.x and then Windows 95 becoming the de facto standard interface on IBM-compatible PCs, far outselling rivals like the Macintosh GUI and OS/2 Presentation Manager[2]. Although alternative GUIs such as Mac OS and X Window System environments persisted and remained important in specific niches, the market outcome by the mid-1990s was characterized by clear vendor dominance rather than a stalemate[2].
