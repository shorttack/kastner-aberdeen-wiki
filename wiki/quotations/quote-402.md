## Quote

> It's highly likely that anything they do will have a hardware product at its core,

## Rationale

From 2003–2006, Apple’s major strategic and growth moves were indeed centered on **hardware**: expansion of the iPod line, continued Mac hardware evolution, and preparation for the iPhone-era shift, all with tightly integrated software and services around those devices.[1][6] While Apple did launch and grow the iTunes Music Store and Mac OS X during this period, these software and service initiatives were primarily positioned to drive and differentiate hardware sales, especially iPods and Macs.[1][6] The claim slightly overstates by implying virtually everything Apple did was hardware-first, but for the three‑year horizon it captured the company’s hardware-centric reality quite well.
