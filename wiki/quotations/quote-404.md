# MarketWatch — 2003-04-26

**Headline**: Apple to answer rumors with facts

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=3, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> Without any changes, it's not ideal because investors won't like a low-growth company that continues to be frustrated by comparisons from the Windows world,

## Rationale

In 2003 Apple was a small-share PC maker (around 2% global market share) and was still often compared directly to Windows OEMs, which did limit its investor appeal at the time. Between 2003 and 2006, however, rapid growth from the iPod and iTunes ecosystem and a modest Mac resurgence turned Apple into a high-growth story that investors embraced, so the low-growth, Windows-framed scenario he warned about did not persist, even though his generic point about investor dislike of low-growth PC vendors was sound.
