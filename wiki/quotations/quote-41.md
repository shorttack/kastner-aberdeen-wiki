## Quote

> I see no letup in reductions in pricing for at least another six to nine months,

## Rationale

From late 1991 through at least mid‑1992, prices for Intel 486 processors and 386/486-based PCs continued to fall as Intel repeatedly cut 486 price lists and responded to clone competition from AMD and others, with street prices for 486DX and 486SX parts dropping sharply over that period. Mail-order PC vendors such as Gateway 2000, Dell, and Zeos stayed locked in aggressive price competition in 1991–1992, using frequent catalog updates and promotions to reduce system prices rather than stabilizing or raising them. The predicted six‑to‑nine‑month window of ongoing price reductions thus did materialize, and the broader dynamic of sustained price erosion in that time frame held true.
