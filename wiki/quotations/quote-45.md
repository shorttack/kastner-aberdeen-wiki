# Computerworld — 1991-09-02

**Headline**: Bull forms systems integration unit

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-5y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> The need for systems integration is so great, I don't see it changing significantly over the next five years,

## Rationale

From 1991 to 1996, demand for systems integration remained very strong as enterprises grappled with client/server migration, networked PCs, and heterogeneous mainframe–minicomputer–LAN environments, leading to rapid growth for integrators such as EDS, Andersen Consulting, IBM Global Services, and CSC. The mid‑90s ERP wave (e.g., SAP R/3 rollouts), early web projects, and large outsourcing deals all reinforced and expanded the systems integration market rather than reducing it. The need did not diminish and in fact grew, so the claim that it would remain high over the five‑year horizon was directionally accurate, though it understated how much integration demand would expand.
