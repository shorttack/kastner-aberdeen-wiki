## Quote

> But it's fair to say the energy required to get the mass of IBM moving in the Internet direction is huge.

## Rationale

Kastner’s remark that it would take huge energy to move IBM’s “mass” toward the Internet direction matches the reality that IBM underwent a painful, multi-year strategic and cultural turnaround in the mid‑1990s, including massive restructuring after its 1992 loss and the Gerstner-led shift to a network-centric, services-heavy model. By roughly 1995, IBM was only beginning to articulate its Internet and “e-business” story, with flagship efforts like the e-business campaign and WebSphere stack not materializing until the late 1990s, underscoring how much organizational effort was required for the pivot. However, the observation is more of a generic statement about big-company inertia than a specific, time-bounded forecast of outcomes, so its prescience is only partial rather than remarkable.
