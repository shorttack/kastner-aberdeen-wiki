# CNET News.com — 2003-04-29

**Headline**: IBM's Itanium Server Goes on Sale

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-5y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> IBM's Itanium support trails that of rival Hewlett-Packard, which is betting the company on Itanium.

## Rationale

By the mid-2000s, IBM had effectively exited serious Itanium investment, having already abandoned its AIX-on-IA-64 Project Monterey and instead doubling down on POWER, leaving only limited and short-lived Itanium server offerings compared with its mainlines.[3][9] Over the same period, HP became the overwhelmingly dominant and effectively sole major system vendor backing Itanium, using nearly all Intel Itanium processors in its Integrity and high-end server lines and tying HP-UX, OpenVMS, and NonStop futures to the architecture.[3][4] The phrase "betting the company" is somewhat hyperbolic given HP’s broader businesses, but as a characterization of relative commitment to Itanium in servers through roughly 2008, it was directionally and substantially correct.
