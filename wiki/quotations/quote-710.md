## Quote

> Aberdeen research indicates that the majority of large IBM sites have pinpointed DB2 as their strategic direction, making the database the de facto standard for large corporations

## Rationale

By the early to mid-1990s, DB2 had indeed become the strategic relational database on IBM mainframes at most large IBM-centric enterprises, and it remained a core system-of-record platform for many Fortune 500 firms well into the 2000s. However, across large corporations overall, Oracle (on Unix and later Windows) emerged as the broader de facto enterprise database standard, so describing DB2 as the de facto standard for large corporations in general overstated its dominance outside IBM mainframe environments.
