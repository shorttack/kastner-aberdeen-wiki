## Quote

> it is also the most vulnerable to the lower costs of downsizing strategies, due in part to the costliness of migrating to DB2

## Rationale

CICS/VSAM shops did face high costs to migrate legacy transaction applications to DB2, and this expense helped make some organizations more receptive to early‑1990s client–server downsizing and replatforming efforts to Unix and Windows with relational databases like Oracle and Sybase. However, over the subsequent five years many large IBM mainframe customers either left CICS/VSAM applications in place or gradually adopted DB2 on the mainframe rather than abandoning the platform, and mainframe transaction processing remained a core workload, so this segment did not become uniquely or predominantly vulnerable in the way implied.
