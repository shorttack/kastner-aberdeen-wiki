# Computerworld — 1991-09-02

**Headline**: Bull forms systems integration unit

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=3, confidence=2, horizon=SH-5y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> Bull has a reasonable opportunity to grow this service aggressively

## Rationale

Demand for systems integration and related services did grow strongly through the mid‑1990s, as enterprises adopted client‑server computing, ERP suites, and large networking projects, creating substantial opportunity for many vendors. Bull did build up a services and integration arm over the decade, later reorganizing around its Integris services business, which by around 2000 represented a large share of company revenue, indicating that services did become a core growth vector. However, Bull struggled financially and remained a relatively niche, regionally focused player compared with leading global integrators like IBM, EDS, and Cap Gemini, so the idea of Bull growing systems integration "aggressively" within five years was only partially borne out.
