## Quote

> Oracle's basic business model is changing,

## Rationale

Through the 1990s Oracle expanded from a primarily database-licensing company into a broad enterprise applications vendor (Oracle Financials, ERP, CRM, E-Business Suite) with a rapidly growing consulting/services arm. By around 2000, applications and related services were a much larger share of revenue even though database and software licenses remained the core, so the prediction of a more applications- and services-driven model was directionally accurate but not a complete business-model transformation.
