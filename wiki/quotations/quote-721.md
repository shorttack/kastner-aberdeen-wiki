## Quote

> They will be more applications- and services-driven, and Ray's credentials with EDS and Booz Allen are perfect for an organization that is highly service-oriented.

## Rationale

By the late 1990s, Oracle’s revenue mix had shifted significantly toward enterprise applications (Oracle Applications/E-Business Suite) and associated consulting, support, and implementation services, validating the claim that it would become more applications- and services-driven. Ray Lane, hired from Booz Allen, became Oracle’s president and was widely credited with driving its applications and services strategy and sales execution throughout the 1990s, suggesting that his background was indeed well matched to the company’s evolving service orientation.
