## Quote

> Our research shows that enterprise buyers who are looking at distributed systems view Unix as a difficult and expensive operating system. They want the same quality of tools they're used to using in the corporate data center,

## Rationale

In the mid-1990s, commercial Unix on proprietary RISC hardware was widely regarded by enterprise buyers as complex to administer and relatively expensive compared with emerging Intel-based Windows NT servers, which rapidly gained share for distributed client–server workloads. At the same time, the growth of systems-management suites such as CA Unicenter, Tivoli, and HP OpenView explicitly targeted bringing mainframe-style monitoring, automation, and operations tools to Unix and other distributed platforms, confirming the demand for “data center quality” tools in the distributed environment over the 1993–1998 horizon.
