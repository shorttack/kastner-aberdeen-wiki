**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P1_tiebreak

## Quote

> probably the most important thing [DEC] could do for their customers this year.

## Rationale

From 1993–1996, DEC’s mixed-architecture VAX/Alpha OpenVMS clusters became a cornerstone of its Alpha migration story, allowing existing VAXcluster customers to add Alpha nodes and preserve their clustering investments while moving workloads gradually. This compatibility was heavily emphasized in DEC and later Compaq/HPE OpenVMS materials and remained a key differentiator of the platform, validating the claim that such mixed clusters were among the most critical customer-focused moves of that period, even though they did not reverse DEC’s broader commercial decline.
