## Quote

> The client/server application market is wide open. A lot of companies haven't delivered products yet, and the reality is that purchases won't be made until 1994,

## Rationale

In 1993, most enterprises were still experimenting with client/server architectures, and large-scale adoption of client/server business applications, including financial suites, ramped materially in 1994–1996 with products such as SAP R/3, PeopleSoft Financials, and Oracle’s client/server financials. While some early client/server financial systems were purchased before 1994, the main wave of broad, enterprise-wide purchasing and competitive vendor activity did in fact emerge around 1994 and later, so his characterization of the market as still wide open in 1993 and poised for real purchasing starting in 1994 was directionally accurate, though somewhat overstated in implying almost no 1993 buying.
