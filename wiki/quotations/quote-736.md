# Computerworld — 1993-05-03

**Headline**: Sybase sets its own direction

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=2, confidence=3, horizon=SH-5y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> Clearly, they can differentiate themselves by finding a better model for distributed enterprise computing, and that's what they believe they've got with System 10.

## Rationale

Sybase System 10 did introduce notable distributed and client/server capabilities, including integrated replication and tools aimed at multi-node, heterogeneous enterprise environments, which provided some technical differentiation. However, between 1993 and the late 1990s Sybase’s overall database market share eroded as Oracle and Microsoft SQL Server became the dominant enterprise platforms, and System 10’s architecture did not translate into a sustained, market-defining advantage in distributed enterprise computing. By the end of the forecast window, Sybase was increasingly a niche player rather than a leader differentiated by a superior distributed model.
