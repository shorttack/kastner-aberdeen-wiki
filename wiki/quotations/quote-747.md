**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=5, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> how the competitive market pressured IBM to add stored procedures capabilities to the next release of DB2

## Rationale

Kastner’s claim that competitive pressure would push IBM to add stored procedures to the next DB2 release was borne out when DB2 for MVS/ESA Version 4 introduced stored procedures as a headline feature in the mid‑1990s.[2][5] This release arrived roughly a year after the 1994 article, and IBM’s own and independent historical accounts consistently describe Version 4 as the point where DB2 for z/OS first gained stored procedure support, aligning with market trends set earlier by Oracle, Sybase, and SQL Server.[2][5][7]
