## Rationale

Within three years of 1994, Tandem did in fact push the NonStop line toward greater openness, including extending the NonStop Kernel with Open System Services (OSS), a POSIX-compliant Unix environment, and adding more standard interfaces and tools. They also introduced ServerNet interconnect technology and worked on interoperability with mainstream platforms such as large Windows NT Server networks, which reduced the architectural isolation of classic NonStop systems even though the underlying architecture remained proprietary and the company was acquired by Compaq in 1997.
