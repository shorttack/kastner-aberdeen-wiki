## Rationale

Kastner suggested that the forthcoming System 11 release would materially help Sybase break through a performance stumbling block and revive its fortunes within the then-current cycle, but Sybase’s next major releases (System 10 and then System 11/ASE 11) arrived later than expected and were widely viewed as troubled by bugs and mixed performance gains. Through the mid-1990s, Sybase continued to lose market share and struggle financially against Oracle and Microsoft SQL Server despite these releases, indicating that any performance improvements did not decisively remove the obstacle or restore momentum in the short term. Sybase did remain a credible high-performance OLTP database vendor, but the prediction that this release would significantly get them past the year’s performance issues and revive the business within the forecast horizon did not hold up well.
