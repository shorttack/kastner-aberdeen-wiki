# The Christian Science Monitor — 2002-10-21

**Headline**: A move to build PCs without Windows

**Verdict**: LOW prescience — score=1, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> I have been out on a limb saying 5 percent of the market could be on Linux a year from now.

## Rationale

Linux never reached anything close to 5% of the global desktop OS market within a year of 2002; contemporary measurements through the mid‑2000s kept desktop Linux in the low single digits and below that threshold according to industry trackers. Even by March 2025, over two decades later, Linux (excluding ChromeOS) is only reported at about 4.2% of the global desktop OS market, still under 5%.[3] Recent data from 2025–2026 likewise shows Linux around 3–5% of desktops worldwide, underscoring that a rapid jump to 5% in a single year from 2002 was far too aggressive.[1][2][3][4]
