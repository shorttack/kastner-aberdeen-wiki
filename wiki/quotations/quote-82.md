## Quote

> CIOs will ask: 'If Sybase doesn't do warehousing well, do I want them to do my OLTP [online transaction processing]?'

## Rationale

From 1992–1995 Sybase remained a respected OLTP platform in financial services and other transaction-heavy industries, even as it lagged in decision-support and warehousing relative to Oracle and specialized vendors. Many enterprises happily ran OLTP on Sybase while using other products for warehousing, and Sybase’s later decline is generally attributed to product-quality issues, competition, and platform shifts rather than a broad CIO backlash tying its OLTP credibility to data-warehousing weakness. The predicted tight linkage between warehousing shortcomings and CIO willingness to trust Sybase for OLTP did not become a dominant decision criterion within the three-year horizon.
