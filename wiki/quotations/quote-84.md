## Quote

> Without a doubt they have become a client/server player. They are currently in year two of a five-year plan to change from a company almost exclusively dependent on a mainframe-oriented customer to be a sales leader of client/server applications through all channels,

## Rationale

Computer Associates did broaden its portfolio in the early-to-mid 1990s with client/server products such as CA-OpenIngres, Unicenter, and various development tools, so it was no longer exclusively mainframe-focused by around 1995. However, it never became a broad "sales leader" in client/server applications; markets for relational databases and client/server platforms in that period were dominated by vendors like Oracle, Microsoft, Sybase, IBM, and SAP. CA continued to derive a large share of revenue and strategic positioning from mainframe systems management and utilities into the 2000s, so the scale of transformation implied by the five-year plan did not materialize within the forecast horizon.
