# Computerworld — 1989-02-20

**Headline**: Users finding ESA alternatives

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> A lot of 4381 users are saying they don't really need ESA,

## Rationale

From 1989 to the early 1990s, ESA/370 and MVS/ESA were adopted first and most aggressively by large IBM 3090 and later ES/9000-class mainframe shops, while many lower-end 4300/4381 customers stayed on 370/XA-era hardware and operating systems or deferred upgrades to ESA-capable ES/4381 models. Smaller 4381 sites often lacked workloads needing ESA features like extended addressing and dataspaces and instead either sweated existing 4381 assets or migrated to AS/400 and emerging UNIX/RISC servers rather than paying for ESA migrations in that three-year window. Over the longer term ESA became the baseline for IBM mainframes, but within roughly three years of the 1989 article many low-end 4381 users indeed behaved as if they did not yet ‘need’ ESA, making the short-horizon claim largely prescient.
