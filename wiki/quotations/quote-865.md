## Quote

> Unisys has quietly become a formidable player in the Unix market through its OEM strategy,

## Rationale

Between 1989 and roughly 1992, the commercial Unix market was dominated by Sun (SPARC/SunOS/Solaris), HP (HP-UX), IBM (RS/6000 with AIX), and DEC, while Unisys’ OEM-based U-series/U6000 Unix systems remained niche and rarely appeared as leading platforms in market-share or mindshare analyses. Unisys spent this period struggling with heavy losses, layoffs, and restructurings and ultimately refocused on its mainframe base and services rather than emerging as a sustained, top-tier Unix vendor, so the characterization of it as a “formidable player in the Unix market” did not hold up over the three-year horizon.
