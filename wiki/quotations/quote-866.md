# Computerworld — 1989-02-27

**Headline**: Unisys U series casts shadow in Unix arena

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=2, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> They can now address anybody's requirements at a competitive price.

## Rationale

Although Unisys did expand its Unix-based U series and distributed systems line around 1989, it did not become a leading Unix supplier able to meet “anybody’s requirements” across the rapidly growing workstation and server market. By the early 1990s, Sun, HP, IBM, and DEC dominated commercial Unix, while Unisys remained better known for proprietary A-series and 2200 mainframes and soon began pivoting toward services rather than competing head‑to‑head on broad, price‑competitive Unix hardware. Within roughly three years, Unisys’ Unix offerings were niche rather than broadly competitive across the market segments implied by the claim.
