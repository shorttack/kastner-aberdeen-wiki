# Computerworld — 1989-04-03

**Headline**: Tandem goes to Relational DBMS tools

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> It should improve the overall usability of Tandem products,

## Rationale

Tandem’s adoption and maturation of NonStop SQL in the late 1980s and early 1990s did in fact make its systems more approachable by letting developers and DBAs use a familiar, standardized relational language instead of Tandem-specific Enscribe and OS interfaces. As SQL tools, training, and third-party products spread, application development on Tandem became easier and the proprietary operating system was increasingly hidden behind the database layer, matching Kastner’s expectation within a few years. The claim is somewhat general and Tandem systems remained specialized and complex, so this is best scored as largely rather than extraordinarily prescient.
