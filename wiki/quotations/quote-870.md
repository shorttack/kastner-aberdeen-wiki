## Quote

> The number of companies using executive workstations are few and far between.

## Rationale

In 1989, dedicated executive information systems and executive workstations from vendors such as Comshare, Pilot, and others were indeed niche, typically deployed only in a limited number of large enterprises rather than broadly across the market. Over the subsequent three years, adoption of specialized executive workstations remained limited as most organizations favored standard PCs, spreadsheets, and emerging client–server decision-support tools, and the classic EIS/executive workstation concept never achieved wide penetration. This makes Kastner’s characterization of their rarity directionally and substantively accurate for the 3-year horizon, even though it was primarily a description of the then-current state rather than a strong forward-looking prediction.
