## Quote

> We are projecting a further consolidation of hardware vendors in the industry,

## Rationale

From 1989 to roughly 1992, the computer hardware market did see continued consolidation, with vendors merging or exiting under pressure from standardized Unix servers, IBM mainframes, and the growing Wintel ecosystem. Examples include large-system and workstation vendors being acquired or merged (such as the HP–Apollo and Siemens–Nixdorf combinations) and several minicomputer and workstation specialists retrenching or disappearing, so the directional call on further hardware vendor consolidation was broadly correct over the stated horizon. The prediction was not especially contrarian or specific, but the industry structure did evolve in the way implied, with fewer, larger hardware suppliers dominating Fortune 1000 accounts by the early 1990s.
