# Computerworld — 1989-07-24

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P1_tiebreak

## Quote

> and you will see it on the software side as well.

## Rationale

Kastner’s prediction that consolidation would occur on the software side as well as hardware was largely borne out, especially in the early 1990s as major enterprise-application and DB2-focused vendors merged or were acquired (for example, the 1990 merger of McCormack & Dodge and Management Science America into Dun & Bradstreet Software). Within roughly three years of 1989, the number of significant mainframe financial and HR application suppliers for large IBM shops had begun to shrink, and this consolidation wave continued through the decade. The forecast was directionally accurate, though it did not anticipate how thoroughly later client-server and ERP suites would reshape the playing field beyond DB2-specific software houses.
