# Computerworld — 1989-08-28

**Headline**: PRODUCT SPOTLIGHT; The DEC market

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> IBM is still trying to plug IBM stuff together.

## Rationale

From 1989 to roughly 1992, IBM’s flagship integration effort, Systems Application Architecture (SAA), remained almost entirely focused on unifying IBM’s own platforms such as MVS, AS/400, and OS/2 rather than embracing true multivendor interoperability. Over this period IBM continued to push proprietary technologies like SNA networking and OS/2/PS/2 integration, while industry momentum in open, multivendor environments centered on UNIX, X11/Motif, and TCP/IP stacks where IBM was more of a follower, so the quip that IBM was still trying mainly to plug IBM components together remained an accurate characterization over the three‑year horizon.
