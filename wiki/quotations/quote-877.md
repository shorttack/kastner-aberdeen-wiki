# Computerworld — 1989-10-09

**Headline**: DEC challenges IBM CASE strategy

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=2, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> This is a horse race, and DEC is literally lengths ahead,

## Rationale

Within three years of 1989, Digital Equipment’s CASE toolset (e.g., DECset and related VAX/VMS development tools) never emerged as the clear market leader over IBM’s AD/Cycle or IBM’s broader mainframe-oriented application development ecosystem, which continued to dominate large enterprises. Over the early 1990s, the CASE market fragmented and leadership attention shifted to specialized CASE vendors and then to client/server and PC-based tools, while DEC entered a period of financial crisis and retrenchment that prevented it from capitalizing on any transient lead in installed CASE users. In retrospect, describing DEC as “literally lengths ahead” of IBM in this “horse race” overstates its sustained advantage and is mostly contradicted by how the CASE and enterprise development tool markets evolved by the early to mid-1990s.
