## Quote

> The bigger context is that the things you used to get for free when you bought a mainframe 20 years ago you are now paying for,

## Rationale

Kastner’s remark captures the shift from bundled mainframe-era practices to the unbundled, fee-based model for software, maintenance, networking, and disaster-recovery services that solidified in the late 1980s and early 1990s. By the early 1990s, IBM and its peers were consistently selling support, DR services, networking assistance, and software licenses as distinct revenue lines rather than as ‘free’ add-ons to hardware, and IBM’s services business (which evolved into IBM Global Services) became a central growth area. The statement is more an accurate characterization of an emerging structural change than a risky forecast, but the economic pattern it describes clearly dominated the following years.
