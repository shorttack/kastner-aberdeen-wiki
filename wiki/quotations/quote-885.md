## Quote

> It looks like no one vendor will win the [graphical user interface] battles,

## Rationale

Within three years of 1990, Microsoft’s Windows 3.0 (1990) and especially 3.1 (1992) had become the de facto standard GUI on Intel-based PCs, with rapidly growing corporate adoption and ISV ecosystems converging around Windows rather than a mix of competing interfaces. While Macintosh and X11-based Unix GUIs remained important in their niches and there was still visible diversity in 1993, the overall trajectory already pointed toward a single dominant vendor in the mainstream GUI “battles,” contrary to the claim that no one vendor would win. The prediction thus understated how quickly Windows would pull ahead, even in the stated short horizon, though it captured that multiple GUI environments still co-existed technically and in specific segments.
