## Quote

> punch their way out of the midrange niche.

## Rationale

Within three years of 1990, HP’s PA-RISC–based HP 9000 Series 800 and emerging T-Class servers, running HP-UX with more sophisticated systems management, were sold and marketed explicitly as mainframe-class, mission‑critical UNIX systems rather than just midrange minicomputers. HP became a leading enterprise UNIX server vendor in large corporate data centers alongside IBM and Sun, so it effectively escaped a narrow midrange niche even though IBM mainframes still dominated the very top end of the market.
