## Quote

> Labor accounts for 60% of the MIS budget,

## Rationale

By the early 1990s and well beyond, multiple TCO and IT finance studies consistently found that personnel and operations consumed the majority of IT/MIS spending, while hardware and even software represented a minority share of lifecycle costs. As computing hardware and packaged software became cheaper and more powerful through the 1990s, the relative weight of staff-intensive activities—operations, administration, help desk, and application maintenance—remained high or even grew, making a ~60% labor share a reasonable and enduring characterization even several years after 1990. The exact percentage varied by organization and year, but the core assertion that labor dominated MIS budgets held up strongly over time.
