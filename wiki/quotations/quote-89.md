# Computerworld — 1992-06-01

**Headline**: DEC energizes Unix OLTP line

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=5, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> DEC needs to make an offering available on Ultrix now, even though the company's strategic Unix operating system will ultimately be OSF/1, said Peter Kastner, a vice president at Aberdeen Group, a market research firm in Boston.

## Rationale

Kastner’s characterization that OSF/1 would be DEC’s strategic Unix proved accurate: DEC OSF/1 was ported to the Alpha AXP line in 1993 and positioned as the company’s flagship 64-bit Unix, while Ultrix on VAX and MIPS was effectively put into maintenance and then discontinued. By 1995, within the 3‑year horizon, DEC had rebranded OSF/1 AXP as Digital UNIX and treated it as the primary Unix platform for Alpha systems, confirming that OSF/1, not Ultrix, became DEC’s strategic Unix offering even as legacy Ultrix offerings persisted for a transitional period.
