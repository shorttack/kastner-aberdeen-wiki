## Quote

> It used to be that you could anticipate workstation prices and changes on an annual basis. Now, you can't even go on vacation without a change in the market,

## Rationale

From 1990–1993, workstation vendors such as Sun, SGI, HP, IBM, and DEC engaged in rapid product refreshes and frequent price cuts, with price/performance improving so quickly that configurations were often repriced multiple times per year instead of on an annual cycle. The broader 1990s saw computer equipment prices falling sharply and product cycles shortening, confirming that hardware markets became increasingly volatile and that buyers could no longer rely on predictable, annualized pricing.
