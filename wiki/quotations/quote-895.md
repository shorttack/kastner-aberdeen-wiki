# Computerworld — 1991-07-22

**Headline**: IBM, Intel, Dell announce benchmark group formation

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> by putting pressure on the suppliers to bring their benchmarks up to snuff.

## Rationale

Within three years of 1991, benchmark consortia like BAPCo (with SYSmark 92/94) and established suites such as SPECint92/SPECfp92 and TPC-C were widely used to compare systems and CPUs, and vendors actively competed to improve their published scores. OEMs and chip suppliers tuned hardware configurations, compilers, and system designs specifically to raise benchmark results, and marketing material prominently featured these scores, so benchmarks did in fact exert pressure on suppliers to be "up to snuff," even if that effect was somewhat obvious in retrospect.
