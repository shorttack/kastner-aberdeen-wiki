## Quote

> I see no letup in reductions in pricing for at least another six to nine months,

## Rationale

Kastner’s short-term forecast that PC price cuts would continue for at least another 6–9 months after mid-1991 proved accurate, as Intel kept reducing 486 prices into 1992 and mail-order clone vendors used ongoing discounts to gain share. Industry coverage in 1992–1993 described a continuing PC price war with no slowdown, indicating that not only did price reductions persist through his minimum horizon, they continued well beyond it.
