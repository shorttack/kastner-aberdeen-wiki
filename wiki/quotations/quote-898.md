# Computerworld — 1991-09-30

**Headline**: Sun seeks Intel Unix

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=2, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> The acquisition is more than hedging Sun's bets; it will make Sun's schedule possible,

## Rationale

Kastner’s claim tied the Interactive Systems acquisition directly to Sun meeting a specific mid‑1992 schedule for a Unix release running on both Intel and SPARC. In practice, Solaris 2.0 shipped for SPARC in mid‑1992, but the Intel/x86 version (Solaris 2.1 for x86) lagged and did not appear until late 1992 or early 1993, missing the implied synchronized mid‑1992 date. While the Interactive acquisition did contribute to Sun’s eventual Intel Unix offerings, it did not make the original schedule possible in the precise way suggested, so the prediction was mostly wrong rather than completely contradicted by events.
