## Quote

> They seem to have come up with the best of both worlds,

## Rationale

Kastner’s optimism that the ACE UNIX environment would deliver “the best of both worlds” and standardize features across Ultrix, OSF/1, and SVR4 did not materialize within three years. The ACE consortium unraveled by the early–mid 1990s, its unified MIPS/UNIX vision never gained significant market traction, and vendors continued to pursue divergent UNIX variants such as Solaris, HP-UX, AIX, and Digital UNIX. Rather than buying benefiting from a standardized ACE UNIX, the UNIX wars and fragmentation persisted through the mid-1990s, with de facto consolidation and standardization only emerging later around POSIX/SUS and especially Linux.
