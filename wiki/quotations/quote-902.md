## Rationale

The statement is a normative management judgment about what Sun 'needs' to do, not a concrete, testable prediction about future technology, products, or market outcomes. Between 1992 and 1995 Sun did roll out new SPARCstation and early UltraSPARC workstations and maintained an active Solaris and tools ecosystem, but there is no clear, falsifiable way to evaluate whether its actions specifically prevented the 'developer camp' from becoming restless or whether such restlessness would have occurred otherwise. Because the claim is too vague and counterfactual to measure against documented history, it cannot be meaningfully scored for prescience.
