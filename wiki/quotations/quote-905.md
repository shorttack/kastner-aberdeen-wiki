## Quote

## Rationale

Between 1992 and roughly 1995, a few vendors and large organizations experimented with take‑back and recycling programs for NiCd batteries and toner cartridges, but broad internal corporate mandates to recycle these items were not yet common. Stronger, more generalized obligations emerged later, with measures like the 1996 U.S. Mercury-Containing and Rechargeable Battery Management Act and the expansion of corporate environmental management and e‑waste policies through the late 1990s and 2000s. The prediction correctly anticipated the long‑term direction, but within the 3‑year horizon it overstated the speed and breadth of corporate recycling mandates.
