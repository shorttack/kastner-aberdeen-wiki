## Quote

> I can get any one of several independent databases today, and they all have good, strong implementations under AIX.

## Rationale

By the early 1990s, AIX was a major Unix platform for commercial databases, and vendors like Oracle, Informix, Sybase, and Ingres all shipped mature, fully supported AIX implementations used in production on RS/6000 systems. Through roughly 1992–1995, these third‑party databases remained the primary choice for many AIX shops even after IBM’s own DB2 for AIX (DB2/6000) arrived, confirming that multiple independent databases with strong AIX support were indeed available and viable. The statement is slightly more descriptive than predictive, but its implied claim that robust non‑IBM options existed and would suffice over the near term held up well.
