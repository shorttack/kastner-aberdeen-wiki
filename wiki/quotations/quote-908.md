# Computerworld — 1992-06-01

**Headline**: DEC energizes Unix OLTP line

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> It should not be difficult for DEC to do the port to Alpha once that environment is production-ready.

## Rationale

DEC delivered its 64-bit Alpha platform with DEC OSF/1 AXP (later Digital UNIX/Tru64) in production form by 1993, and most of its core system software stacks, including OpenVMS and its TP infrastructure, were ported relatively quickly and without widely reported, intractable engineering crises. The ACMS transaction processing monitor and related middleware were successfully brought onto OpenVMS Alpha, and DEC made Alpha the primary target for its strategic Unix and VMS offerings within a few years of launch. While the Unix OLTP business on Alpha never became dominant in the market, the technical task of porting DEC’s environments to Alpha did not prove unusually difficult, so the prediction about porting effort was largely borne out within the 3-year horizon.
