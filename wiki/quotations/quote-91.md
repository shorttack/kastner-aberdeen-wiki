# Computerworld — 1992-06-08

**Headline**: 3,700 depart in DEC retirement plan

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=5, confidence=3, horizon=SH-5y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> “DEC is focused on Alpha as the answer to all future questions, and they're willing to rewrite history at the very dear expense of their [Mips Computer Systems, Inc.-based] customers,'' Kastner said.

## Rationale

Within five years of 1992, DEC had effectively terminated its MIPS-based DECstation workstation line and bet the company strategy on the Alpha architecture, offering only difficult migration paths rather than a smooth, cost-effective continuity for MIPS customers. Contemporary and retrospective accounts both highlight that DEC’s abrupt shift from MIPS to Alpha badly damaged customer confidence and stranded prior MIPS investments, matching Kastner’s warning that DEC would pursue Alpha at the dear expense of its MIPS-based customers.
