## Quote

> DEC's product sales have been deteriorating for the past several years. While the service business has been growing by around 18%, it's not enough to take up the slack.

## Rationale

Throughout the early 1990s, DEC’s traditional hardware and VAX system product sales kept declining, driving revenue drops, losses, and repeated restructuring despite major cost-cutting.[1] DEC’s services and support operations did grow and became a more important part of the business, but this shift failed to restore sustained profitability, leaving the company in a weakened position that ultimately led to its 1998 acquisition by Compaq.[1] This shows that deteriorating product sales were not offset by expanding services in the three years after 1992 or thereafter, matching the analyst’s assessment.[1]
