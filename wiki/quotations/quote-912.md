## Quote

> DEC is focused on Alpha as the answer to all future questions, and they're willing to rewrite history at the very dear expense of their [Mips Computer Systems, Inc.-based] customers,

## Rationale

Within three years of 1992, DEC had clearly bet the company's future on the 64-bit Alpha architecture, positioning it as the successor to both VAX and its Unix workstation lines.[5][11] DEC rapidly phased out its MIPS-based DECstation workstations in favor of AlphaStation and AlphaServer systems, leaving MIPS customers with limited upgrade paths and strong pressure to migrate to Alpha or other Unix vendors.[5][10] This matches the analyst’s characterization that DEC was focused on Alpha as the answer to future questions and was willing to disadvantage its existing MIPS customer base.
