## Rationale

Between 1992 and 1995, Oracle expanded aggressively beyond its core database licenses into Oracle Applications (ERP/financials, HR, manufacturing) and grew its consulting and support businesses, so its revenue mix moved steadily toward applications and services even though database licensing remained central. Over that three‑year window the shift did not overturn the basic license-plus-maintenance model, but the company’s strategic positioning and product portfolio clearly evolved in the applications- and services-driven direction Kastner described.
