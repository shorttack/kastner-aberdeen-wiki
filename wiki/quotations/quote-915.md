## Quote

> The 66-MHz DX2 vs. a 50-MHz chip should not be that much faster,

## Rationale

In practice, the 66 MHz 486DX2 delivered a clearly measurable but not transformative performance gain over a 50 MHz 486DX, with synthetic and application benchmarks typically showing on the order of a few tens of percent improvement rather than anything like a doubling. At the same time, his emphasis that memory and system bottlenecks would limit perceived speed aligned with how 486-era systems behaved and foreshadowed the later, well-recognized “memory wall” issue, so the spirit of caution about raw clock speeds was directionally sound even if he understated the DX2-66’s advantage over a 50 MHz part.
