## Quote

> Clearly, by the time this is all over, the industry is going to wish this had been the summer that never happened,

## Rationale

In the 1992–1993 PC price war triggered by Compaq’s cuts, average PC prices fell sharply and vendors saw margins compressed, leading to consolidation pressure and widespread concern about "profitless prosperity" for manufacturers.[4][7][10] Several second-tier PC makers struggled or exited over the mid-1990s as competition and low prices persisted, even as unit sales boomed and customers benefited, so the episode was painful for many suppliers but not an unambiguous disaster for the industry as a whole.[4][9][10]
