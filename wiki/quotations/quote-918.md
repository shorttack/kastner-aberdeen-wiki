## Quote

> IBM has never been a price leader. It looks like they're being dragged down kicking and screaming,

## Rationale

In the early 1990s, IBM’s PS/2 and later ValuePoint systems remained priced above clone competitors, and IBM responded to the 1992–1995 price war with reluctant but substantial price cuts that compressed its margins[1][11]. IBM never became a low-cost PC price leader during this period; instead its PC market share and profitability continued to deteriorate, setting the stage for its long-term retreat from commodity PCs in favor of higher-value systems and, eventually, divestiture of the PC business[6][9][15].
