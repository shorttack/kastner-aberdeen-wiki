**Headline**: Streamlining restores Informix to health

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> They had a database, some office automation tools and [Innovative Software, Inc.'s] Wingz [graphical spreadsheet]. The synergy of those products wasn't all that obvious, and the company wasn't big enough to keep all those major product areas up to snuff,

## Rationale

Within a few years of 1992, Informix effectively exited the office tools and Wingz spreadsheet markets and doubled down on its core Unix relational database products such as Informix Online and the Dynamic Scalable Architecture (DSA) servers, confirming that it could not sustain multiple major, non-synergistic product lines. From roughly 1992–1995, Informix’s growth and competitive position came almost entirely from its database technologies rather than any integrated office/desktop suite, validating the view that the product mix lacked synergy and was too much for the company’s scale to keep competitive. The statement is somewhat descriptive rather than a bold forward forecast, but subsequent strategy and product cancellations align closely with the analyst’s characterization.
