## Quote

> The open systems planning arena is one where users are crying for planning directions and products,

## Rationale

From 1992–1995, large IT shops were indeed struggling to plan migrations from proprietary mainframes to “open systems” such as Unix variants and emerging Windows NT, fueling demand for methodologies, migration tools, and architecture guidance from vendors and consultancies. Enterprise-architecture frameworks and standards efforts like The Open Group’s early work and TOGAF’s first mid‑1990s releases explicitly targeted this planning gap, showing that users were looking for direction and products in exactly this arena. However, Kastner’s statement mainly describes an already-visible need rather than making a specific, risky forecast about how the market would evolve, so its prescience is real but limited.
