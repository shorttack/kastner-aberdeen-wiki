**Headline**: Stratus still rock solid as it moves CISC users to RISC

## Quote

> Stratus right now is in a state of transition; on the product side, the company is moving to a wide range of RISC-based products on both its proprietary VOS and Unix System V, Release 4 operating systems,

## Rationale

Within three years of late 1992, Stratus rolled out PA-RISC-based fault-tolerant systems (such as its Continuum line) and offered multiple RISC server models, making RISC a major focus of its hardware roadmap. Both its proprietary VOS and its Unix SVR4-derived environment (FTX/HP-UX) were available on these PA-RISC platforms, so the company was clearly in a real transition toward RISC even though CISC systems and customers continued to be supported beyond that window.
