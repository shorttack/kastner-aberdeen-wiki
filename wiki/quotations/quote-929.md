# Computerworld — 1992-11-09

**Headline**: DEC restructuring plan includes separate PC unit

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=3, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> DEC's PC business has caught fire this year, growing from 2% to 3% to close to 10% [of DEC's overall revenue] _ generating sales if not profits,

## Rationale

Kastner accurately captured that DEC’s PC business was growing in revenue terms and driving sales but not meaningful profits, as DEC’s push into commodity Intel-based PCs in the early 1990s produced volume without restoring overall profitability. Over the following three years, DEC remained financially weak, went through repeated restructurings and layoffs, and its PC line never achieved strong margins or major market share against Compaq, IBM, and HP, so the idea of a sustainably “on fire” business did not hold up even though the short-term sales surge was real.
