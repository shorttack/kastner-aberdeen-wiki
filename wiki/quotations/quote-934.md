# Computerworld — 1992-12-21

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> We're finding that gateway technology is definitely going into production,

## Rationale

Between 1992 and roughly 1995, middleware and gateway products such as database gateways (e.g., Oracle and IBM mainframe/database gateways), protocol-conversion gateways (TCP/IP-to-SNA, NetWare/IP, LAN Manager-to-NetWare), and email gateways (linking LAN-based mail with X.400 and Internet SMTP) moved rapidly from pilots into mainstream production in large enterprises. This period coincides with the client/server boom, where packaged integration and gateway products became a standard part of enterprise architectures rather than experimental tools. The statement is somewhat observational and near-term, but it correctly captures that gateway technology was not just theoretical and was broadly entering production use in the early to mid‑1990s.
