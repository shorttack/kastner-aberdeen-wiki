# Computerworld — 1992-12-21

**Headline**: Middleware speeds client connection

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=5, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> That means that users are getting their sleeves rolled up to do client/server applications.

## Rationale

From roughly 1992–1995, enterprises did in fact "roll up their sleeves" and build large amounts of custom client/server software, enabled by database gateways and middleware and tools such as PowerBuilder, Visual Basic, and Oracle client/server development environments, as client/server architectures moved into mainstream production use[1][5]. Within three years of the quote, client/server networks and applications had become a dominant enterprise computing model and a major focus of corporate IT projects, validating the observation that users themselves were actively developing client/server applications rather than waiting solely for packaged solutions[1][5].
