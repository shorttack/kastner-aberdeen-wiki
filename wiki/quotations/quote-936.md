**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> SQL is like Victorian children: It should not be seen or heard,

## Rationale

Within about three years of 1992, mainstream client/server development heavily adopted tools such as PowerBuilder, Gupta SQLWindows, Oracle Forms, Visual Basic data controls, Delphi, and various 4GLs that auto-generated most CRUD SQL from higher-level form and report definitions, so many business developers interacted with databases without writing much raw SQL. From the 2000s onward, dominant application stacks increasingly hid SQL behind ORMs and database abstraction layers (Hibernate, Rails ActiveRecord, Django ORM, Entity Framework), although SQL skills remained essential for DBAs, tuning, and complex queries, meaning SQL became less visible to most front-end developers but was never completely “unseen or unheard.”
