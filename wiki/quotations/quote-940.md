## Quote

> Bringing these products over to Unix means not only a port but also opening them up to work in the interoperable world of open systems,

## Rationale

Kastner’s point that moving mainframe products to Unix would require more than a straight port and instead demand opening them up for interoperable open systems was borne out by the mid‑1990s, as vendors had to re‑architect for client/server, POSIX, TCP/IP networking, and standard interfaces like SQL/ODBC and later CORBA rather than just recompiling code. Software AG’s Adabas/Natural and related middleware on Unix evolved over several years into fuller open-systems offerings that integrated with heterogeneous environments, validating that this transition took time and methodical engineering rather than a quick lift‑and‑shift. The statement is somewhat generic, but directionally accurate about the real complexity and timeline of mainframe-to-Unix migration in that 3‑year window.
