## Quote

> If users want to enter into enterprisewide development projects, version control is mandatory,

## Rationale

By the mid‑1990s, serious multi-developer, enterprisewide client/server projects almost universally adopted source-code control tools such as Intersolv PVCS, Atria ClearCase, and later Microsoft Visual SourceSafe as mandatory infrastructure. Configuration management and version control were formalized in methodologies (e.g., CMM) and became a de facto requirement for large-scale enterprise application development through the late 1990s and 2000s, validating Kastner’s characterization that version control is mandatory for such projects.
