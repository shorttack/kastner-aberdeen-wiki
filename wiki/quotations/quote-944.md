## Rationale

Within three years of 1993, CA and Microsoft were actively co-marketing Unicenter on Windows NT, and Unicenter for NT became an important part of CA’s systems-management story as NT server adoption surged in large enterprises. CA introduced NT-focused Unicenter capabilities (including the TNG generation) and positioned them specifically to existing mainframe Unicenter customers who were experimenting with downsizing and client/server architectures, indicating substantial interest and pilots even if full migrations were limited. However, precise data on what fraction of mainframe Unicenter sites actually evaluated the NT version is lacking, so the directional call about broad ‘tire-kicking’ looks right, but the quantified “more than 50%” cannot be firmly verified.
