## Quote

> The client/server application market is wide open. A lot of companies haven't delivered products yet, and the reality is that purchases won't be made until 1994,

## Rationale

In 1993 the client/server application market—particularly for packaged financial and ERP software—was still immature, with many vendors only then rolling out offerings and relatively few enterprises having fully deployed such systems. The main purchasing wave for client/server financial and business applications did in fact ramp up from roughly 1994 onward as products like SAP R/3, PeopleSoft, and Oracle Applications gained traction, so the prediction that most purchases would wait until 1994 was directionally and temporally sound, even though some early adopters bought earlier.
