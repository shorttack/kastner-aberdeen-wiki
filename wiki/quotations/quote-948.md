# Computerworld — 1993-05-03

**Headline**: Microsoft, D&B sign on NT applications

## Quote

> Working with Microsoft will yield a superior product for D&B, who is behind some of their competitors but ahead of their customers in that they are just now grasping the opportunities for enterprise downsized [decision support systems].

## Rationale

Within three years, D&B Software was struggling against fast-growing enterprise competitors such as SAP, Oracle, and PeopleSoft and was sold to Geac in 1996, indicating that its Microsoft-aligned offerings did not translate into a clearly superior market position or product franchise. Over the same period, Windows NT and client/server decision support did gain traction, so the idea that many traditional D&B customers were only beginning to grasp downsized enterprise DSS was directionally accurate, but this did not materially elevate D&B’s standing relative to rivals.
