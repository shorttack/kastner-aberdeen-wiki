# Computerworld — 1992-07-13

**Headline**: Price war to hurt PC industry

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=3, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> It looks like they're being dragged down kicking and screaming,'' Kastner said.

## Rationale

Kastner’s remark characterizes IBM as a reluctant follower in PC price-cutting, and over the following year IBM did participate in the early‑1990s price war but trailed more aggressive discounters like Compaq and the clone vendors. IBM’s PS/2 line remained premium-priced and lost share in the early 1990s, reinforcing the view that IBM was uncomfortable with low-margin price competition rather than leading it. While this was more an accurate real-time description than a bold forward-looking prediction, the pattern of IBM being dragged into commodity pricing rather than embracing it continued as IBM’s PC business weakened and ultimately exited the market in the 2000s.
