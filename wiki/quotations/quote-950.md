**Headline**: Sybase sets its own direction

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=2, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> Sybase has always been known for blazing their own technology path and not necessarily following the rest of the industry,

## Rationale

Sybase did help pioneer commercial client/server relational databases in the late 1980s and early 1990s, so there was some basis for saying it had charted its own path up to 1993. However, over the 1993–1996 horizon its core database architecture and product roadmap converged with the mainstream RDBMS market, while competitors like Oracle, IBM, and Microsoft drove many of the major new directions (e.g., Windows NT, large-scale data warehousing) and Sybase lost share. By the mid‑1990s, industry perception was less that Sybase was blazing a unique technology trail and more that it was a solid but struggling follower, so the strong form of this characterization did not really hold up.
