**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> probably the most important thing [DEC] could do for their customers this year,

## Rationale

DEC’s ability to run mixed VAX/Alpha OpenVMS clusters did become a central mechanism for customers to test and adopt Alpha incrementally between 1993 and 1996, preserving investments in existing VAX systems while adding new Alpha capacity. Over this period, AlphaServers gained a reputation for high performance and began to make inroads in DEC’s installed base, largely via gradual migrations rather than forklift replacements. However, while crucial for existing DEC customers, this did not reverse DEC’s overall competitive decline, so calling it the single most important thing they could do slightly overstates its ultimate impact.
