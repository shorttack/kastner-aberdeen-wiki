## Quote

> It means that those customers can plan to install mixed clusters almost immediately and that their investment in clustering has been protected,

## Rationale

DEC’s OpenVMS Cluster (VMScluster) on Alpha did in fact support mixed VAX/Alpha configurations in the mid-1990s, and customers were able to deploy these mixed clusters in production shortly after the announcement. This provided a clear, widely used upgrade path that preserved existing clustering investments—hardware, software, and operational models—for many years, well within and beyond the three-year horizon, even as DEC transitioned to Alpha and later passed through Compaq to HP. The specific promise of protecting prior clustering investments via mixed clusters was thus fulfilled both technically and commercially in that period.
