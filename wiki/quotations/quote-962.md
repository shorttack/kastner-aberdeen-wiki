## Quote

> And if FTX is not perceived as rock-solid, that kind of blows away the whole marketing rationale

## Rationale

Stratus’ FTX Unix line never displaced its proprietary VOS systems in the core financial-transaction and SNA-hosting niches in the mid‑1990s, and banks largely continued to view VOS/mainframe platforms as the more rock‑solid choice. FTX-based fault-tolerant Unix servers remained a niche and were eventually overshadowed by Stratus’ later x86 ftServer products running Windows and Linux, suggesting that whatever marketing rationale existed for FTX as a high-reliability SNA host did not translate into broad market traction. However, specific evidence tying FTX’s limited success directly to perceived reliability shortfalls versus other factors (SNA’s decline, shifts to TCP/IP, and competitive SNA gateways) is sparse, so the causal claim can only be partially validated.
