## Rationale

DEC’s VAXft fault‑tolerant systems remained a niche product and never approached a several hundred million dollar annual business, with limited sales and minimal impact compared to Tandem and Stratus through the mid‑1990s. The VAXft line was effectively discontinued within a few years and DEC’s overall decline culminated in its acquisition by Compaq in 1998, confirming that the scale implied by the original expectation was not achieved. Thus, the characterization of VAXft as a prospective several hundred million dollar per year business was contradicted by subsequent market outcomes.
