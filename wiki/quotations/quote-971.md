## Quote

> can get several months or even a year ahead of the general public on figuring out how a new technology may or may not fit into their operations,

## Rationale

From the mid-1990s onward, enterprises that served as beta sites for technologies such as Windows NT, Lotus Notes, and early web servers and groupware platforms typically piloted these systems well before broad corporate and consumer adoption, gaining a substantial head start in understanding integration, process change, and support needs. The same dynamic persisted in later waves—design-partner customers for SaaS applications, public cloud platforms like AWS, mobile operating systems, and machine-learning services accumulated practical experience months to years ahead of the mainstream. Kastner’s claim that beta testers can be several months to about a year ahead of the general public in figuring out how technology fits their operations is broadly borne out, though in some cases the lead time was even longer than he implied.
