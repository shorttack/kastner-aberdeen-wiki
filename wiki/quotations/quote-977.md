## Quote

> Buyers should not be fooled you don't get a lot of support with a shrink-wrapped product,

## Rationale

Through the mid‑1990s, popular shrink‑wrapped development tools such as Microsoft Visual Basic, Borland Delphi, and PowerBuilder were sold at relatively low prices with limited bundled vendor support, typically offering manuals, patches, and paid-per-incident assistance rather than extensive hand-holding. In contrast, higher-priced enterprise platforms from vendors like IBM, Oracle, and SAP routinely packaged substantial implementation help, training, and ongoing support into large contracts, and this basic divide between low-cost mass-market tools with minimal direct support and high-end platforms justified partly by intensive vendor support continued into the 2000s, validating Kastner’s warning within and beyond the 3‑year horizon.
