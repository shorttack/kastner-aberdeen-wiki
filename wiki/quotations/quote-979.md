## Quote

> IBM is belatedly waking up to the fact that Compaq [Computer Corp.] is walking off the playing field with the server marketplace just when it seems to be taking off,

## Rationale

Within three years, Compaq had become a clear leader in PC-compatible servers, with its ProLiant line driving strong growth and share gains in the Intel-based server segment, while IBM’s PS/2 and early PC Server offerings lagged.[2][4][7] Over the same period, IBM’s server strength was concentrated in proprietary platforms like AS/400 and mainframes rather than the rapidly growing commodity PC server market, and it took the rest of the decade and the Netfinity/xSeries line for IBM to mount a strong x86 competitive response.[3][8][15] This makes the quote directionally accurate about Compaq "walking off the playing field" in the emerging volume server space, though somewhat overstated for the overall server market where IBM remained a major player.[2][3]
