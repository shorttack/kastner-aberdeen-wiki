## Quote

> IBM really needed to jump-start its server business.

## Rationale

By 1994, Compaq had taken a clear lead in Intel-based servers while IBM’s PS/2 and PC Server lines lagged, even though IBM remained strong in mainframes and AS/400. Over the next three years the x86 server market grew rapidly, IBM introduced refreshed PC Server and AS/400e offerings to stay relevant, and servers became a strategic battleground, confirming that revitalizing its server business was indeed necessary, but this was more an obvious contemporaneous assessment than a sharply predictive insight.
