# Computerworld — 1994-06-20

**Headline**: PC servers join pricing free fall

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=3, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> One-third of the price of servers is in the CPU and memory,

## Rationale

In the mid-1990s, CPU and DRAM were indeed major portions of x86 server bills of materials, and falling Intel CPU prices combined with commodity DRAM trends contributed meaningfully to declining prices for PC servers between roughly 1994 and 1997. However, the exact “one-third” ratio was only a rough rule of thumb, and other cost drivers such as disks, chassis, networking, and bundled support and software remained large enough that system prices did not track CPU price declines in a simple, linear way.
