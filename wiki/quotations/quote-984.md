# Computerworld — 1994-08-15

**Headline**: Reaction split on CA's plan for ASK products

**Verdict**: MEDIUM prescience — score=3, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> Reading between the lines of CA's white papers, these are not maintenance releases CA has planned. They're planning to integrate ASK technologies into [their] broad product set and into the CA '90s architecture,

## Rationale

Within a few years of acquiring ASK in 1994, CA rebranded Ingres as CA-OpenIngres and tied it into elements of its broader portfolio such as CA-OpenROAD and the Unicenter management framework, which aligns with the prediction that ASK technologies would be integrated rather than merely put in maintenance mode. However, the integration was uneven: core database and tools saw some architectural alignment, while other ASK products like the MANMAN ERP line remained relatively isolated and over time CA treated much of the ASK/Ingres estate as a maintenance business, eventually spinning Ingres out in 2005, suggesting the prediction was only partially borne out even in spirit.
