## Rationale

Between 1994 and 1997 Tandem rolled out the MIPS-based NonStop Himalaya S- and K-series with ServerNet interconnects, improving price/performance and positioning the line more competitively for mainstream OLTP and commercial workloads. In the same period Tandem introduced POSIX/Open System Services, Unix-style interfaces, standard networking, and closer integration with Windows NT, so NonStop systems did become substantially more open than earlier Guardian-only generations, even though they remained a specialized proprietary platform.
