## Quote

> All companies will build [data warehouses] in the nextfive years,

## Rationale

By the 3–5 year window after 1994, data warehousing had become a major trend among large enterprises, but adoption was still in the diffusion phase rather than universal, as shown by late‑1990s research explicitly studying the *adoption and diffusion* of data warehousing technology rather than treating it as ubiquitous.[1] Even through the 2000s and 2010s, data warehouses became standard primarily for medium and large organizations, while many small firms continued to operate without formal warehouses, and the market for data warehousing and data‑warehouse‑as‑a‑service was still growing rather than saturated.[4][6][9] The quote was directionally right that data warehousing would become a core enterprise pattern, but its strong claim that *all companies* would build one within about five years was substantially overstated in both timing and universality.
