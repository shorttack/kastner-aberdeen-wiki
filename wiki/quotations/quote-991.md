## Quote

> It's not for the faint of heart and not for people who need applications to get into production right away,

## Rationale

From 1994 through roughly 1997, building three-tier client/server systems with amalgamated tools (PowerBuilder plus application servers, TP monitors, and heterogeneous databases) was widely regarded as complex, brittle, and demanding of highly skilled teams, not something organizations could usually push into production quickly. Most rapid-application-development success in that period remained in simpler two-tier client/server or departmental apps, while scalable three-tier deployments lagged and required substantial infrastructure and expertise, so his caution that it was not for the faint of heart or for rapid rollout largely held true over the stated horizon.
