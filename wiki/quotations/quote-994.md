# Computerworld — 1995-06-19

**Headline**: IBM, Lotus iron out agreement

**Verdict**: HIGH prescience — score=4, confidence=3, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> They will continue to whip their companies [and] continue to innovate so that IBM or anybody else can never catch up,

## Rationale

From 1995 to 1998, Microsoft and Intel aggressively iterated on Windows 95/NT and the Pentium Pro/Pentium II lines, cementing the Wintel PC platform as the de facto enterprise standard while IBM’s OS/2 and PC hardware strategies failed to regain leadership. IBM increasingly pivoted toward services and middleware in this period and did not come close to catching up to Microsoft or Intel in desktop operating systems or volume client computing, matching the short‑term thrust of the prediction. However, the absolutist “never catch up” framing is overstated in a longer historical sense, as subsequent waves like web, mobile, and cloud shifted the competitive landscape beyond the PC-centric race he was describing.
