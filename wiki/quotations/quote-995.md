## Quote

> So far as I can see, this arrangement doesn't compromise any functionality of either the Express [multidimensional DBMS] or Oracle,

## Rationale

Over the three years after Oracle acquired IRI in 1995, Oracle continued to ship **Express Server** as a distinct multidimensional engine while maintaining its core relational **Oracle Database** line (Oracle7 and then Oracle8), so neither product’s core functionality was immediately removed or folded into the other.[3][10] Oracle did not begin replacing Express with the integrated **Oracle OLAP** database option until the Oracle 9i timeframe in the early 2000s, which is beyond the stated three‑year horizon.[3][10] The longer‑term move to deprecate Express contradicts the spirit of long‑run independence, but within the short horizon the claim that the arrangement did not compromise either product’s functionality was largely borne out.
