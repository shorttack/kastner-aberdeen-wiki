# Computerworld — 1995-12-11

**Headline**: DBMS tool meets demand

**Verdict**: LOW prescience — score=1, confidence=2, horizon=SH-3y, pipeline=P2

## Quote

> No other vendor has anything close to this.

## Rationale

By the 1995–1998 horizon, other major RDBMS vendors had very comparable GUI-based administration suites, including Oracle Enterprise Manager, IBM DB2’s Control Center and related tools, and Sybase Central for Sybase SQL Server and Adaptive Server. These tools similarly aimed to ease DBA workload with graphical consoles, monitoring, and configuration, so the assertion that no other vendor had anything close to the cited Enterprise Manager did not hold even within a few years. The comment thus reads more as short-term marketing hype than a durable, accurate characterization of competitive reality.
