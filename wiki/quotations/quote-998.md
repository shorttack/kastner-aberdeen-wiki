## Quote

> I can have a network of 2100s, with part of that memory being used for [on-line transaction processing] and part being used for replication,

## Rationale

Within three years, clustering networks of midrange servers like DEC AlphaServer 2100s for OLTP and database replication was entirely feasible and aligned with products such as Oracle Parallel Server and Informix’s parallel database offerings, which used multiple nodes and substantial RAM for transaction processing and caching. However, OLTP and replication continued to rely heavily on disk-based logging and storage, and DEC’s Alpha/2100 ecosystem remained relatively niche and short‑lived compared with the broader industry move toward larger SMP Unix/NT boxes and, later, commodity x86 clusters, so the specific efficiency gains and architectural centrality implied for 2100-based memory-centric replication did not become broadly decisive.
