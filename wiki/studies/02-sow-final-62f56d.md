# Statement of Work: Aberdeen-Maxtor MaXLine Marketing Program

> Formal Statement of Work governing the Aberdeen Group-Maxtor Corporation consulting engagement for marketing the MaXLine ATA disk drive product line. The SOW defines a two-task program: Task 1 covering market research and segment definition (75 enterprise interviews plus survey analysis for $25K) and Task 2 covering market education via an Executive White Paper and webcast ($35K). The document establishes IP licensing terms giving Maxtor exclusive perpetual worldwide rights to the white paper and delivery of 1000 copies plus web distribution.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — MaXLine announcement date: MaXLine products quietly announced September 9 2002; market development campaign initiated thereafter
- **2003** — Engagement objective — market segment definition: Fundamental program element is to define a new market segment in the storage hierarchy between disk and tape — 'active archiving' or 'rapid/instant archiving'
- **2003** — SCSI cannibalization prevention objective: Maxtor wants to avoid cannibalizing its existing higher-margin SCSI disk drive product families when offering lower-priced ATA drives
- **2003** — Competitive blunting objective: Maxtor wants to blunt efforts of competitors with different technology portfolios and competing market development agendas
- **2003** — Task 1 survey scope: Minimum 75 interviews across wide variety of vertical markets and enterprise sizes including face-to-face interviews
- **2003** — Task 1 deliverables: Survey results and analysis report plus outline for Aberdeen white paper — both due May 2 2003
- **2003** — Task 2 white paper IP terms: Aberdeen grants Maxtor exclusive unrestricted royalty-free worldwide perpetual license to copy publish distribute the Executive White Paper
- **2003** — White paper exclusivity review window: Maxtor has 60-day period after delivery to review white paper and consider waiving exclusivity — allowing Aberdeen to publish more broadly
- **2003** — Lead generation deliverable: For 6 months after posting Aberdeen will provide download demographics to Maxtor for all who download white paper after registration
- **2003** — Webcast deliverable: Aberdeen hosts webcast advertised 'sponsored by Maxtor' reviewing storage market issues from white paper with Maxtor executives on panel
- **2003** — Briefings deliverable: Aberdeen provides minimum 4 briefings for Maxtor executives post-publication reviewing near-line market category development and trends
- **2003** — Task 1 fee: $25000 for market research and segment definition task
- **2003** — Task 2 fee: $35000 for market education (white paper + webcast + briefings)
- **2003** — Travel expenses budget: $6000 estimated travel budget for face-to-face interviews and California workshop
- **2003** — Webcast teleconferencing budget: $3000 estimated for webcast for 100 participants
- **2003** — Project start date: Mid-March 2003 expected project start; governed by Services Agreement effective March 17 2003
- **2003** — Aberdeen storage model claim: Aberdeen described as having 'well-developed storage model illustrated below' including MaXLine in emerging cost-effective storage class
- **2003** — Survey instrument review process: Aberdeen will review survey instrument with Maxtor before fieldwork begins
- **2003** — White paper page count: Executive White Paper will be 10-20 pages; intended audience is IT executives and enterprise executives
- **2003** — Total Phase 1 engagement value: $60000 plus expenses for Tasks 1 and 2 combined (initial engagement)
- **2003** — Kastner role as executive lead: Peter Kastner Executive VP will be the executive leading the overall project
- **2003** — Optional services mentioned: Optional services include standards groups outreach trade show presentations direct customer sales calls software partnership advisory and eLearning curriculum development
