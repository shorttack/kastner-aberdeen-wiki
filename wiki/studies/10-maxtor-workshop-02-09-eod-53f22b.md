# Maxtor Messaging Workshop: Creating the Midline Market Category

> Full slide deck from the February 13 2004 Maxtor midline category creation workshop at Shrewsbury MA. Covers Aberdeen's ILM and Pools of Storage framework, competitive analysis of Seagate/WD/Hitachi, the 10-step category creation methodology, a detailed market awareness-building roadmap with quarterly milestones, and competitive positioning analysis of MaXLine vs Atlas disk drives.

- **2004** — Pools of Storage strategy rationale: Unlike ILM software, Pools of Storage can be implemented now; has value proposition beyond TCO/ROI
- **2004** — ILM adoption survey: Only 15% have done ILM program now; 41% will consider in future; 33% have not considered; 11% don't know
- **2004** — Pools of Storage familiarity: 36% familiar; 50% somewhat familiar; only 15% little or not familiar
- **2004** — Pools of Storage 2004 implementation intent: Only 19% very likely to implement in 2004; 43% somewhat likely; 38% unlikely
- **2004** — Credible threats to midline category: SCSI drives increasing capacity to near midline; PC ATA drives in RAID offering comparable availability at lower cost
- **2004** — Seagate midline stance: Seagate will deny existence of midline because midline would threaten its hegemony in FC disk
- **2004** — Hitachi midline stance: Hitachi has IBM heritage bringing technical respect but too many diverse interests to focus on midline
- **2004** — WD as recommended category co-creator: WD should be positioned as midline competitor; already refers to Raptor as Enterprise Serial ATA; press doesn't need to understand subtleties
- **2004** — Midline formal definition: Midline storage includes enterprise-class high-capacity ATA disks that bridge the gap between high-use high-performance FC/SCSI disk arrays and magnetic tape storage
- **2004** — 10-step category creation model: 1.Define 2.Differentiate 3.Debunk 4.Define argument/position/messages 5.Deliver to influencers 6.Create awareness 7.Evangelize 8.Measure 9.Let influencers take over 10.Nurture
- **2004** — Category definition criterion: A category is a distinct market segment which is watched and measured independently of other segments by market influencers
- **2004** — Influencer engagement rule: Must minimize Maxtor and Aberdeen visibility; avoid any opportunity for critics to say this is all a MaXLine product play
- **2004** — Gartner/IDC midline reporting prediction: IDC or Gartner start reporting midline metrics by Q3 2004
