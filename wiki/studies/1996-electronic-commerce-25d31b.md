# Electronic Commerce: Virtual Corporations Selling to a Virtual Marketplace

> Aberdeen Group white paper summarizing field research on electronic commerce. Identifies three types of EC (B2B, B2C, C2C), outlines key Internet EC technologies, and recommends that IT organizations architect their EC endeavors carefully, considering make-vs-buy decisions. Projects over $7.2 billion in EC IT investment over three years.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner; Christopher Stevens · **Date:** 1996 · **Type:** white-paper
**Importance:** high — *Early analytical framework for classifying B2B/B2C/C2C commerce written before widespread internet commerce; prescient categorization still in use today.*
**Prescience:** high — *Accurately predicted $7B+ EC IT investment wave, importance of supply-chain EDI, personalization in B2C, and eventual dominance of buy-vs-build for EC platforms.*
