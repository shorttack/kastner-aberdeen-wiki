# Bay Networks: Concentrating on Remote Access

> This Aberdeen Announcement Profile examines Bay Networks' two new remote access concentrator products: the 5399 (high-density, modular, for service providers and large enterprises) and the Model 8000 (stand-alone, for smaller ISPs and enterprises). Both feature BayDSP modem technology from Bay's acquisition of Penril Datability Networks. Aberdeen evaluates the emerging 56K modem market and how Bay's solutions address high-density remote access requirements.

**Author:** Virginia Brooks · **Date:** 1997-10-01 · **Type:** announcement-profile
**Importance:** medium — *Profiles Bay Networks at a pivotal moment in remote access evolution when 56K modem standards were still contested between x2 and K56flex; captures the enterprise remote access market circa 1997 before DSL/cable broadband rendered dial-up concentrators obsolete.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Study noted 56K modem standard fragmentation as a challenge; ITU V.90 standard was ratified in September 1998, resolving the x2/K56flex conflict as implied by the study's concerns about compatibility issues.*
