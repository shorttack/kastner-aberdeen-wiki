# DataReach: Finding Business Value in Extracting Data

> Aberdeen Group vendor profile of DataReach, a joint product by EMC Corporation and BMC Software that bypasses mainframe scheduling constraints to accelerate delivery of critical data to open systems environments. Aberdeen predicts the product will help Global 2000 enterprises unlock mainframe data for data warehouses and data marts.

**Author:** David Hill · **Date:** 1997-11-01 · **Type:** Vendor Profile
**Importance:** low — *Brief abstract-only profile of a narrow joint product; limited analytical depth and no survey data; niche mainframe-to-open-systems integration topic with small addressable audience at the time*
**Prescience:** medium — *Correctly identified mainframe data as a significant bottleneck for decision-making; the prediction that the EMC-BMC partnership product would succeed long-term did not materialize as expected; ETL and cloud integration supplanted purpose-built mainframe bypass tools*
