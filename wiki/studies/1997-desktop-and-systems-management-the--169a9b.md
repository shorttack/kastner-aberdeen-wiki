# Desktop and Systems Management The HP Way: Using Open Standards To Lower Costs and Complexity

> Aberdeen Group evaluation of HP's DMI-based desktop management strategy. Argues HP's open-standards Vectra Corporate PC and TopTOOLS suite will help enterprises reduce PC total cost of ownership by 50% or more and give HP a competitive edge over Compaq's proprietary Intelligent Manageability approach. DMI certification predicted to become a de facto enterprise standard in 1997.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1997-03-01 · **Type:** Vendor Strategy Profile
**Importance:** medium — *Captures a key 1997 inflection point in enterprise PC management standards debates; TCO framing and open-vs-proprietary argument remain instructive; substantive content with IS executive interview data*
**Prescience:** medium — *Aberdeen correctly predicted DMI would become near-term de facto standard; however overestimated HP's ability to use DMI to overtake Compaq (the two merged in 2002); WMI ultimately became dominant desktop management standard rather than DMI; TCO projections were reasonable but optimistic*
