# Expanding Universes For BusinessObjects And Its Users

> Aberdeen Group strategy profile evaluating Business Objects as a top-tier BI software company with 475,000 licenses across 4,000 enterprises in 60 countries. Covers BusinessObjects 4.0 with integrated Microcube OLAP engine, Project Darwin web-based BI initiative, BusinessMiner data mining tool, and SAP partnership. Aberdeen predicts sustained growth and market leadership.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **1996** — revenue_1996: Business Objects 1996 revenue: $85.1 million; 40% growth from 1995 to 1996
- **1996** — installed_base: Business Objects has 475,000 licenses representing broad usage within 4,000 enterprises across more than 60 countries
- **1996** — q3_1996_loss: Q3 1996 loss of $300,000 due to difficulty stabilizing BusinessObjects 4.0 architectural transition
- **1996** — q4_1996_profit: Q4 1996 profits of $1.8 million from revenues of $25.8 million
- **1996** — cash_position: Business Objects has more than $40 million in cash reserves and zero debt
- **1996** — employee_count: Business Objects workforce approximately 550 employees; dual headquarters in Paris France and San Jose California
- **1996** — var_count: Business Objects has approximately 450 value-added resellers (VARs)
- **1996** — revenue_mix: Business Objects books approximately 70% of revenue from direct sale of software licenses; average size sale approximately $150,000
- **1996** — telemarketing_range: Telemarketing is responsible for sales between approximately $20,000 and $50,000
- **1997** — product_investment: BusinessObjects 4.0 development required approximately 150 person-years and $15 million investment
- **1997** — microcube_advantage: BusinessObjects Microcube Engine builds compact multidimensional cubes on-the-fly obviating time-consuming pre-built cube construction; enables workgroup sharing via email
- **1997** — universe_approach: BusinessObjects schemaless approach to multidimensionality works with any relational schema; no need for star or snowflake schemas or proprietary MOLAP tools
- **1997** — reporter_pricing: BusinessObjects 4.0 Reporter (query and reporting): $595; Explorer (OLAP): $695; Designer module: $1,995; Supervisor module: $1,995; Document Agent Server: $4,995
- **1997** — businessminer_pricing: BusinessMiner: $995 standalone or $495 when purchased with BusinessObjects 4.0
- **1997** — project_darwin_prediction: Aberdeen believes Project Darwin will significantly reduce maintenance and configuration management costs and drive down total cost of ownership; business users can reuse existing Universes
- **2000** — project_darwin_outcome: Project Darwin was released as WebIntelligence; became successful web-based BI product; concept validated as web delivery became standard for enterprise BI by 2000s
- **1997** — businessminer_prediction: Aberdeen believes BusinessMiner will augment decision-support desktop and pressure high-end data mining software suppliers to offer more sophisticated tools at more competitive prices
- **2010** — data_mining_democratization: Data mining and predictive analytics democratized through platforms like RapidMiner, Weka, and eventually Python/R; Aberdeen's directional prediction was accurate; BusinessMiner itself discontinued
- **1997** — company_viability: Aberdeen believes Business Objects demonstrates well-established pattern of excellence; positions company as stable supplier choice in rapidly shifting decision-support marketplace
- **2008** — acquisition_outcome: SAP acquired Business Objects January 2008 for $6.8 billion — largest acquisition in SAP's 35-year history; Business Objects had grown from $85M (1996) to $1.5B revenue (2007)
- **1997** — sap_partnership: SAP designated BusinessObjects 4.0 as the sole decision-support tool provider in the SAP Complementary Software Partner Program — a highly privileged and exclusive designation
- **1997** — microsoft_alliance: Business Objects holds privileged position in Microsoft Alliance for Data Warehousing
- **1993** — growth_history: Business Objects had compound annual growth rate of 80% during the three years prior to study
- **1997** — user_testimony_strategic: Large user with 5,000-copy rollout plans concluded that combination of strong technology and maturity would translate into a sustainable platform for competitive advantage
- **1997** — code_reuse: Business Objects executives report over 90% code reuse from BusinessObjects 4.0 in Project Darwin development
