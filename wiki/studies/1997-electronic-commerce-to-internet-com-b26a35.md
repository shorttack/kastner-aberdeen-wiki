# Electronic Commerce to Internet Commerce: The Evolution of The InterNetworked Enterprise

> Aberdeen Group Executive White Paper concluding that organizations are 'on the threshold of a revolution in Electronic Commerce (EC).' Focuses on the convergence of EC and Internet technologies into 'Internet Commerce (IC).' Explores benefits of Internet Commerce, its relationship to EDI, and impacts on internal and external business processes. Provides analytical framework for understanding virtual channel impacts on business equilibrium and for assessing EC/IC supplier technologies.

**Author:** David Alschuler · **Date:** 1997-11-01 · **Type:** White Paper
**Importance:** medium — *Captures a pivotal moment in the transition from EDI-based electronic commerce to Internet-based commerce. The 'Internet Commerce' framing and the analytical framework for EC/IC supplier assessment are historically important as early attempts to systematize the e-commerce vendor evaluation process.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Aberdeen's core prediction that Internet Commerce would grow explosively and transform business processes proved spectacularly correct.
