# Knowledge Discovery One's Retail Discovery Suite: Promoting Retail Data Mining Applications

> Aberdeen Group profiles KD1 and its Retail Discovery Suite (RDS), a parallel-processing data mining platform designed to deliver POS analytics to retail professionals without requiring dedicated data mining specialists. Aberdeen highlights RDS's ability to automate labor-intensive mining tasks and scale with growing data volumes.
