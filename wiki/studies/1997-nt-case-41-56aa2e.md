# NT Case 41: Migration Migraines

> Aberdeen Group OnSite case study of a Fortune 100 company's troubled migration from IBM OS/2 to Windows NT across call center operations (~12,000 clients). A 1995 business executive mandate drove total Microsoft adoption despite IS concerns. Migration began Spring 1996, missed mid-1997 deadline at 70% completion. Results: 33% performance decrease, 50% management cost increase, daily workstation reboots, SQL Server instability, IS staff exodus, and suppressed help desk reporting. Aberdeen frames this as a governance failure: business executives dictated both the what and the how of IT implementation.
