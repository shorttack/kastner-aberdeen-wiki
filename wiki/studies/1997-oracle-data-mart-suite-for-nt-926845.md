# Oracle Data Mart Suite for NT: Hitting The Mass-Market Sweet Spot

> This 1997 Aberdeen Group profile evaluates Oracle's Data Mart Suite for NT, a pre-packaged data mart solution designed to lower cost and complexity barriers for line-of-business managers. The study argues that Oracle's bundled approach—combining database, ETL, OLAP, query tools, and administration into a single NT-native package—positions it to capture the emerging mass-market for departmental data warehousing. Aberdeen rates the product highly for cost-of-ownership advantages over ad hoc tool-integration approaches.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1997-06-04 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *Aberdeen's endorsement of a pre-packaged data mart on NT carried significant weight in 1997 when fragmented, expensive custom implementations dominated the market; this study helped validate the emerging concept of data mart appliances.*
**Prescience:** high — *The study's prediction that pre-packaged data marts would dominate over custom-assembled solutions proved correct—modern cloud data warehouses (Snowflake, Redshift, BigQuery) all follow the integrated-suite model Aberdeen endorsed.*
