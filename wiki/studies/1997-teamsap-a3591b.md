# TeamSAP: SAP's Mantra For Assuring Customer Success

> Aberdeen Group evaluates SAP's TeamSAP initiative, a coordinated framework of products, processes, and people designed to improve R/3 implementation success rates and reduce the risk of large-scale ERP deployments. The white paper analyzes the three pillars of TeamSAP — the Business Framework (product), AcceleratedSAP Programs (process), and Solutions Expertise (people) — and identifies two key challenges: defining SAP's professional services role relative to Global Logo Partners, and potential partner resistance to SAP's increased coordination controls.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **1997** — SAP R/3 customer count: >7,000 companies worldwide
- **1997** — SAP annual revenue growth rate: >40% growth rate
- **1997** — SAP global sales (1996): $2.4 billion (38% increase)
- **1997** — TeamSAP positioning: SAP as coach throughout full R/3 lifecycle; coordinated network of products, processes, people
- **1997** — AcceleratedSAP program types: AcceleratedSAP Partner; Powered By AcceleratedSAP (Global Logo); Certified Business Solutions (sub-$200M revenue)
- **1997** — Customer demand #1: Business results over re-engineering: Customers want rapid business results, not multi-year re-engineering — especially with Y2K and Euro urgency
- **1997** — Customer demand #2: SAP participation in procurement/deployment: Greater SAP involvement in selection, procurement and full deployment scope
- **1997** — Customer demand #3: Comprehensive solution range: Need for R/3 plus complementary products for supply chain and other requirements
- **1997** — Customer demand #4: Quality assurance for multi-component implementations: Comprehensive QA for Business Framework with SAP + partner software components
- **1997** — Customer demand #5: Rapid operational improvement while re-engineering: Ability to turn on a dime — simultaneously reengineer and gain rapid operational improvements
- **1997** — ASAP methodology differentiators: First branded implementation solutions directly supported by SAP; SAP-derived resource estimates; shared project strategies; standardized across geographies
- **1997** — Global Logo Partner role: Largest SAP implementation partners; 300 Andersen technicians specialized in Siebel/SAP installation by 1997; take project lead
- **1997** — TeamSAP challenge #1: SAP professional services role ambiguity: Lack of clear formula for SAP involvement level beyond basic coaching; risk of duplicated efforts with partners
- **1997** — TeamSAP challenge #2: Global Logo Partner resistance: Global Logo Partners may view SAP standards/controls as challenge to their own successful business models
- **1997** — TeamSAP adoption impact: TeamSAP will provide real benefit to customers seeking rapid and ongoing R/3 results
- **1997** — Business change paradigm shift: Inert model of re-engineer-then-implement being replaced by smaller continuous technology-enabled changes
- **2015** — AcceleratedSAP methodology evolution: AcceleratedSAP evolved into SAP Activate methodology (2015); continues as standard SAP implementation framework for S/4HANA
- **2015** — SAP's next-generation ERP: SAP S/4HANA generally available 2015; built exclusively on HANA in-memory database; successor to R/3 and ECC
- **2001** — Andersen Consulting renamed Accenture: Andersen Consulting rebranded as Accenture in January 2001; remains world's largest management consulting firm
- **1997** — Certified Business Solutions VAR target: Businesses with annual revenues under $200 million
- **1997** — Complementary Software Partner certification types: Joint Development Partners (co-development with SAP); Certified Interface Partners (certified interoperability)
- **1997** — SAPNet knowledge transfer platform: Online competency center for SAP, partner, and customer knowledge transfer
- **1997** — SAP EarlyWatch proactive diagnostics: Proactive system diagnostics program recognizing and remedying potential problems early
