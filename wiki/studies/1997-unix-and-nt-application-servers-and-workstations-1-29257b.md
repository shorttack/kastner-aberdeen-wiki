# Unix and NT Application Servers and Workstations: 1997-2000 Projections

> This Market Viewpoint abstract projects worldwide shipment trends for Unix and NT workstations (units) and application servers (dollars) from 1997 through 2000. Aberdeen summarizes actual 1996–1997 baseline shipments and identifies market dynamics — Year 2000 (Y2K) impact, lower prices, new technology introductions, and technical support capabilities — as key drivers shaping each market segment through 2000. The full study provides size and change projections for each year.

**Author:** John R. Logan / Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1997-01-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** medium — *Timely 1997 market sizing study for the Unix/NT server and workstation battle — one of the central technology market questions of the late 1990s; Aberdeen's market viewpoints were influential with enterprise IT buyers and CIOs at the time.*
**Prescience:** medium — *The abstract explicitly names Y2K impact and lower prices as 1997-2000 growth drivers — both proved correct — and implicitly suggests NT's continued growth over Unix, consistent with verified historical outcomes showing NT outpacing Unix in unit shipments by 1998.*
