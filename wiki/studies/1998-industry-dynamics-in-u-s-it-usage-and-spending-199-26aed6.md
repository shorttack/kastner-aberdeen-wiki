# Industry Dynamics in U.S. IT Usage and Spending, 1998

> Aberdeen Group's 106-page study analyzes information technology spending and usage patterns across eleven U.S. vertical industries in 1998, combining primary survey data with secondary sources. The report introduces proprietary Aberdeen barometers tracking Internet adoption, mainframe usage, and outsourcing trends across financial services, manufacturing, retail, healthcare, and other commercial sectors. It presents IT spending as a proportion of revenue by industry and benchmarks enterprise size effects on IT investment patterns.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1998-12-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *Published at the peak of the dot-com investment boom, this report provided the first comprehensive multi-vertical cross-industry benchmark of U.S. IT spending ratios; Aberdeen's quantitative barometers were widely used by IT vendors and analysts to size market opportunities.*
**Prescience:** high — *Aberdeen predicted rapid Internet adoption across all verticals and continued outsourcing growth; both proved correct — Internet penetration became universal in commercial IT by 2005 and IT outsourcing grew into a multi-hundred-billion-dollar global market.*
