# Internet Procurement

> Aberdeen Group's summary overview presents findings from two companion publications — a Viewpoint and a White Paper — on Internet-based procurement automation for non-production goods and services. Based on a November 1998 survey of early adopters, Aberdeen concludes that Internet procurement has the potential to dramatically redesign corporate purchasing processes and deliver measurable cost and process improvements. The study identifies leading vendors Ariba, Commerce One, and SAP as primary players and argues supply chain management — not retail e-commerce — will be the highest-value application of Internet technology for corporations.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1998-11-01 · **Type:** white-paper
**Importance:** high — *Among the earliest analyst-backed research to identify B2B procurement — rather than consumer e-commerce — as the primary value driver of Internet technology; this thesis proved foundational to the $4.3B Ariba acquisition by SAP and the entire e-procurement software industry.*
**Prescience:** high — *Aberdeen's prediction that Internet procurement would deliver 'rapid and measurable process, cost, and profit improvements' proved correct; Ariba and Commerce One achieved multi-billion-dollar valuations on exactly this premise, and e-procurement is now a standard enterprise capability.*
