# Aberdeen Group's Analysis of The AS/400e in 1998: Changing The Game — Striving To Change Perceptions

> A February 1998 Aberdeen Group presentation by Chief Research Officer Peter S. Kastner analyzing the competitive position of the IBM AS/400e platform against NT Server and UNIX. The study benchmarks the AS/400e Model S40 at 25,150 tpmC and assesses its advantages and weaknesses across scalability, TCO, e-business readiness, and ISV/distributor ecosystem gaps. It concludes that the AS/400e is technically credible but faces significant perception and channel challenges heading into 1998.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner (with John Logan) · **Date:** 1998-02-24 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *Captures a pivotal inflection point in IBM AS/400 history — the August 1997 Model S40 launch — with quantified TPC-C benchmarks and structured competitive assessments that remain primary-source evidence of midrange computing competitive dynamics in the late 1990s.*
**Prescience:** high — *Accurately forecast the AS/400e's perception problem and ISV/distributor gap as key barriers — issues that did constrain AS/400 growth through the early 2000s; the framing of e-business and 24x7 availability as table-stakes presaged the dominant application requirements of the next decade.*
