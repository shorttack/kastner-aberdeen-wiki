# Professional Services Automation: Increasing Efficiencies and Profitability in Professional Services Organizations

> This Aberdeen Group report defines and maps the emerging Professional Services Automation (PSA) software market in 1999, providing 14 in-depth vendor profiles, market dynamics analysis, and growth projections through 2003. The study positions PSA as the services-sector equivalent of ERP for manufacturing, projecting significant efficiency improvements for IT, engineering, legal, advertising, and other fee-based services organizations. Key vendors profiled include PeopleSoft, SAP AG, Niku, Changepoint, Evolve Software, and a dozen others.

**Author:** David Hofferberth · **Date:** 1999-11-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *First comprehensive independent market study of the emerging PSA software category; established PSA as a distinct market segment and provided the first competitive landscape covering 14 vendors plus 10 additional providers — influential in shaping investor and buyer understanding of the space.*
**Prescience:** high — *Aberdeen's core prediction that PSA would achieve ERP-equivalent adoption for services organizations proved substantially correct: market leaders like Niku were acquired by CA Technologies; Changepoint survived to Compuware and beyond; PeopleSoft/SAP remained dominant platforms.*
