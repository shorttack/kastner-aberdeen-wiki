# Enterprise Application Integration: Evolving to Meet e-Business Demands

> This Aberdeen Group report (Third Edition) examines the Enterprise Application Integration (EAI) market as it evolves to meet e-Business demands in early 2000. The report argues that EAI and e-Commerce are the two most important CIO initiatives in the Global 2000 for 2000, analyzes the convergence of e-Business Integration (eBI) with traditional EAI, and profiles 50 EAI suppliers (up from 19 in the prior edition). The report reviews 1998 predictions for 1999 outcomes and makes new predictions for 2000.

**Author:** Tom Dwyer · **Date:** 2000-02-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *Third edition of a major Aberdeen EAI series at the peak of the e-Business integration wave; with 50 vendor profiles and market sizing, this was one of the most comprehensive EAI market studies available in early 2000; the EAI-to-eBI convergence thesis was highly influential with CIOs.*
**Prescience:** high — *Aberdeen correctly identified EAI-eBI convergence as the defining industry dynamic; EAI vendors did consolidate (TIBCO, IBM MQ, WebMethods acquired by Software AG, Vitria acquired by SAP-adjacent); cloud integration (MuleSoft, Boomi, Zapier) fulfilled the eBI vision.*
