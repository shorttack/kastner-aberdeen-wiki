# Aberdeen Group Recent Publications — Peter S. Kastner Edition (2001)

> A Wayback Machine archive of multiple Aberdeen Group 'Recent Publications' index pages from 2001, compiled under Peter S. Kastner's research portfolio. The catalog lists approximately 80 Aberdeen InSights, Profiles, Impacts, and White Papers spanning June–December 2001 across domains including ASPs, blade servers, Linux, Windows XP, enterprise applications, storage, CRM, supply chain, e-business, and information security. Several entries are authored by Kastner himself, including two InSights on Windows XP and one co-authored Profile on BizWorks/CA interBiz.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2001** — Linux server OS market position by decade end: Linux will join IBM z/OS and Microsoft Windows as primary server OSs before end of decade (2010)
- **2010** — Linux HPC market dominance: Linux dominated TOP500 HPC list by 2004+; became primary server OS by 2010
- **2001** — Linux HPC imminent dominance: Linux will be successful alternative to proprietary systems in high-performance computing
- **2001** — ASP sector viability: Significant majority of ASPs will be seeking additional funding rounds; many will fail
- **2003** — ASP market consolidation: Most first-generation ASPs did fail; model pivoted to SaaS by mid-2000s
- **2001** — Windows XP user adoption outlook: Almost all previous Windows users are going to enjoy living with Windows XP
- **2014** — Windows XP adoption and longevity: Windows XP became most widely deployed OS in history; Microsoft supported until April 2014
- **2001** — Pocket PC vs Palm OS market leadership: Pocket PC will eclipse Palm OS as leading handheld platform by 2005
- **2007** — Palm OS market leadership outcome: Palm OS declined but iPhone (2007) disrupted both platforms; Pocket PC did gain enterprise share but was itself displaced
- **2001** — Blade server Aberdeen coverage depth: Two InSights published Nov 2001: Manter (density/cost) and Tanner (SAN virtualization pairing)
- **2001** — Exodus Communications bankruptcy and C&W acquisition: C&W paid $850M for majority of Exodus assets; 30 data centers; ~3,500 customers
- **2001** — Exodus bankruptcy verified: Exodus filed Chapter 11 Sep 2001; C&W acquired assets for $755M-$850M Nov 2001
- **2001** — HP acquisition of Compaq announcement: HP announced acquisition of Compaq; Aberdeen published multiple InSights examining implications
- **2002** — HP/Compaq merger completion: HP completed acquisition of Compaq in May 2002 for ~$25B
- **2001** — SAP market position: SAP is 'Once and Future King' — not ready to cede ERP market hegemony
- **2026** — SAP market position outcome: SAP remains #1 ERP vendor globally with >400,000 customers in 2025
- **2001** — Microsoft CRM market entry impact: Microsoft's entry into CRM is single biggest threat AND single largest opportunity in market
- **2015** — Microsoft Dynamics CRM market position: Microsoft Dynamics became #2 CRM market share by 2015; Salesforce #1 but Microsoft major challenger
- **2001** — Unisys ES7000 SAP benchmark: Unisys ES7000 set new SAP standard on Windows/Intel platform
- **2001** — Hosting services market condition: HSP market at crossroads: slow adoption and strategy changes opening door for market reordering
- **2001** — Post-9/11 IT spending impact: Aberdeen analysis: events of Sep 11 impact on world's 800-pound gorilla economy
- **2001** — e-billing adoption catalyst: DSO, anthrax postal disruption will likely increase appeal for EBPP and e-payment technologies near term
- **2010** — e-billing adoption outcome: EBPP market grew significantly through 2005-2010; now dominant billing method in US
- **2001** — IT spending categories despite downturn: Despite downturn: customers, security, and software infrastructure driving spending
- **2001** — Enterprise WLAN readiness: Enterprise WLANs need security/management systems to become enterprise-strength
