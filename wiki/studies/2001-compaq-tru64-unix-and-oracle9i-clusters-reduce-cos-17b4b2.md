# Compaq Tru64 Unix and Oracle9i Clusters Combine to Reduce Costs: Can SAP Resist This Fit?

> This Aberdeen InSight examines the technology combination of Compaq's TruCluster Server technology with Oracle9i Real Application Clusters, arguing it delivers scalability, availability, manageability, and cost-of-ownership advantages. Oracle and Compaq had announced a joint marketing agreement and Tru64 Unix was selected as the development platform for Oracle9i. The study argues SAP should enthusiastically support Oracle9i and the Compaq/Oracle technology partnership against SAP's competing 'One Database' mySAP.com initiative.

**Author:** Robert Dorin · **Date:** 2001-12-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** medium — *The study identified a significant technology partnership at a pivotal moment in enterprise computing consolidation. It addressed a real competitive tension between Oracle/Compaq and SAP's database strategy, relevant to enterprise IT decision-makers in 2001.*
**Prescience:** low — *The study's prediction that SAP should adopt Oracle9i and Compaq/Oracle clustering proved incorrect: the HP-Compaq merger disrupted the Compaq/Oracle partnership, Tru64 was eventually discontinued, and SAP pursued its own HANA database strategy rather than deepening Oracle ties.*
