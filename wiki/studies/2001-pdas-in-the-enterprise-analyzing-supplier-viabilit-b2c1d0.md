# PDAs in the Enterprise: Analyzing Supplier Viability

> In September 2001, Aberdeen Group evaluated leading PDA vendors using its Market Positioning Pyramid (MPP) framework to assess both product quality and supplier viability. The study argues that brand awareness and market share alone do not determine PDA market leadership. The analysis covers Palm, Microsoft/Pocket PC, Compaq, Handspring, HP, Symbol Technologies, Sony, Toshiba, Casio, Sharp, NEC, Psion, and emerging players.

**Author:** Isaac Ro; David Wright; Christopher Fletcher · **Date:** 2001-09-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *Published at the height of PDA market fragmentation, this study provided a rigorous multi-vendor viability framework at a time when enterprises faced real risk from supplier consolidation and platform obsolescence.*
**Prescience:** low — *The study's premise that brand and share don't determine PDA leadership had merit, but the entire PDA market was disrupted and rendered obsolete by the iPhone in 2007 — none of the predicted winners achieved lasting enterprise handheld dominance.*
