# SAP: The Once and Future King

> Based on observations at SAP's 2001 SAPPHIRE conference in Orlando, this Aberdeen InSight argues that SAP's three-year transformation effort has yielded a mature and genuinely integrated product family. The study contends that SAP's mySAP.com suite — including Portals, CRM, and Exchanges — has moved from immaturity to market leadership, and predicts that SAP will retain ERP hegemony against narrower competitors.

**Author:** David Alschuler; Bill Brandel; Chris Fletcher; Tim Minahan · **Date:** 2001-07-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *This study captured SAP at a critical inflection point — the transition from defensive uncertainty (SAPPHIRE 2000) to confident ERP leadership (SAPPHIRE 2001). It helped enterprise buyers understand that SAP's component suite was now credibly competitive.*
**Prescience:** high — *SAP did retain ERP hegemony and its component applications (CRM, Portals, SCM) did compete effectively against narrower specialists. SAP S/4HANA is the dominant cloud ERP platform as of 2026, validating the 'lion' metaphor.*
