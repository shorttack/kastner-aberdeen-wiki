# 802.11a,b Wireless Comes of Age

> This Aberdeen InSight, published October 2002, assesses the 802.11a,b wireless chipset market following Atheros Communications' launch of its dual-band AR5001X chip set, which positioned the company as the leading vendor. The piece predicts competitive shakeout, warning that many vendors would not survive in the market a year from the publication date.

**Author:** Russ Craig · **Date:** 2002-10-04 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *Published at the inflection point when 802.11 Wi-Fi was transitioning from niche to mainstream; Atheros's dual-band chipset directly catalyzed the consumer Wi-Fi boom and Aberdeen's call was timely and market-shaping.*
**Prescience:** high — *The prediction that many vendors would exit the market proved accurate: Intersil sold its WLAN unit to GlobespanVirata in 2003, and the market consolidated around a handful of survivors led by Atheros (acquired by Qualcomm 2011).*
