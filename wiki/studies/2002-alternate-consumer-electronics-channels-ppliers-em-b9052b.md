# Alternate Consumer Electronics Channels and Suppliers Emerge, Threatening Incumbents

> This Aberdeen InSight, published July 2002, examines how cable operators, telecom companies, and direct-broadcast satellite providers were emerging as alternative consumer electronics channels, threatening incumbents like Best Buy, Circuit City, Sony, and Panasonic. The study argues that service and content providers were leveraging hardware bundles to deepen customer lock-in, and projects that emerging technologies would amplify long-term disruption of traditional CE retail channels.

**Author:** Russ Craig · **Date:** 2002-07-11 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *Presciently identified the service-provider-as-hardware-distributor model that became dominant over the subsequent two decades through cable set-top boxes, telco handset subsidies, and streaming device ecosystems.*
**Prescience:** high — *The prediction that S&C providers would threaten traditional CE incumbents proved accurate: Circuit City went bankrupt in 2008, Best Buy survived only through transformation, while cable/telco hardware bundling and streaming device ecosystems (Roku, Fire TV) became dominant CE distribution channels.*
