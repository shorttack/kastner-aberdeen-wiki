# CA Does the Right Thing; Customers Say CRO Rocks

> Computer Associates (CA) undertook a major internal reform program beginning in 1999 to address systemic weaknesses in its products, processes, and customer relations. The centerpiece of this transformation was the Client Relations Organization (CRO), a non-quota-carrying service unit focused on achieving customer satisfaction and trust. This Aberdeen InSight documents the CRO initiative with customer testimonials and positions CA as a substantially reformed enterprise software vendor.

**Author:** Valerie O'Connell · **Date:** 2001-11-01 · **Type:** insight
**Importance:** medium — *CA was widely known for adversarial customer relations prior to this reform; documenting the CRO initiative addressed a significant knowledge gap for enterprise IT buyers evaluating CA's trustworthiness. Aberdeen was an authoritative voice in enterprise software at the time.*
**Prescience:** medium — *CA's cultural transformation was partially validated: the company did improve its reputation and survive through multiple leadership changes. However, it later faced an accounting scandal and eventual acquisition by Broadcom in 2018, suggesting the transformation was incomplete and not fully durable…*
