# IT Purchasing: The View From the End of the Tunnel

> An analysis of Top 20 IT company revenues in August 2002 suggests the IT spending recession may be at or near its trough. Aberdeen identifies significant directional changes in IT management that will become evident when spending recovers, including shifts toward EAI, Security, and CRM investment. The study warns that IT organizations and suppliers that ignore these structural changes will miss opportunities and lose market share when the recovery arrives.

The identified technology priorities (security, EAI/integration, CRM) all received increased investment as spending recovered from 2003 onward.*
