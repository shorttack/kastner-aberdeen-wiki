# IT Spending Shows Encouraging Signs

> Aberdeen Group analyzed Q3 2002 financial results for the top 20 IT suppliers and found revenues 3.4% higher than Q3 2001, suggesting the IT spending contraction was ending. The study identifies Microsoft, HP/Compaq, and security/EAI/CRM segments as early growth indicators. Aberdeen expressed cautious optimism that modest IT purchasing growth was forthcoming.

**Author:** Hugh Bishop, James Tsai · **Date:** 2002-11-14 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** medium — *Timely snapshot of IT market inflection point at bottom of post-dot-com recession; one of the first analyst takes calling a recovery in late 2002.*
**Prescience:** high — *The prediction of modest IT spending growth proved correct; IT spending recovered through 2003-2004 and grew substantially, validating Aberdeen's cautious optimism at the trough.*
