# Undercutting and Upselling: Dell's Foray into the Handheld Market

> This InSight profiles Dell Computer Corp.'s anticipated entry into the PDA market with a competitively priced Pocket PC device and analyzes the likely winners and losers in the enterprise handheld segment. The study examines Dell's probable pricing strategy, business model, and competitive implications for incumbent vendors including Palm, Handspring, and Sony.

**Author:** Isaac Ro, Peter Kastner · **Date:** 2002-08-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** medium — *Dell's PDA market entry was a significant competitive event in enterprise mobile computing in 2002; Aberdeen was an influential analyst house and this study shaped buyer expectations for the pending product launch.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Dell did enter the PDA market with the Axim X5 (Pocket PC) at $299 in fall 2002 — closely matching the study's predictions. However, the enterprise PDA market never reached its predicted scale; the segment was disrupted by smartphones by 2007 when Dell discontinued the Axim line.*
