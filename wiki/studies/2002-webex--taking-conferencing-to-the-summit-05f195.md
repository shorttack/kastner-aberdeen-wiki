# WebEx: Taking Conferencing to the Business Communications Summit

> An Aberdeen Group profile of WebEx Communications examining its web conferencing platform, private Mediatone network infrastructure, and new Training Center service. The study introduces a five-level model of real-time business communications, argues that WebEx is more akin to a telephone utility than a software company, and assesses how the post-9/11 environment and 2001 recession are accelerating the shift from face-to-face to web-based meetings. Published at a moment when WebEx had over 5,000 enterprise customers and was pulling decisively ahead of roughly 40 competitors.
