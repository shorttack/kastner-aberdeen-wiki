# Aberdeen Group Free Research: Aberdeen InSights Catalog (2001-2003)

Each entry provides the InSight title, publication date, subject domain, author(s), and a brief abstract, collectively representing Aberdeen's research output across that two-year period.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — InSights Published in Catalog: ~100 InSight briefs published Feb 2001 – Feb 2003
- **2003** — CRM InSight Coverage Count: ~15 InSights on CRM topics spanning 2001-2003
- **2003** — Storage InSight Coverage Count: ~10 InSights on storage management and SAN/NAS
- **2002** — Wireless/Semiconductor InSight Coverage: ~12 InSights on WiFi, Bluetooth, 3G, semiconductors 2001-2003
- **2001** — Linux Server Trajectory: Linux will join IBM z/OS and Windows as primary server OSs before end of decade
- **2010** — Linux Server Dominance - Actual Outcome: Linux became dominant server OS by mid-2000s; now runs majority of cloud infrastructure
- **2002** — WiFi Market Shakeout: Shakeout in wireless LAN market started; many vendors will not remain in market a year from now
- **2005** — WiFi Market Shakeout - Actual Outcome: WiFi market consolidated; Broadcom, Atheros, Intel emerged dominant; many early vendors exited
- **2001** — 3G Rollout Delays: 3G rollout delays signal global emerging trend, not just hype
- **2007** — 3G Rollout Timeline - Actual Outcome: Global 3G rollout significantly delayed from 2001 projections; mass-market 3G emerged ~2007-2009
- **2001** — Pocket PC vs Palm OS by 2005: Pocket PC will eclipse Palm OS as leading handheld platform by 2005
- **2007** — Pocket PC vs Palm - Actual Outcome: Neither won; smartphone revolution (iPhone 2007) rendered both obsolete; prediction was incorrect
- **2002** — Nextel Youth Market Strategy: Nextel Boost subsidiary expected to help company be a key player in mobile youth market
- **2005** — Nextel - Actual Outcome: Nextel merged with Sprint 2005; Boost Mobile became successful prepaid brand
- **2001** — Exodus Communications Bankruptcy: Exodus filed bankruptcy Sept 2001; C&W acquired assets for $850M
- **2001** — Baan Financial Recovery: Baan appears on sound financial footing after turnaround under Invensys
- **2003** — Baan - Actual Outcome: Baan sold by Invensys to SSA Global 2003; eventually acquired by Infor; viability prediction broadly correct short-term
- **2003** — Agile/XP Methodology Assessment: Agile and Extreme Programming promote development reengineering rather than just control
- **2002** — E-mail CRM Growth Prediction: Growing number of IT buyers will divert resources from broad CRM suites to e-mail-centric customer lifecycle messaging
- **2003** — IP Storage Networking Viability: Enterprise IT planners should maintain open mind toward using IP for storage networking
- **2002** — Siebel CRM Methodology in Spotlight: Siebel 7.5 exposes business processes as Web services; methodology-driven CRM
- **2006** — Siebel Viability - Actual Outcome: Siebel acquired by Oracle 2006 for ~$5.85B after losing market share to SAP and Salesforce.com
- **2002** — .NET Market Confusion: Microsoft has significant .NET technical progress but still cannot answer IT's fundamental question: 'What is it?'
- **2005** — .NET Identity Clarification - Actual Outcome: .NET became well-established enterprise framework by 2004-2005; identity question resolved by mainstream adoption
- **2002** — WorldCom/MCI Status: WorldCom filed bankruptcy July 2002; Michael Cappellas joined as CEO; emerged as MCI in 2004; acquired by Verizon 2006
