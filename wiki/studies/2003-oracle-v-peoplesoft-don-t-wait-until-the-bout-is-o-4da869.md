# Oracle v. PeopleSoft: Don't Wait Until the Bout is Over

> Aberdeen Group examines Oracle's hostile takeover pursuit of PeopleSoft in its third month as of August 2003, framing it as a competitive prizefight with both parties absorbing damage. The study advises PeopleSoft customers and prospects not to wait for the battle's resolution, implying a strategic posture for ERP acquisition decisions amid uncertainty. It poses the core question of whether PeopleSoft can survive as an independent software vendor.
