# Digital LinkWorks: Delivering Solutions to MIS and End-Users

> Aberdeen Group profile evaluating Digital Equipment Corporation's LinkWorks, a document management and workflow platform for enterprise groupware. Introduces the concept of 'Enterprise Groupware' built on distributed object technology and contrasts it with first-generation products such as Lotus Notes and Microsoft Exchange. Argues that LinkWorks uniquely satisfies both MIS and end-user requirements through open databases, OLE integration, and a component-based development model.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1995-07-01 · **Type:** product-profile
**Importance:** medium — *Aberdeen endorsed Digital LinkWorks as the Enterprise Groupware leader, a vendor-sponsored product profile that may have influenced enterprise groupware purchasing. However DEC was already losing market position in 1995.*
**Prescience:** low — *Core predictions failed: LinkWorks was discontinued after DEC acquisition; Lotus Notes was not displaced by object technology; CORBA-based groupware never materialized. Aberdeen's object-centric vision was wrong about the winning architecture.*
