# HP Smart ContAct/CIS: Customer Interaction Software - The New Market Weapon

> Aberdeen Group profile evaluating HP's Smart ContAct/CIS customer interaction software suite, positioned as a new market weapon for CTI and customer service automation.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1995-12-01 · **Type:** product-profile
**Importance:** high — *Aberdeen's December 1995 profile of HP Smart ContAct/CIS covered a market growing 40%+ annually that would become the multi-billion dollar CRM sector. The study identified all five major CRM ISVs that dominated the late 1990s market (Clarify, Remedy, Siebel, Scopus, Vantive).*
**Prescience:** medium — *Aberdeen correctly predicted 40%+ market growth (confirmed by Siebel's explosive trajectory) and CTI middleware criticality; but HP did not become CIS leader (Siebel won), and the ISV ecosystem consolidated differently than predicted. Market trajectory correct, winner prediction wrong.*
