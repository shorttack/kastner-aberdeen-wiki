# Hewlett-Packard's C++ SoftBench 5.0 -- A Powerful Platform for Professional Developers

> Aberdeen Group executive white paper evaluating HP's C++ SoftBench 5.0 IDE for object-oriented software development. Examines OO development advantages, SoftBench features including CodeAdvisor and graphical class editor, and HP's consulting services.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1995-12-01 · **Type:** white-paper
**Importance:** medium — *Aberdeen endorsed HP SoftBench as an industrial-strength OO IDE at a pivotal moment in enterprise software development transition; solid analysis of a real product, but HP's Unix-centric development ecosystem was a crowded field with Sun, IBM, and open source alternatives.*
**Prescience:** medium — *OO development and distributed computing predictions were directionally correct (both became mainstream), but Aberdeen's specific vehicle—HP SoftBench + CommonPoint—failed: Taligent dissolved 1998, SoftBench discontinued; Java/Eclipse supplanted the predicted C++ toolchain path.*
