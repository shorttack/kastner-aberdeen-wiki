# IBM's AS/400 and SAP's R/3: Making the Complex and Slow Both Simple and Fast

> Profile examining the IBM AS/400 and SAP R/3 partnership, evaluating AS/400 Advanced Series with PowerPC AS processors and SAP R/3 v3.0 as a platform for enterprise reengineering.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1995-09-01 · **Type:** product-profile
**Importance:** high — *Documented the formative IBM-SAP partnership that became a cornerstone of enterprise ERP for three decades; AS/400 evolved into IBM i still running 30+ years later; SAP R/3 on AS400 was announced to IBM's 325,000-system installed base representing billions in enterprise infrastructure.*
**Prescience:** high — *SAP R/3 on AS/400 was delivered in 1996 as predicted; AS/400 platform proved extraordinarily durable becoming IBM i; SAP became the dominant global ERP vendor; all three core predictions confirmed by subsequent decades of market outcomes.*
