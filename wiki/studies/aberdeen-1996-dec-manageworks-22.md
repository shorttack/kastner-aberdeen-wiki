# ManageWORKS 2.2 Market Profile

> This Aberdeen Group product profile evaluates Digital Equipment Corporation's ManageWORKS 2.2, a multi-NOS LAN management platform designed for site and workgroup environments of 10 to 800 nodes. The study assesses ManageWORKS' architecture, partner ecosystem, and competitive positioning against McAfee and Symantec's Norton Administrator for Networks, concluding that its open, partner-extensible approach is unique in the market. Aberdeen highlights the product's advantage in enabling best-of-breed integrations across disparate network operating systems.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1996-03-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** medium — *The profile documents an early 1990s LAN management platform at a pivotal moment in multi-NOS network administration; Aberdeen Group's endorsement carried weight in IT purchasing decisions, but ManageWORKS was a niche product in a crowded field.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Aberdeen's prediction that Digital's open partner-integration approach was unique and appealing proved partially correct in the short term, but the platform did not survive Digital's 1998 acquisition by Compaq, undercutting the forward-looking claim of strategic advantage.*
