# HP's Networking Vision: Designed with Workgroups in Mind

> Aberdeen Group examines HP's Workgroup Networks Division strategy for small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs), finding that HP's premise — workgroups grow up into enterprise networks — accurately maps to the bottom-up, haphazard LAN growth patterns observed in target verticals (education, healthcare, government, manufacturing). The study validates HP's workgroup-centric networking vision and endorses its standards-based approach.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1996-07-01 · **Type:** white-paper
**Importance:** medium — *Provided independent validation of HP's networking strategy for the SMB segment at a time when 100Base-T and 100VG standards competition was unresolved; influenced HP's positioning in a key growth market.*
**Prescience:** medium — *HP's prediction of workgroup-driven network growth proved correct; however HP's networking business was reorganized and its enterprise networking assets were eventually absorbed into HP's broader portfolio. 100VG lost to 100Base-T as the dominant fast Ethernet standard.*
