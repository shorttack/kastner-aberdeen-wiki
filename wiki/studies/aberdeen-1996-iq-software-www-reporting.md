# IQ Software: Applying the WWW To Reporting Problems

> Aberdeen Group profiles IQ Software Corporation's IQ/LiveWeb product, which applies World Wide Web technologies to enterprise reporting and decision support. The study argues that IQ/LiveWeb's three-tier architecture, object-oriented query capabilities, and Internet/Intranet publishing engine positions IQ Software ahead of competitors in WWW-enabled business intelligence, predicting the product will maintain a leadership position if IQ Software sustains its innovation pace.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1996-06-01 · **Type:** product-profile
**Importance:** medium — *Published at the dawn of web-enabled BI in 1996, this study captured an early and prescient vision of enterprise reporting on the Internet; however IQ Software was a mid-tier player and the study had limited industry impact compared to larger platform studies.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Aberdeen accurately predicted WWW-enabled reporting as the future of enterprise BI and correctly identified architectural requirements (three-tier, thin client); however IQ Software itself was acquired in 1998 and did not maintain its predicted leadership position.*
