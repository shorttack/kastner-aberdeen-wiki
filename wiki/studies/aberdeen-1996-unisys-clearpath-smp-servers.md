# Unisys Corporation - ClearPath SMP Servers

> Aberdeen Group profiles Unisys Corporation's ClearPath SMP Server product line following Unisys's 1995 reorganization into three business groups. The study examines Unisys's strategy of providing 'full-service' computing to enterprises overwhelmed by open systems complexity, covering entry-level (SMP5200/5400), mid-range (SMP61000), and high-end server offerings, clustering via Veritas Vx Reliant, and the balance between Unix openness and proprietary ClearPath reliability/availability/security (RAS) requirements.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1996-04-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** medium — *This Aberdeen Profile captures Unisys at a key strategic pivot—from proprietary mainframe heritage to Intel-based SMP servers—at a time when the enterprise server market was consolidating around Intel/Unix architectures; Unisys's 'one-stop-shop' strategy was a significant market positioning bet.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Aberdeen's prediction of Unisys's solution-centric evolution proved partially correct—Unisys did shift to services and the ClearPath line survived; however, Unisys's broader server market share eroded significantly and the company exited commodity Intel server markets, a trajectory Aberdeen did not…*
