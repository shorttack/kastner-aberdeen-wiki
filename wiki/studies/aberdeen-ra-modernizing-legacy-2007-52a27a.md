# Modernizing Legacy Applications: Maximizing the Investment

> Aberdeen Group benchmark report by Perry Donham on enterprise approaches to legacy application modernization. Identifies best-practice patterns for maximizing the investment in legacy systems through modernization rather than replacement, framing the approach within Aberdeen's PACE methodology.

**Author:** Perry Donham · **Date:** 2007-06-01 · **Type:** benchmark-report
**Importance:** medium — *Captures 2007 industry consensus on modernization-vs-replacement strategies for legacy applications during the SOA wave.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Donham's emphasis on modernization-via-SOA-wrappers proved durable as enterprises continued running mainframes well into the 2020s. The reframing from 'rip and replace' to 'wrap and extend' became dominant.*
