# Aberdeen Workshop Qualifying Questions, Training Plan, and 2001 Customer Success Stories

> Seven Aberdeen Group internal artifacts from 2001 supporting the rebranded Advisory Access Service workshop family. ## Key observations (top 25)

- **2001** — Go-To-Market Workshop price: $50,000
- **2001** — Go-To-Market Workshop deliverables: 2-day onsite engagement + 4-6 page findings report + 1-hour teleconference + 1-2 page future-engagement recommendations
- **2001** — Go-To-Market Workshop lifecycle target: Development planning and launch planning stages
- **2001** — Go-To-Market sales pitch framing: Priced as 'just a half-percent of your total funding'
- **2001** — Go-To-Market qualifying criteria: Target funding >$2M or pending Series B; alpha-stage development
- **2001** — Go-To-Market engagement ladder (recommended next steps): Market Knowledge Workshop -> Positioning Strategy -> Messaging Strategy -> Strategy Validation (RAMP) -> Custom Research -> Retainer
- **2001** — Market Knowledge Workshop qualifying-question coverage: Market overview, competitive analysis, market landscape segmentation, current company/product position, market trends, future strategy preparation
- **2001** — Market Knowledge 3-5 year vision probe: 'What is your 3-5 year vision on market evolution? Will the market be prey or predator?'
- **2001** — Market Knowledge competitive analysis scope: Top three competitors, niche competitors, differentiation, future competitors, top five functional strengths, recent wins/losses
- **2001** — Messaging Workshop Ad/PR agency leverage probe: Results from agency, agency alignment with marketing strategy, compelling written PR/Ad materials, results of latest ad campaign
- **2001** — Messaging Workshop internal staff alignment probe: Sales team aligned with market strategy; all customer-facing employees can give same elevator pitch
- **2001** — Positioning Workshop perception-gap probe: 'What is the perception in the market as to how you are positioned?' vs. 'Do you think your company has the capabilities to meet the market needs?'
- **2001** — Positioning Workshop competitor analysis questions: Competitors' actions, differentiation from competitors, top five functional strengths, competitor weaknesses, five biggest market challenges, recent bid losses
- **2001** — Training Plan tier structure: Tier 1: company-wide 45-minute overview; Tier 2: research training per technology group; Tier 3: streaming-media vignettes follow-up
- **2001** — Training Plan technology groups named: CEC, EBA, Network Services, Platforms
- **2001** — Streaming-media vignettes participants: Tom Willmott and/or Practice Directors
- **2001** — LogicLibrary engagement outcome (Tom Dwyer, 2001): Defined target market for component-based development; substantiated Go To Market, Product, and Professional Services strategies; secured partners and investors
- **2001** — XML Global Technologies engagement outcome (Fessenden, 2001): Branding guidance separating products into foundation technology and turnkey XML enterprise platform suites; accelerated sales cycle
- **2001** — XMLSolutions/Vitria engagement outcome (Fessenden, 2000-2001): EDI/XML positioning for emerging market; ongoing assistance maintained through Vitria acquisition
- **2001** — Pequot Capital due-diligence engagement outcome (Fessenden, 2001): Aberdeen due-diligence study confirmed VC firm's investment opinion
- **2000** — Critical Path engagement outcome (Dana Gardner, 2000): Aberdeen coined 'mixed-source messaging' concept; client led subsequent dramatic increase in mixed-source sales and went on to lead global mixed-source market
- **2001** — BoldFish engagement outcome (Dana Gardner, 2001): Defined tunneled bulk-email infrastructure niche; headed startup toward profitability
- **2001** — Link Software engagement outcome (Dana Gardner, 2001): Advised Web-based and collaboration-market pivot for US market entry; client set up first US sales office
- **2000** — Axiom8 engagement outcome (Dana Gardner, 2000): Advised packaging service components as voice-centric unified communications suite augmenting rather than replacing PBXes; first product debut fall 2000
- **2000** — Digital signature supplier engagement outcome (Hurley, 2000): Full go-to-market plan via field market research covering product concept, pricing, and channel partners
