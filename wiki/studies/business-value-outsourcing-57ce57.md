# The Business Value in IT Outsourcing

> Aberdeen Hypothesis paper (6pp) previewing planned benchmark on IT outsourcing business value. Identifies five drivers for IT outsourcing: core competency focus, cost savings, IT resource constraints, time-to-market pressure, and culture wars. Hypothesizes up to 30% of IT outsourcing engagements are seriously misaligned. Finds 50-67% of contracts running at sub-optimal levels. Estimates only 5-10% of multi-million dollar IT outsourcing agreements drive all anticipated business value. Uses IT maturity model: Best-in-Class (20%), Average (50%), Laggard (30%). Best-in-Class measure ROI after every application change (50%) vs. Laggard (25%).

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2006** — ITO driver - core competency: Focusing on core competencies: decision that IT excellence is best achieved by an outsider
- **2006** — ITO driver - cost savings: Saving money: most expected benefit; palming off dysfunctional IT on naïve outsourcer is a pipe dream
- **2006** — ITO driver - resource constraints: IT resource constraints: lack of talent, costly talent, staff turnover, wrong skill-sets
- **2006** — ITO driver - time to market: Time-to-market concern in highly competitive industries where IT agility is critical
- **2006** — ITO driver - culture wars: Culture wars: LOB management fed up with IT intransigence; outsourcing as 'blow it up' nuclear option
- **2006** — Misaligned ITO engagements hypothesis: Up to 30% of IT outsourcing engagements seriously misaligned; customers may not realize it
- **2006** — Sub-optimal ITO contracts hypothesis: 50-67% of IT outsourcing contracts running at sub-optimal levels
- **2006** — High-value ITO contracts: Only 5-10% of multi-million dollar IT outsourcing agreements drive all anticipated business value
- **2006** — Best-in-Class ROI measurement: 50% of Best-in-Class measure ROI after every application change vs. 25% for Laggards
- **2006** — IT maturity distribution: Best-in-Class: 20% of respondents; Average: 50%; Laggard: 30%
- **2006** — ITO failure factors - SLA: Poor choices in metrics and SLAs, confusing low cost with value delivered, inflexible contract language
- **2006** — Aberdeen ITO tracking since: Aberdeen tracking IT planning and outsourcing issues quantitatively since 2004
- **2006** — Aberdeen IT survey scale: Over 2,000 companies surveyed on IT in past two years
- **2006** — ITO make-or-buy gap: Many enterprises have never done a serious make-or-buy outsourcing decision on any IT segment
- **2006** — Outsourcing lesson universality: Cannot name a single company that has outsourced IT and not learned painful lessons
- **2006** — ITO sub-optimal probing dimensions: SLAs, governance, preparation, transition, operations, change management, pricing, conflict resolution, metrics
- **2006** — ITO benchmark scope: Report will benchmark all-size companies in Global 5000 across all industries
