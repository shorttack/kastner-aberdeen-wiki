# Peter Kastner's Wish List

> Kastner's personal 2002 holiday wish list of six digital consumer technology products: HP Scanjet 5500c, Dell Axim X5 Pocket PC, Viewsonic NextVision N6 PC-HDTV bridge, Gateway Profile 4 XL all-in-one, nVidia GeForce FX graphics card, and Davis Instruments Vantage Pro Plus weather station. Each item is framed as both a consumer product and an industry signal.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner · **Date:** 2002-12-01 · **Type:** dct
**Importance:** medium — *Personal wish list format, but captures the 2002 DCT zeitgeist: Pocket PC/WiFi convergence, PC-HDTV bridges, all-in-one PC renaissance, GPU innovation race, and consumer sensor networks.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Correctly identified Gateway Profile 4 industrial design direction, GPU importance for future Windows UI (Vista Aero delivered 2006), and PDA/phone convergence trajectory. PC-as-HDTV-source (NextVision N6) did not scale.*
