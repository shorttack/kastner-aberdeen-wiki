# PC Deals Weekly Update: July 6, 2003

> EMachines has released a new line-up of desktops in time for the opening of the \"back-to-school\" shopping season. As we've come to expect from eMachines, these PCs offer lots of features for not a lot of cash. The entry-level model, the T2341, costs only $399 (unbundled) and comes with a CD-RW.
