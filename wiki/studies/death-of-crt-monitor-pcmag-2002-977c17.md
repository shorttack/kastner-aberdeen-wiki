# The Death of the CRT Monitor

> PC Magazine / PCMag.com article (Nov 1 2002, Cade Metz) on the LCD-vs-CRT monitor transition. Peter Kastner, Aberdeen Group vice president and senior researcher, provides key market data: LCDs at 40% of desktop-display market with ~2M units/month at $800M revenue/month (average $400/unit). iSuppli/Stanford Resources' Rhonda Alexander offers more conservative 29% LCD share, with tracking from 3% (Q1 2000) → 5% (early 2001) → 21% (Jan 2002). Both analysts predict LCDs will outnumber CRTs as the standard PC monitor in 2003. Planar Systems' Rob Baumgartner addresses video-latency concerns. U Minn Carlson School professors Murtha and Lenway predict LCDs will outnumber CRTs 5:1 by 2006. Kastner's pre-CRO Aberdeen era (VP/senior researcher).

**Author:** Cade Metz, PC Magazine · **Date:** 2002-11-01 · **Type:** news-article
**Importance:** high — *Documents the moment (late 2002) when LCD-CRT transition crossed the analyst-consensus tipping point. Kastner and Alexander both publicly predicting LCD primacy in 2003 gave OEMs cover to accelerate LCD standardization.*
**Prescience:** high — *Kastner and Alexander's 2003 LCD-dominance prediction was validated — 2003 was the first year LCD shipments exceeded CRT shipments in monitors per DisplaySearch/iSuppli data. By 2006 LCDs were essentially universal in new PCs; by 2010 CRT was a niche/legacy category only. Murtha/Lenway's 5:1 by 2006…*

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2002** — LCD share of desktop-display market: LCDs account for only 40 percent of the desktop display market, with roughly 2 million units sold per month
- **2002** — Monthly LCD revenue: LCD sales at $800 million a month, with roughly 2 million displays selling for an average price of $400 each
- **2002** — LCD share (alternative lower estimate): LCD market share at around 29 percent
- **2000** — LCD share Q1 2000: 3 percent in the first quarter of 2000
- **2001** — LCD share early 2001: 5 percent in early 2001
- **2002** — LCD share January 2002: 21 percent in January of this year
- **2003** — LCDs to outnumber CRTs as standard PC monitor in 2003: In 2003, they say, we'll finally see LCDs outnumber CRTs as the standard monitor sold with PCs.
- **2002** — LCDs weak on full-motion video; CRTs retain niche: Because there's often a latency when it comes to displaying full-motion video on an LCD, games freaks and DVD aficionados are going to quibble with the picture and probably will stick with CRTs for the time being
- **2002** — LCD response-time improving with vendor effort: There are still issues with watching movies or video clips on the Internet, but response time is getting better and better
- **2006** — LCDs to outnumber CRTs 5:1 by 2006: By 2006, LCDs will outnumber CRTs by 5 to 1.
- **2003** — LCD shipments first exceeded CRT 2003 globally per DisplaySearch: 2003 was the first year LCD monitor shipments exceeded CRT monitor shipments per DisplaySearch/iSuppli tracking
- **2010** — CRT fully phased out of mainstream PC monitors: Major CRT monitor manufacturers exited by 2008; last Sony CRT TV production ended 2008; CRT PC monitors essentially unavailable in retail by 2010
