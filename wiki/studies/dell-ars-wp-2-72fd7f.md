# Dell Asset Recovery Services: Remove, Recycle, or Resell — An Executive White Paper

> Aberdeen executive white paper (underwritten by Dell) examining the business, data-security, and environmental case for proactive IT-asset disposition. Models total reclamation cost at $115-$387 per device and evaluates Dell's Asset Recovery Services ($49-$69/system) as a compelling alternative. Recommends treating disposal as an up-front TCO element rather than a sunk cost.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner · **Date:** 2003-11-01 · **Type:** white-paper
**Importance:** medium — *Early mainstream positioning of IT-asset disposition as a C-level TCO issue and an early warning on e-waste regulatory exposure; not first-of-kind but widely circulated and framed the category for large enterprises.*
**Prescience:** high — *Correctly predicted tightening e-waste regulations (EU WEEE took effect, state e-waste laws proliferated) and the shift of PC disposal window inside 36 months; secondary-market growth also materialized as predicted.*

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — cost-savings-from-asset-disposition: Annually removing 3,000 PCs saves $1M/year in unnecessary storage and disposal costs.

- **2003** — three-benefit-areas: cost/value, data security, environmental concerns
