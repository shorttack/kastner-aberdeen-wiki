# Data Knowledge: 1998 Practice Summary

> Aberdeen Group's 1998 practice summary for the Data Knowledge and Complex Decision Support practice, covering data warehousing, data marts, OLAP, query/reporting/analysis tools, data mining, and Customer Relationship Management (CRM). The report frames these technologies as components of an enterprise Data Knowledge strategy and assesses their integration with CIS, ERP, and Electronic Commerce applications. Thirteen primary vendors receive detailed abstracts including NCR/Teradata, IBM, Oracle, Cognos, Business Objects, SAS, Arbor/Essbase, and MicroStrategy, plus a second tier of over a dozen additional suppliers.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **1998** — Data warehouse-related technology market size 1996: $5.2 billion (includes hardware software and systems integration consulting)
- **1998** — Data warehouse-related technology market size 1999 projection: $20.6 billion projected
- **1998** — Decision support tools-only market growth: From current $1 billion to approximately $2.5 billion in 1999
- **1998** — Data warehouse cost range: $1 million to $1.5 million typical cost
- **1998** — Data mart cost range: $50000 to $400000 depending on type
- **1998** — Query/reporting/analysis tool cost: Approximately $50000 for web server version; $1500 per component for full client and administration
- **1998** — Multidimensional server cost: Approximately $40000 per server
- **1998** — High-end data mining system cost: $250000 to $400000 for high-end scalable systems
- **1998** — CRM system cost: $200000 to $2.5 million depending on size and existing infrastructure
- **1998** — Data mining as data preparation: Implementing data mining is approximately 75% data preparation
- **1998** — CRM professional services proportion: Professional services can represent as much as 60% of CRM contract
- **1998** — Data warehouse implementation timeline: Data warehouses can take well over a year to implement
- **1998** — Data mart implementation timeline: Between two weeks and six months depending on type and application
- **1998** — CRM implementation timeline: About six to nine months for implementation
- **1998** — NT as data mart platform shift: NT is now considered data mart platform-of-choice; suppliers formerly Unix-focused have tuned for NT; NT has driven down data mart prices
- **1998** — Microsoft Plato market impact prediction: Microsoft Plato acquisition from Panorama will increase consolidation trend; many suppliers embracing Plato and planning partnerships despite uncertainty about what Plato actually is
- **2000** — Microsoft Plato market impact outcome: SQL Server 7.0 OLAP Services (1999) did indeed catalyze market consolidation; several pure-play OLAP vendors declined or were acquired
- **1998** — DSS supplier consolidation prediction: Start-ups without true differentiation will fall on road to IPO or be acquired by predominant suppliers; consolidation becoming more common
- **1998** — Arbor-Hyperion merger: Arbor Software and Hyperion Software announced intentions to merge into Hyperion Solutions; targeting leadership in custom on-demand and packaged analytic applications
- **1998** — IBM data knowledge market position: IBM holds top-ranking position in Data Knowledge marketplace based on combined installed base in hardware software and services; DecisionEdge CRM for finance and insurance
- **1998** — NCR Teradata market position: Reigning monarch in decision support; WorldMark 4700 handles up to 600GB; 5150 for larger; 128-node scalability; prices now compare favorably to Unix competitors
- **1998** — Cognos installed base and portfolio: Cognos has installed more than 700000 seats of Impromptu and PowerPlay; tight resource-sharing linkage between client-server and web versions
- **1998** — SAS Enterprise Miner adoption: SAS Enterprise Miner has supplanted many third-party suppliers' data mining environments of choice in CRM systems; SEMMA methodology considered leading approach
- **1998** — MicroStrategy market position: Approximately 500 installed sites; selected as tool of choice among information providers; DSS Broadcaster narrows information to web/email/pager subscribers
- **1998** — Integrated CIS-DSS solution timeline: Aberdeen believes more integrated CIS-DSS solutions will be introduced in next 12-24 months
