# Future of DVD Panel — DVD Forum 2003 U.S. Conference

> DVD Forum 2003 U.S. Conference panel 'Future of DVD' held Nov 21 2003 in Beverly Hills CA, moderated by Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group. Panelists: Brian Heuckroth (Cirrus Logic) and Matt Jarman (ClearPlay). Documented in Jim Taylor's publications listing (Taylor is author of the 'DVD Demystified' McGraw-Hill book series). The panel occurred just before the Blu-ray vs HD-DVD format war (2006-2008) and during the rise of ClearPlay's parental-filtering DVD technology (which faced lawsuits from Hollywood studios). Kastner's moderator role cements Aberdeen's presence in consumer-electronics industry events alongside his established enterprise-IT coverage.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — Moderated 'Future of DVD' panel at DVD Forum 2003 US Conference: Nov 21 2003, Beverly Hills CA: Panel speaker: Future of DVD (Panelists: Peter Kastner - Aberdeen Group [moderator], Brian Heuckroth - Cirrus Logic, Matt Jarman - ClearPlay)
- **2003** — DVD Forum 2003 US Conference venue and date: Nov 21 2003, Beverly Hills CA
- **2006** — DVD sales peaked 2006 at ~$23B globally: Global DVD sell-through peaked 2006 at approximately $23B; Blu-ray launched 2006 failed to replace sales decline
- **2008** — HD DVD abandoned; Blu-ray wins format war: Toshiba discontinued HD DVD 2008-02-19; Warner's Jan 2008 Blu-ray-exclusive announcement precipitated collapse
- **2007** — Netflix launched streaming service: Netflix Watch Instantly launched Jan 2007; disrupted physical-media rental business and accelerated DVD decline
- **2005** — Family Movie Act 2005 legalized ClearPlay filtering: Family Entertainment and Copyright Act 2005 included the Family Movie Act providing legal safe harbor for third-party content-filtering technology; passed Apr 27 2005
- **2023** — DVD+Blu-ray US sales declined to niche: US DVD+Blu-ray combined physical-media revenue fell to ~$1.5B (2023) from peak ~$16B (2006); category now <5% of video revenue vs streaming
