# HP Pre-Summit Analyst Phone Call — Kastner Feedback

> Transcript of a pre-summit analyst phone call in which HP sought Kastner feedback on corporate messaging, Adaptive Enterprise positioning, divisional execution (PSG, IPG, ESG, Services), and consumer/SMB/enterprise capabilities. Kastner delivers harsh ratings: 'low TCO' message not believable; Adaptive Enterprise too IT-facing; HP-Compaq brand confusion in retail; mobility late to market; strong in digital imaging and printing.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner (Aberdeen Group, Chief Research Officer) · **Date:** 2003-09-01 · **Type:** transcript
**Importance:** high — *Unfiltered primary-source snapshot of leading-analyst HP critique in real time; rare oral-history document capturing both the questions HP was asking and Kastner's verbatim assessment.*
**Prescience:** high — *Kastner's skepticism on Adaptive Enterprise, HP-Compaq brand confusion, and HP's trouble generating new business beyond installed base all proved accurate over the 2004-2015 period (eventual HP-HPE split in 2015).*

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — kastner-hp-focus-areas: Primary focus: PSG and IPG; as Chief Research Officer, covers HP as a company.
- **2003** — hp-messaging-weak: HP corporate messages have been 'very simplistic' and not communicated or drilled down at AR/analyst-facing events.
- **2003** — low-tco-not-believable: HP message of 'low total-cost-of-ownership provider' — not heard, not believable.
- **2003** — reduced-complexity-partial-credit: Reduced-complexity message: believable on consumer side; lesser on IPG; no proof points on ESG; only marginal on services.
- **2003** — ae-too-it-facing: Adaptive Enterprise is too IT-facing and too little business-executive-management-facing.
- **2003** — ae-missing-psg-ipg: Adaptive Enterprise is an ESG and Services initiative; PSG and IPG not tied in.
- **2003** — ae-analyst-beef-request: Analysts want beef — 'too much vision and not enough what-can-I-do-and-how-can-I-do-it.'
- **2003** — ae-vs-ondemand: Neither HP Adaptive Enterprise nor IBM On Demand winning mindshare; On Demand 'too web-services-oriented and not enough infrastructure-metric-oriented.'
- **2003** — ae-rip-and-replace: Adaptive Enterprise essentially says 'Rip and Replace.'
- **2003** — hp-services-capacity-limit: HP Services can handle individual projects but lacks capacity for Fortune 500 company transformation.
- **2003** — hp-divisional-execution: ESG new management due to lack of profitability; PSG barely profitable; IPG very profitable and executing well (high-end printing could do more); consumer space doing best but not profitably.
- **2003** — hp-mobility-late: HP was very late to market with Centrino-based notebooks; has products but has not earned stripes in 'Mobility as a lifestyle.'
- **2003** — hp-imaging-leadership: HP is a leader in digital imaging and photography.
- **2003** — hp-labels-assessment: HP is a technology components company moving to technology solutions; least believable label is 'business solutions company.'
- **2003** — hp-business-value-not-communicated: HP has not demonstrated to analysts that customers achieve business value from HP relationships; not a subject in briefings.
- **2003** — hp-installed-base-problem: Too much HP business comes from installed base — implies trouble generating new customers.
- **2003** — hp-business-it-linkage-spotty: HP's capability to measure and maintain IT-to-business value-chain linkage is spotty — not a core competency.
- **2003** — hp-heterogeneous-integration: HP heterogeneous integration is adequate but not world-leading; modest IBM-mainframe integration skills.
- **2003** — hp-microsoft-underplayed: HP has Unix expertise and growing Linux share but is underplaying the Microsoft market.
- **2003** — hp-smb-seamless-products: 'Products that work seamlessly together' across all geographies/product lines is not believable; IPG in SMB doing good delivery/satisfaction.
- **2003** — hp-smb-partner-network: HP SMB partner network could be better; still 4 different stovepipes at divisional level; SAP solutions initiative earns B-.
- **2003** — hp-partner-loyalty-buys: HP tries to buy channel partner loyalty — probably not enough.
- **2003** — hp-camera-spectrum: HP is not a full-spectrum camera company; no interchangeable-lens model; well-focused for consumers.
- **2003** — hp-compaq-brand-confusion: HP has not adequately differentiated Compaq and HP brands in retail channel; marketing dollars not effective; Dell ruthlessly focused.
- **2003** — hp-radical-simplicity-gap: Not yet the leader in connectivity/end-to-end processing ('radical simplicity'); Internet Home Alliance research showed more customer-faced interaction needed.
