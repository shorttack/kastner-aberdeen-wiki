# IBM + Webify = Industry SOA Application Jumpstart

> Analysis of IBM's acquisition of Webify Solutions and its strategic implications for the nascent SOA application development market. Argues that 'SOA Lite' general-purpose approaches are insufficient for enterprise needs and that IBM with Webify's industry-specific component library is positioned uniquely to provide Enterprise SOA with vertical market focus in insurance, healthcare, banking, and telecoms.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner · **Date:** 2006-08-03 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** high — *Captures a pivotal moment in enterprise SOA/middleware market consolidation; IBM's vertical SOA strategy influenced the industry direction for years.*
**Prescience:** high — *Correctly identified that general-purpose SOA tooling (SOA Lite) would prove insufficient for enterprise-grade applications and that vertical specialization would become essential; consistent with subsequent market evolution.*
