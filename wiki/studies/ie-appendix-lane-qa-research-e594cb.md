# Research Findings: The Role of Professional Services Suppliers' Internal Quality Assurance Organizations in Large-scale Systems Integration Engagements

> Comprehensive comparative study of internal QA programs at eight major IT professional services firms: Ernst & Young, CSC, KPMG, Deloitte Consulting, Keane, EDS, PricewaterhouseCoopers, and Andersen Consulting. Based on telephone interviews with QA practice leaders and document review. Concludes that Andersen Consulting's QA standards and practices were equal to or above industry norms.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **1999** — centralized-qa-org-prevalence: 6 of 8 firms have centralized QA organizations that actively conduct project reviews; remaining 2 have centralized standards bodies with reviews done by senior partners
- **1999** — qa-report-sharing-practice: Only 3 of 8 firms (Deloitte CSC Keane) share complete QA reports with clients as general practice
- **1999** — qa-review-not-for-performance: Majority of firms do not use QA reviews to determine employee performance to encourage openness
- **1999** — pre-engagement-risk-sharing: None of the 8 suppliers share pre-engagement risk assessments with potential clients
- **1999** — qa-legal-use-precedent: None of the interviewees could recall a single instance where internal QA documents were used in legal actions
- **1999** — overall-qa-assessment: Andersen's internal QA standards and practices are equal to or above industry norms in centralized coordination and mandated processes
- **1999** — mandatory-qa-review-policy: Andersen requires all projects undergo at least one independent QA review; this is above industry norms where reviews are typically risk/revenue-based
- **1999** — qms-architecture: Quality Management System (QMS) covers all quality management from internal best practices to external client satisfaction; CQM component manages engagement quality
- **1999** — engagement-risk-assessment: Every engagement requires Engagement Risk Assessment (ERA) at opportunity stage; engagement partner decides based on risk level whether independent CQMA is needed
- **1999** — cqma-review-stages: CQMAs may occur at opportunity assessment and proposal preparation and contractual commitment and execution stages; at least one during execution is mandatory
- **1999** — quality-advisor-program: Quality Advisor program started 1992; outgrowth of internal restructuring triggered by unhappy client engagements from Ernst and Whinney / Arthur Young merger
- **1999** — qa-threshold-revenue: Quality Advisor assigned to all engagements with revenues above $1M; advisor at partner level or higher and recognized expert in practice area
- **1999** — qa-review-frequency: Quarterly reviews lasting up to 2-3 days; red/yellow/green flag system for findings; poor QA review can negatively affect engagement manager bonus
- **1999** — qa-report-sharing-ey: Provides summary but not detailed reports of quarterly review to clients based on advice of internal counsel
- **1999** — cmm-maturity-levels: TMG at CMM Level 2 working toward Level 3; Federal unit at Level 4; two federal units (SEAS and Aerospace IT) at Level 5
- **1997** — seas-iso-certification: SEAS earned ISO 9001 certification in 1997
- **1999** — tmg-qa-role: TMG overseeing QA for commercial business units since 1996; does not conduct QA testing but ensures processes modeled after SEI practices
- **1999** — qa-reporting-method: QA reports based on Balanced Scorecard approach tracking errors and corrections and progress on outstanding errors
- **1999** — corporate-knowledge-program: Corporate Knowledge Program supported by Lotus Notes database capturing plans and processes and procedures available worldwide
- **1999** — no-legal-actions-since-tmg: Interviewee could not recall a single legal action against CSC for software projects since 1996 when TMG was established
- **1999** — qa-model-transition: KPMG moved from post-engagement quality review to proactive QA model in 1996 coinciding with consulting reorganization
- **1999** — qap-threshold-and-staffing: QAP under National Risk Management Partner staffed by 3 senior partners; any engagement over $1M assigned QAP team member
- **1999** — qa-review-process-kpmg: 4-5 day reviews including client interviews (2-4 key personnel); red/yellow/green risk flags; monthly follow-up for red-flagged projects
- **1999** — km-rollout: KPMG in process of phased rollout of knowledge management tool to consolidate engagement histories and best practices with practice-specific templates
- **1999** — risk-qa-separation: Deloitte separates Risk Management (corporate function with National Partner) and QA/QC (practice-level Senior Partner) into entirely separate functions
