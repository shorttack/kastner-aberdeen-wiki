# Industry Analyst Relations at Spring IDF 2005 — Peter Kastner Delegate Confirmation Letter

> Intel Analyst Relations personalized schedule for Peter Kastner (Vericours, Inc., P.O. Box 3480, Westport MA) for Spring IDF 2005 at Moscone West, San Francisco, March 1-3.
