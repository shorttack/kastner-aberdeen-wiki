# Making the Corporate Platform Decision (ITT Hartford)

> Aberdeen Group consulting presentation for ITT Hartford on corporate platform decision-making. Background is 1994 Commercial Lines Coop review. Covers desktop OS selection (Windows 95 vs OS/2 Warp vs NT Workstation), workgroup OS strategy (Windows NT/AS and Novell NetWare), platform management, network management, groupware, electronic mail, and distributed software management. 9-slide deck.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1995 · **Type:** consulting-report
**Importance:** high — *Consulting engagement for major insurance firm; demonstrates Aberdeen methodology for corporate platform decision-making; historically significant as Windows 95 era enterprise OS selection document.*
**Prescience:** high — *Correctly selected Windows 95 as desktop OS winner; correctly predicted NT/NetWare dual workgroup-OS strategy would be pragmatic; Windows 95 indeed became dominant desktop OS, validating Aberdeen recommendation.*
