# PC Lifecycle Dynamics (SIPP Florida speech)

> Speech delivered by Peter S. Kastner (Executive Vice President and Chief Research Officer, Aberdeen Group) at the SIPP conference in Florida on June 30, 2003. The 25-slide deck on PC Lifecycle Dynamics covers client-side goals vs realities, Aberdeen's Best Practices framework prioritized by ROI, image stability, total cost of ownership analytics, and case studies in Communications, Financial Services, and Beverages industries. Audience polls open the deck on TCO analytics maturity. Aberdeen Best Practices ranking: standard images on common platforms, automated backup/restore/healing, software distribution, device synchronization, remote control, and thin clients.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner · **Date:** 2003-06-30 · **Type:** presentation
**Importance:** high — *Primary-source Kastner speech artifact: full Aberdeen Best Practices framework for enterprise PC lifecycle management as he was presenting it at an industry conference in mid-2003 — a uniquely complete view of his analytical framing in his prime as Aberdeen EVP/CRO.*

His emphasis on closing the gap between IT goals and business reality presaged modern platform-engineering and FinOps disciplines.*

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — Audience poll on TCO analytics maturity: Poll #1: How do you analyze and control your desktop/laptop TCO? Options ranged from 'good TCO reporting and strong strategies' to 'we don't do much; putting off purchases and cannibalizing/repairing'
- **2003** — Goals-vs-realities framing: Goals: flexible acquisition, right-sizing, user-driven choices, low lifecycle costs. Realities: project-driven acquisition, user-driven config, high support costs, config variability, constant changes, innumerable platforms
- **2003** — Best Practices TCO impact: Best Practices lower TCO across downtime, end-user operations, admin, operations, and hardware/software cost categories on annual cost per user basis
- **2003** — Best Practices ROI ranking — top: Aberdeen Best Practices Prioritized by ROI (faster to slower): Standard Images on Common Platforms (highest ROI)
- **2003** — Best Practices ROI ranking — second: Automated Backup/Restore/Healing — second highest ROI
- **2003** — Best Practices ROI ranking — third: Software Distribution — mid ROI tier
- **2003** — Best Practices ROI ranking — fourth: Device Synchronization — mid ROI tier
- **2003** — Best Practices ROI ranking — fifth: Remote Control — lower ROI tier
- **2003** — Best Practices ROI ranking — slowest: Thin Clients ranked slowest ROI in Aberdeen 2003 PC lifecycle Best Practices framework
