# Less Is More: Stylin' PCs

> Wired article on the emerging shift of desktop PCs from utilitarian beige boxes into smaller, design-forward living-room form factors. Quotes Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group on how 'the next form factor battles are beginning to be fought in the living room' and documents Gateway, Sony, and HP's early thin/quiet SFF PCs. Frames the consumer-PC aesthetic/size convergence with consumer electronics as a central competitive axis.

**Author:** Elisa Batista (reporter); Peter Kastner (quoted source) · **Date:** 2004-04-28 · **Type:** press-article
**Importance:** medium — *Captures a 2004 inflection point where PC vendors began treating aesthetics and living-room placement as product strategy, prefiguring the SFF/HTPC/Mac mini era and ultimately the living-room media PC and Apple TV categories.*
**Prescience:** high — *Kastner's prediction that 'next form factor battles are being fought in the living room' anticipated Mac mini (2005), Windows Media Center / HTPC growth, Apple TV (2007) and the broader mini-PC category; quiet small form-factor PCs became mainstream.*
