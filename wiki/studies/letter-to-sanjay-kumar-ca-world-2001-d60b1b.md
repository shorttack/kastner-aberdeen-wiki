# Letter to Sanjay Kumar on CA World 2001

> Post-CA-World-2001 thank-you letter from Peter S. Kastner (Founder and Chief Research Officer, Aberdeen Group) to Sanjay Kumar (CEO, Computer Associates), dated July 19, 2001. Observations include: CA's analyst-relations team (Debra Cattani, Janice Thomas) as industry-leading; CA customer-satisfaction at a nine-year high; the 'CA animosity factor' reduced; press perception lagging reality; dismissive assessment of the 'Ranger situation' (Sam Wyly/Ranger Governance proxy contest); positive review of the 3x6 CA World branding framework.

- **2001** — Kastner role 2001: Signed as 'Founder and Chief Research Officer', Aberdeen Group.
- **2001** — CA analyst-relations team quality: Aberdeen rated CA's analyst-relations team as 'at the top of the industry'; Debra Cattani leading, Janice Thomas providing 'superlative' day-to-day support.
- **2001** — CA customer satisfaction trajectory: CA customers 'more satisfied today than at any time since I started following CA in 1992' — Kastner nine-year perspective.
- **2001** — 'CA animosity factor' reduced: Long-standing CA reputation problem with customers was substantially reduced by mid-2001.
- **2001** — Press lag vs customer reality: Uninformed press still operating from 'old, bad CA' mindset; Kastner recommends quietly focused PR campaign to mitigate over time.

- **2001** — Ranger proxy contest actual outcome: Ranger Governance lost the August 2001 CA proxy contest (~73% of shareholders voted with CA management).
- **2001** — CA World 2001 branding assessment: The '3x6 theme' (six business values across three market segments) produced cohesive branding across executive presentations, show floor, press conferences; 'something for everyone'; energized sales force and channel partners.
- **2001** — CA momentum framing: 'The 'new CA' gathering momentum — and I mean that in the physics sense.' Kastner used the physics metaphor (mass x velocity) deliberately.
