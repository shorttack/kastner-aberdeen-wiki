# Apple's Enterprise IT Battle Plan, Part One

> LinuxInsider/MacNewsWorld feature on Apple's enterprise IT ambitions post-Xserve, framed through Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group. Kastner argues Apple needs 'a specialized SWAT team out of Cupertino' to make business cases to skeptical enterprises, and flags Apple's weak indirect channel as the key obstacle. Article covers Xserve, Xgrid grid-computing, education/scientific footholds, and Apple's pricing strategy.

**Author:** Keith Regan (reporter, MacNewsWorld/LinuxInsider); Peter Kastner (quoted source) · **Date:** 2004-05-21 · **Type:** press-article
**Importance:** medium — *Mid-2000s documentation of Apple's enterprise strategy just after Xserve's entry, with Kastner providing a crisp analyst diagnosis (channel weakness) that framed subsequent trade-press coverage.*
**Prescience:** high — *Kastner's 'weak indirect channel' diagnosis proved correct: Xserve was discontinued in 2011, Xgrid deprecated in OS X Lion (2011), and Apple retreated from server hardware — broadly the outcome predicted by the channel-limitation thesis.*
