# Microsoft Asked To Change Misleading Linux Ad

> LinuxInsider coverage of a UK Advertising Standards Authority action requiring Microsoft to change a misleading Linux comparison ad. Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group frames the European regulatory context, noting growing government adoption of Linux and that while Microsoft has 'a lot of friends' in Europe it is increasingly under scrutiny. Article ties ad-compliance ruling to broader open-source political momentum in the EU.

**Author:** Elizabeth Millard (reporter, LinuxInsider); Peter Kastner (quoted source) · **Date:** 2004-08-25 · **Type:** press-article
**Importance:** medium — *Notable mid-2000s datapoint on Microsoft's European PR/compliance posture toward Linux and Kastner's read on EU regulatory dynamics favoring open source.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Kastner's framing of growing EU government Linux adoption broadly tracked with subsequent munich-limux, gendarmerie, and EU open-source mandates, though Munich eventually reversed. His 'Microsoft has many friends in Europe' hedge also proved accurate.*
