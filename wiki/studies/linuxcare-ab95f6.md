# Linuxcare, Inc. -- Enterprise Service and Support for All Things Linux

> Aberdeen Group profiles Linuxcare, Inc., a San Francisco-based specialist in enterprise Linux service and support founded in 1998. The report evaluates Linuxcare's four service pillars -- technical support, professional services, education/training, and benchmarking/certification -- and its partnerships with Dell, IBM, Sun, and TurboLinux. Aberdeen concludes Linuxcare has proven viability as a Linux expertise source but faces challenges scaling headcount and quality assurance.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1999-11-01 · **Type:** white-paper
**Importance:** high — *First detailed Aberdeen profile of a pure-play Linux services firm; captures the nascent commercial Linux support ecosystem at a critical inflection point in 1999.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Aberdeen correctly identified Linux adoption acceleration and need for professional services; Linuxcare itself did not survive as an independent company (acquired/restructured by 2001).*

## Key observations (top 25)

- **1999** — Linuxcare founding and mission: Founded 1998 as Linux consulting firm; evolved to full enterprise Linux specialist
- **1999** — Linuxcare venture funding: Kleiner Perkins Caulfield and Byers chose Linuxcare as first Linux-related investment
- **1999** — Linuxcare enterprise viability: Aberdeen views Linuxcare as having proven viability as source of Linux expertise
- **2001** — Linuxcare survival as independent company: unknown
- **1999** — Linux adoption trajectory in enterprise: IT departments increasingly using Linux for email, file/print, web servers; emerging for decision support, caching, storage, DNS, web commerce
- **1999** — Dell-Linuxcare support contract scope: Dell contracted Linuxcare for back-end Linux support on OptiPlex, Dimension desktop lines, Precision workstations, PowerEdge Servers
- **1999** — IBM-Linuxcare contract: IBM contracted Linuxcare to support Linux on RS/6000 line
- **1999** — Sun-Linuxcare contract: Sun signed Linuxcare to support Star Office suite (acquired from Star Division)
- **1999** — Linuxcare platform support breadth: Supports all Linux hardware platforms: Alpha, Intel, MIPS, PowerPC, Sparc, StrongArm
- **1999** — Linuxcare support tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum tiers varying in price, response time, contacts
- **1999** — Linuxcare University curriculum: Courses: Linux System Administration, Network Administration, Security, Overview for NT Professionals, Samba, Linux for Executives
- **1999** — Linuxcare Labs certification: Third-party hardware/software certification; Dell and Compaq DeskPro already certified
- **1999** — Linuxcare Knowledgebase: Free online interactive support database; searchable answers, FAQs, patches, migration tips
- **1999** — Key management hires: Sarrat (CEO, ex-Cylink/IBM), Pollace (CFO, 30+ yr experience), Nassaur (CIO, ex-E*Trade), Lambs (VP Services, ex-DEC 18yr), Phillips (VP Sales, 25yr)
- **1999** — Linux application port availability: Within next year IT managers will have Linux alternative in every major application area
- **2001** — Linux coverage across application areas: unknown
- **1999** — Aberdeen conclusion on Linuxcare: Credible source of Linux expertise; should be considered by IT executives deploying Linux as alternative enterprise OS
- **1999** — TurboLinux-Linuxcare support deal: TurboLinux tapped Linuxcare to provide services/support to North American customers
- **1999** — Linuxcare geographic support availability: All services in English at time of profile; Asian support facilities targeted for completion (date TBD)
