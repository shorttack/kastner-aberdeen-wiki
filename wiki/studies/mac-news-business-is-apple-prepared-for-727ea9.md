# Is Apple Prepared for a Succession?

> MacNewsWorld analysis of Apple's succession planning after Steve Jobs's pancreatic cancer disclosure and 30-day medical leave (August 2004). Peter Kastner, executive vice president at Aberdeen Group, plays down near-term governance risk — the head of sales is running the company during back-to-school season — but the article broadly raises concern about Apple's lack of disclosed succession plan.

**Author:** Blane Warrene (reporter, MacNewsWorld); Peter Kastner, Chad Mader (quoted sources) · **Date:** 2004-08-03 · **Type:** press-article
**Importance:** medium — *Captures Kastner's 2004 governance read of Apple at the moment of Jobs's cancer disclosure; an important inflection point in Apple governance history.*
