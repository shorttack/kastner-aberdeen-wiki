# The Science of This Year's WWDC

> MacNewsWorld WWDC 2004 preview focused on Apple's scientific-computing push. Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group notes Xgrid has a 'solid foothold at universities' and is worth watching as grid computing matures into the mainstream. Article also covers the Apple Workgroup Cluster for Bioinformatics Award Program and Apple's positioning around Tiger previews.

**Author:** Elizabeth Millard (reporter, MacNewsWorld); Peter Kastner (quoted source) · **Date:** 2004-06-18 · **Type:** press-article
**Importance:** medium — *Captures a specific, datable Kastner framing of Apple Xgrid as university-grounded with watchful upside — an archival record of Apple's brief scientific-computing moment around Xserve G5.*
**Prescience:** low — *Kastner's 'worth watching as grid computing matures' was tentative but ultimately did not pan out for Xgrid specifically: grid computing broadly evolved into HPC cluster management and cloud, with Xgrid deprecated.*
