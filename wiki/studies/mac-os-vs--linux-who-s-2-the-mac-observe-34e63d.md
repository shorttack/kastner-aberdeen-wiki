# Mac OS vs. Linux: Who's #2?

> The Mac Observer analytical piece reconciling competing claims about #2 desktop OS share between Mac OS X and Linux. Authors aggregate data from HP, IDC, Wired, Gartner, Google browser logs, and analyst commentary — including Peter Kastner's ~3% Mac share estimate (originally to Wired) as a secondary-source quote. Concludes that definitions matter: Linux installed seats (including piracy, embedded, preloaded non-Windows PCs) and active Mac desktop users are measured inconsistently.

**Author:** Bryan Chaffin and Vern Seward (The Mac Observer); Peter Kastner (secondary-source quote) · **Date:** 2004-08-11 · **Type:** press-article
**Importance:** medium — *Most thorough single-article reconciliation of the 2004 Mac-vs-Linux #2 debate; preserves Kastner's numeric claim alongside IDC, HP, Gartner, and Google-browser datapoints.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Kastner's secondary-sourced ~3% Mac / lower Linux framing proved durable in broad strokes; Chaffin/Seward's broader thesis that measurement definitions distort share comparisons is still operative in analyst disputes.*
