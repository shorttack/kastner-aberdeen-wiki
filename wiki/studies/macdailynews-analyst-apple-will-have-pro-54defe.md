# Analyst: 'Apple will have problems filling' G5 orders; demand 'will not impact Apple's market position'

> MacDailyNews reprint of a NewsFactor story on the Power Mac G5 launch. Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group tells Mac buyers they 'should run, not walk' to check out the G5, calling Apple 'arguably a performance leader' with the new PowerPC 970 dual-2GHz machines priced at $1,999 / $2,399 / $2,999. Roger Kay of IDC provides counter-balancing skepticism about G5 impact on Apple's overall desktop market share. Article captures analyst divide on whether supply constraints will matter.

**Author:** Wrolstad (reporter, via NewsFactor/MacDailyNews); Peter Kastner and Roger Kay (quoted sources) · **Date:** 2003-08-19 · **Type:** press-article
**Importance:** medium — *Primary-source record of Kastner's enthusiastic G5-launch framing at a major Apple inflection — the arrival of the IBM PowerPC 970 as the first 64-bit Mac CPU and the closing chapter of the AIM (Apple-IBM-Motorola) alliance.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Kastner's 'performance leader' claim was directionally valid at launch (G5 SPEC scores competitive with x86 at the time), but G5 heat/yield problems ultimately forced Apple to switch to Intel (2005 announcement, 2006 transition), validating the supply-concerns angle.*
