# Make claiming rebates easier for consumers (CA SB 1154 support letter)

> Consumers Union letter to California Governor Arnold Schwarzenegger urging signature of SB 1154 (Figueroa), a bill making it easier for consumers to claim mail-in rebates. The letter cites Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group (via a NY Times article of April 17, 2003) as an authoritative source on why consumers fail to collect rebates. Represents Kastner's analyst commentary feeding into legislative policy advocacy.

**Author:** Shelley Curran (Consumers Union / Consumer Reports); Peter Kastner (secondary-source citation via NY Times) · **Date:** 2004-09-03 · **Type:** policy-advocacy
**Importance:** medium — *Rare example of Kastner's Aberdeen commentary being cited in a consumer-protection policy letter — an unusual citation context for technology-analyst research.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Kastner's diagnosis (consumers forget, miss deadlines, or submit incomplete paperwork) was validated by continued high rebate slippage rates through the 2000s-2010s and by the industry's eventual migration to instant rebates.*
