# Microsoft Launches BackOffice Small Business Server: First Integrated Server Suite Optimized for Small Business

> Microsoft press release announcing general availability of BackOffice Small Business Server at the October 1997 Small Business Server Technology Conference in San Jose. Peter S. Kastner (Aberdeen Group VP) provides a strong third-party endorsement, recommending that any U.S. business without a full-time IT department 'run, not walk' to the product for its ease of installation, attractive price, and integrated Internet access.

**Author:** Microsoft Corporation (press release) · **Date:** 1997-10-22 · **Type:** press-release
**Importance:** medium — *First-of-its-kind integrated server bundle for the SMB market. Launched a product line (SBS) that shipped through 2012.*
**Prescience:** high — *Kastner's enthusiasm was borne out: SBS shipped continuously 1997-2012 and seeded today's SMB all-in-one stacks (Windows Server Essentials, Microsoft 365 Business, managed-service-provider bundles).*

## Key observations (top 25)

- **1997** — Kastner adoption recommendation for SMB: Businesses without a full-time IT department should 'run, not walk' to BackOffice SBS; installation and administration are ingeniously easy, price attractive, Internet access commendable.
- **1997** — BackOffice SBS general availability: Announced at sold-out Small Business Server Technology Conference with 1,500 industry partners in attendance; immediate availability.
- **1997** — Target market: Integrated suite optimized for small businesses; enables them to share information, resources, and establish Internet presence.
- **1997** — SMB ease-of-use viability: Kastner endorsement predicts that the simplified bundled install/admin model will succeed with non-IT-staffed SMBs.
- **2012** — SBS lifecycle outcome: SBS shipped continuously through SBS 2011, discontinued in favor of Windows Server Essentials in 2012; succeeded by cloud bundles (Microsoft 365 Business).
