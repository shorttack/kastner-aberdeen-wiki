# Mirror Image Internet Advances Delivery Services for Current and New Customers

> Aberdeen vendor profile of Mirror Image Internet, a CDN (content delivery network) service provider, produced in May 2004 and underwritten by Mirror Image. Profiles the company's Content Access Point (CAP) architecture, two customer case studies (Fastclick online advertising; anonymous international toy retailer), and the new Smart Content services platform combining XML-based rules engine with CDN delivery. Aberdeen concludes Mirror Image meets CDN availability/cost-cutting requirements and recommends evaluation of next-generation application delivery capabilities.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner · **Date:** 2004-05 · **Type:** case-analysis
**Importance:** medium — *Vendor profile captures CDN market dynamics post-dot-bomb shakeout (40 vendors to fewer than 12); illustrates how CDN survivors pivoted to application delivery.*
**Prescience:** high — *Correctly forecast that CDN value would move beyond static/streaming content to application delivery; predicted XML rules-based content personalization and adaptive content; all became mainstream CDN features.*

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2001** — CDN vendor count at peak: more than 40 vendors
- **2004** — CDN vendor count after shakeout: fewer than a dozen
- **2004** — Mirror Image market position: one of top CDN vendors post-shakeout
- **2004** — Mirror Image employee count: 75 employees
- **2004** — Mirror Image founding date: 1997
- **2003** — Fastclick ad impression volume: more than 5 billion impressions per month (late 2003)
- **2003** — CDN cost reduction benefit for Fastclick: fewer servers, less room, less power, less floor space
- **2003** — Security benefit of CDN for Fastclick: traffic filtering/IDS not needed at origin; all via CDN
- **2004** — CDN usage growth trend: rose dramatically 2001-2004 despite vendor shakeout
- **2004** — CDN evolution direction: moving beyond static/streaming to application delivery
- **2004** — Custom reporting tool development time: less than 45 days from request to production
- **2004** — CDN baseline pricing pressure: significant price pressure on straight image/web site delivery
- **2004** — Smart Content platform capabilities: XML rules engine; geotargeting; time-of-day; rotation; wild-card replacement
- **2004** — Adaptive caching interest from retailers: central to on-the-fly content decisions by region
- **2004** — CDN evaluation recommendation: IT groups + marketing/e-commerce/creative teams should evaluate distributed application delivery capabilities
- **2004** — CDN verifiable data requirement: customers need verifiable data before shifting more content/app delivery to CDNs
- **2004** — CAP architecture benefit: minimizes Mirror Image infrastructure costs; translates to lower client costs
- **2004** — Non-IT content management via CDN: allows non-IT groups to manage content delivery directly
- **2004** — Mirror Image long-term positioning: positioned to succeed as CDN evolves to application delivery
