# NCR: Bringing Highly-Available NT to Transaction-Intensive Production Environments

> This 1997 Aberdeen Group vendor profile abstract documents Aberdeen's findings from in-depth interviews with IS managers who deployed NT servers, specifically comparing NCR's NT offerings against Compaq, Hewlett-Packard, and IBM. Aberdeen concludes that NCR's NT products are distinguishable in processing power, applications focus, and service/support availability, and recommends NCR for IS decision makers seeking High Availability Transaction Processing (HATP) servers.
