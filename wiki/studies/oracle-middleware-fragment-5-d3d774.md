# Middleware/EAI Alternatives and Portera Pre-Integration (Draft Fragment)

> Draft fragment from Katherine Jones to Tom Dwyer revising a section on middleware/EAI alternatives for the Aberdeen Oracle ROI white paper. Defines middleware, documents a Senior VP of Infrastructure's negative experience with EAI tooling, and analyzes Portera's pre-integrated bundle (Oracle Financials + PeopleSoft HR + Siebel CRM) as a 'buy pre-integrated' alternative.

**Author:** Katherine Jones (Aberdeen Group) · **Date:** 2001-05-06 · **Type:** white-paper
**Importance:** low — *Internal editorial fragment of the larger Oracle ROI paper — modest on its own, but captures Aberdeen's working middleware definition and a documented customer rejection of EAI tooling.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Anticipated XML-standard-driven integration improvements; accurately predicted that integrator viability (not just integrated-vendor viability) becomes a new risk vector.*
