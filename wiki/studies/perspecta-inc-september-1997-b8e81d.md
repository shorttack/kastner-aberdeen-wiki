# Perspecta Inc. — September 1997

> Aberdeen Group profiles Perspecta Inc., a San Francisco startup that developed OLAP-I (OLAP for Information) — a server-based technology applying OLAP-style analytics to non-numeric content. The SmartContent system dynamically reorganizes information in real-time based on user behavior. Aberdeen endorsed Perspecta as a strong candidate for applications requiring more effective information navigation interfaces.

**Author:** Aberdeen Group (Matt Nerney) · **Date:** 1997-09-01 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** medium — *Aberdeen's endorsement of an early non-numeric OLAP startup was noteworthy in 1997 when the intersection of OLAP analytics and unstructured content was novel; the study identified a real market gap but covered only a single small vendor.*
**Prescience:** [DEFERRED] — *Perspecta Inc. was a small startup that did not achieve commercial scale; the specific OLAP-I concept was superseded by XML, semantic search, and modern AI-driven content discovery, limiting ongoing applicability.*
