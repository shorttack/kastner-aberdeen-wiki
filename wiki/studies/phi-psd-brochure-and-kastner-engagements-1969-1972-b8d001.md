# PHI Computer Services Professional Services Division: Brochure with Kastner Profile, Memoir of Junior Programmer Era, and PHI Engagements (1969-1972)

> Combines a primary-source PHI Update brochure (~1971-1972) describing PHI's Professional Services Division (PSD) — including Kastner's profile as a Systems Analyst in the Financial Systems Group — with Kastner's personal memoir of his March 1969 entry into PHI as a junior programmer at $7,200/year, the 1960s-era apprenticeship learning model, and his work on Marine Midland bond-portfolio Y2K-precursor changes, the First Chicago correspondent-bank DDA system, the Cumberland Farms gas-station accounting system, and the optimization of PHI's internal accounting system.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **1959** — PHI founding: PHI began as a cellar operation in 1959 with four 'whiz kids' applying technical theory to specific problems; brochure-stated; Kenney book says 1958
- **1971** — PHI scale: PHI grew to one of leading full-service data processing companies in the nation, providing services to 400+ clients across three operating divisions
- **1971** — PHI ranking: PHI 'third in the nation' per back-cover recruiting ad
- **1959** — Hankins as founder: Philip Hankins founded PHI; later research showed he had programmed parts of Apollo moon project navigation
- **1968** — Wang acquires PHI: Wang Laboratories acquired PHI in 1968
- **1969** — Junior programmer hire: Kastner started at PHI March 1969 as junior programmer at $7,200/year
- **1969** — Salary context: Bank vice presidents earned ~$6,500; Kastner's $7,200 programmer salary exceeded VP salaries — 'titles are cheap'
- **1969** — Computer time vs labor cost: CPU time was $360/CPU-hour at PHI; 20 hours equaled Kastner's annual pay; computer time was precious, programmer time was not
- **1969** — PHI accounting optimization project: Kastner hired to fix PHI internal accounting system that was costing thousands of dollars/month in CPU time
- **1969** — SMF billing optimization: PHI billed customers by I/O calls (not elapsed time) using SMF; Kastner discovered MVT consumed significant billable time itself
- **1969** — 6-to-2 program consolidation: Kastner combined six PHI accounting COBOL programs into two with internal sorts, cutting operating costs by half (per ADL bio)
- **1969** — Apprenticeship learning: PHI data center systems programmers taught Kastner JCL, I/O minimization, dump reading; apprenticeship was main path
- **1969** — Promotion to PSD: After 90 days, Kastner promoted from junior programmer to programmer/analyst in PHI Professional Services Division
- **1971** — Brochure profile: PHI brochure describes Kastner as 'Systems Analyst with PSD's Financial Systems Group' working on PHI internal accounting and First Chicago correspondent-bank DDA
- **1970** — First Chicago DDA: Kastner programmed correspondent-bank DDA system for First Chicago covering 14 correspondents with custom-tailored apps based on run-time parameters and flexible model software in COSMOS
- **1970** — Cumberland Farms: Kastner designed/programmed retail gas-station accounting system in IBM COBOL; daily cash transmittal proved to pump meter readings
- **1970** — Marine Midland Y2K-precursor: Kastner designed bond-portfolio analysis changes allowing 30-year bonds traded in 1970 — early Y2K-precursor
- **1971** — PHI Payroll on-line batch: Kastner designed modifications to PHI Payroll for online batch entry plus concurrent master-file processing for client with 460 customers
- **1971** — PSD groups: PHI PSD composed of four groups: Commercial Programming, Financial Systems, Systems Analysis & Design, Systems Programming
- **1971** — TPS major accomplishment: Group developed PHI Telecommunications Programming System (TPS), regarded as most comprehensive front-end software for IBM telecom environment; plug-replacement for IBM 2700-series TCU
- **1971** — Interface Laboratory: PSD operated 'PHI Interface Laboratory' for testing System/360 channel interfaces for Honeywell H-516, Bunker Ramo, Data General Nova, DEC PDP-11, Tempo 1; nicknamed 'Underwriters Laboratory of the 360 Interface'
- **1971** — IBM 360/20 utilities: PHI developed tape/disk utilities for IBM System/360 Model 20 provided as Class A support by IBM
- **1971** — SANDAC 200 OS: PHI developed real-time operating system for Sanders SANDAC 200 communications processor
- **1971** — HP 2100 simulator: PHI developed System/360-resident simulator for HP 2100 series
- **1971** — Wang 3300 R&D: PHI R&D group helped develop software for new Wang 3300 computer series using third-generation computer technology
