## Key observations (top 25)

- **1988** — Aberdeen Group tenure start: Joined Aberdeen as VP in 1988; currently EVP; 30+ years IT experience
- **1987** — DEC marketing role: Marketing executive at Digital Equipment Corporation 1987-1988; commercial market competitive analysis
- **1979** — Stratus Computer role: Marketing executive at Stratus Computer (prior to DEC)
- **1979** — Arthur D. Little tenure: Over 8 years as consultant/program manager at Arthur D. Little Inc.; dozens of complex technology development projects
- **1969** — Wang subsidiary programmer: 3 years as programmer/analyst at Wang Laboratories subsidiary; internal accounting systems; application software factory
- **1992** — Case: Computer Consoles vs GTE Government Systems: Kastner supported plaintiff counsel Blanc Gilburne; reviewed CCI's RISC-Unix technical documentation; proposed as expert witness in commercial computer performance; case settled with substantial damages for plaintiff
- **1994** — Case: GTE Products Corporation v. American Software, Inc.: Kastner supported defense counsel Fulbright & Jaworski; software development breach of contract; Aberdeen assessed technical/project management aspects of IBM AS/400 and 3090 mainframe application development case
- **1989** — Case: Unisys vs Florida HRS: Kastner supported defense counsel Carlton Fields for Unisys; Unisys protested EDS/IBM bid for distributed human resources system; served as plaintiff expert witness; while plaintiff not sustained Florida cancelled $110M EDS contract afterward
- **1992** — Florida HRS EDS contract cancellation: Florida cancelled $110M EDS contract for non-performance; precisely the technical areas Kastner predicted were inadequate
- **1995** — Case: Crossroads Software vs Open Systems Advisors: Kastner supported defense counsel Fish & Richardson for defendant Crossroads Software; trademark infringement claim on 'crossroads' name; judge denied preliminary injunction citing Kastner's affidavit; case settled out of court
- **1997** — Case: IE vs Andersen Consulting LLP (Chester County PA No. 96-04673): Kastner retained by Montgomery McCracken for Andersen Consulting as defendant's fact expert witness; IE accused Andersen of failing $17M ERP project (order management, inventory, warehouse, financial accounting); Kastner produced 129-page expert witn…
- **1997** — IE vs Andersen: HP 3000 fitness for purpose issue: Key issue: whether Andersen should have recommended replacing IE's existing HP 3000 computer system; technology maturity and fitness for purpose in mid-1990s context
- **1998** — Published works count: Author or co-author of three book-length computer technology market research studies; numerous white papers; widely quoted in trade press; TV appearances; guest lecturer at MIT and Federal University Rio de Janeiro
- **1998** — Kastner expertise domains: OLTP, decision support, client/server architectures, distributed commercial systems development, electronic commerce, commercial benchmark reviewing/analysis; pioneer in industry-standard commercial performance benchmarks
- **1998** — Aberdeen Group litigation practice: Aberdeen performs custom projects; 50-person research organization; principals quoted extensively in publications; frequent conference/seminar speakers
