## Key observations (top 25)

- **1999** — Aberdeen Group role: Executive Vice President and Chief Research Officer; responsible for ~40 analysts; officer and board member
- **1988** — Aberdeen Group VP start year: Joined Aberdeen as Vice President in 1988
- **1988** — DEC role: Marketing Executive at Digital Equipment Corporation 1987-1988; commercial market competitive analysis for enterprise marketing campaign
- **1987** — Stratus Computer role: Market Development Manager 1981-1987; alternate channels, sales support, trade shows, advertising, partnering, press/analyst relations, customer visits
- **1981** — Prime Computer role: Market Development Manager 1979-1981; government, finance, manufacturing markets; business planning, sales training, vertical market seminars
- **1979** — Arthur D. Little tenure: Group Program Manager/Program Manager/Consultant 1971-1979; dozens of complex technology development projects spanning manufacturing, banking, health care, government; US and overseas
- **1971** — PHI Computer Services role: Senior Programmer/Analyst at PHI Computer Services (Wang subsidiary) 1969-1971; commercial applications and systems development; developed payroll/personnel systems, banking applications, application development tools
- **1969** — Cornell University consulting: Independent consultant at Cornell University 1967-1969; contract application development of commercial, scientific, academic applications
- **1990** — 1990 OLTP research study authored: Aberdeen's 1990 research study: Transaction Processing into the 1990s: The Hardware Suppliers
- **1991** — 1991 microprocessor multiprocessing study: 1991 study: Evaluating Options in Microprocessor Multiprocessing
- **1992** — 1992 clustering technology study: 1992 study on clustering technology
- **1999** — Total Aberdeen publications: Over 60 publications authored during Aberdeen tenure; contributed research to and edited several hundred
- **1999** — TPC benchmark role: Auditor of TPC benchmarks; now industry advisor to Transaction Processing Council; frequently quoted in trade press
- **1999** — Conference speaking clients: Spoke on behalf of Apple Computer, AT&T, Computer Associates/CA Ingres, CEO Institute, Digital Equipment, Hewlett-Packard, IBM, Informix, Intel, NCR, Oracle, Sequent, Software AG, Stratus Computer, Tandem Computers
- **1999** — OLTP expertise depth: One of computer industry's leading analysts of OLTP, decision support, client/server, distributed development, e-commerce, commercial benchmark auditing
- **1992** — Litigation case: Computer Consoles vs GTE: Plaintiff support (Blanc Gilburne); RISC-Unix technical documentation; proposed expert witness; settled substantial damages for plaintiff
- **1994** — Litigation case: GTE Products vs American Software: Defense support (Fulbright & Jaworski); AS/400 and 3090 mainframe expertise; breach of contract
- **1989** — Litigation case: Unisys vs Florida HRS: Plaintiff expert witness (Carlton Fields for Unisys); distributed IBM mainframe systems; $110M EDS contract later cancelled
- **1999** — Aberdeen Group research staff size: Approximately forty analysts under Kastner's leadership
- **1967** — Career start year: Career began 1967 at Cornell University (Langmuir Computer Laboratories); independent consulting 1967-1969
