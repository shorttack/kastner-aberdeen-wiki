# Restructure plans weigh on Gateway

> CBS.MarketWatch.com news (2003-01-06) on Gateway's share-price reaction after CEO Ted Waitt said the financially troubled PC maker would reshuffle management and unveil a new business strategy following weak holiday sales. Stock closed at $3.30, down 4.6% on 2.3M volume, after Waitt's WSJ interview. Peter Kastner, chief research officer with the Aberdeen Group, assesses the situation: 'It looks to us as if Gateway's whole business model is on the boardroom table.' The article documents Gateway's Q3 2002 loss ($50M, 15c/share), 21% revenue decline (from $1.41B to $1.12B), lowered full-year guidance ($4.3-4.5B vs $4.5-5B), IDC's #3 US PC shipments ranking, 272 retail stores under expiring leases, and the November 2002 pivot into consumer electronics (100+ new digital products including a $2,999 42-inch plasma TV).

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — Gateway business model under review: It looks to us as if Gateway's whole business model is on the boardroom table.
- **2002** — Gateway Q3 2002 loss: $50 million loss, 15c per share, matching Wall Street consensus.
- **2002** — Gateway Q3 2002 revenue: $1.12 billion, down 21% from $1.41 billion in Q3 2001.
- **2002** — Gateway full-year guidance cut: Full-year revenue estimate lowered to $4.3-$4.5B from $4.5-$5B.
- **2002** — Gateway Q3 2002 PC shipments: 729,000 units, down from 817,000 in Q3 2001; IDC ranked Gateway #3 in US PC shipments.
- **2002** — Gateway retail footprint: 272 Gateway Country Stores; leases on several expiring in 2003.
- **2002** — Gateway CE expansion: November 2002: Gateway began carrying more than 100 new digital products in retail stores, including a 42-inch plasma TV at $2,999 — about half the price of similar models.
- **2002** — PC market assessment: The market for PCs is brutal; the current market is difficult.
- **2007** — Gateway acquired by Acer: Gateway acquired by Acer in October 2007 for approximately $710 million (~$1.90/share), ending independent operations; retail stores had already closed in April 2004.
- **2004** — Gateway Country Stores close: April 2004: Gateway closed all 188 remaining Gateway Country Stores, laid off 2,500, and became a retail-distribution brand ahead of the eMachines merger.
