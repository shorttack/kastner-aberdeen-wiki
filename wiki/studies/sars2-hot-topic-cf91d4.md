# SARS May Impact Global Electronics Industry

> Aberdeen hot topic analyzing the potential impact of the 2003 SARS epidemic on the global electronics industry, focusing on semiconductor manufacturing in Asia-Pacific (PRC, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore), supply chain disruption risks, just-in-time logistics vulnerabilities, and investment implications for the trillion-dollar electronics sector.

**Author:** Russ Craig and Peter Kastner · **Date:** 2003-04 · **Type:** topic-analysis
**Importance:** medium — *Timely and important risk analysis for electronics supply chain; first major analysis of pandemic supply chain risk in the industry.*
**Prescience:** high — *Remarkably prescient: identified China single-source supply chain risk, just-in-time fragility, and government transparency issues 17 years before COVID-19 revealed identical vulnerabilities at vastly greater scale.*
