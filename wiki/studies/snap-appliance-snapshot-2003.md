# AberdeenGroup SnapShot: Snap Appliance Inc.

> This Aberdeen Group SnapShot profiles Snap Appliance Inc., the self-described number-one volume NAS provider, which was reborn as a private company in 2002 after being spun out of Quantum. The profile details Snap's product line spanning workgroup to enterprise NAS using inexpensive ATA disks, and explicitly maps Snap's coverage of Aberdeen's midline storage categories including staging, fixed content, consolidation, replication, and PC backup. The document positions Snap as a key validation of Aberdeen's midline disk storage thesis.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner · **Date:** 2003-10-15 · **Type:** case-analysis
**Importance:** high — *An important contemporaneous vendor profile validating Aberdeen's midline/ATA storage thesis; one of the earliest published profiles of a dedicated NAS appliance vendor aligned to the emerging tiered storage taxonomy. Kastner's connection to the midline thesis makes this a primary source document.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Correctly identified NAS + ATA as a winning combination for the midline tier — the NAS category thrived enormously. However Snap Appliance itself was acquired by Overland Storage (2008) and absorbed into Sphere 3D (2014); the company did not survive independently despite a strong product thesis.*

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — Snap Appliance market rank — NAS volume: Number one, volume leading provider of NAS solutions from workgroup to enterprise
- **2003** — Snap Appliance installed base: Over 130,000 NAS units installed as of October 2003
- **2003** — Snap Server product origin: First Snap Servers launched by Meridian Data in 1998; Quantum acquired company in 2000
- **2002** — Snap Appliance re-privatization: Spun out of Quantum in 2002 as private company; original IPO plans shelved
- **2003** — Snap Appliance business viability: Private company with strong product line and 130k+ installed base; positioned for growth in NAS market
- **2008** — Snap Appliance corporate fate — actual outcome: Acquired by Overland Storage in 2008; Overland later merged with Sphere 3D in 2014. Company did not survive as independent entity.
- **2003** — Snap Appliance geographic reach: Sells through manufacturers, distributors, and resellers in North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific
- **2003** — ATA disk cost advantage for NAS: Snap storage appliances use inexpensive ATA disks in variety of redundancy configurations — key cost advantage
- **2003** — Aberdeen midline categories covered by Snap: Snap creatively covered all mid-line storage categories envisaged by Aberdeen
- **2003** — Midline use cases served by Snap: (1) SCSI-to-tape staging, (2) fixed content long-term storage, (3) light/moderate applications with storage consolidation, (4) data replication, (5) PC client backup
- **2003** — IT professional demand drivers for NAS: IT professionals increasingly vocal about urgent need: keep up with storage growth, low-cost alternatives, simple products
- **2003** — Primary target market for Snap Appliance: SMB companies and enterprises with minimal or no IT staffing — heterogeneous environments
- **2003** — Snap Server 1100 market position: World's best selling NAS server as of 2003; capable and portable file server
- **2003** — Snap Server 2200 capability: Snap Server 2200: larger storage capacity with RAID 1 data protection
- **2003** — Snap Server 4100 RAID capability: Snap Server 4100: entry-level RAID 5 in slim rack-mountable chassis
- **2003** — Snap Server 4200/4500 disk configuration: Built around 4 ATA-100 disks; 320 GB to 1 TB total; dual gigabit-Ethernet; 1U rack; expandable to 3 TB with Snap Disk 10 arrays
- **2003** — SnapServer 14000 enterprise specs: 1.44 TB to 3.0 TB across 12 disks in 3U rack; ADS and NIS security protocol support for Unix/Windows datacenters
- **2003** — GuardianOS features: Linux-based OS for departmental and enterprise Snap Servers; snapshots and Windows ADS support; same ease of use as workgroup SnapOS
- **2003** — DataKeeper backup software bundling: Unlimited user license of DataKeeper desktop backup software for Windows clients included with every Snap Server
- **2003** — NAS as winning midline + ATA combination: Snap's NAS + ATA product line validated as winning combination for midline storage tier
- **2010** — NAS category growth — actual outcome: NAS became ubiquitous in enterprise storage by 2008-2012 exactly as predicted. Every major vendor built out NAS product lines.
- **2003** — Snap Appliance founding year: Founded 1994 per company facts sidebar
- **2003** — Aberdeen research cited in profile: Aberdeen research cited: Mid-Line Disk Storage Emerging As Significant Cost-Saving Opportunity (October 2003)
- **2003** — Snap Appliance software partnership breadth: Software partners: APC, Computer Associates, Legato, PowerQuest, SyncSort, Veritas, Sun Microsystems
- **2003** — Midline storage as cost-saving opportunity: Aberdeen framework positions midline ATA/NAS as significant cost-saving opportunity vs FC/SCSI for non-OLTP workloads
