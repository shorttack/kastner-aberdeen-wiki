# Aberdeen Vendor SnapShot: Snap Appliance Inc.

> Aberdeen vendor snapshot profile of Snap Appliance Inc. (formerly Meridian Data, then Quantum subsidiary), the number-one volume NAS provider with 130,000+ unit installed base. Covers product line from workgroup (Snap Server 1100/2200) through departmental (4100/4200/4500) and enterprise (SnapServer 14000) tiers, software partnerships, and ATA-based midline storage positioning.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner · **Date:** 2003 · **Type:** case-analysis
**Importance:** medium — *Documents a significant NAS market participant at a pivotal time before the company was ultimately absorbed; useful for understanding SMB NAS market ~2002-2003.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Snap Appliance correctly positioned ATA-based NAS as cost-effective midline storage tier; this positioning proved broadly correct as SATA NAS became dominant in SMB/enterprise.*
