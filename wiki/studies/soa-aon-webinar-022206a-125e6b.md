# Enterprise Integration Technology: Aberdeen Group's Market Trends & Research for 2006

> Slide-format webinar presentation by Aberdeen Group covering SOA adoption findings from 2005 research and the 2006 enterprise integration research agenda. Key findings: 92% of companies at or below 25% SOA adoption rate; over half predict 50%+ SOA-based software in 5 years; top SOA motives include managing IT integration costs (50%), business alignment (44%), and implementation speed (43%). Best-in-class KPIs show 29.6% innovation spend vs 18.5% average, and 12.4% maintenance costs vs 27.3% average. Outlines Q1-Q4 2006 research roadmap covering applications on the network, SOA development, edge computing, and mid-market strategies.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner · **Date:** 2006-02-22 · **Type:** webinar-presentation
**Importance:** medium — *Provides a snapshot of SOA adoption maturity at a critical inflection point; useful as a benchmark of enterprise middleware strategy during SOA's peak hype cycle.*
**Prescience:** high — *Correctly predicted majority SOA adoption within 5 years (by 2011 most enterprises had substantial SOA/web services); accurately forecast that SOA would shift IT budgets toward innovation; the evolution from SOA to microservices/cloud-native validated the core architectural thesis.*
