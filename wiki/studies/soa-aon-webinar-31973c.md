# Enterprise Integration Technology: Aberdeen Group's Market Trends & Research for 2006

> 24-slide Aberdeen Group webinar presentation deck (SOA_AON_webinar_022206a). Covers enterprise integration market challenges/opportunities, 2005 SOA benchmark findings, and Aberdeen's full 2006 Enterprise Integration Research Agenda (Q1-Q4). Key 2005 findings: 92% of companies at or below 25% SOA adoption; over half predict 50%+ SOA-based software in 5 years. Best-in-Class SOA adopters spend 29.6% of IT budget on innovation vs. 18.5% average; software maintenance costs 12.4% vs. 27.3% average. Research agenda spans NAP, SOA app development, edge computing, data center integration, real-time BI, and knowledge worker productivity.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2005** — SOA adoption rate - current: 92% at or below 25% SOA adoption rate in 2005
- **2005** — SOA adoption prediction - 5 year: Over half of respondents predict 50%+ of software will be SOA-based in five years
- **2005** — Best-in-Class IT innovation spend: 29.6% of IT budget on innovation (vs. 18.5% average); +11pp advantage
- **2005** — Best-in-Class software maintenance cost: 12.4% of IT budget on software maintenance (vs. 27.3% average); -15pp advantage
- **2005** — Top SOA adoption motive: Development of new business capabilities or products: 50% of respondents
- **2005** — SOA motive - complexity management: Management of IT complexity: 44% of respondents
- **2005** — SOA motive - web services reuse: Re-usage of applications via Web Services: 43% of respondents
- **2005** — SOA motive - business alignment: Alignment with the business: 37% of respondents
- **2005** — IT KPI tracked most frequently: IT cost of delivering/building new applications: 64% of respondents tracking
- **2005** — IT budget as % of revenues tracking: 54% of respondents tracking IT budget as percent of revenues
- **2005** — IT organizational change for SOA: Changes within IT organization required to mirror service-delivery aspects of SOA
- **2006** — Q1 2006 research agenda - NAP: Network traffic shifting from binary data packets to XML-based content routing and web services messaging due to SOA
- **2006** — Network-software architecture convergence: Lines between network and software architecture blurring due to SOA and XML traffic
- **2006** — Q2 2006 research agenda - SOA apps: Changing technology landscape as SOA development integrates with legacy applications and development tools
- **2006** — Q3 2006 research agenda - edge computing: RFID and POS devices driving volume and complexity at SOA edge; edge vs. core computing tradeoffs
- **2006** — Q3 2006 research agenda - RTBI: Integrating Real-Time Technology and Business Management Benchmark
- **2006** — Q2 2006 research agenda - legacy integration: Q2 2006: Integrating the Data Center: New Architectures and Opportunities
- **2006** — Q4 2006 research agenda - mid-market: Q4 2006: Enterprise Integration Strategies and Best Practices for the Mid-Market
- **2006** — Aberdeen research scale: 137,000+ research members; 100+ primary research studies in past 12 months; 500+ derivative reports; 17,100 business/technology executives benchmarked
- **2006** — Aberdeen G1000 penetration: 900 of G1000 companies in Aberdeen's research community; 465 of Software 500 are Aberdeen clients
- **2005** — SOA motive - speed of implementation: Speed of IT implementations: 27% of respondents
- **2005** — SOA motive - integration costs: Managing IT integration costs: 24% of respondents
- **2005** — SOA market state characterization: Companies Are Bullish on SOA Adoption, But the Market is Immature
- **2006** — Enterprise integration technology scope: SOA, EAI, ETL, B2BI, EII, XML, CORBA named as core enterprise integration technologies
- **2006** — IT efficiency driver: SOA enables IT to be more productive under ongoing budget pressures
