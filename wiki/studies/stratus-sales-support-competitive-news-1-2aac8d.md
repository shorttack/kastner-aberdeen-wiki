# Stratus Sales Support Competitive News, Vol 6 No 3, May 1987 — Tandem Competitive Update (CLX/LXN, VLX, EXT/TXP price cuts)

> Stratus Sales Support Competitive News, Volume 6 Number 3, May 1987. Editor Peter Kastner (Manager, Sales Support Programs, Marlboro x2192) publishes a detailed competitive analysis of Tandem's April 20 and May 4, 1987 announcements: the new low-end CLX and LXN 'network extender' products, smaller VLX configurations (2- and 3-CPU models at $585K entry), and price cuts across the EXT/TXP line. Kastner also notes Tandem's quiet restatement of ET-1 performance numbers under NonStop SQL (2, 4.5, 6.5 tps/CPU for NS-II/EXT10, TXP/EXT25, VLX) with relaxed 2-second 90th-percentile response time. Contributor Raphael Frommer authored the detailed CLX/LXN technical analysis. The newsletter quotes Omri Serlin (Fault Tolerant Systems Newsletter No. 55, March 16 1987). Signature Kastner quote: 'Business has only two basic functions — marketing and innovation.'

## Key observations (top 25)

- **1987** — Kastner editorial summary: Tandem has added new low-end and high-end products, cut prices, and cut performance to go with a new SQL database product
- **1987** — CLX model 620 announcement: 2-CPU CLX 620: 5 NonStop SQL ET-1 tps; $85,000 unit (4Q87); $59,500 in qty 25-39
- **1987** — CLX 640 announcement: 4-CPU CLX 640: 10 tps; $161,000 unit; $112,700 in qty 25-39 (1Q88)
- **1987** — CLX 660 announcement: 6-CPU CLX 660: 15 tps; $240,000 unit; $168,000 in qty 25-39 (2Q88)
- **1987** — CLX 610 entry model: Uniprocessor non-fault-tolerant CLX 610: 2.5 tps; $57,000 unit; $39,900 in qty 25-39
- **1987** — LXN pricing: $23,700 unit (2MB, 80MB disk, 10-port comm); $18,012 in qty 25 under Networking Discount Plan
- **1987** — Smaller VLX configurations: New 2- and 3-CPU VLX models; minimum VLX now $585K (down from ~$1M 4-CPU)
- **1987** — Restated ET-1 under NonStop SQL: NonStop SQL-based transaction-protected ratings are 2, 4.5, 6.5 tps/CPU for NS-II/EXT10, TXP/EXT25, VLX with 2-second 90%-ile response time (relaxed from sub-second)
- **1987** — Serlin on relaxed response-time criterion: Official reason given is that this permits comparison across entire product line including low end
- **1987** — Kastner LXN skepticism: Time will tell how well Tandem can market this customized UNIX product against other less costly UNIX offerings
- **1987** — Stratus prospects should understand: Tandem's trend toward proven comparator-based self-checking design is NOT a departure from their software approach to hardware fault tolerance; checkpointing still critical
- **1987** — CLX per-CPU software cost: $8,000 per CPU for GUARDIAN XL (GUARDIAN 90, ENCOMPASS, NonStop SQL, TRANSFER, PS MAIL, EXPAND)
- **1987** — Kastner on Stratus ORACLE comparison challenges: Stratus ET-1 measurements to date have been based on file system, not ORACLE SQL environment — apples-to-apples comparison complicated
- **1987** — Kastner signature slogan: Business has only two basic functions — marketing and innovation
- **1987** — LXN OEM sourcing: LXN is an Altos 3068-based product resold by Tandem — 'unprecedented reselling of another company's product'
- **1987** — Kastner outlook: In spite of Tandem's numerous announcements, Stratus maintains a strong competitive position
