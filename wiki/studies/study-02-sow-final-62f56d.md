# Statement of Work: Aberdeen-Maxtor MaXLine Marketing Program

> Formal Statement of Work governing the Aberdeen Group-Maxtor Corporation consulting engagement for marketing the MaXLine ATA disk drive product line. The SOW defines a two-task program: Task 1 covering market research and segment definition (75 enterprise interviews plus survey analysis for $25K) and Task 2 covering market education via an Executive White Paper and webcast ($35K). The document establishes IP licensing terms giving Maxtor exclusive perpetual worldwide rights to the white paper and delivery of 1000 copies plus web distribution.


_Published 2003, author **Aberdeen Group / Peter Kastner**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- MaXLine products quietly announced September 9 2002; market development campaign initiated thereafter
- Fundamental program element is to define a new market segment in the storage hierarchy between disk and tape — 'active archiving' or 'rapid/instant archiving'
- Maxtor wants to avoid cannibalizing its existing higher-margin SCSI disk drive product families when offering lower-priced ATA drives
- Maxtor wants to blunt efforts of competitors with different technology portfolios and competing market development agendas
- Minimum 75 interviews across wide variety of vertical markets and enterprise sizes including face-to-face interviews
- Survey results and analysis report plus outline for Aberdeen white paper — both due May 2 2003
- Aberdeen grants Maxtor exclusive unrestricted royalty-free worldwide perpetual license to copy publish distribute the Executive White Paper
- Maxtor has 60-day period after delivery to review white paper and consider waiving exclusivity — allowing Aberdeen to publish more broadly
- For 6 months after posting Aberdeen will provide download demographics to Maxtor for all who download white paper after registration
- Aberdeen hosts webcast advertised 'sponsored by Maxtor' reviewing storage market issues from white paper with Maxtor executives on panel
- Aberdeen provides minimum 4 briefings for Maxtor executives post-publication reviewing near-line market category development and trends
- $25000 for market research and segment definition task
- $35000 for market education (white paper + webcast + briefings)
- $6000 estimated travel budget for face-to-face interviews and California workshop
- $3000 estimated for webcast for 100 participants
- Mid-March 2003 expected project start; governed by Services Agreement effective March 17 2003
- Aberdeen described as having 'well-developed storage model illustrated below' including MaXLine in emerging cost-effective storage class
- Aberdeen will review survey instrument with Maxtor before fieldwork begins
- Executive White Paper will be 10-20 pages; intended audience is IT executives and enterprise executives
- $60000 plus expenses for Tasks 1 and 2 combined (initial engagement)
- Peter Kastner Executive VP will be the executive leading the overall project
- Optional services include standards groups outreach trade show presentations direct customer sales calls software partnership advisory and eLearning curriculum development
