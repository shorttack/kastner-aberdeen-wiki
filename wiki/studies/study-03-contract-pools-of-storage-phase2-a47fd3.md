# Pools of Storage Executive Evangelism Program — Phase 2 Contract

> Revised proposal and contract for Aberdeen Group's Phase 2 engagement with Maxtor Corporation covering the 'Pools of Storage Executive Evangelism Program' — a four-phase program at $114000 total. The program defines a four-tier storage lexicon (Online/Midline/Near-line/Offline) and proposes a strategy workshop in Shrewsbury MA (December 9 2003) followed by decision-tool development message testing with 100-250 internet surveys and a press tour in the US and Europe. The document introduces Serial Storage in a Box as a packaging concept and positions Maxtor as the ecosystem orchestrator for a new storage lexicon.


_Published 2003, author **Aberdeen Group / Peter Kastner**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- On-line (Transactional Processing) / Mid-line (Fixed Content) / Near-Line (Disk Backup & Staging & Spooling) / Off-line (Tape Archive)
- If ILM is 'getting control over storage' then Pools of Storage is the economic and flexibility 'best approach'
- All players in the Serial Storage ecosystem will adopt Maxtor's 'Pools' lexicon as imperative to successfully launching midline storage within a year
- [UNVERIFIED]
- Maxtor and Aberdeen have mutually identified launching new midline storage category within a year (by end 2004) as imperative
- [UNVERIFIED]
- Maxtor must prevent misperception that Pools of Storage means hierarchical storage management (HSM) — it is a set of systems adapting to business objectives
- Enterprise customers must feel the TCO pain of storing fixed content on highly priced drives designed for speed instead of capacity
- Endorsement for Pools of Storage required from industry publications in US and Europe and from at least four Fortune 500 company CIOs
- [UNVERIFIED]
- A SAS/SATA backplane allowing plug play and population of any mix of SAS and SATA drives in detached storage arrays — next-generation enterprise storage packaging
- Value-based decision tools published to public web sites to validate efficiencies in capital and labor from Pools of Storage program
- $114000 total project cost across four phases
- $20000 payment on facilitation of Phase 1 Strategy Workshop
- $10000 payment on delivery of Phase 2 approval copies
- $20000 payment on delivery of Phase 2 final copies — January 9 2004
- $24000 total for message testing phases (survey instrument $8K + report $8K + white paper $8K)
- Press tour of east-coast west-coast and two cities in England and continental Europe to evangelize Pools of Storage white paper coordinated by Aberdeen PR counsel
- Aberdeen to prepare detailed plan schedule and budget for sustained program throughout 2004 to build market awareness around Pools of Storage
- In synchronization with external program Maxtor aligns specific HDD brands with Pools of Storage — empowering Maxtor to have significant voice in next-generation enterprise storage
- Presentations to user storage groups including SNIA as part of evangelism phase
- Aberdeen retains sole copyright ownership of Phase 3 white paper; it will be publicly displayed with Aberdeen logo and note of Maxtor's sponsorship
- Plan to create web site Center of Excellence on Pools of Storage where users can learn model savings and learn best practices
- 100-250 internet surveys plus 5-8 face-to-face or telephone interviews testing attractiveness and resonance of proposed Pools of Storage messages
- $3000 for Phases 1-3 travel; $5500 for Phase 4 press tour travel
