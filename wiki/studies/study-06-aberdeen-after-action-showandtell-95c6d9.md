> Internal Aberdeen Group presentation reviewing the completed Maxtor Pools of Storage engagement. The 11-slide deck covers project background ($128K fixed price / 60-day schedule that took 150 days starting December 2003) client issues Aberdeen selection rationale client satisfaction challenges and lessons learned. Key findings: the project funded $75K in Aberdeen tool R&D; produced the first Flash-based research-delivery simulation tool; developed 50 maturity grids; and attracted Macromedia EVP Al Ramadan's interest.


_Published 2004, author **John Boyne, Peter Kastner (Aberdeen Group)**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Maxtor makes disk drives — a commodity; needs to move up the storage value chain; influence end-user buyers instead of purchasing agents at computer companies `[ps=4]`
- Must factor in client organization's ability to absorb and efficiently use our research `[ps=4]`
- Getting decision maker buy-in and sales close does not mean the organization has bought in `[ps=4]`
- Funded development of Pools of Storage Flash-based web browser simulation — Aberdeen's first research-delivery-by-simulation next-generation tool `[ps=3]`
- It's not hours charged but gaining and maintaining client satisfaction while getting deliverable done; 'Deliverable % completion' metrics needed `[ps=3]`
- Traditional 16-page white papers that ramble are a huge waste of effort `[ps=3]`
- Tool development is like programming; requires complete specs and user sign-off `[ps=2]`
- Client said 'Aberdeen created Unix-RDBMS and CRM markets' and selected us; sought Aberdeen principals not the analyst `[ps=1]`
- $128000 fixed price `[ps=0]`
- 60 days `[ps=0]`
- 150 days (2.5x planned) `[ps=0]`
- December 1 2003 `[ps=0]`
- Sales: Kastner and Tubbs `[ps=0]`
- Team: Beganny Boyne Conrad Conant Creese Elbaum Erickson Hill C. Jones Kastner Kernochan Lane McLaughlin Ryan Veystel `[ps=0]`
- First project proposal November 2002; started spring 2003 ($60K): research and white paper `[ps=0]`
- This project took three months to scope propose get buy-in and budget `[ps=0]`
- Could Aberdeen influence internal storage engineers who love IDC's storage analyst? Could we simplify positioning and messages? Maxtor sales reps not solutions-oriented; Maxtor enterprise marketing not Aberdeen's friend; senior executives skeptical about cost `[ps=0]`
- Market influence without pandering measured by pick-up on Aberdeen's terms and thought leadership `[ps=0]`
- In spite of specs and pilot one tool failed to impress; by re-doing the disliked tool Aberdeen showed commitment `[ps=0]`
- Positioning workshop did not work on the spot but definitely influenced the outcome our way `[ps=0]`
- Difficult to pin client down due to changing political climate absorption rate and quality time `[ps=0]`
- $75000 in tool R&D funded by Maxtor engagement helped TTCM site come up in weeks on budget `[ps=0]`
- Funded development of 50 maturity grids and training/process for doing same — received applause `[ps=0]`
- Allows Aberdeen to sell into top-tier storage and computer companies with actionable compelling research and a simple yet sexy tool `[ps=0]`
- Macromedia EVP Al Ramadan saw PoS tool demo and immediately asked if Aberdeen would work with them on a case study on Aberdeen's use of Macromedia development tools — to be turned into a sales opportunity `[ps=0]`
