# Maxtor Pools of Storage — After-Action Review (Show and Tell)

> Internal Aberdeen Group presentation reviewing the completed Maxtor Pools of Storage engagement. The 11-slide deck covers project background ($128K fixed price / 60-day schedule that took 150 days starting December 2003) client issues Aberdeen selection rationale client satisfaction challenges and lessons learned. Key findings: the project funded $75K in Aberdeen tool R&D; produced the first Flash-based research-delivery simulation tool; developed 50 maturity grids; and attracted Macromedia EVP Al Ramadan's interest.


_Published 2004, author **John Boyne, Peter Kastner (Aberdeen Group)**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- $128000 fixed price
- 60 days
- 150 days (2.5x planned)
- December 1 2003
- Sales: Kastner and Tubbs
- Team: Beganny Boyne Conrad Conant Creese Elbaum Erickson Hill C. Jones Kastner Kernochan Lane McLaughlin Ryan Veystel
- Maxtor makes disk drives — a commodity; needs to move up the storage value chain; influence end-user buyers instead of purchasing agents at computer companies
- Client said 'Aberdeen created Unix-RDBMS and CRM markets' and selected us; sought Aberdeen principals not the analyst
- First project proposal November 2002; started spring 2003 ($60K): research and white paper
- This project took three months to scope propose get buy-in and budget
- Could Aberdeen influence internal storage engineers who love IDC's storage analyst? Could we simplify positioning and messages? Maxtor sales reps not solutions-oriented; Maxtor enterprise marketing not Aberdeen's friend; senior executives skeptical about cost
- Market influence without pandering measured by pick-up on Aberdeen's terms and thought leadership
- In spite of specs and pilot one tool failed to impress; by re-doing the disliked tool Aberdeen showed commitment
- Positioning workshop did not work on the spot but definitely influenced the outcome our way
- Difficult to pin client down due to changing political climate absorption rate and quality time
- $75000 in tool R&D funded by Maxtor engagement helped TTCM site come up in weeks on budget
- Funded development of Pools of Storage Flash-based web browser simulation — Aberdeen's first research-delivery-by-simulation next-generation tool
- Funded development of 50 maturity grids and training/process for doing same — received applause
- Allows Aberdeen to sell into top-tier storage and computer companies with actionable compelling research and a simple yet sexy tool
- Macromedia EVP Al Ramadan saw PoS tool demo and immediately asked if Aberdeen would work with them on a case study on Aberdeen's use of Macromedia development tools — to be turned into a sales opportunity
- Tool development is like programming; requires complete specs and user sign-off
- Schedules are advisory not deadlines for tool development projects
- Hard sales press for end-Q1 extension was a client turn-off
- Must factor in client organization's ability to absorb and efficiently use our research
- It's not hours charged but gaining and maintaining client satisfaction while getting deliverable done; 'Deliverable % completion' metrics needed
