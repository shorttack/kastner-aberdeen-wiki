# Aberdeen-Maxtor Phase 2: Pools of Storage Market Development

> Project kickoff document for Aberdeen Group's Phase 2 engagement with Maxtor Corporation to develop the 'Pools of Storage' concept and launch the midline storage category. Defines scope covering messaging workshop, message testing, and evangelization roadmap. Outlines Aberdeen's interdisciplinary team and a 10-week project timeline beginning November 24, 2003.


_Published 2003, author **Peter Kastner, Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Gain internal consensus on Pools of Storage messaging; establish strategic foundations; provide tools to validate new efficiencies
- If ILM is 'getting control over storage' then Pools of Storage is the economic and flexibility 'best approach'
- Online (Transactional Processing); Midline (Fixed Content); Nearline (Disk Backup/Staging/Spooling); Offline (Tape Archive)
- All players in the Serial Storage ecosystem will adopt Maxtor's 'Pools' lexicon
- [UNVERIFIED]
- Launch new storage category of midline storage within a year (by end 2004)
- Enterprise customers must not perceive Pools of Storage as hierarchical storage management
- Endorsement required from industry publications in US and Europe and from at least four Fortune 500 company CIOs
- One-day Strategy Consulting Workshop (Dec 17 Milpitas); Pools of Storage Strategy Report; Tool Implementation Workshop Plan
- Pools of Storage Decision Tool; Competitive Value Prospect Tool; Tool Implementation WebEx; Tool Demo Guides
- 100-250 internet surveys and 5-8 face-to-face/telephone interviews; messaging resonance analysis; buying behavior questions
- East/West coast and two European city press tour to evangelize Pools of Storage white paper
- November 24 2003
- December 17 2003 in Milpitas CA
- February 2004
- Approximately 10 person-weeks
- Aberdeen cannot replace named key staff without prior written Maxtor consent
- Executive VP Aberdeen Group; oversees Aberdeen-Maxtor relationship; contributor to Tasks 1-4
- VP Strategic Solutions; executive responsible for Task 2 Tools Development
- Research Director; primary contributor to ILM/Pools strategy workshop and messaging interviews
- Plan to create web site as one-stop-shop for Pools of Storage subject for industry
- Maxtor will have significant voice in next generation of enterprise storage via Serial Storage in a Box
- [UNVERIFIED]
- Market science requires test messages before launch to avoid 'ready fire aim' situation
- Presentations to user storage groups including SNIA planned
