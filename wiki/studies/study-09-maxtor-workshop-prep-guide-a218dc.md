# Midline Messaging Workshop Prep Guide

> Pre-workshop preparation guide distributed to Maxtor executives and core team before the February 13 2004 midline category creation workshop in Shrewsbury MA. Provides session goals and pre-read questions for three workshop sessions covering category definition, go-to-market strategy, and messaging finalization. Includes appendices defining Pools of Storage concepts and messaging foundations.


_Published 2004, author **Peter Kastner, David Hill, John Boyne**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- High capacity disk storage that has lower price per GB as well as relatively lower performance than higher performance disk storage; suitable for low I/O duty applications `[ps=5]`
- Manages 'content' through different stages of its life using policy-driven software automation; assigns data to least expensive storage device without compromising service `[ps=5]`
- Hitachi Global Storage focuses on 'right product at right time'; product and technology focus rather than business solutions `[ps=4]`
- A pool is a homogeneous group of storage hardware that meets service level requirements for access to data with a certain level of capital and management intensity; target of ILM process `[ps=3]`
- WD does not have extensive experience selling to 'business'; sells products not solutions `[ps=3]`
- Take market share from competitors by being externally focused; establish basis for sustainable competitive advantage beyond price `[ps=3]`
- Literal messaging: functions and features; Experiential messaging: emotional responses and problem solutions `[ps=3]`
- Seagate wants to protect its dominance in FC disk market; will be reluctant to compete aggressively in midline market `[ps=1]`
