# In Si ht g

> This study analyzes In Si ht g using Aberdeen Group research material and prepared extraction artifacts.


_Published 2001, author **Peter Kastner**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- The combined HP/Compaq will have one-third of its $87 billion in revenues in what HP calls “access devices,” which include desktop PCs, laptops and notebooks, and personal digital assistants (PDAs) such as iPaq and Jornada. `[ps=4]`
- We estimate Compaq and HP lost over $300 million on PCs in calendar Q2 this year alone — the kind of red-ink bath that gets serious boardroom attention — even while losing market share. `[ps=4]`
- A $550 iPaq PDA costs less to make than a $550 low-end PC, so it’s a more profitable sale. `[ps=1]`
- Such access devices represent $29 billion of today’s combined revenues. `[ps=0]`
