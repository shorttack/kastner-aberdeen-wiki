# Maxtor Messaging Workshop: Creating the Midline Market Category

> Full slide deck from the February 13 2004 Maxtor midline category creation workshop at Shrewsbury MA. Covers Aberdeen's ILM and Pools of Storage framework, competitive analysis of Seagate/WD/Hitachi, the 10-step category creation methodology, a detailed market awareness-building roadmap with quarterly milestones, and competitive positioning analysis of MaXLine vs Atlas disk drives.


_Published 2004, author **Aberdeen Group (Peter Kastner, David Hill, John Boyne)**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Kastner (VP Tech); Hill (VP Storage Research); Bunting (VP Sales); Elbaum (VP Strategic Solutions); Boyne (Director Client Engagements)
- Aberdeen founder 1988; Chief Research Officer; prior 10 years at DEC Stratus and Prime; 10 years systems integration
- 35+ years IT experience; ran Data General's IT corporate data center; ran EMC market research organization
- 15 years IT market research; deep knowledge of IT buying factors; value-chain approach to business value; experience in category creation
- Phase 1 completed Jul-03: survey storage buyers; white paper on emerging midline opportunity; sales force training materials
- Jan 2004 trial messaging survey; Feb 13 workshop; Feb 2004 call-to-action white paper; Feb 2004 business case tools; Feb-Mar 2004 evangelization road show
- ILM will come to be the big storage-related mindshare and market battle of the decade
- [UNVERIFIED]
- ILM is a next-decade initiative; no single vendor can deliver on ILM vision anytime soon
- Unlike ILM software, Pools of Storage can be implemented now; has value proposition beyond TCO/ROI
- Only 15% have done ILM program now; 41% will consider in future; 33% have not considered; 11% don't know
- 36% familiar; 50% somewhat familiar; only 15% little or not familiar
- Only 19% very likely to implement in 2004; 43% somewhat likely; 38% unlikely
- SCSI drives increasing capacity to near midline; PC ATA drives in RAID offering comparable availability at lower cost
- Seagate will deny existence of midline because midline would threaten its hegemony in FC disk
- Hitachi has IBM heritage bringing technical respect but too many diverse interests to focus on midline
- WD should be positioned as midline competitor; already refers to Raptor as Enterprise Serial ATA; press doesn't need to understand subtleties
- Midline storage includes enterprise-class high-capacity ATA disks that bridge the gap between high-use high-performance FC/SCSI disk arrays and magnetic tape storage
- 1.Define 2.Differentiate 3.Debunk 4.Define argument/position/messages 5.Deliver to influencers 6.Create awareness 7.Evangelize 8.Measure 9.Let influencers take over 10.Nurture
- A category is a distinct market segment which is watched and measured independently of other segments by market influencers
- Must minimize Maxtor and Aberdeen visibility; avoid any opportunity for critics to say this is all a MaXLine product play
- IDC or Gartner start reporting midline metrics by Q3 2004
- [UNVERIFIED]
- Goal is 200 press mentions by Q3 2004
- 50% of IT practitioners recognize midline category by Q4 2004
