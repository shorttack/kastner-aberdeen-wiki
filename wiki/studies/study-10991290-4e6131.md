## Top observations

- ## **Nearly 80% of a product’s costs are embedded by the time that product is tossed over-the-wall to manufacturing.** `[ps=5]`
- Aberdeen research indicates that nearly 80% of a product’s costs are built-in by the time that product is tossed over-the-wall to manufacturing. `[ps=5]`
- This means that no matter how efficient a discrete manufacturing enterprise is with ERP and SCM initiatives, such transaction-based applications only assist in lowering the remaining 20% of a product’s cost. `[ps=4]`
