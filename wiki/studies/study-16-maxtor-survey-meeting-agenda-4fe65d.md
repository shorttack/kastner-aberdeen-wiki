# Maxtor PoSEE Phase 3 Survey Organizational Meeting Agenda

> An internal Aberdeen Group meeting agenda for the Maxtor Pools of Storage Enterprise Edition (PoSEE) project Phase 3 — Message Testing. The document outlines plans to conduct 100–250 internet surveys and 5–8 interviews to test market receptivity to the Pools of Storage messaging, with intent to merge results with Aberdeen's April 2003 midline storage survey data. Sample survey questions reveal the research instruments aimed at gauging enterprise storage buyers' awareness, pain points, and openness to the midline storage value proposition.


_Published 2003, author **Peter Kastner, David Hill, John Boyne, Beth Conant**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Phase Three: Message Testing
- 100-250 internet surveys plus 5-8 face-to-face or telephone interviews
- April 2003 Aberdeen survey results on midline storage
- Report on market survey intelligence and Aberdeen's recommended approach to the market
- Short white paper as Pools of Storage talking piece for press and evangelization collateral
- Re-align proposed messages with market intelligence from survey
- Confirm internal lead for survey
- Confirm format of survey — internet or phone
- Assemble targets (survey population)
- Plan development and execution timeline
- Discuss extension of survey dates and potential project impacts
- Peter/Brian to identify internal resources to develop/conduct survey
- Is storing data getting easier or more complicated?
- How much additional storage capacity did you purchase in 2003?
- Have you ever purchased the wrong type of storage for your needs?
- What do you believe will be the next big thing in storage management?
- Does your storage reseller provide a complete solution?
