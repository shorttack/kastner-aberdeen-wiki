# Encore Computer Series 91 & 93: Proposed 1991-1993 Market Strategy for Financial and Information Services Industries

> Aberdeen Group advises Encore Computer Corporation on how to market its Series 91 and 93 parallel computing systems to financial services and information services organizations by positioning the architecture as 'Adaptive Computing.' The report sizes the worldwide financial and information services midrange/mainframe market at $11.3 billion (1990) with 19% projected annual growth, recommends a direct-sales rifle-shot strategy targeting niche segments, and provides a detailed competitive analysis against IBM S/390, Tandem, DEC VAXcluster, Teradata, and Sequent. The study makes bold predictions about Encore's potential to displace mainframe incumbents with superior price/performance clustering technology.


_Published 1991, author **Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Adaptive Computing — next-generation midrange/mainframe alternative via RMS clustering
- $11.3 billion (1990)
- 19% per annum
- Reliability
- Availability (24x7 uptime)
- Expandability
- Performance (throughput)
- Investment protection
- Applications availability
- Price
- Rifle-shot niche strategy via Advanced Technology Planning organizations
- SMP + Clustering + Memory-Coupling + Single-System View + High Availability
- 1000-2000 transactions per second
- Two dual-processor Series 91s + 16 MB RMS + 4 GB DASD = ~$250,000
- Adaptive 893: 8x Series 93 + 1 GB RAM + 4-64MB RMS + 12 GB DASD = ~$5 million
- Market leader with strong grip despite high costs and DB2 performance limitations
- ~50% of product revenues from financial sector
- Migrating from proprietary to MIPS Risc/Unix; transition incomplete during planning period
- 20000 VAXcluster installations
- $1M-$1.2M initial + $0.5M upgrades over 18 months
- $100K-$150K primary sales range
- Can displace IBM S/390 in RDBMS applications with superior price/performance
- Failed to penetrate financial services; ceased operations 1999
- Can establish new architectural standard in financial/information services through 1990s
- Sold storage business to Sun Microsystems for $185M in July 1997 to retire JEC debt
