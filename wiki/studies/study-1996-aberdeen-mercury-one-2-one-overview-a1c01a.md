# Aberdeen Group Overview — Mercury One-2-One

> This nine-slide deck is a corporate and capabilities overview for Aberdeen Group that was customized for Mercury One-2-One. It summarizes Aberdeen's charter, research practices, technology-practice taxonomy, solution focus areas, and custom consulting offerings rather than presenting a deep analytic study. As an employer-record for Aberdeen Group, it functions as a capabilities-overview and records Peter S. Kastner in the Vice President role context supplied for this ingest.


_Published 1996, author **Peter S. Kastner (Aberdeen Group)**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Founded in 1988 to provide custom consulting with a realistic business perspective.
- Aberdeen says its emphasis is identifying and advancing the enterprise value of computing technologies.
- Aberdeen states that it provides market and technology dynamics assessment to both technology user and supplier clients.
- Aberdeen contrasts its custom-research orientation with what it calls the one-size-fits-all approach of other market researchers and presents itself as an industry resource quoted by business, industry, and financial media.
- The presentation is explicitly customized for Mercury One-2-One as the client audience.
- Aberdeen organizes its practice taxonomy around Enterprise Topology, Application Development & Acquisition, Networking & Systems Management, Systems Software, and Hardware.
- Aberdeen says customers are focusing planning energy on RDBMS and supply chain management topics.
- Aberdeen lists client-server tools and objects among the solution areas where customers are concentrating planning effort.
- Aberdeen lists ERP and workflow among the solution areas where customers are concentrating planning effort.
- Aberdeen lists OLAP, electronic commerce, and wireless among the solution areas where customers are concentrating planning effort.
- Aberdeen describes its research methods as RAMP, focus groups, surveys, ongoing consultation through Professional Executive Services clients, and regular briefings with cross-industry IT supplier executives.
- Aberdeen says its custom consulting for IT users and business executives includes IT audits and reviews, evaluation of new market opportunities, strategic and tactical IT planning, acquisition and evaluation support, and implementation strategies.
