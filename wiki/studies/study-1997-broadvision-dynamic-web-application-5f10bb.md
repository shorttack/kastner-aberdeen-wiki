# BroadVision Dynamic Web Applications Enhance Employees, Clinch Customers, and Pamper Partners

> Aberdeen Group's Product Viewpoint (Volume 10, Number 11, July 7, 1997) evaluates BroadVision's One-to-One Application System as a high-end dynamic Web application platform for corporate relationship management. Aberdeen defines a three-tier hierarchy of dynamic web servers (low-end CGI, mid-range ODBC, high-end CORBA-based object-oriented) and positions One-to-One as the industry leader in the high-end tier for personalized content delivery across Internet, intranet, and extranet channels. The study profiles One-to-One's capabilities for customer management, employee knowledge management, and partner/supplier extranets, citing customers including Liberty Financial, Banco Santander, USWest, and Kodak Picture Network.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **product-viewpoint**._


## Top observations

- CGI scripts; poor scaling with large users/transactions/databases; little flexibility or system integration
- ODBC/native drivers with SQL-like query; basic cross-sell/up-sell business rules; improved database access
- Object-oriented middleware foundation with TP-monitor technology; maximum scalability, performance, system integration
- 4,737 pages per hour (custom CGI on single-processor Sun UltraSPARC)
- 5,455 pages per hour (custom NSAPI on single-processor Sun UltraSPARC)
- 21,176 pages per hour (One-to-One with CGI on single-processor Sun UltraSPARC)
- 51,428 pages per hour (One-to-One with NSAPI on single-processor Sun UltraSPARC); ~9,990% improvement vs. Custom CGI
- Interfaces with approximately 30 mainframe systems, customer/job scheduling apps, inventory management, telephone and billing systems
- USWest bought 'at least six months of development time as well as linear scalability' vs. building in-house
- 1-2% of total sales from new online customers in most large conventional catalog industries
- Web-based customer service costs an order of magnitude less than call center at $2 per call
- Cost of securing new customer is ten times as great as selling to existing customer
- Aberdeen strongly recommends all Fortune-1000 corporate web site buyers evaluate BroadVision; predicts rest of industry will choose similar relationship management strategy
- BroadVision stock peaked at $26B market cap in 2000; crashed in dot-com bust; nearly delisted; Salesforce.com (founded 1999) captured CRM market; BroadVision became marginal player; acquired by ESW Capital (private equity) May 2020
- Smart cards will allow financial institutions to bridge internet storefronts and brick-and-mortar stores; enable cross-pollination of promotions
- Smart card (EMV chip) became universal by 2015 for payment security but did not bridge online/offline personalization as Aberdeen predicted; that role was filled by loyalty programs, cookies, and mobile apps
- Push technologies merely shift where information resides and do not significantly improve worker productivity or personalize end-user interaction
- PointCast declined and was sold to entrypoint.com in 1999 for approximately $7M after failing to achieve $400M expected IPO; push technology category disappeared by 2000
- Consumer (end-user) data is the competitive advantage; serious internet businesses in financial services and computer sales already recognize this
- Customer data became the defining competitive advantage of the internet era; data-driven personalization (Netflix, Amazon, Google) confirmed Aberdeen's prediction; market for CRM and CDP reached $50B+ by 2020
- Three differentiators: (1) integrated multi-tech system; (2) management/dev tools matching user skills; (3) industry-leading user profile management for custom interaction
- CORBA-based system scales by replacing or adding servers; communicates with existing apps via screen scraping or other CORBA systems
- Return on investment achievable in as little as six months with third-party solutions per large internet catalog vendors
- Aberdeen believes BroadVision is NOT too early for the market; serious internet businesses already recognize customer data as competitive advantage
- Interaction manager maintains persistent connections; caches user profiles, content, categories; amortizes database cost over multiple sessions
