# Infinite Resource Planning: A Manifesto For 21st Century Enterprise Applications

> Aberdeen Group introduces Infinite Resource Planning (IRP) as the inevitable next paradigm in enterprise computing driven by convergence of Object-Oriented Technology, Internet technology, and Network-Centric Computing Architectures. IRP is presented as enabling true cross-organizational supply chain integration and best-of-breed horizontal business process applications delivered via Internet-enabled component architectures.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **Executive White Paper**._


## Top observations

- Inevitable adoption predicted
- Partially realized as cloud ERP/SaaS
- Internet will be first business context for OO line-of-business adoption
- Confirmed: J2EE/web services became dominant enterprise OO platform by 2000-2005
- Ready for enterprise deployment via Internet context
- Reaching market; enabling distributed component apps
- Reaching market alongside Java
- Reaching market; enabling on-the-fly distributed component apps
- Geographic dispersion and multi-pronged supply chain complexity is primary driver
- Interdependence of supply chain partners to reduce WIP inventory and cycle time pressure
- Best-of-breed definition shifting from vertical function to horizontal process
- All enterprise app suppliers will remake products with IRP architecture
- Confirmed: SAP NetWeaver/ESA (2003) and Salesforce (1999) represented architectural remake of EBA
- Real-time demand-side orders and near-immediate supply-side materials access achievable
- Substantially realized through SAP APO/i2 Technologies/JDA by 2005-2010
- IRP makes virtual/networked corporation technically and operationally feasible
- Largely realized through SaaS platforms; Alibaba/Amazon supply networks exemplify virtual corporation
- IRP enables no-maintenance thin clients hiding existing system complexity
- Browser-based SaaS fulfilled thin client vision; traditional thin client (Citrix/NC) failed
- High-value lesson from BPR: enterprise apps must be cross-functional not stovepiped
- BPR exercises proved operations are cross-functional; applications must match
- IRP enables B2B e-commerce foundation for virtual corporation operations
- Confirmed: EDI evolved to XML/web services B2B; Ariba/Commerce One built on these concepts 1999-2002
- Leading enterprises will fundamentally alter best practices to adopt IRP
- Enterprises are on the cusp of watershed technology convergence event
