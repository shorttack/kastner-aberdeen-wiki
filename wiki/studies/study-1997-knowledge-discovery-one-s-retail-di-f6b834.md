# Knowledge Discovery One's Retail Discovery Suite: Promoting Retail Data Mining Applications

> Aberdeen Group profiles KD1 and its Retail Discovery Suite (RDS), a parallel-processing data mining platform designed to deliver POS analytics to retail professionals without requiring dedicated data mining specialists. Aberdeen highlights RDS's ability to automate labor-intensive mining tasks and scale with growing data volumes.


_Published 1997, author **David Hill / Aberdeen Group**, type **Analyst Profile**._


## Top observations

- RDS eliminates much of the labor required to create a retail data mining system while providing analytical depth
- Comprehensive retail solutions have strong data analysis but lack drill-down detail capability; data mining has depth but requires substantial ongoing labor
- Buyer-loyalty patterns and need to predict customer behavior are increasing competitive requirements for retailers
- RDS parallel processing allows system to scale-up and speed-up as retailer brings more data into system
- KD1 and RDS well-positioned to address retail data mining market with democratized access to results
- KD1 did not survive as independent company; no record of activity post-late 1990s; retail analytics market won by larger vendors
- Retail data mining market poised for growth as retailers seek to understand and predict customer behavior
- Data mining software market grew from ~$50M in 1996 to ~$800M by 2000; retail analytics became a major enterprise software segment
- Data mining tools have not become proficient at delivering results to all retail professionals charged with daily decision making
- Retailers face continuously changing POS data requiring tools for value-added decisions on revenues, margins and cost reduction
- KD1 targets gap between comprehensive retail solutions and specialist data mining by eliminating specialist dependency
- RDS parallel processing enables both scale-up and speed-up capabilities for growing retail data
