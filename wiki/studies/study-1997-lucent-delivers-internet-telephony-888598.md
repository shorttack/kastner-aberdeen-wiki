# Lucent Delivers Internet Telephony and Carrier-Grade Scale with Internet Telephony Server SP

> Aberdeen evaluates Lucent's Internet Telephony Server SP as first scalable carrier-grade turnkey VoIP gateway platform. Study covers elemedia voice compression POTS-to-POTS capability service provider market opportunity and Lucent's advantage in large-scale implementation over competitors.


_Published 1997, author **Daniel A. Taylor and Traver H. Kennedy / Aberdeen Group**, type **Impact Brief**._


## Top observations

- Lucent developed first scaleable carrier-grade turnkey gateway platform for internet telephony
- PC-based client requirement has alienated internet telephony from mass markets; incomplete solutions from gateway vendors
- Internet telephony promises significantly lower costs for long distance and international voice and fax traffic
- Skype (2003) and Vonage (2004) demonstrated massive cost reductions for long distance; international VoIP calls fell to near-zero by mid-2000s; Aberdeen's prediction was fully validated
- Scaleable from 24 ports upward with real-time alternate routing and flexible call routing
- System falls back to another data network or PSTN when data network goes down; near toll-grade sound quality
- Lucent is first supplier capable of wide-scale implementation required for internet telephony and fax to reach mass-markets
- Lucent merged with Alcatel in 2006 before achieving predicted VoIP dominance; Cisco VOIP infrastructure and Sonus softswitch became more influential in carrier VoIP buildout; Lucent's specific product line was superseded
- elemedia SX7300P codec delivers near toll-grade sound quality through extensive call processing experience
- elemedia codec was superseded by open standards (G.729 G.711 and later Opus); elemedia absorbed into Lucent; Netscape Media Server died with Netscape; Opus open-source codec now dominates
- ISPs cable TV MSOs utilities RBOCs CAPs CLECs and IXCs all cited as potential internet telephony service providers
- All major service providers (AT&T Verizon BT Deutsche Telekom) eventually deployed IP-based voice switching; circuit-switched PSTN being phased out globally; Aberdeen's service provider VoIP prediction fully validated
- End-users retain phones and fax machines with no behavior change; Lucent handles IP routing transparently
- SIP (Session Initiation Protocol RFC 2543 1999) became dominant VoIP signaling standard; H.323 declined; proprietary gateway approaches like Lucent's initial implementation were superseded
- Lucent targets all service provider types (ISPs cable TV MSOs utilities RBOCs CAPs CLECs IXCs) with turnkey internet telephony backed by 24x7 international support
- Lucent merged with Alcatel in 2006 (Alcatel-Lucent); Nokia acquired Alcatel-Lucent in 2016 for approximately $16.6B; Lucent brand retired
- Netscape Media Server adopted elemedia codec and echo cancellation technologies
- Many internet telephony technologies give poor quality of service and spotty reliability; Lucent solution delivers near toll-grade sound quality
