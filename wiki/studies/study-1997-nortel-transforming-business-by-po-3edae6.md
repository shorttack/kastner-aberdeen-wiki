# Nortel: Transforming Business By Powering The Enterprise

> Aberdeen Group vendor profile positioning Nortel as world leader in end-to-end multimedia networking. Covers Nortel's multimedia calling vision (ATM voice/data/video convergence), Magellan ATM switch product line (#1 claim in multimedia networking), Meridian PBX, Global Enterprise Services (2,000 engineers in 100 countries), and competitive position. Customer testimonials document $435,000/year line cost savings for a 1,600-user frame relay network. Aberdeen concludes Nortel is a best-of-breed benchmark for networking providers. Study was sponsored by Nortel Enterprise Networks. Nortel filed for bankruptcy January 14, 2009.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **Vendor Profile**._


## Top observations

- World leader in end-to-end multimedia network solutions in the Integrated Enterprise
- Magellan ATM/Frame Relay switches #1 in multimedia networking
- 2,000 professionals (80% engineers) in 100 countries
- Data traffic surpassed voice traffic on world's networks for first time in 1996
- $435,000 per year in reduced line costs for 1,600-user 10-county frame relay network
- 24-36 months payback for Nortel Magellan multimedia network
- Nortel well-positioned as market leader; best-of-breed benchmark for networking industry
- Nortel filed for bankruptcy January 14, 2009; liquidated by end of 2009
- Accounting fraud discovered 2003-2004; executives fired and charged; $6B+ revenue misreported 1998-2003
- $297-400 billion peak market cap; ~35-38% of Toronto Stock Exchange
- Approximately 60,000-70,000 employees at time of study (pre-peak)
- ATM will consolidate multimedia (voice/data/video) traffic on enterprise and carrier backbones
- IP/Ethernet/MPLS displaced ATM for most enterprise and carrier applications by mid-2000s
- Six sigma (99.999%) reliability required for multimedia calls and carrier networks
- Nortel Magellan Passport enables true multimedia network carrying voice, data, and video for telemedicine
- Cisco lacks integrated LAN/WAN ATM solutions and end-to-end integration capability
- Cisco became the dominant enterprise networking vendor; Nortel collapsed
- Many large enterprises facing make vs. buy decision on WAN management
- Network services becoming carrier/WAN-centric; NSP market emerging
- Computer Telephony Integration as Nortel competitive advantage vs. routing-only vendors
- Nortel-Sun Microsystems partnership for next-generation multimedia client-server telephones
