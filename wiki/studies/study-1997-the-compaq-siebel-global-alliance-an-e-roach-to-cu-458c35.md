# The Compaq Siebel Global Alliance: An Enterprise Approach to Customer Interaction Software

> Aberdeen Group evaluates the Compaq Siebel Global Alliance, a partnership program targeting the large-scale enterprise Customer Interaction Software (CIS) market. The program bundles pre-sales, post-sales, and implementation support for Siebel Enterprise Applications on Compaq hardware. Aberdeen concludes the alliance provides significant advantages to organizations seeking to reduce the risk, cost, and implementation time of CIS deployments.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **strategy-profile**._


## Top observations

- Global hardware-software partnership for enterprise CIS deployment
- Enterprise-grade sales force automation and customer service/support
- Pre-sales, post-sales, and implementation support for Siebel on Compaq hardware
- Large-scale enterprise organizations
- Alliance will reduce implementation risk, cost, and time for CIS deployments
- 45% CRM market share at peak
- Oracle acquired Siebel for $5.85 billion in January 2006
- HP acquired Compaq for approximately $19-25 billion in May 2002
- High implementation risk, cost, and complexity without vendor partnership
