# 1999 Aberdeen Group Retainer Services Proposal to Computer Associates

> November 20, 1998 Aberdeen Group retainer services proposal from Peter S. Kastner to Herb Siegel (VP Analyst Relations) at Computer Associates, proposing five Knowledge Center retainer subscriptions (Information Security, Internet Infrastructures, Application Development & SDM, Systems & Network Management, Electronic Commerce) plus Industry Services for calendar year 1999. Documents the commercial relationship structure between Aberdeen and CA at the height of the CA-Aberdeen analyst partnership.


_Published 1998, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Aberdeen offers Consulting Services (white papers, market surveys, sales-force seminars, management consulting) AND Strategic Analysis Retainer Services across multiple Knowledge Centers.
- Information Security; Internet Infrastructures; Application Development and Strategic Data Management; Middleware; Network Operating Systems (NOS); Systems and Network Management.
- Electronic Commerce; Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP); NT Systems.
- Industry-vertical retainer services covering specific industries.
- Proposal structure covered five Knowledge Center retainers for calendar year 1999, targeting Herb Siegel's Analyst Relations function at CA.
- Peter S. Kastner signed as Executive Vice President, Aberdeen Group, Inc., Boston MA.
