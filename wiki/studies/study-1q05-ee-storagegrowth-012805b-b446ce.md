# Storage Growth in the Enterprise: Infinite in All Directions

> This benchmark report, led by analyst Bill Malik in 1Q05, examines the risks and management challenges posed by uncontrolled enterprise storage growth. It references vendor pricing data, including Hitachi and IBM DS6000 storage systems, as context for CIOs assessing storage strategy.


_Published 2005, author **Bill Malik / Aberdeen Group**, type **benchmark**._


## Top observations

- The recent announcements of both the Hitachi 500 GB ATA drive (street Oprice under $500) – for a price of $1/GB; and at the high end the IBM DS6000 storage system which runs from 256 GB to 67 TB at an initial list price of $97,000 - for a price of $380/GB. `[ps=0]`
- This study analyzes Storage Growth in the Enterprise: Infinite in All Directions using Aberdeen Group research material and prepared extraction artifacts. `[ps=-1]`
