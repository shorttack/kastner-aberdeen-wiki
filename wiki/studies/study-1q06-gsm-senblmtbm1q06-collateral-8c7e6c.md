# Supplier Enablement Benchmark

> This benchmark study, led by analyst Rick Saia in 1Q06, examines the challenges organizations face enabling suppliers for e-procurement, building on a 2004 benchmark of 155 supplier-enablement implementations. It assesses adoption maturity and identifies best practices in supplier enablement.


_Published 2006, author **Rick Saia / Aberdeen Group**, type **benchmark**._


## Top observations

- Evidence: the typical e-procurement deployment currently has only 17% of its total planned suppliers enabled and manages less than 40% of indirect purchases online. `[ps=0]`
- This study analyzes Supplier Enablement Benchmark using Aberdeen Group research material and prepared extraction artifacts. `[ps=-1]`
