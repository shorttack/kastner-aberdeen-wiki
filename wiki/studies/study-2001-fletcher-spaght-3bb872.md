# Fletcher Spaght Inc. — Growth Strategies for New/Evolving Businesses (Aberdeen Engagement)

> Fletcher Spaght Inc. (FSI) strategy consulting presentation (40 slides) prepared for Aberdeen Group. The primary case study covers Ascendant (Omnigy commerce platform) — a B2C/B2B e-commerce ASP — with FSI conducting 45 customer interviews across e-tailers, 3rd-party fulfillers, and component providers. FSI analyzed Omnigy's market positioning, segment priorities (computer H/W & S/W, apparel/sporting, food/beverage as top segments), and recommended focus on 'Supplier Enablement' over 'B2C' given >$10M/sale economics. Also documents the FSI-Aberdeen collaboration model and FSI's firm profile (est. 1983, Boston). Reveals Aberdeen's business model, partnership strategies, and growth plans circa 2001.


_Published 2001, author **Fletcher Spaght Inc.**, type **consulting-report**._


## Top observations

- 1983
- Successful commercialization; differentiating competitive strategy; increased growth (rate and new sources); enhanced profitability
- New ventures 50%; Established corps launching ventures 40%; Operating divisions 10%
- Northeast 30%; West Coast 25%; Europe 15%; Other U.S. 25%; Japan 5%
- FSI-Aberdeen joint engagements: FSI provides strategy consulting; Aberdeen provides market research publications and analyst access during research phase
- $37,500
- FSI client gets Aberdeen research reports, profiles, white papers, impacts, viewpoints; Aberdeen joins FSI kickoff; FSI coordinates findings/insights with Aberdeen during research
- Market wants rapid time-to-market, low investment 'commerce platform'; ASP model preferred for low capital expenditure and scalability
- Computer H/W and S/W: $9.2B est. revenue, 60% growth; Apparel/Sporting: $2.7B, 230% growth; Food/Beverage: $0.7B, 780% growth
- Difficult to articulate simple compelling description; targeted new undefined space; semantics issue with similar language from competitors
- Customers want ASPs with established hosting track record; performance risks drive preference for proven vendors; Digital River had 700+ stores as proof
- Existing ERP, Siebel, and OMS installations create go/no-go hurdle; financially and emotionally difficult to scrap existing systems
- Focus on 'Supplier Enablement' opportunity; B2C low priority; Supplier Enablement = >$10M/sale vs B2C $1M+/-; 100s of customers per sale vs 1; viral marketing vs one-offs
- eCRM importance and complexity highest for Clicks&Bricks/e-Tailer; lowest for e-Marketplace; varies by supply chain position
- Healthcare Practice Group (healthcare IT, pharma/biotech, medical devices); Corporate Growth (mature growth, new business innovation, competitive differentiation); High Technology (Internet, e-commerce, software, imaging, networking, telecom)
- Phase 1: Project Planning/Analysis Internal Data; Phase 2: Market Research; Phase 3: Competitive Analysis; Phase 4: Develop/Assess Strategic Alternatives; Final Review and Action Plan
- 45 interviews across B2C/B2B companies, 3rd party fulfillers, web consultants, component/point solution providers
- Digital River; Escalate; OrderFusion; CommerceOne; multi-point solution providers with order management and fulfillment management
- Multi-functional C-level sale: CTO/IT (CRM); Sales & Marketing; Operations; Finance; Customer Service — multiple decision makers with different value propositions, seldom concurrent purchases
- IPO clients include: Anadigics $26M; C.P. Clare $113M; Cytyc $48M; Ingram Micro $414M; Wang $144M; others; Acquisition clients: Lotus Development $3.3B; McData $1B+; others
- Bill only time spent productively; commit to not-to-exceed total; frequently put portion at risk; progress payment schedule; reimbursement for incurred expenses
- Bias toward action; profit/value oriented; aid decision making; emphasize answers/options; commitment to cost/deadlines; client loyalty; integrity/objectivity; strategy development; practice area expertise; cost effective
- Leverage 3rd party fulfillment as primary channel; investigate web consulting channel; focus direct sales on very few target markets (B2B e-marketplaces, B2C e-tail if resources permit)
- White Paper ($37,500); Retainers; Seminars; Webcasts; Training; FSI brings Aberdeen into client kickoffs
- Strategy consultants from premier firms; extensive high technology experience; more senior people on average than other firms
