> This Aberdeen InSight, published October 2002, assesses the 802.11a,b wireless chipset market following Atheros Communications' launch of its dual-band AR5001X chip set, which positioned the company as the leading vendor. The piece predicts competitive shakeout, warning that many vendors would not survive in the market a year from the publication date.
