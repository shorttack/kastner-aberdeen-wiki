# IT Spending Shows Encouraging Signs

> Aberdeen Group analyzed Q3 2002 financial results for the top 20 IT suppliers and found revenues 3.4% higher than Q3 2001, suggesting the IT spending contraction was ending. The study identifies Microsoft, HP/Compaq, and security/EAI/CRM segments as early growth indicators. Aberdeen expressed cautious optimism that modest IT purchasing growth was forthcoming.
