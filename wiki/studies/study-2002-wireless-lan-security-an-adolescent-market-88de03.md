# Wireless LAN Security: An Adolescent Market

> This InSight addresses the challenge IT decision-makers face in securing wireless LAN environments and identifies three critical capabilities: VPNs, personal firewalls, and seamless WAP hand-off solutions. The study profiles the emerging WLAN security vendor landscape including AirDefense, Blue Socket, Cranite, Ecutel, Fortress Technologies, ReefEdge, and others, framing WLAN security as an adolescent market in need of maturation.


_Published 2002, author **Eric Hemmendinger**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Market described as adolescent — growing but lacking mature security solutions
- As wireless LAN access increases so too do the problems of IT administrators
- VPN is one of three essential capabilities for WLAN security
- Personal firewall is one of three essential capabilities for WLAN security
- Seamless hand-off between WAPs is one of three essential capabilities for WLAN security
- WEP insufficient for enterprise WLAN security; dedicated solutions required
- Wi-Fi Alliance deprecated WEP in 2004; replaced by WPA/WPA2
- WLAN security and monitoring vendor in emerging security segment
- Acquired by Motorola in 2008; became Motorola AirDefense
- Wireless gateway and WLAN security vendor
- Acquired by Adtran in 2011; previously acquired Pingtel in direction change
- Small WLAN security startup competing in adolescent market
- ReefEdge dissolved circa 2005; Blue Socket offered rebates to stranded customers
- Adolescent market expected to mature through vendor consolidation
- Most specialist WLAN security startups dissolved or acquired by 2010; Cisco, Aruba, Motorola dominated
- Major WLAN infrastructure vendor; potential to add security capabilities
- Cisco became dominant enterprise WLAN security vendor post-2005
- Acquired by Airbus Defence and Space in 2016; survived due to military-grade niche
