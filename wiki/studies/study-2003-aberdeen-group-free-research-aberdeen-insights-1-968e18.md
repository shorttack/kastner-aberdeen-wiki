# Aberdeen Group Free Research: Aberdeen InSights Catalog (2001-2003)

> This publication catalog from Aberdeen Group's free research portal lists approximately 100 Aberdeen InSight briefs published between February 2001 and February 2003, spanning domains including CRM, storage management, wireless technologies, Linux/open source, enterprise applications, supply chain, semiconductors, and IT spending forecasting. Each entry provides the InSight title, publication date, subject domain, author(s), and a brief abstract, collectively representing Aberdeen's research output across that two-year period.


_Published 2003, author **Aberdeen Group (Multiple Authors)**, type **publication-catalog**._


## Top observations

- ~100 InSight briefs published Feb 2001 – Feb 2003
- ~15 InSights on CRM topics spanning 2001-2003
- ~10 InSights on storage management and SAN/NAS
- ~12 InSights on WiFi, Bluetooth, 3G, semiconductors 2001-2003
- Linux will join IBM z/OS and Windows as primary server OSs before end of decade
- Linux became dominant server OS by mid-2000s; now runs majority of cloud infrastructure
- Shakeout in wireless LAN market started; many vendors will not remain in market a year from now
- WiFi market consolidated; Broadcom, Atheros, Intel emerged dominant; many early vendors exited
- 3G rollout delays signal global emerging trend, not just hype
- Global 3G rollout significantly delayed from 2001 projections; mass-market 3G emerged ~2007-2009
- Pocket PC will eclipse Palm OS as leading handheld platform by 2005
- Neither won; smartphone revolution (iPhone 2007) rendered both obsolete; prediction was incorrect
- Nextel Boost subsidiary expected to help company be a key player in mobile youth market
- Nextel merged with Sprint 2005; Boost Mobile became successful prepaid brand
- Exodus filed bankruptcy Sept 2001; C&W acquired assets for $850M
- Baan appears on sound financial footing after turnaround under Invensys
- Baan sold by Invensys to SSA Global 2003; eventually acquired by Infor; viability prediction broadly correct short-term
- Agile and Extreme Programming promote development reengineering rather than just control
- Growing number of IT buyers will divert resources from broad CRM suites to e-mail-centric customer lifecycle messaging
- Enterprise IT planners should maintain open mind toward using IP for storage networking
- Siebel 7.5 exposes business processes as Web services; methodology-driven CRM
- Siebel acquired by Oracle 2006 for ~$5.85B after losing market share to SAP and Salesforce.com
- Microsoft has significant .NET technical progress but still cannot answer IT's fundamental question: 'What is it?'
- .NET became well-established enterprise framework by 2004-2005; identity question resolved by mainstream adoption
- WorldCom filed bankruptcy July 2002; Michael Cappellas joined as CEO; emerged as MCI in 2004; acquired by Verizon 2006
