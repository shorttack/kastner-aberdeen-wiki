## Top observations

- Previous Aberdeen Reports including _Data Clogs Mid-Market Retail Channels: No Money for a Plumber?,_ have illustrated that 58% of mid-market retailers claimed that they currently collect CRM data but are not using it effectively, and in many cases not at all. `[ps=5]`
- Aberdeen research shows that ubiquitous tools like site search and security/privacy encryption are prerequisites for a strong eCommerce experience, yet do little for those trying to catch up to Best-in-Class online merchants in terms of sales lift (Figure 9). `[ps=5]`
- However, a growing number of companies (including 12% of Best-in-Class) are migrating to On Demand solutions that offer monthly rates for use of online eCommerce applications. `[ps=4]`
- Although a significant 22% of the market is currently shopping for new eCommerce platforms, the majority are sticking with their existing platforms and making incremental improvements in the form of online tools designed to capture customers and revenue. `[ps=4]`
- But things are looking up; this holiday season, nearly 90% of consumers will research products T online and about 30% of them will actually transact online **[1]** . `[ps=4]`
- Today, 42% of all retailers face pressures to improve data integration processes by centralizing databases and sharing information throughout their enterprises. `[ps=4]`
- 94% of Best-in-Class will use personalization next year to bring in profits. `[ps=4]`
- 65% of Best-in-Class online merchants are deploying tools to improve the customer experience and in turn the bottom line. `[ps=4]`
- In contrast, nearly 23% of all merchants are considering dropping their existing platforms and working with a new eCommerce software provider or On Demand solutions provider (although Best-in-Class is leading the On Demand charge). `[ps=4]`
- Best in class merchants are concerned with functionality, architecture and flexibility when purchasing online technologies. `[ps=4]`
- Best-in-Class retailers are also feeling pressure from customers to improve and upgrade their sites. `[ps=4]`
- ## **Figure 7: Personalization is in the Works for 87% of Online Class** `[ps=4]`
- Ninety-three percent of all BIC retailers plan on using personalization in some form or another (Figure 7). `[ps=4]`
- Yet, 59% of BIC firms make this data actionable by strategically up-selling and crossselling products using personalized collaborative filtering, also known as affinity selling. `[ps=4]`
- Rather, tools that are designed to capture revenue by providing better information (e.g., interactive imaging), targeted messaging (e.g., alerting) and community driven feedback (Web 2.0 technologies) have resulted in the greatest lift in sales from those trying to catch up to Best-in-Class. `[ps=4]`
- What’s interesting here is that 53% of BIC merchants list Web 2.0 technologies on their roadmaps for development initiatives in the next 24 months. `[ps=4]`
- So it is not surprising that 65% of Best-in-Class firms cited developing or buying online tools as their primary strategy for attaining market leadership, and 59% specifically noted extending their online toolset with add-ons and incremental investments as a means to this end. `[ps=4]`
- All retailers are concerned with ROI yet 29% of companies concede to the fact that they don’t measure ROI as much as they should. `[ps=3]`
- ## **Figure 1: ROI Expectations Accelerate** `[ps=3]`
- As a result, 59% of Best-in-Class list keeping pace with competitors as a top driver for their online initiatives. `[ps=3]`
- Yet, when adding tools, Best-in-Class merchants are watching their customers carefully to determine how customers behave so that they can deliver appropriate tools to maximize ROI from their existing customers. `[ps=3]`
- Technology selection is based on a number of factors, but Best-in-Class B2C companies prioritize product functionality, product architecture and flexibility as the top drivers when selecting technology partners (Figure 6). `[ps=3]`
- Nearly half of the Best-in-Class are combating these pressures by both listening to what their customers are saying and watching what they do. `[ps=3]`
- While 35% calculates ROI based on direct conversions to sales, 29% of BIC revealed that they don’t calculate ROI as much as they should. `[ps=3]`
