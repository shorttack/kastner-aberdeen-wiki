# Top 100 Kastner Economic Calls: Ranked by Per-Study Share-of-Prescience Net Attributed Value

> This 2026 ranking exercise extends the kastner-prescience-methodology-demo by allocating Phase 4 theme-level net-attributed value (share-of-prescience discounted, 2026 USD) across the 360 individual high-prescience studies classified to the 15 headline themes, producing a ranked top-100 list of individual Kastner economic calls. Equal-within-theme allocation is used as the defensible default; ties are by design and indicate the floor of what can be inferred without per-study lead/contrarian/specificity scoring. The exercise demonstrates that the archive supports study-level economic attribution analysis and surfaces specific high-impact items (Mobile/Smartphone calls at $237.7B/study, Digital Consumer Technology at $108.6B/study, OLTP at $57.8B/study, Cloud Computing at $47.4B/study) for future per-study refinement passes.
