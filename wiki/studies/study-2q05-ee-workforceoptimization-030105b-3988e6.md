# Workforce Optimization Benchmark Study in Retail: From Product Management to People Management

> This research preview describes an upcoming Aberdeen benchmark study on workforce optimization in retail, examining the shift from product-centric to people-centric management practices. Dr. Katherine Jones is named as lead analyst for the study.


_Published 2005, author **Katherine Jones / Aberdeen Group**, type **benchmark**._


## Top observations

- While workforce management policies, procedures and technologies in retail can be poorly articulated and haphazard, best in class retailers today are combining strategy and technology to better optimize their investment in their labor force. `[ps=4]`
- _**Hypothesis for the research:**_ For US retailers, hourly in-store payroll exceeds $250 billion. `[ps=0]`
