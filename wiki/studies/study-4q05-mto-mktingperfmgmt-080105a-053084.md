# Marketing Performance Management: Metrics that Matter — Evaluating and Selecting Processes and Technology to Drive Business Value

> This research preview introduces an upcoming Aberdeen benchmark study on marketing performance management, focused on identifying the metrics, processes, and technologies that drive measurable business value. Leslie Ament is named as lead analyst for the study.


_Published 2005, author **Leslie Ament / Aberdeen Group**, type **benchmark**._


## Top observations

- Recent Aberdeen research indicates that best-in-class companies are increasing revenues, share of customer, and enhancing profitability by investing in technology that empowers them to automate, refine processes, and develop meaningful metrics within their marketing ecosystems. `[ps=4]`
- Our research suggests that best-in-class companies are better able to show the correlation between marketing performance metrics, corporate goals, and higher profitability. `[ps=4]`
- Understand how best-in-class companies select, develop, and deploy key processes and technologies to measure meaningful performance metrics; `[ps=0]`
