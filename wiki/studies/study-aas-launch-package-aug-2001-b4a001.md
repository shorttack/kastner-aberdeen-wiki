# Advisory Access Service (AAS) Launch Package: Kastner-to-Willmott Status Memo + Marketing Collateral (August 2001)

> Aberdeen Group's August 2001 product launch package for the rebranded Advisory Access Service (AAS), driven by Peter Kastner and reported to founding partner Tom Willmott in a status memo dated August 7, 2001. The package combines four artifacts: (1) the Kastner to Willmott internal memo defining five workshops (Go-to-Market, Market Knowledge, Market Positioning, Messaging Strategy, Go-to-Market Readiness) and the rationalized AAS pricing model; (2) the AAS sales product overview detailing Six-Month Introductory ($6,000) and Twelve-Month Multi-Day retainer packages with explicit pricing tiers from Associate ($10,200) to Executive ($21,000+); (3) the Aberdeen Advisory Service SLIC narrative; and (4) the Advisory Services slip-sheet marketing brochure. New price list effective September 1, 2001. Cc'd on the memo: Chet Verceglio, Margie Koenig (workshop methodologies with Kathleen Roche).


_Published 2001, author **Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Kastner to Willmott, cc Chet Verceglio and Margie Koenig; subject: New Product Launch Status; date August 7 2001
- Five workshops defined for AAS launch package: Go-to-Market; Market Knowledge; Market Positioning; Messaging Strategy; Go-to-Market Readiness
- Reviews market, competition, positioning, and messaging
- Covers market segments, dynamics, evolution, and competition
- Positioning the company or product for maximum market advantage
- Development of key messages for customers, press, analysts, and investors
- Reviews presentations and launch collateral prior to public launch; two variations
- Alliances workshop to examine channel and partner opportunities; planned for future (not yet defined as of Aug 2001)
- Margie Koenig and Kathleen Roche working on Workshop methodologies; Workshop SLIC in production
- Each workshop has: 50-page sales proposal template + 25-page product overview with selling tips
- AAS is a rebranded retainer family; new program name is Aberdeen Advisory Services (AAS); retainer packages built from base relationship deliverables plus consulting days plus optional Pod services such as surveys
- $6,000 upfront; includes analyst access, pubs access online, press access, and half-day consulting
- Offered one-time only to first-time clients; targeted at new clients with minimal resources
- $7,000 (12-month ad hoc access) plus per diem rate x minimum 2 consulting days; invoiced quarterly
- $10,200 = $7,000 + ($1,600 x 2 days)
- $13,000 = $7,000 + ($3,000 x 2 days)
- $16,000 = $7,000 + ($4,500 x 2 days)
- $17,400 = $7,000 + ($5,200 x 2 days)
- $19,000 = $7,000 + ($6,000 x 2 days)
- $21,000 = $7,000 + ($7,000 x 2 days)
- New price list effective September 1, 2001; rationalized and simplified; eliminates unsold services; returns to labor + retainers + publications + content basics
- Free to all active Aberdeen clients; includes ad hoc analyst access, pubs access online, and press access during course of engagement
- Six-Month Introductory AAS renews free if client contracts: any consulting day; or MAS at $22,500; or amplification project >= $15,000; or RAMP/Strategy Acceleration project
- $2,000 add-on (20% discount off standard price) when practice area MAS research is complete and available
- Half-day consulting usable as: block time for go-to-market strategy; quarterly briefing updates; research preparation; or labor credit toward Workshop
