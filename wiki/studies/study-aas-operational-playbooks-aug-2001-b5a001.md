# Aberdeen AAS Operational Playbooks (August 2001): Engaging Aberdeen Workshop, Briefing Workshop, Market Knowledge Methodology, and Activity Methodologies

> Five internal Aberdeen Group playbooks dated August 2001, comprising the operational backbone for the post-bubble Advisory Access Service (AAS) launch driven by Peter Kastner. The set includes the Engaging Aberdeen Workshop (positioning, branding, lifecycle consulting, competitive landscape, solution-selling), the Briefing Workshop (pre-briefing due diligence, vendor/product profiles, NDA ground rules, lead-up agenda control, wrap-up pitch), the Market Knowledge Workshop methodology (competitive analysis, product SWOT, market overview, segmentation, current positioning), and two activity-methodology spreadsheets (Go-to-Market and Market Knowledge) enumerating step-by-step tasks, task times, analyst outputs, and required skill sets. Together these define how Aberdeen analysts operationalized the rebranded AAS workshop family in late 2001.


_Published 2001, author **Aberdeen Group / Peter S. Kastner (program owner)**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Ensure all employees understand who Aberdeen is and how it differentiates from competitors
- Positioning; Branding; Lifecycle Consulting Model
- Research Made Personal
- Aberdeen positioned against Gartner, META Group, Forrester, IDC
- Step 1: determine client needs via sales data sheet and qualifying questions; Step 2: propose product via sales scenarios; Step 3: handle client questions
- Workshop includes 'And Always' (best practices) and 'And Never' (worst practices) sections
- Ensure consistent briefing methodology; train on mechanics and Q&A; provide specific tools to capture supplier information
- A planned meeting between Aberdeen Analysts/Account Managers and IT suppliers
- Gather information on supplier; complete vendor/product profile template; coordinate team for major accounts
- Analysts must understand the role of the supplier's PR representative before the briefing
- Reconcile scheduling; take agenda control; brief Aberdeen overview; introduce practice area; clarify NDA ground rules
- Stay awake and ask questions; specific tips for evaluating supplier presentations during briefing
- Reflect and summarize what was heard in briefing; present pitch to client for follow-on engagement
- Workshop includes explicit 'Always' best practices and 'Never' prohibitions sections
- Dedicated Briefing Sales Strategies section concludes the Briefing Workshop manual
- 2-day interactive engagement; 4-6 page report; 1-hour teleconference; 1-2 page proposal for follow-on
- Competitive Analysis; Competitive Landscape; Product Analysis (SWOT); Market Overview (trends, segmentation, size, drivers, growth, strategic shifts); Current Positioning Analysis (current position, internal market perception)
- Supplier internal marketing material; supplier website; S1 filings; MSN investor.com/Quicken.com; trade publications; financial analyst reports; chat rooms; competitor websites
- Task time: 1/2 day; performed by Research Associate
- I. Introduction/Get Acquainted; II. Market Overview; III. Market Attractiveness; IV. Positioning Strategy; V. Messaging & Communications; VI. Channel & Partnership; VII. Review & Next Steps
- 4-6 page assessment report; analysis must demonstrate vision and thought leadership in the client's market
- 2 hours; teleconference with client to identify additional data needs
- 1 day; on-site meeting reviewing client technology, strategy, business plan, and operating plans
- Market Overview: 1/2 day; Competitive Analysis: 1/2 day; Product Analysis (SWOT): 1/2 day; Current Position Analysis: 1/2 day
- Ability to conduct SWOT analysis on competitors; ability to capture data relating to the client's competition
