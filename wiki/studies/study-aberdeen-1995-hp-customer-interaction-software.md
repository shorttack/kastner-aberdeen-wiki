# HP Smart ContAct/CIS: Customer Interaction Software - The New Market Weapon

> Aberdeen Group profile evaluating HP's Smart ContAct/CIS customer interaction software suite, positioned as a new market weapon for CTI and customer service automation.


_Published 1995, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **product-profile**._


## Top observations

- open APIs enabling wide range of applications and phone systems `[ps=5]`
- Partially correct near-term; HP did execute Smart ContAct partnerships but CRM market consolidated around Siebel Systems (dominant 1997-2004) and Salesforce.com (founded 1999). HP exited CIS as distinct business. `[ps=5]`
- application- and communication-device-independent `[ps=4]`
- partnership-based not monolithic `[ps=4]`
- packaged middleware reduces implementation time and costs `[ps=4]`
- surround existing systems with new technology `[ps=4]`
- Aberdeen predicts CIS market will sustain 40%+ annual growth through mid-to-late 1990s, reaching multi-billion dollar scale `[ps=4]`
- Mixed: ISV ecosystem prediction correct as concept but wrong winner. Siebel dominated CRM (acquired by Oracle 2006 for $5.85B); Clarify acquired by Nortel 1998; Remedy acquired by BMC 2002; Vantive acquired by PeopleSoft 1999. HP not ecosystem winner. `[ps=4]`
- too complex for most user organizations to implement alone `[ps=3]`
- video clips, images, customer records, mainframe 3270 screens simultaneously `[ps=3]`
- ISCs forming for telecom and financial services in Europe `[ps=3]`
- CIS market needs major systems supplier to take the lead `[ps=3]`
- Aberdeen predicts CTI (Computer Telephony Integration) middleware will be a critical competitive differentiator in enterprise CIS architectures `[ps=3]`
- Confirmed: CTI integration remained central to call-center CRM through 2010s; evolved into cloud contact centers (Genesys, Avaya, Amazon Connect); Cisco UCCX became dominant CTI middleware by 2005 `[ps=3]`
- CIS systems-supplier-of-choice in 1996 `[ps=1]`
- firmly endorses Smart ContAct as premier CIS program `[ps=1]`
- Aberdeen predicts HP will be the CIS systems-supplier-of-choice in 1996, cementing leadership through Smart ContAct initiative `[ps=1]`
- Aberdeen predicts HP's partnerships with leading CIS ISVs (Clarify, Remedy, Scopus, Siebel, Vantive) will create a dominant ecosystem for enterprise CIS delivery `[ps=1]`
- 40% annually or better `[ps=0]`
- 40% per year `[ps=0]`
- >200 call center sites `[ps=0]`
- December 1995 `[ps=0]`
- 3800 individuals `[ps=0]`
- $30 billion `[ps=0]`
- $300-$800 per user `[ps=0]`
