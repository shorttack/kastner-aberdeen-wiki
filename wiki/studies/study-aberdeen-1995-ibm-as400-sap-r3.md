_Published 1995, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **product-profile**._


## Top observations

- SAP America near-100% YoY growth; R/3 at 85% of North American revenues; first vendor to $1B; high R&D spend signals continued innovation `[ps=5]`
- SAP became the global ERP leader, dominating the market through 2000s. R/3 evolved to SAP ECC, then S/4HANA (2015). IBM became SAP's largest customer in 1994 and partnership deepened. SAP SE had revenues of ~$35B by 2023. `[ps=5]`
- Study positioned AS/400 Advanced Series as a long-term enterprise platform due to simplicity, integration, and 64-bit RISC advantages `[ps=4]`
- AS/400 evolved through iSeries (2000), System i (2006), IBM Power Systems/IBM i (2008-present). As of 2025, 32.1% of install base still on IBM i 7.1+. Platform remained commercially successful for 30+ years confirming Aberdeen's thesis. `[ps=3]`
