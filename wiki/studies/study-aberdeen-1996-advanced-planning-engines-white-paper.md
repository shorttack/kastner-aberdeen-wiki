# Advanced Planning Engine Technologies: Can Capital-Generating Technology Change the Face of Manufacturing?

> Aberdeen Group executive white paper examining Advanced Planning Engine Technologies (APET) for manufacturing, logistics and distribution. Profiles seven key vendors — American Software, FACT Inc., i2 Technologies, Manugistics, Numetrix, Red Pepper Software, and ILOG/others — detailing product capabilities, ROI case studies, and technical evaluation criteria. Argues APET should be the first solution acquisition on the path to enterprise reengineering.
