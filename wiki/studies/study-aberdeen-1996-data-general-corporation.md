# Data General Corporation — Aberdeen Group Company Profile

> Aberdeen Group profiles Data General Corporation's strategy of delivering integrated open-systems solutions through deep ISV partnerships with SAP, Oracle, and PeopleSoft, anchored by its AViiON UNIX/NT servers and CLARiiON storage subsystems. The study documents DG's revenue mix transformation from proprietary minicomputers to over 90% open-systems revenues, and validates partner and customer satisfaction through primary interviews. Aberdeen concludes DG has found a sustainable balance between responsiveness and global scale.


_Published 1996, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- $1.0B to $1.2B
- >90% of product revenues
- >$620M
- Alternate-channel dominant: >50% revenues from VARs and distributors
- Level 3 (joint development + joint marketing + cross-trained support)
- Mid-market enterprises $50M to $1B revenue
- 1 to 32 processors with near-linear scaling
- Fault-tolerant high-availability storage for UNIX and NT
- First vendor to offer R/3 in combined UNIX/Windows NT Server configuration
- 5,000 to 80,000 employees
- DG has found sustainable balance: small enough to respond flexibly, large enough for worldwide support
- Impressive pace of R/3 sales will continue for foreseeable future
- 25 jointly hired engineers at Oracle headquarters
- DG sent 5 engineers to SAP Germany HQ in 1994; established Competency Center in North Carolina
- Strong viability predicted based on ISV partnership depth and customer satisfaction
- PeopleSoft has quickly become leading supplier of client-server HR applications
- Acquired by EMC for $1.1 billion (Oct 1999); AViiON discontinued; only CLARiiON continued
- Acquired by Oracle for $10.3B (January 2005); brand integrated into Oracle HCM suite
- Customers cited exceptional price:performance as primary selection criterion
- Near-linear SMP scaling experience cited as key differentiator
- CLARiiON integration provides great HA features
- Demonstrated ability to support systems on a worldwide basis
- DG provides consulting and training on AViiON + ISV app + database simultaneously
