# Managing Customers with Next-Generation Software Applications: 1996 Edition

> Aberdeen Group's second report on the Customer Interaction Software (CIS) market defines the category encompassing sales force automation, helpdesk, field service, telemarketing, and related technologies. The study profiles over 40 CIS suppliers, identifies market dynamics, and advises IS executives that CIS is the key to building long-term profitable customer relationships while warning that most of the hundreds of suppliers will not survive long-term.


_Published 1996, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Issue tracking, escalation, knowledge base for internal IT and external customer support `[ps=5]`
- Companies rushing to install CIS systems cannot afford to make mistakes. Market filled with hundreds of suppliers — only a few will be around over the long term `[ps=5]`
- Internet-enabled CIS will create unexpected implications; future CIS systems will leverage web technologies `[ps=5]`
- Siebel Systems became dominant CRM vendor with ~$1.5B revenue before Oracle acquisition for $5.8B (2006); SAP CRM and Salesforce.com (2000) also emerged; hundreds of small vendors were consolidated or disappeared `[ps=5]`
- Salesforce.com (founded 1999) pioneered SaaS/web-based CRM and reached $300M revenue by 2006; web-based CRM became standard; prediction proved highly accurate `[ps=5]`
- Contact management, opportunity tracking, forecasting, sales territory management `[ps=4]`
- Outbound/inbound calling campaigns, lead management, scripting `[ps=4]`
- Growth and profit-oriented companies gaining market share through coordinated sales tactics and superior customer service via CIS `[ps=4]`
- Most of hundreds of CIS suppliers will not survive long-term; only a few will remain to support users; buyers must choose right the first time `[ps=4]`
- Three audiences: (1) business executives choosing CIS, (2) IS executives implementing CIS, (3) CIS suppliers analyzing market position `[ps=3]`
- Dispatch, scheduling, parts management, on-site service tracking `[ps=3]`
- Literally hundreds of CIS suppliers in the market in 1996; study profiles over 40 `[ps=0]`
- Second Aberdeen CIS report; first published May 1995; 1996 edition priced at US$695 `[ps=0]`
- Computer-Telephony Integration identified as important related technology for CIS; links customer phone interactions with data systems
- Aberdeen explains 'unexpected implications' of Internet-enabled CIS; web-based customer interaction beginning to emerge
- Profiled CIS supplier; founded 1993 by Tom Siebel; focused on enterprise sales force automation; early-stage high-growth vendor
- Profiled CIS supplier; strong in IT helpdesk/service management; Action Request System product
- Profiled CIS supplier; focused on customer support and CRM; later acquired by Nortel for $2.1B in 1998
- Over 40 CIS software suppliers profiled; 5 systems integrators profiled; 3 hardware/platform suppliers profiled
