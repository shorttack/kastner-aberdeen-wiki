# Remote Access: Cisco's Untold Story

> Aberdeen Group examines Cisco Systems' remote access strategy as of November 1996, revealing Cisco's 'untold story' of quietly dominating the enterprise branch-to-branch segment (60%+ market share) while making aggressive inroads into the high-end ISP and carrier markets with the AS5200 access server. The study evaluates Cisco's multi-product portfolio (AS5200, 3600, 1600 series), IOS software competitive advantages, and the Telebit MICA technology acquisition, predicting Cisco is better positioned than competitors to serve the rapidly evolving ISP/carrier market.


_Published 1996, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Four weapons: IOS software, acquisition capability, large loyal customer base, excellent support `[ps=5]`
- Cisco became dominant ISP/carrier infrastructure vendor by 1999-2000; backbone routers and access servers `[ps=5]`
- Internet access did commoditize rapidly 1997-2000; ISP consolidation followed (AOL, MindSpring mergers) `[ps=5]`
- Robustness and depth of IOS software is Cisco's primary value-add; cross-platform consistency `[ps=4]`
- Timely acquisition of technologies for rapidly changing market; Combinet, Newport, Telebit MICA `[ps=4]`
- Web browser-based configuration and monitoring; installs in minutes with any browser `[ps=4]`
- Security, availability, reliability, QoS, and management cited as five ISP differentiation factors `[ps=4]`
- Aberdeen believes Cisco better positioned long-term than major competitors for ISP/carrier market `[ps=4]`
- IOS software will prove attractive to ISPs differentiating services with value-added capabilities `[ps=4]`
- 1997 will be year of consolidation at upper levels; internet access becomes commodity `[ps=4]`
- 1997 will be year of 'price per port' as Service Providers drive infrastructure expansion `[ps=4]`
- Ascend acquired by Lucent Technologies in 1999 for $24B; validated Aberdeen's consolidation prediction `[ps=4]`
- US Robotics acquired by 3Com in 1997 for $7.3B; removed from independent competition `[ps=4]`
- Majority of ISP connectivity upgrades will be sub-T3 installations over next 12 months `[ps=4]`
- Enables construction of single integrated information systems infrastructure; MPPP, VPDN, adaptive routing, security `[ps=4]`
- Cisco's depth and diversity in remote access makes company force to be reckoned with at all market levels `[ps=4]`
- High-density digital modem concentration for next-gen ISP access servers; enables sub-T3 installations `[ps=3]`
- Virtual Private Dial-up network capabilities cited as key IOS feature for service differentiation `[ps=3]`
- Flexible platforms needed for both digital (ISDN) and analog dial-in access; both segments growing `[ps=3]`
- Support for TACACS, TACACS+, RADIUS, token cards, certificate authentication `[ps=3]`
