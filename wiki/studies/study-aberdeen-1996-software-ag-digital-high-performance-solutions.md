> Aberdeen Group evaluates the Software AG ADABAS D RDBMS combined with Digital Equipment Corporation's 64-bit Alpha servers with Very Large Memory (VLM) technology. The study finds that VLM technology delivers 10-100x performance improvement for in-memory database operations, positioning the Software AG/Digital combination as an enterprise solution for OLTP, data marts, and application servers. Aberdeen concludes the combination meets a broad range of user needs for high-performance data management.


_Published 1996, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- 10-100x performance improvement for in-memory database operations over non-VLM systems `[ps=5]`
- Acquired by Compaq for $9.6B in June 1998; largest computer industry merger at the time `[ps=5]`
- VLM technology extends high-performance database to broader enterprise applications; orders-of-magnitude improvements achievable `[ps=5]`
- VLM enables ultra-high-performance OLTP by keeping hot data in main memory `[ps=5]`
- In-memory databases (SAP HANA 2011, Redis, Memcached, VoltDB) became mainstream; Aberdeen's directional prediction proved accurate `[ps=4]`
- VLM enables effective end-user access to larger databases with complex queries `[ps=4]`
- 32-bit architectures address less than 1GB main memory; 64-bit Alpha increases addressable memory by several orders of magnitude `[ps=3]`
- Available on HP-UX, IBM AIX, Sun Solaris, SCO Unix, Digital Unix, Windows NT, OS/2; broad cross-platform strategy `[ps=3]`
- Software AG survived and grew; acquired by Silver Lake in 2023 for €2.2B; ADABAS & Natural being spun off as independent business in 2025 `[ps=3]`
- VLM costs less than adding another system or solid-state disk; boosts topped-out SMP systems `[ps=2]`
- Aberdeen recommends IS buyers consider Software AG/Digital for RDBMS and VLM deployments; implies long-term viability `[ps=2]`
- Near-lights-out automated distributed-database administration; lower cost of ownership than Oracle/DB2 `[ps=2]`
- Digital has large installed VAX customer base currently upgrading to Alpha `[ps=2]`
- VLM delivers competitive advantage for ERP including SAP R/3 at medium enterprise scale `[ps=2]`
- Aberdeen implies DEC has strong market position via Alpha; large VAX install base upgrading to Alpha `[ps=1]`
- ADABAS D V6 supports 10-gigabytes-and-over data caches `[ps=0]`
- Upwards of $500 million in revenues in fiscal 1995 `[ps=0]`
- More than 15,000 Alpha servers shipped in the past year `[ps=0]`
- ADABAS D integrates with mainframe Oracle, ADABAS C, and DB2 via ENTIRE networking `[ps=0]`
- Available on HP-UX, AIX, Sun Solaris, SCO Unix, Digital Unix, Windows NT, OS/2 `[ps=0]`
