# Unisys Clears the Path Ahead for A & OS 2200 Series Customers

> Aberdeen Group profiles Unisys ClearPath HMP (Heterogeneous Multi-Processing) servers, which combine CMOS versions of MCP/AS and OS 2200 enterprise systems with Intel-based Unix/NT SMP servers in a single cabinet. Aberdeen examines how ClearPath gives Unisys's 8,500+ mainframe customers a migration path to open client-server architectures while preserving existing investments. The study notes that Unisys is transitioning from hardware to services, while hardware remains 40% of $6.2B in 1995 revenue.


_Published 1996, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Unisys successfully transitioned to services-dominant company; ClearPath OS 2200 and MCP still actively supported; OS 2200 now available on AWS (June 2025) `[ps=5]`
- ClearPath OS 2200 and MCP remain in active production; ClearPath Forward product line updated through 2024; OS 2200 deployed on AWS June 2025 `[ps=5]`
- Clear path for A Series and OS 2200 customers to migrate to multi-tier open architectures while preserving existing investments `[ps=4]`
- 0.5-micron CMOS technology (IBM-fabricated) expected to deliver 40-60 MIPS; replaces ECL systems `[ps=4]`
- Customers capitalize on combined benefits of robust mainframe OS environment and open Windows NT or Unix environment in single cabinet `[ps=4]`
- New enterprise server models preserve customers' investments in mission-critical applications; natural evolution rather than forced migration `[ps=4]`
- Unisys committed to transforming into services-based information management company while sustaining hardware revenue `[ps=4]`
- ClearPath HMP announcement is important for Unisys and its customers; provides effective architecture for modernization and re-engineering `[ps=4]`
- Pre-integration by Unisys substantially enhances using HMP for sophisticated client/server applications; single-cabinet management `[ps=3]`
- $6.2 billion `[ps=0]`
- More than 8,500 mid- to large-range A Series and 1100/2200 customers worldwide `[ps=0]`
- Unisys promises to integrate OPUS MPP system into ClearPath HMP in future releases `[ps=0]`
- 0.5-micron CMOS technology designed by Unisys and fabricated by IBM; new high-end processors expected to deliver 40-60 MIPS `[ps=0]`
- 40% of $6.2 billion (approximately $2.48 billion) from large-scale computer systems
- Unisys becomes first supplier of large-scale enterprise servers to replace entire line -- including high-end -- with CMOS alternatives
- CMOS delivers smaller footprint, power savings vs ECL; cost-effective upgrades preserve customer investments
- Unisys discontinued OPUS MPP line by early 2000s; OPUS MPP not integrated into ClearPath as promised; prediction did not materialize
- ClearPath HMP provides effective architecture for modernization; gives 8,500+ customers clear path to multi-tier applications of the future
- Transitioning from large-scale computer hardware company to services-based information management company; hardware still 40% of revenue
- ClearPath A Series (MCP) customers can implement client/server at sensible pace; path to new cost-effective CMOS upgrades
