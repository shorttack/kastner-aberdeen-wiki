# 3Com Accelerates into Remote Access Market

> Aberdeen Group assesses 3Com Corporation's strategic expansion into the remote access market as of early 1997, following a year of acquisitions (Centrum, AccessWorks, Primary Access, Sonix) and product launches. The study describes the four market segments of remote access (enterprise, SOHO, individual user, ISP/carrier), evaluates 3Com's three solution families (AccessBuilder, NetBuilder, Impact), and concludes that while 3Com is not yet the market leader it has a logical and executable strategy, with its greatest strength in the SOHO segment and its channels as its most valuable competitive asset.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **white-paper**._


## Top observations

- Billion-dollar-plus market `[ps=4]`
- 30-35% of cost of ownership is line costs; 40-45% is support requirements `[ps=4]`
- Data-centric mainframe roots; needs lower TCO, legacy support, security, investment protection; 3Com targets with AccessBuilder high-performance servers `[ps=3]`
- Fastest growing segment; Aberdeen considers 3Com best-established and most competitive here `[ps=2]`
- Foundation of remote access market; 3Com targets with Impact ISDN modems and AccessBuilder Internet `[ps=2]`
- Coherent framework for scaling performance, growth management, and network reach from core to remote; 3Com's consistent management philosophy `[ps=2]`
- Two global business units: one for low/mid-tier customers, one for high-end enterprise/ISP/telco; positions company to address full remote access spectrum `[ps=2]`
- Growing in excess of 150% per year `[ps=0]`
- Acquired June 1995; dominant provider of integrated remote access to network service providers worldwide `[ps=0]`
- Free ISDN line ordering via 800-number team; 3Com provisions ISDN for homes, small offices and branch offices `[ps=0]`
- 7 and 17-slot chassis; up to 256 WAN ports; multiple LAN segments; RISC processors; Ethernet or Token-Ring `[ps=0]`
- Range from 15-minute install terminal adapters to complex integrated access switching platforms with ISP/carrier-grade redundancy `[ps=0]`
- Systems approach: full-range solutions across all four market segments (enterprise, SOHO, individual, ISP/carrier) with focus on total cost of ownership reduction
- New segment requiring robustness, reliability, scalability; 3Com targeting with AccessBuilder 5000 and 8000
- Centrum Communications acquired February 1994; brought AccessBuilder product line
- Aberdeen expects 3Com to continue enjoying increasing success in remote access, especially SOHO; enterprise/ISP segments will be harder to crack
- 3Com gained dominance in SOHO/consumer modems via USRobotics merger; failed to establish enterprise/ISP leadership; exited enterprise networking entirely in 2000; acquired by HP 2010 for $2.7B
- 3Com's channels are its most valued asset and most important element in its remote access strategy
- Channel strength proved durable for SOHO segment but insufficient for enterprise/carrier; 3Com's Enterprise segment discontinuation in 2000 confirmed Aberdeen's enterprise-challenge prediction
- ISDN availability becoming more widespread and tariffing more reasonable; key technology for 3Com's SOHO growth
