# AcceleratedSAP: Fast Implementation, Rapid Realization of SAP Benefits

> This 1997 Aberdeen Group white paper profiles SAP's AcceleratedSAP (ASAP) rapid-implementation methodology, designed to deploy R/3 in four to ten months for mid-market enterprises. It describes the six-step ASAP process (Project Preparation, Business Blueprint, Simulation, Validation, Final Preparation, Go Live), the TeamSAP partner ecosystem, and positions ASAP as the preferred path for Year 2000 legacy replacement. Aberdeen assesses ASAP as a credible differentiator that reduces implementation redundancy and creates a reusable knowledge foundation.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **white-paper**._


## Top observations

- 4 to 10 months
- $200 million to $2.5 billion annual revenue
- 1,000 consultants (goal: 1,200 by year-end 1997)
- Project Preparation — Organizational Readiness
- Business Blueprint — Define Business Requirements (3-4 weeks in 6-month project)
- Simulation — R/3 Knowledge Transfer To Customers
- Validation — Configuration Finalized and Knowledge Transfer Continued
- Final Preparation — Testing interfaces and end-user acceptance
- Go Live and Support — Self-sufficient operations
- Emerging: rapid configuration, modeling, and continuous improvement tool
- Not suitable for large-scale reengineering; optimal for standard process adoption
- Rapid deployment / best-practice adoption strategy
- ASAP as Y2K remediation path: replace legacy systems with R/3 in compressed timeline
- SAP plans to certify implementation partners for ASAP methodology
- Common ASAP methodology enables parallel implementations across geographic/LOB boundaries
- First branded implementation solution developed and directly supported by SAP
- Most customers selected R/3 implementors on basis of trust and comfort through past relationship or strong peer references
- Most National Implementation Partners strongly embraced ASAP and plan to incorporate into service offerings
- ASAP will become the standard implementation path for mid-market R/3 globally
- Companies completing ASAP Phase 1 will need far fewer external resources for subsequent ASAP phases
- End-of-1997 R/3 4.0 release will offer further configuration capabilities in Business Engineer
- ASAP became global standard; superseded by SAP Activate methodology for S/4HANA in 2015
- Dissolved 2002 following Enron accounting scandal conviction
- Merged with USinternetworking to form marchFIRST (2000); bankrupt 2001
- 2 days for expectation alignment exercise
