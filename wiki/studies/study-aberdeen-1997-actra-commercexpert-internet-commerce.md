# No Longer an Internet Commerce Paper Tiger, Actra's CommerceXpert is Now in the Hunt

> This one-page Aberdeen Group abstract profile assesses Actra Corp., a joint venture of Netscape Communications and GE Information Services, concluding that the company delivered on its ambitious 1997 product roadmap. Aberdeen notes that Actra shipped its Internet EDI product (ECXpert), sales-to-order product (SellerXpert), procurement product (BuyerXpert), and updated PublishingXpert on or near promised release dates. The combined CommerceXpert product family, backed by the distribution power of Netscape and GEIS, positions Actra as a formidable competitor in the emerging business-to-business Internet commerce market.


_Published 1997, author **David Alschuler, Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Delivered 4 major products on or near promised dates in 5-month window
- Channel-only distribution via Netscape and GEIS; no dedicated Actra sales force
- Shipped to Beta and GA; best-of-breed candidate in Internet EDI market
- Shipped to Beta and GA; best-of-breed candidate in sales-to-order automation
- Delivered to early customers; best-of-breed candidate in procurement automation
- Work in progress but formidable when combined; individually best-of-breed candidates
- Other suppliers in B2B Internet commerce market are now looking over their shoulders towards Sunnyvale
- Spring 1997: Aberdeen questioned whether Actra could deliver — 'Runs great on the overhead projector'
- ~200 employees
- CommerceXpert is now a formidable offering; distribution via Netscape/GEIS gives market leadership potential
- Netscape+GEIS channel combination creates distribution advantage competitors cannot easily replicate
- Netscape acquired GEIS share in Actra for $56M stock (Nov 1997); Actra absorbed into Netscape; products rebranded Netscape CommerceXpert
- Netscape acquired by AOL for $4.2B in 1998; Netscape brand discontinued 2008
- 4 of 5 planned products shipped to Beta/GA within 5-month window (Apr to Nov 1997)
- $56 million in Netscape stock (1.7M shares at ~$33/share)
