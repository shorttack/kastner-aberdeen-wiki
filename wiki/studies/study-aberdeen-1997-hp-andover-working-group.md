# HP's Andover Working Group Makes Significant Headway in Establishing Interoperability in Healthcare Computing

> This April 1997 Aberdeen Group announcement profile assesses HP's Andover Working Group for Healthcare Interoperability, an initiative HP spearheaded in March 1996. Aberdeen documents the group's first-year achievements: alpha HL7 Enterprise Communication Framework (ECF) software delivered to 18 core members, membership growth from 12 to 200+, and new standards initiatives for DICOM and IEEE MIB in 1997. The report positions HP as a uniquely capable total healthcare solutions provider combining medical devices, enterprise computing, and standards leadership — and predicts the ECF will achieve commercial availability by end-1997 with full embedding in healthcare applications by 1998.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- HP Medical Products Group sold to Philips Electronics for ~$1.7B in 2001; ended HP's healthcare device leadership `[ps=5]`
- Encapsulates HL7 standard in Enterprise Communicator software objects; eliminates need for app developers to parse raw HL7 `[ps=4]`
- 1997 target: incorporate DICOM imaging standard into ECF; simultaneous HL7+DICOM adds highly desirable EPR dimension `[ps=4]`
- Industry support and actual standardization development efforts rarely aligned so succinctly in traditionally fragmented healthcare market `[ps=4]`
- Computing Technologies & Professional Services — reliable scalable computing, open systems, Internet/intranet, HP OpenView, enterprise architecture consulting `[ps=3]`
- AWG Enterprise Communicators will bring application interoperability closer to reality than ever before `[ps=3]`
- ECF adopted by HL7 for Version 3.0 standard; published in IEEE Transactions on IT in Biomedicine (1998); used in PACS/RIS interoperability `[ps=3]`
- Transcends common interoperability interests; two highly influential groups consolidated vision and technical specs for standardization `[ps=2]`
- HP in enviable position; care providers increasingly want total solution partner with individual products plus lead supplier for implementation and support `[ps=2]`
- HP will continue as systems-supplier-of-choice in healthcare; AWG will continue gaining momentum `[ps=2]`
- ECF commercially available by end-1997; fully embedded in leading healthcare IS supplier applications by 1998 `[ps=1]`
- 18 core members `[ps=0]`
- 12 core members, 18 supporting `[ps=0]`
- 1997 target: incorporate IEEE MIB for medical device connectivity into ECF `[ps=0]`
- Clinical Information Management Solutions — point-of-care for critical care, cardiology, surgery, perinatal, lab `[ps=0]`

- Alpha software distributed to core members August 1996; proof-of-concept demo October 1996
