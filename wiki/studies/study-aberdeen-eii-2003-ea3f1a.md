# Enterprise Information Integration: The New Way to Leverage E-information (Second Edition)

> 74-page Aberdeen Group second edition market report on Enterprise Information Integration (EII), the predecessor to SOA/ESB thinking. Author: Wayne Kernochan, Managing VP Platform Infrastructure. EII market sized at under $200 million, projected 80% growth in 2003. IBM leads with $60M EII revenue estimate; BEA $25M; Business Objects $15M. Report introduces Aberdeen EII Technology Segmentation Model covering EAI, database, XML messaging, and EII solution categories. Taxonomy of information aggregation tools: data migration, replication/ETL, data warehouses, operational data stores, EII, federated databases. MetaMatrix featured as case study supplier. SAP OEM deal with MetaMatrix for NetWeaver noted. Related markets: EAI $2.2B→$3.0B, content management $1.2B→$2.0B, portal $1.5B→$2.8B (2002-2004).


_Published 2003, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- EII market less than $200 million; 80% growth projected for 2003
- IBM EII revenue estimate: $60 million (2003 projected)
- BEA EII revenue estimate: $25 million (2003 projected)
- Business Objects EII revenue estimate: $15 million (2003 projected)
- MetaMatrix EII revenue estimate: $10 million (2003 projected)
- Venetica EII revenue estimate: $7-10 million (2003 projected)
- Attunity, XAware, Nimble Technologies: each $4-7 million EII revenue estimate (2003 projected)
- EAI market: $2.2B (2002), $2.6B (2003 projected), $3.0B (2004 projected)
- Content management market: $1.2B (2002), $1.6B (2003 projected), $2.0B (2004 projected)
- Portal market: $1.5B (2002), $2.2B (2003 projected), $2.8B (2004 projected)
- EII projected to grow to $250M+ market within 2 years if major hardware/database suppliers successfully enter market
- EII to become key technology in $7.5 billion market as incorporated in portal, EAI, BI, and BPI solutions
- Database administration costs now dominate TCO of applications below 500-user level and continue to increase in importance for all sizes
- Slight web site disadvantage can cause 10-20% of installed base to walk away even in early e-commerce era
- For every $1 spent on EII software licenses, $1.25 to $1.50 spent on professional services for implementation
- MetaMatrix is Cadillac of EII solutions — comprehensive features, developer/admin support, sophisticated cross-database application development
- MetaMatrix announced OEM agreement with SAP to provide MetaMatrix Server embedded in SAP NetWeaver (Enterprise Services Architecture)
- EII is now a market separate from EAI and ETL; distinction driven by need to integrate information across enterprise as strategic infrastructure decision
- Business benefit of EII increases exponentially with successive integration of each related application and database
- Aberdeen expects all EII suppliers to wrap transactions in XML within next 2 years (by 2005)
- Most M&A activity over next 2 years will center on larger infrastructure solution providers acquiring newcomer EII suppliers
- IBM and BEA quickest to respond to EII opportunity; Oracle and Microsoft have not shown full appreciation of EII potential
- EII solutions provide specific value-add to enterprise portal upgrades by enabling cross-database data and transactions, simplifying developer job
- Web-servicized EII solution can provide easier access by other Web services to local, remote, and cross-company data
- Distinct EII market exists, beginning 'high-end user per-project' stage; driven by need to rationalize multiple database access to cut admin costs and leverage cross-database proprietary information
