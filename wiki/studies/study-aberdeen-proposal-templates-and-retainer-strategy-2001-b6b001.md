> Four Aberdeen Group internal artifacts comprising the contractual and strategic backbone of the 2001 Advisory Access Service rollout. Includes three boilerplate sales proposal templates — the Aberdeen Profile ($25,000 research report), the AAS Starter (six-month $6,000 introductory retainer with designated analyst, briefings, half-day consulting, and portal access), and the Executive White Paper ($35,000 in-depth positioning study) — plus Peter Kastner's confidential 'Retainer Business' draft slide deck proposing the Aberdeen Advisory Access Service (AAAS) as a $500/month entry retainer, explicitly diagnosing retainer sales objections and positioning AAAS as an added-revenue kicker following an initial Profile or workshop engagement rather than a standalone cold-call product.


_Published 2001, author **Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Designated relationship-manager analyst; briefings; telephone/email access; PR/press support; trade-show access; Aberdeen.com research portal; half-day consulting time; preferential consulting rates `[ps=4]`
- Ad hoc telephone/email access by two designated client contacts; for 'what about?' questions on market conditions, competitors, strategy `[ps=4]`
- Foster long-term trusted advisory relationship; avoid single-engagement clients (too costly cost-of-sales); avoid 'write a Profile' as only briefing solution; create entry vehicle for ongoing consulting `[ps=4]`
- Analyst trust required before sale closes; cost/value perceived low early in relationship; long-term services vs. short-term budget mismatch; spotty execution quality leads to spotty renewals `[ps=4]`
- Explicit: 'I don't know or trust you guys enough to commit to a one-year retainer'; not enough services to justify $6,000 cold; requires prior engagement to establish trust `[ps=4]`
- Standalone (low-cost advisory door-opener); bundled (incremental continuity after one-off engagement); add-on to task-specific proposals `[ps=4]`
- Entry retainer described as 'basis for many other bundled service offerings'; creates foundation for up-sell to analyst days and larger engagements `[ps=4]`
- 6-8 page document printed on Aberdeen Profile paper stock; delivered electronically as HTML/PDF `[ps=3]`
- Non-exclusive 1-year license commencing on final HTML/PDF delivery date; intranet or WWW posting permitted `[ps=3]`
- Services base without analyst-day add-ons; $500/month billable quarterly ($6,000 annual); low-cost entree to Aberdeen 'club' `[ps=3]`
- Ad hoc same-business-day access for 'What if / Why / Do you think' questions on market dynamics, competitive moves, M&A impact, position or message ideas `[ps=3]`
- Becomes an added-revenue kicker component after Aberdeen has done other work for client; follow-on to Profile project; bundled with consulting workshop, publication, or content subscription `[ps=3]`
- $25,000 ($20,000 research + $5,000 publication) `[ps=0]`
- 500-999: $5.00/copy; 1,000-2,499: $3.25; 2,500-4,999: $2.25; 5,000-7,499: $1.50; 7,500-9,999: $1.25; 10,000+: $1.10 `[ps=0]`
- 50% invoiced on Research Meeting completion; 50% on draft delivery; net 30 days `[ps=0]`
- Contract Execution: 10%; Research Meeting completion: 50%; First draft: 90%; Final delivery: 100% `[ps=0]`
- 10 business days written notice to Aberdeen project manager + copy to CFO via Certified Mail or FedEx `[ps=0]`
- 2-4 client customers interviewed by phone; Aberdeen reserves right to identify additional independent customers `[ps=0]`
- Massachusetts governing law; arbitration by three arbitrators under American Arbitration Association rules; 2-year confidentiality period `[ps=0]`
- 30 days; signed letter or verbal-then-written purchase order accepted as authorization `[ps=0]`
- US$6,000 for six-month introductory Advisory Access Service; invoiced at beginning of service period; net 30 days `[ps=0]`
- Renews at no additional charge for active clients; active defined as half-day or more consulting/projects per quarter `[ps=0]`
- $35,000 ($30,000 research + $5,000 web rights) `[ps=0]`
- 5-16 page document on Aberdeen paper stock; HTML/PDF format; 500 reprints included in Research Fee `[ps=0]`
- $17,500 on first Research Meeting; $17,500 on first draft delivery; net 30 days `[ps=0]`
