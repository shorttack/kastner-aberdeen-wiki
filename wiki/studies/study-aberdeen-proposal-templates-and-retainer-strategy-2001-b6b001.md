# Aberdeen Proposal Templates (Profile, AAS-Starter, Executive White Paper) and Retainer Business Strategy Deck (PSK Draft, 2001)

> Four Aberdeen Group internal artifacts comprising the contractual and strategic backbone of the 2001 Advisory Access Service rollout. Includes three boilerplate sales proposal templates — the Aberdeen Profile ($25,000 research report), the AAS Starter (six-month $6,000 introductory retainer with designated analyst, briefings, half-day consulting, and portal access), and the Executive White Paper ($35,000 in-depth positioning study) — plus Peter Kastner's confidential 'Retainer Business' draft slide deck proposing the Aberdeen Advisory Access Service (AAAS) as a $500/month entry retainer, explicitly diagnosing retainer sales objections and positioning AAAS as an added-revenue kicker following an initial Profile or workshop engagement rather than a standalone cold-call product.


_Published 2001, author **Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- $25,000 ($20,000 research + $5,000 publication)
- 6-8 page document printed on Aberdeen Profile paper stock; delivered electronically as HTML/PDF
- 500-999: $5.00/copy; 1,000-2,499: $3.25; 2,500-4,999: $2.25; 5,000-7,499: $1.50; 7,500-9,999: $1.25; 10,000+: $1.10
- 50% invoiced on Research Meeting completion; 50% on draft delivery; net 30 days
- Contract Execution: 10%; Research Meeting completion: 50%; First draft: 90%; Final delivery: 100%
- 10 business days written notice to Aberdeen project manager + copy to CFO via Certified Mail or FedEx
- 2-4 client customers interviewed by phone; Aberdeen reserves right to identify additional independent customers
- Non-exclusive 1-year license commencing on final HTML/PDF delivery date; intranet or WWW posting permitted
- Massachusetts governing law; arbitration by three arbitrators under American Arbitration Association rules; 2-year confidentiality period
- 30 days; signed letter or verbal-then-written purchase order accepted as authorization
- US$6,000 for six-month introductory Advisory Access Service; invoiced at beginning of service period; net 30 days
- Designated relationship-manager analyst; briefings; telephone/email access; PR/press support; trade-show access; Aberdeen.com research portal; half-day consulting time; preferential consulting rates
- Renews at no additional charge for active clients; active defined as half-day or more consulting/projects per quarter
- Ad hoc telephone/email access by two designated client contacts; for 'what about?' questions on market conditions, competitors, strategy
- $35,000 ($30,000 research + $5,000 web rights)
- 5-16 page document on Aberdeen paper stock; HTML/PDF format; 500 reprints included in Research Fee
- $17,500 on first Research Meeting; $17,500 on first draft delivery; net 30 days
- Validate client's positioning thesis through customer interviews and independent analysis; test and validate marketing/technical proposition believed to establish competitive advantage
- Massachusetts governing law; three-arbitrator AAA panel; 2-year confidentiality; IP remains Aberdeen property except web rights
- PSK draft -- Aberdeen Confidential; 8 slides; confidential internal strategy document
- Foster long-term trusted advisory relationship; avoid single-engagement clients (too costly cost-of-sales); avoid 'write a Profile' as only briefing solution; create entry vehicle for ongoing consulting
- $17,500 two-day retainer; $11,000 one-day emerging company starter; eBusiness@Aberdeen Alschuler surveys; Hurley security retainers
- Analyst trust required before sale closes; cost/value perceived low early in relationship; long-term services vs. short-term budget mismatch; spotty execution quality leads to spotty renewals
- Services base without analyst-day add-ons; $500/month billable quarterly ($6,000 annual); low-cost entree to Aberdeen 'club'
- 1:1 assigned analyst access; quarterly telephone briefings; ad hoc telephone consultation; press/investor quotes; annual briefing review; pod publications online access; preferred per-diem rates; ETI/marketing newsletter
