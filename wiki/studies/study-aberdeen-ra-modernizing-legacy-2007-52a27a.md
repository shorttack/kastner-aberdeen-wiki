# Modernizing Legacy Applications: Maximizing the Investment

> Aberdeen Group benchmark report by Perry Donham on enterprise approaches to legacy application modernization. Identifies best-practice patterns for maximizing the investment in legacy systems through modernization rather than replacement, framing the approach within Aberdeen's PACE methodology.


_Published 2007, author **Perry Donham**, type **benchmark-report**._


## Top observations

- Best-in-Class enterprises maximize legacy-system investment through modernization rather than rip-and-replace
- Mid-2000s preferred modernization approach is wrapping legacy systems with SOA/web-service endpoints to enable reuse without replacement
- Aberdeen PACE framework applied to legacy-modernization Pressures, Actions, Capabilities, and Enablers
- Author: Perry Donham, Director, Enterprise Integration Research (perry.donham@aberdeen.com)
