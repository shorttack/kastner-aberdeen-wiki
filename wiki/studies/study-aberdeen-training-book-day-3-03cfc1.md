# How To Write A Profile and How To Conduct A RAMP

> Aberdeen Group's Day 3 training manual for new analyst hires. Covers how to write Aberdeen Profiles (objective third-party supplier assessments), how to conduct a RAMP (Rapid Accurate Market Positioning) engagement, and how to communicate research results to clients and media. Documents the anatomy of Aberdeen's core deliverables: Profile, RAMP, Impact, Viewpoint, Executive Briefing Paper, and White Paper. Provides interview techniques, writing standards, and client management guidelines. Represents Aberdeen's core methodology documentation circa 2000.


_Published 2000, author **Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Objective third-party review of supplier's go-to-market strategy or major product families
- Rapid Accurate Market Positioning — in-depth primary customer and prospect market research and analysis within short time frame
- Increase comfort with clients; describe research conduct; coach on communicating results; provide starting point for organizing thoughts
- Kickoff > Primary field research (interviews) > Analysis > Deliverable writing > Client presentation
- Users/prospects/customers of client's technology; typically 10-20 interviews
- Executive summary; company overview; product/service review; market positioning; Aberdeen recommendation
- Positive, actionable direction; avoid belittling; translate technology into business benefit language
- One-on-one analyst-client relationship; analyst as trusted advisor
- $20,000 - $50,000 typical
- Open-ended questions; active listening; hypothesis testing; avoid leading questions
- Analyst synopsis of how supplier announcement/product intro affects market and supplier future prospects
- In-depth report on emerging technologies or common end-user problems; educates market on supplier product
- Analyst draft > Technical edit > Client fact-check > Aberdeen approval > Publication
- Technology understanding; business analysis; writing; interviewing; client communication; market context
- Research report; presentation deck; executive briefing; recommended action plan
- Provide objective quotes; background briefings; do not reveal client confidences; maintain analytical independence
- Analysts are primary business developers; relationships lead to consulting revenue
- Monitor Gartner, IDC, Forrester, Meta for conflicting coverage; counter with own analysis
- Factually accurate; technology placed in business context; recommendations actionable; writing clear and concise
- Aberdeen analyst perspective/opinion publication; shorter than profile; focused on single trend or issue
- Day 1 (Aberdeen overview, market context); Day 3 (Profile writing, RAMP methodology)
- Maintain analytical independence; client cannot dictate conclusions; may review for factual accuracy only
- Typically 4-8 weeks from kickoff to deliverable
- Structured agenda; send pre-read; follow up with written summary; document key decisions
- Supplements primary research; never substitutes; used for market context and background
