# Design-to-Procure: Looking at the Whole Value Chain

> This 2004 research agenda outlines Aberdeen's planned Design-to-Procure studies on total cost management across sourcing, procurement, design, and supply chain operations. Planned Q3 and Q4 2004 topics include contract labor management, e-sourcing best practices, spend analytics, category spend management (advertising, MRO, packaging), design for compliance, and outsourced manufacturing.


_Published 2004, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **research-agenda**._


## Top observations

- It is commonly known that more than 70% of the cost of product is locked in after the initial design stage. `[ps=4]`
- However, the successful use of contract manufacturing requires new strategies and supporting solutions to align with best in class competencies and take advantage of economies of scale while managing an extended the supply chain and controlling of the product quality. `[ps=4]`
- This study will identify best-in-class spending analysis strategies, procedures, and system implementations and profile the 10 best practice initiatives and solution and service deployments in this area. `[ps=0]`
- U.S. businesses spend more than $260 billion on advertising, marketing, and printing services each year. `[ps=0]`
