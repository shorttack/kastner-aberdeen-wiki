# Next-Generation Retail — The Post Wal-Mart World

> This 2004 retail research agenda describes Aberdeen's planned studies on retail strategy after Wal-Mart's market dominance, covering proactive merchandising, retailer-supplier collaboration, next-generation strategic sourcing, the empowered store, and integrated multichannel selling. It outlines forthcoming research themes rather than presenting results.


_Published 1988, author **Stan Elbaum / Aberdeen Group**, type **research-agenda**._


## Top observations

- The merchandising infrastructure of the 21st century is enabled by best-in-class applications that combine predictive forecasting and advanced analytics. `[ps=5]`
- **Practical Collaboration —** Retailers and their suppliers digging out from the effects of purchase order errors waste a well-documented $40 billion. `[ps=3]`
