# Letter to Mark Weber (Gibson Dunn) re: AMD v Intel — Offering Expert Witness Services

> One-page cover letter from Peter S. Kastner (Scott-Page Associates, Westport MA) to Mark Weber, Esq. of Gibson Dunn & Crutcher in Los Angeles, following up with John Casey of Intel regarding the AMD v Intel antitrust case. Encloses a 2003 Internetweek column based on Kastner's survey of 250 companies on PC replacement buying plans, and a copy of the 1999 Intelligent Electronics v Andersen Consulting expert witness report as demonstration of his analytical style. Kastner endorses Intel's public 'affirmative defense' stance and offers services.


_Published 2007, author **Peter S. Kastner (Scott-Page Associates)**, type **memoir**._


## Top observations

- Context: follow-up to a prior-month meeting between Kastner, Mark Weber (Gibson Dunn), and John Casey (Intel) regarding the AMD v Intel antitrust case.
- Enclosure 1: Internetweek column reporting a 2003 survey by Kastner of 250 companies on PC replacement buying plans (column no longer on the web at time of letter).
- Enclosure 2: copy of Kastner's June 1999 expert witness report in Intelligent Electronics v Andersen Consulting, cited as demonstration of his analytical style for complex technology litigation.
- Kastner's self-described analytical style: 'what is the impact of technology on business and how do business people deal with deploying technology for business advantage.'
- Kastner, having read many of the public documents in the case, stated he 'can agree with Intel's publicly stated conclusion that it has an affirmative defense' in AMD v Intel.
- Kastner offered his services to Gibson Dunn's AMD v Intel team, stating he has 'the experience to add value.'
