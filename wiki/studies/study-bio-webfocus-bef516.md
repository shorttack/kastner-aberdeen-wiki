# Peter Kastner - Aberdeen Group Biography (~2001)

> This professional biography summarizes Peter Kastner's role as Chief Research Officer at Aberdeen Group circa 2001, describing his responsibilities for research content and consulting services spanning OLTP, decision support, client/server, e-commerce, and the newly launched Personal IT practice. It documents his 30-plus years of IT experience spanning user and supplier organizations, including prior executive roles at Digital Equipment, Prime Computer, and Stratus Computer, and over a decade as a consultant and program manager at Arthur D.
