## Top observations

- Survey respondents acknowledged their own efforts, but clearly understood that there was much more compliance work to be done (Figure 3). `[ps=4]`
- While fear (of penalties, fines, bad PR, etc.) has been the primary catalyst in funding and driving most compliance initiatives, Best in Class enterprises are ahead of the curve, using these initiatives to drive process improvement and supply risk mitigation strategies `[ps=4]`
- The two main motivators behind compliance initiatives are **fear** and **cost savings** (Figure 7). `[ps=4]`
- As Figure 1 shows, nearly 50% of enterprises are “clueless” as to the number of non-compliant events they have recently experienced. `[ps=3]`
- Not surprisingly, most enterprises have a compliance initiative underway (74%), but these are largely manual (73%, Figure 8). `[ps=3]`
