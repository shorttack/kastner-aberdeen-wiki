## Top observations

- In today’s environment, even Best in Class organizations have room for improvement. `[ps=5]`
- As Figure 1 shows, nearly 50% of enterprises are “clueless” as to the number of non-compliant events they have recently experienced. `[ps=4]`
- Survey respondents acknowledged their own efforts, but clearly understood that there was much more compliance work to be done (Figure 3). `[ps=4]`
- Compliance alone is the second-ranked issue driving source-to-settle technology adoption among survey participants (Figure 4). `[ps=4]`
- While fear (of penalties, fines, bad PR, etc.) has been the primary catalyst in funding and driving most compliance initiatives, Best in Class enterprises are ahead of the curve, using these initiatives to drive process improvement and supply risk mitigation strategies `[ps=4]`
- Figure 5 shows that, on average, over 50% of organizations do not know how many non-compliant events they have experienced. `[ps=4]`
- The two main motivators behind compliance initiatives are **fear** and **cost savings** (Figure 7). `[ps=4]`
- Not surprisingly, most enterprises have a compliance initiative underway (74%), but these are largely manual (73%, Figure 8). `[ps=4]`
- Our research showed that of the enterprises that measure contract performance, just over 50% use regularly scheduled audits to check purchase orders or invoices against contracts (Figure 9). `[ps=4]`
