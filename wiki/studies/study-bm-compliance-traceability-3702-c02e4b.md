## Top observations

- 93% of manufacturers still relying on manual processes to manage compliance and traceability programs were unable to achieve Best in Class status. `[ps=5]`
- To become Best in Class manufacturers should utilize automated solutions to “build in” compliance and traceability to production processes. `[ps=5]`
- 73% of Best in Class manufacturers have integrated compliance and traceability solutions with other enterprise applications. `[ps=5]`
- Best in Class manufacturers are more than 5x’s as likely to measure performance in real-time than other manufacturers. `[ps=5]`
- To become Best in Class manufacturers should measure operational metrics in real time and utilize this information to gauge success. `[ps=5]`
- When asked, 64% of manufacturers surveyed are integrating compliance and traceability programs with mature quality initiatives and 54% of manufacturers surveyed are “building in” compliance and traceability to production processes. `[ps=5]`
- Best in class manufacturers are more than twice as likely as other manufacturers to integrate disparate software solutions. `[ps=5]`
- Best in class manufacturers are 75% more likely to have process visibility and automated traceability than other manufacturers. `[ps=5]`
- Best in class manufacturers are more than five times as likely as other manufacturers to measure compliance and traceability performance in real or near real-time. `[ps=5]`
- Best in class manufacturers are more than twice as likely as other manufacturers to have compliance and traceability centrally managed at the corporate level. `[ps=5]`
