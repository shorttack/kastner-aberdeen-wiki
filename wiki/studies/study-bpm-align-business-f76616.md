# Aligning IT to Business Processes: How BPM is Complementing ERP and Custom Applications

> 29-page Aberdeen Group benchmark report on BPM adoption, ROI, and ERP integration. Based on survey of 125+ enterprises. 51% of respondents say ERP systems don't provide adequate functionality. Only 15% believe applications afford desired flexibility. Over 50% turning to BPM in 2007. BPM investment leads middleware: 57% investing in BPM tools vs 45% ESB/SOA vs 25% MDM. SOA technology and web services cited by 67% as integration glue between BPM and ERP. Large companies 50% more likely to invest in BPM vs SMEs. Underwriters: Appian, CTSpace, Ramco.


_Published 2007, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- 51% of respondents say ERP systems or best-of-breed supply chain solutions don't provide adequate functionality
- Only 15% believe applications afford desired flexibility they need today
- Over 51% employ manual processes to meet customer requirements; 63% use spreadsheets for critical business process functions
- 37% use legacy mainframe applications to manage critical business process functions
- More than 50% of surveyed companies turning to BPM in 2007
- 67% indicate will use SOA technology and web services as glue to tie BPM to ERP and enterprise applications
- 57% of companies investing in BPM tools for creating business workflows (top middleware investment)
- 55% investing in business intelligence or business performance management/monitoring tools
- 45% investing in ESB and SOA middleware software
- 25% investing in Master Data Management (MDM) tools
- 38% of companies have current SOA projects; 29% planning to start within 12 months
- 41% have SOA-based solutions already in place; 27% budgeted to start within 12 months
- Close to 75% of large companies (>$1B revenue) investing in BPM tools; 71% in ESB/SOA middleware
- Small companies (<$50M): 55% BPM, 18% ESB/SOA, 27% EAI, 9% MDM
- More than 2/3 of supply-chain-intensive industries say ERP systems impede visibility due to customized workarounds
- 63% of supply-chain organizations say SOA upfront costs higher due to design/architecture needs vs 56% overall
- 63% of supply-chain companies lack IT governance/process systems to ensure SOA success vs 43% overall
- 63% of supply-chain companies say teams can't adjust to culture change needed for reusable services vs 45% overall
- 54% of Best-in-Class have SOA projects underway vs 33% Industry Average and 32% Laggard
- BPM tool satisfaction: 17% Best-in-Class vs 6% Industry Average vs 3% Laggard
- 62% cite need to support new agile business processes as top SOA driver
- 44% cite ERP system or supply chain solution not providing desired functionality as SOA driver
- 43% cite lower cost integration and application upgrades as SOA driver
- BPM can deliver dramatically lower software maintenance costs due to ease of process changes without massive IT reprogramming
- 63% consider ability to integrate into any system with critical data as very important IT capability
