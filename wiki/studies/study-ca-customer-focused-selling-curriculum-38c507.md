# Customer Focused Selling — Computer Associates Sales Training Curriculum

> Two-day Customer Focused Selling sales-training curriculum for Computer Associates, developed by Aberdeen Group. Day 1 covers behavior styles (DiSC), credibility/trust, communication, questioning technique, and customer-focused selling; Day 2 covers value proposition, price-vs-value, closing, and roleplay. The document is the structured course outline with time allocations per topic. Reflects Aberdeen's sales-force-education practice during the CA retainer era.


_Published 1998, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Intro (1h) + Behavior Styles/DiSC (4h) + Establishing Credibility & Trust (3h).
- Customer-Focused Selling value proposition, price-vs-value framing, qualifying, closing, and roleplay.
- Four behavioral styles (Dominance, Influence, Steadiness, Conscientiousness) taught for sales conversation pacing.
- Four Elements of Trust; pacing techniques for common ground; Personal Listening Profile; Active Listening.
- Aberdeen packaged a structured 2-day curriculum combining behavioral assessment (DiSC) with consultative-selling skills for enterprise-software sales teams.
- Sales-force education seminars were a named Aberdeen Consulting Service line (confirmed in 1999 retainer proposal).
