## Top observations

- With rich performance data, best-in-class companies are able to identify problems and opportunities, cut costs, increase revenue generated by the contact center, and improve overall customer experience and brand awareness. `[ps=5]`
- Regardless of the technology in use, best-in-class companies measure thoroughly and often. `[ps=5]`
- Regardless of the technology in use, the call volume, whether the operators are onpremises or part of a dedicated hosted service provider’s staff, or whether there is a self-service function, best-in-class companies measure thoroughly and often. `[ps=5]`
- Best-in-class contact centers proactively diagnose and predict customer requests. `[ps=5]`
- In each of these categories, survey results show that the firms exhibiting best-in-class contact center characteristics also enjoy best-in-class customer experience and financial performance (Table 2). `[ps=5]`
- Best-in-class analytics software makes recommendations for improvement as well as delivering valuable metrics for measuring the success of the center. `[ps=5]`

- While customer experience and workforce productivity remain a high priority for contact center managers in every class, Aberdeen research shows that best-in-class companies are nine times more likely than the industry norm to be looking to the contact center to improve branding (figure 2). `[ps=4]`
- In follow-up interviews with contact center managers at the best-in-class companies, the technology which they cited as having the greatest impact on delivering a branded message to customers is speech-enablement. `[ps=4]`
- Therefore, in order to advance to the level of a best-in-class organization, one must begin with simpler, more easily measurable applications, get the technology on the way toward ROI, and then find ways to leverage it. `[ps=4]`
- It is not surprising, therefore, to find that 100% of the best-in-class performers are using path analytics to assess the effectiveness of the contact center design (Figure 3). `[ps=4]`

- It should also come as no surprise that 60% of best-in-class companies are recording calls and running those recordings through a speech analysis system to discover where improvements can be made. `[ps=4]`
- However, while 13% of the industry average companies are using voice technology in the self-service contact center, 60% have yet to employ any speech analytics. `[ps=4]`
- Laggard organizations have an opportunity to leapfrog the industry norm into best-in-class status by adopting and applying these two technologies. `[ps=4]`
- Across industries, only 36% of respondents indicated that they solve this problem by hiring a consultant or data integration service provider, preferring to defer the problem to a later time and begin with a simpler set of objectives. `[ps=4]`
