## Top observations

- With rich performance data, best-in-class companies are able to identify problems and opportunities, cut costs, increase revenue generated by the contact center, and improve overall customer experience and brand awareness. `[ps=4]`
- Looking at the best-in-class companies however, one can find that there are many choices when it comes to finding ways to turn the contact center into a profit center beyond simple cost-economy. `[ps=4]`
- While customer experience and workforce productivity remain a high priority for contact center managers in every class, Aberdeen research shows that best-in-class companies are nine times more likely than the industry norm to be looking to the contact center to improve branding (figure 2). `[ps=4]`
- Regardless of the technology in use, best-in-class companies measure thoroughly and often. `[ps=4]`
- Despite this renewed focus, 60% of polled L companies said that it is difficult to determine whether the contact center is achieving its goals because it is difficult to measure performance. `[ps=4]`
- It should also come as no surprise that 60% of best-in-class companies are recording calls and running those recordings through a speech analysis system to discover where improvements can be made. `[ps=4]`
- Laggard organizations have an opportunity to leapfrog the industry norm into best-in-class status by adopting and applying these two technologies. `[ps=4]`
- Regardless of the technology in use, the call volume, whether the operators are onpremises or part of a dedicated hosted service provider’s staff, or whether there is a self-service function, best-in-class companies measure thoroughly and often. `[ps=4]`
- Best-in-class contact centers proactively diagnose and predict customer requests. `[ps=4]`
- • Constant analytical measures allow best-in-class companies to innovate in the selfservice system continuously and deliver targeted training to the live operators. `[ps=4]`
- In each of these categories, survey results show that the firms exhibiting best-in-class contact center characteristics also enjoy best-in-class customer experience and financial performance (Table 2). `[ps=4]`

- The incremental gains of tuning a system to complete a call quickly are negligible compared with adding real value to the customer contact session (Figure 5). `[ps=4]`
- Across all polled industry categories, cross-channel sales analysis is the leading solution investment category (Figure 6), with telecom and high technology companies on top (64% have planned investments). `[ps=4]`
- Best-in-class companies monitor all calls and review them to find areas of confusion, redundant or inefficient routing through the system, and requests which are consistently misunderstood by the system. `[ps=4]`
- Best-in-class analytics software makes recommendations for improvement as well as delivering valuable metrics for measuring the success of the center. `[ps=4]`
- Used correctly, Speech-enablement has the ability to help move a laggard’s contact center into the best-in-class category. `[ps=3]`
- With the basic problems of cost optimization solved, best-in-class companies are addressing subtler and more complex issues in the contact center. `[ps=3]`
- It is not surprising, therefore, to find that 100% of the best-in-class performers are using path analytics to assess the effectiveness of the contact center design (Figure 3). `[ps=3]`
- In follow-up interviews with contact center managers at the best-in-class companies, the technology which they cited as having the greatest impact on delivering a branded message to customers is speech-enablement. `[ps=2]`
- In best-in-class contact centers, speech self-service delivers a branded customer experience and offers all of the options available to the caller through other channels. `[ps=2]`
