## Top observations

- In any enterprise reducing the sales cycle is critical and through our research we found that an average of 18% of an enterprise’s sales cycle is attributed to contract creation, negotiation and approval. `[ps=4]`
- An recent Aberdeen study found that on average **9% of revenues** are lost due to regulatory penalties, missed deadlines, lost sales, inappropriate payment terms, “maverick” pricing and transactional errors. `[ps=3]`
- When compared to Best in Class enterprises, Upside customers performed slightly better in the reduction of their Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) which is certainly one of the most valuable attributes of contract management. `[ps=2]`
- Digging into this further, we concluded that a one day reduction in the sales cycle is worth approximately **$80,000** on average **.** Of course this number varies across industries but it does demonstrate the magnitude of savings that can be achieved. `[ps=0]`
- For example, survey respondents that reported using **Oracle** (>30 enterprises) showed on average 72% compliance to service agreements. `[ps=0]`
- Best in Class companies may include customers of these solution providers. `[ps=0]`
