## Top observations

- One of the major challenges reported by 68% of survey respondents is **the disconnect between the various processes on the sell-side,** i.e., the quote-to-cash cycle. `[ps=5]`
- **Best in Class** enterprises have shown improved performance in terms of process efficiency and well as substantial financial results. `[ps=5]`
- We also found that Best in Class enterprises have a higher percentage (82% vs. 33%) of contracts stored in an electronic/searchable repository with approximately 17% of contracts having nonstandard language compared to the 35% at all other enterprises. `[ps=5]`
- When asked how critical contract management is in the entire quote-to-cash cycle, over 50% said that _is_ essential, making it clear that this is a critical component of managing customers and revenue (Figure 2). `[ps=5]`
- One of the major challenges reported by 67% of survey respondents is **the disconnect between the various processes on the sell-side** (i.e., the quote-to-cash cycle). `[ps=5]`
- In many cases contract management roles and processes are going to involve multiple parties, as shown below (Figure 7). `[ps=5]`
- • Best in Class enterprises outperform their peers in the following: � Percentage of contracts actively tracked through a system (post-execution) � Percentage of contracts stored in a searchable repository � Reduction in Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) `[ps=5]`
- Fifty-six percent of the Best in Class measure the percentage of contracts actively being tracked through a system after execution, whereas only 27% of all other enterprises do the same. `[ps=5]`
- Aberdeen research shows that, on average, revenue leakage amounts to 9% of an enterprise’s revenues. `[ps=4]`
- The percentage of revenues managed by a contract is expected to increase by on average 21% over the next two years. `[ps=4]`
- Our research has shown that, on average, 18% of the sales cycle is attributed to creating, negotiating and approving contracts. `[ps=4]`
- An average of 9% of revenues are being leaked away, this is definitely an issue that deserves significant attention. `[ps=4]`
- In Figure 6, we can see that 48% of Best in Class enterprises have formal organizations specifically for the management of sales contracts either throughout the company or within various business units or regions. `[ps=4]`
- If we consider the automation of contract lifecycle management, on average, just over half of the survey respondents use a manual process (Figure 8). `[ps=4]`
- Of the Best in Class enterprises, however, 30% use an automated process. `[ps=4]`
- Analysis and reporting on various contract data and the way it is used is also a key area for improvement (Figure 9). `[ps=4]`
- Revenue leakage, on average, amounts to 9% of total revenue. `[ps=4]`
- With regards to revenue leakage, survey respondents reported that, on average, their enterprises lose 9% of their revenue due to regulatory penalties, missed deadlines, lost sales, maverick pricing and transactional errors, clearly a substantial amount. `[ps=4]`
- Additionally, in any enterprise the reduction of sales cycles is critical and through our research we have found that, on average, 18% of an enterprise’s sales cycle is attributed to contract creation, negotiation and approval, which if reduced can have a significant impact. `[ps=4]`
- Exploring this further, we concluded that on average a one day reduction in the sales cycle is worth approximately **$80,000** . `[ps=4]`
- For the larger enterprises this is worth over $215,000; in several cases, this number was much larger and the fact is that Best in Class companies have cycle times that are 10 to 15 days shorter than other companies, showing that there is a fairly significant opportunity. `[ps=4]`
- “Our sales cycle is approximately 45 days; a one day reduction is worth well over $250,000” - VP of Finance, Large Brand Name Electronics Manufacturer `[ps=4]`
- Interestingly, if we look at Best in Class companies, 48% reported that they are currently using a contract management solution, whereas only 15% of the remainder are using such a solution. `[ps=4]`
