# About Aberdeen Weekly PC Deals

> Program documentation for Aberdeen Group's Weekly PC Deals Section. Describes methodology for tracking online retailers' lowest prices on name-brand PCs from Compaq, Dell, eMachines, Gateway, HP, and Sony — manufacturers covering over 60% of U.S. PC market share. Defines nine price-point targets ($550–$1,550+) and weekly winner selection criteria: processor speed, memory, hard drive, optical drives, and bundled components. Aberdeen factors in shipping and mail-in rebates; may note in-store-only deals. The first bold row in each price band is the weekly winner.


_Published 2002, author **Caroline S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Compaq, Dell, eMachines, Gateway, HP, Sony cover over 60% of US PC market share
- $550, $750, $900, $1000, $1100, $1200, $1350, $1550, $1550+
- approximately $1,700
- processor speed, memory size and bandwidth, hard drive speed and capacity, DVD/CD optical drives, monitor bundling
- Bundled PC (with monitor+printer) evaluated separately from unbundled PC with faster processor/more memory
- PC prices do not always change week to week; best deal may remain on Weekly Deals Section for multiple weeks
- Listed prices factor in retailers shipping charges and rebates including mail-in rebates
- Weekly analysis discusses criteria making winners the best deals; current PC market trends and component prices are considered
