# About Aberdeen Weekly PC Deals

> Program documentation for Aberdeen Group's Weekly PC Deals Section, describing methodology for tracking online retailers' lowest prices on name-brand PCs from Compaq, Dell, eMachines, Gateway, HP, and Sony. Defines nine price-point targets from $550 to $1,550+ reflecting the mass-market U.S. consumer PC segment, with weekly winner selection based on processor speed, memory, hard drive, optical drives, and bundled components.


_Published 2002, author **Caroline S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- One-stop place to find the best deal on a name-brand PC
- Compaq, Dell, eMachines, Gateway, HP, Sony
- over 60%
- $550, $750, $900, $1,000, $1,100, $1,200, $1,350, $1,550, $1,550+
- not above ~$1,700
- processor speed, memory size and bandwidth, hard drive speed and capacity (primary)
- Bundled PC with monitor and printer judged best for first-time buyers or students
- Updated weekly
- Factor in shipping charges, rebates, and mail-in rebates
- Best deal may remain on list multiple weeks
- 'This Week's Analysis of PC Deals' commentary
- Online retailer links; store locator for in-store deals
- Caroline Kastner ran Aberdeen's retail PC tracking research
