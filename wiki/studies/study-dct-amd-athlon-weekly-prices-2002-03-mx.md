# AMD Athlon XP Weekly CPU Pricing, Jul 2002 – 2003

> Weekly tracking of AMD Athlon XP 1800+, 2000+, 2100+, 2200+, 2400+, and 2600+ channel pricing across ~40 selling dates from July 2002 through early April 2003. Captures AMD's competitive pricing posture against the Intel Pentium 4 in the consumer/channel CPU market during the peak of the PC price war. Source: AMD Price Data.xls, sheet AMD (43 rows x 7 cols).


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._
