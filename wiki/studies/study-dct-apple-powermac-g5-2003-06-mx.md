# Apple Ships Hot PowerMac G5

> Hot Topic analysis of Apple's Power Mac G5 launch. Single-processor models began shipping; dual-processor version weeks behind. Using SPEC CPU 2000 benchmarks, 2.0 GHz PowerPC G5 beats Intel Xeon and Pentium 4 in floating point but loses integer; dual-G5 wins both. G5 is 64-bit (PowerPC 970) supporting up to 8 GB DDR-400 memory. Configured $3,998 dual-2GHz system with ATI 9800 Pro, 160 GB SATA, SuperDrive, Apple 17in Studio Display. Aberdeen concludes G5 is legitimate workstation competitor renovating Apple base rather than attacking PC market.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- G5 renovates Apple base rather than serving as attack product against PC world `[ps=4]`
- Enterprise Mac users should benchmark G5 with work-related files; productivity improvement expected even without 64-bit-tuned apps `[ps=4]`
- G5 will renovate existing Apple enterprise customer base; not a mass-market PC attack vehicle `[ps=4]`
