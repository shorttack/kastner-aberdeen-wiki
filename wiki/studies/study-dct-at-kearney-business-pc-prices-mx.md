# A.T. Kearney Benchmark: Flagship Business Desktop Price Tracking, Aug 2002 – Mar 2003

> Six-date longitudinal price benchmark (2002-08-20, 2002-10-02, 2002-11-08, 2002-12-02, 2002-12-17, 2003-03-10) tracking five flagship business desktop configurations on a standardized spec (2.4 GHz P4, 256 MB DDR, 40 GB 7200 RPM HDD, CD-ROM, no monitor, Windows XP Pro, 3-year NBD warranty, Office XP, AV). Models: Dell Optiplex GX260, Gateway E-4000 Special Deluxe, HP Compaq Evo D510 minitower SB, HP Compaq Evo D510 minitower (standard), IBM NetVista M42 8303. Built for A.T. Kearney consulting engagement. Monthly delta: Dell +$40 (+3%), Gateway +$350 (+35%), HP SB +$333, IBM -$20, HP std +$196.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._
