# Business PC Deals: November 2002

> Inaugural issue of Aberdeen's Business PC Deals series (November 6, 2002). Introduces Aberdeen corporate desktop spec: 2.0 GHz P4 minimum (AMD Athlon 2000+ considered equivalent), 256 MB DDR, 40 GB 7200 RPM HDD, CD-ROM, Windows XP Pro SP1, 3-year NBD warranty, Office XP SBE, AV. Recommends small form factor; argues big-box tower days are over. Prices Dell Optiplex GX60 $1,141; Gateway E4000 Deluxe $1,184; HP Compaq Evo D310 $977; IBM NetVista M42 8305 $1,245 (small business, November 6). Large enterprise prices: Dell Optiplex 260 $875, HP Evo D510 $964 (up to 50,000 units). Microsoft Office XP volume $234 (Standard upgrade) to $465 (new).


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._

- Small desktop form factor recommended; days of big-box tower are over; SFF saves desk space
- 256 MB DDR minimum for Windows XP Pro; at least 10% throughput improvement vs 128 MB
- 7200 RPM; 40 GB priced for consistency; 20 GB adequate for most users
- Dell including gigabit Ethernet on desktops and servers, betting on GigE changeover over next several years
- $234 (Office XP Standard upgrade) to $465 (new license); XP Pro ~$100 more per seat
- 17in CRT best real estate per dollar; 15in LCD equivalent to 16in CRT; flat panel more restful on eyes
- Apple Mac OS X viable but PC Deals follows mainstream PCs; Linux not ready for mass corporate fat-client deployment
