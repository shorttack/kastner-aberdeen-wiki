# Business PC Deals: December 17, 2002

> Desktop Currents December 2002 edition. Key development: Dell, HP, and IBM added 2.4 GHz Pentium 4 to their single-price lower P4 tier — effectively a free processor upgrade. Aberdeen upgrades reference spec from 2.0 to 2.4 GHz (533 MHz FSB vs 400 MHz). New BIOS image required for 533 MHz machines. Tracks Dell GX260, Gateway E-4000 Special Deluxe, HP Compaq Evo D510SB, IBM NetVista M42 8303. Tower prices Dec 17: Dell $1,089 (lowest, $115 below Gateway); SFF comparison shows Dell SX260 costs $300 more than DX260 and uses 5,400 RPM notebook HDD vs competition's 7,200 RPM desktop drives — up to 10% slower in high-I/O. In SFF: Gateway $1,204 price leader, IBM +$10, Compaq +$54; Dell at $49–$185 disadvantage.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._
