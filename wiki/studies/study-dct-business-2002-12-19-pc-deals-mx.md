# Business PC Deals: December 19, 2002

> Desktop Currents January 2003 edition (dated December 19, 2002). Contains same 2.4 GHz P4 upgrade narrative as Dec 17 issue plus updated December 19/20 pricing. Dec 19 tower prices: Dell GX260 $1,089; Gateway E-4000 $1,204; HP Compaq Evo D510SB $1,286; IBM NetVista M42 $1,288. SFF prices same as Dec 17. Large enterprise prices from October: Dell $875, HP $964 (50,000 units). Adds explicit LCD flat panel trend prediction: LCD flat panels will overtake CRTs in 2003. Adds Symantec AV note: Dell shipping client license for Symantec enterprise server-based AV. Volume Office pricing $234 (Standard upgrade) to $465 (new); XP Pro ~$100 more. Microsoft profits built on Office business sales.
