# Business PC Deals: December 2002

> Business PC Deals December 2, 2002 edition. Reference spec still at 2.0 GHz P4. Prices from November 6 via small business portals: Dell Optiplex GX260 $1,079; Gateway E-4000 Special Deluxe $1,004; HP Compaq Evo D510 minitower $1,193; IBM NetVista M42 8303-21 $1,124. Month-over-month changes: Dell -$131, Gateway -$180, HP +$216, IBM -$121. HP D510 price elevated because new HP site bundles 15in LCD display with the D510 making it very competitive only if display is needed. Large enterprise prices: Dell $875, HP $964 (up to 50,000). Microsoft Office XP volume pricing: $234-$465; businesses >500 employees ineligible for SBE.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._
