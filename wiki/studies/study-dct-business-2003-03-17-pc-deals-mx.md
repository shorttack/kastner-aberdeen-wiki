# Business PC Deals: March 17, 2003

> March 2003 edition covering two topics: (1) business desktop pricing update and (2) Intel Centrino notebook platform analysis. Desktop prices Mar 17: Dell GX260 $1,167 (+$78); Gateway E-4000 $1,359 (+$155); HP Compaq Evo D510SB $1,310 (+$24); IBM NetVista $1,268 (-$20). Large enterprise prices: Dell $884, IBM $900, HP $914 (50,000 units; $50 lower than Sep and includes 2.4 GHz upgrade). Prices generally higher over prior 3 months but Intel price cuts expected to flow through next quarter. Centrino = Intel Pentium M + chipset + 802.11 radio; built for 5+ hour battery life. Kastner predicts Centrino-class Dothan + convertible + Tablet PC + Office 11/OneNote notebook under $2,500 by autumn 2003; >10% of corporate clients in 2004.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Centrino = Intel Pentium M mobile processor + chipset + 802.11 wireless Ethernet adapter and radio; built from ground up for 5+ hour battery life and better performance than P3/P4 mobile `[ps=5]`
- Most important Intel introduction of 2003; Intel spending tens of millions to build worldwide wireless hotspot network `[ps=4]`
- LCD flat panels overtaking CRTs in 2003; 17in and 19in now economically attractive `[ps=4]`
- Cap Gemini Ernst & Young: additional costs of wireless notebooks achieve ROI in 6 months or less; Aberdeen research: campus meeting-attendee gains 30 min/day productivity; physicians 1 hr/day `[ps=4]`
- Over next quarter Aberdeen expects prices to decline due to flow-through of lower Intel microprocessor prices `[ps=3]`
- Perfect notebook: Centrino Dothan + convertible form factor + Tablet PC + MS Office 11/OneNote; under $2,500 including Office by autumn 2003; under 5 lbs; 4+ hr battery `[ps=3]`
- Centrino+Tablet+OneNote platform will represent more than 10% of corporate clients in 2004, including all desktops `[ps=2]`
