# CMP Corporate PC Replacement Policy Survey

> Survey instrument tracking corporate desktop and notebook replacement policies for U.S. IT buyers, comparing past policy against next-12-months plans. Desktop replacement intervals: <18, 18-24, 24-30, 30-36, 36-42, 42-48, 48-54, >54 months, when-they-break, by-application-refresh, when-need-justified, other. Laptop replacement intervals: <12 months through >54 months plus same non-time buckets. Q3-2003 mainstream desktop config options surveyed across processor GHz, memory, HDD, optical, LAN, OS, monitor. Target purchase prices and purchase volume ranges surveyed. Financing modalities (purchase, lease, both). Demographic questions. Instrument fielded in partnership with CMP Media; workbook contains question framework and partial response tabulations used in Kastner's DCT corporate PC lifecycle commentary.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._
