> Internal Aberdeen memo on the Digital Consumer Electronics and Information Technology (DCEIT) sector at a Q2-2002 inflection: Intel's $400M revenue shortfall, aggressive CPU price cuts, and AMD's 22% share gains driving PC OEM repositioning. Documents retail observations from Caroline Kastner's tracking research and catalogs vendor-specific line moves at Gateway and Dell ahead of the 2002 back-to-school season.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Semiconductor-sector sell-off; semis viewed as leading tech out of two-year recession `[ps=5]`
- 300mm wafers at 0.13 micron yield more chips at much lower cost per chip `[ps=5]`
- Price cuts + seasonal setup will move PCs through H2 2002 `[ps=4]`
- P4 mainstreaming confirmed: Pentium 4 with HT became the dominant Intel desktop SKU through 2003-2005, capturing >70% of Intel desktop shipments until Core 2 Duo replaced the NetBurst architecture in July 2006. `[ps=4]`
- HP and Compaq formerly emphasized CPUs alone; now pushing PC + monitor + sometimes printer bundles `[ps=3]`
- At higher clock speeds (2.566 GHz) P4 outperforms AMD; tech advantage vs AMD price advantage `[ps=2]`
- Faster microprocessors due to Intel price reductions `[ps=2]`
- As CPU speeds increase, memory and HDD GB offered decreases at same price point `[ps=1]`
- Upgraded optical drives and better monitors compensate for lower memory `[ps=1]`
- Offers unchanged in past three weeks; many already rock bottom `[ps=1]`
- $400M shortfall; Europe new soft spot `[ps=0]`
- Aggressive desktop and laptop chip price cuts (two weeks prior to memo) `[ps=0]`
- 22% of Intel-compatible microprocessor market `[ps=0]`
- Prep for Q3 back-to-school and Q4 Christmas buying seasons `[ps=0]`
- Aberdeen's retail PC tracking research (Caroline Kastner) documented month-over-month changes `[ps=0]`
- Lower HDD costs due to IBM inventory dumping `[ps=0]`
- PC sales down as much as 22% this quarter `[ps=0]`
- $60-$110 free shipping on popular models at HP, Sony, Dell, Gateway, Compaq, eMachines `[ps=0]`
- Gateway 80GB HDD upgrade early May -> DVD-ROM/CD-RW upgrades or $100 mail-in rebate later `[ps=0]`
- 1.3 GHz Celeron -> 1.7 GHz Celeron at same price `[ps=0]`
- 2.0A GHz -> 2.26 GHz for same cost `[ps=0]`
- Faster P4; less HDD GB `[ps=0]`
- Launch 1.7 GHz Celeron -> P4 1.8/2.0 GHz same price `[ps=0]`
