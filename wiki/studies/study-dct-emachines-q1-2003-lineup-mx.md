# eMachines Q1 2003 Product Lineup

> Compact snapshot of the eMachines Q1 2003 desktop product lineup: T1842 ($399 Celeron 1.8 GHz), T2042 ($499 Celeron 2.0 GHz), T2260 ($599 Athlon XP 2200+), T2460 ($649 Athlon XP 2400+). All ship with 128-256 MB memory, 40-60 GB HDD, CD-ROM or CD-RW/DVD optical, sold direct via www.emachines.com.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- eMachines T1842: Celeron 1.8 GHz, 128MB, 40GB, CD-ROM; $399 with shipping and rebates via www.emachines.com
- eMachines T2042: Celeron 2.0 GHz, 128MB, 40GB, CD-RW+DVD; $499 with shipping and rebates via www.emachines.com
- eMachines T2260: Athlon XP 2200+, 256MB, 60GB, CD-RW+DVD; $599 with shipping and rebates via www.emachines.com
- eMachines T2460: Athlon XP 2400+, 256MB, 60GB, CD-RW+DVD; $649 with shipping and rebates via www.emachines.com
- eMachines Q1-2003 lineup anchors the sub-$500 consumer desktop segment with Celeron-based models starting at $399, demonstrating successful value positioning at the bottom of the U.S. consumer PC market
- eMachines differentiates its higher-end models ($599-$649) with AMD Athlon XP processors and 60GB HDDs, providing stronger value-per-dollar than competing Celeron-based machines at similar price points
