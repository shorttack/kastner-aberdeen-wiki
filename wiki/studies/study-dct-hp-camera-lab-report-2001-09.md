# HP's Consumer IT Leadership Needs More Focus On Consumers (Sep 5 2001 draft)

- **3-year verdict:** high — 3y Rule A: mean=3.56 over 18 usable obs (0 prefiltered, 0 pending) -> high [high>=3.5, medium>=2.0].
- **5-year verdict:** high — 5y Rule A: mean=3.61 over 18 usable obs (0 prefiltered, 0 pending) -> high [high>=3.5, medium>=2.0].

> September 5, 2001 Aberdeen Lab InSight draft on HP's consumer IT execution, based on hands-on experience with HP's C315 digital camera. Earlier version of the 'Caveat Emptor' InSight later published December 5, 2001. Documents the Windows XP upgrade-in-place compatibility failure caused by HP's bundled photo-transfer software interfering with Windows XP native camera support — and the 45-minute support call that cost HP more than the camera's retail price per Aberdeen help-desk research.


_Published 2001, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Simplified, intuitive; quick-start; searchable web; published FAQs `[ps=4]`
- Do NOT install HP photo transfer software — use native XP support `[ps=4]`
- Can't afford many customer support phone calls and still make money on mass-market products `[ps=4]`
- Qualitatively test consumer experience with IT products; evaluate real products as consumers would `[ps=4]`
- Many sophisticated tech consumers are more afraid of and less patient with IT than suppliers expect `[ps=4]`
- HP exited the consumer digital camera market in 2008, selling the Photosmart camera line and licensing the HP brand; HP's consumer imaging strategy ultimately centered on printers rather than capture devices as the prediction's direction anticipated. `[ps=4]`
- Camera ROM upgrade failed because XP formatting had altered camera media `[ps=3]`
- Lead the market in consumer IT — access PCs, imaging, new consumer electronics `[ps=2]`
- Upgrade-in-place + bundled photo-transfer software = native-XP camera support broken `[ps=2]`
- Many unhappy HP camera owners will emerge on XP upgrade; new-PC + camera bundles also affected `[ps=2]`
- HP gave analysts C315 at May conference (amateur-photographer context) `[ps=0]`
- Photographs high quality; transfer straightforward; HP photo web site publishes free `[ps=0]`
- Difficult to recommend without strong caveats about installation and ease-of-use `[ps=0]`
- Minor hitch: ACD See missing .dll; installed alternative; off in <30 minutes `[ps=0]`
- HP photo transfer interferes with Windows XP native camera support `[ps=0]`
- Windows XP sees camera as removable disk; formatting fails with severe error `[ps=0]`
- HP support technician solved immediate problem in ~45 minutes — high marks to tech `[ps=0]`
- Single call cost HP more than the retail price of the camera `[ps=0]`
