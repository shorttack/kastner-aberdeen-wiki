# Analysis of Intel Processor Prices in PC Deals (January 2003)

> Analysis of Intel Celeron and Pentium 4 processor price tiers in Aberdeen's PC Deals tracking from August 2002 through January 2003. Documents the bifurcation of P4 into 400 MHz and 533 MHz front-side-bus families, the introduction of hyperthreading 3.06 GHz P4, and how older CPUs are pushed down price bands by new releases. Predicts near-term obsolescence of the P4 2.0 GHz.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- P4 2.4 GHz falling to same $900 category as P4 1.8/2.0 GHz in January 2003, driven by November 3.06 GHz HT P4 introduction; P4 1.8 GHz beginning to disappear; P4 2.0 GHz remains strong option `[ps=4]`
- Kastner predicts 'end is near for the 2.0 GHz processor' because with HT launch Intel now produces three differently structured P4 processor families; P4 2.4 and 2.0 GHz cannot occupy same price category long `[ps=4]`
- Kastner predicts if PC companies want to move inventory in the slow spring quarter, prices will have to fall; first week of January 2003 has not revealed reversal of holiday price hikes `[ps=4]`
- New faster P4s push slower predecessors down price bands rather than eliminating them; 400 MHz FSB models persist as fillers between Celeron floor and 533 MHz FSB ceiling `[ps=4]`
- P4 split into two families: 400 MHz FSB (1.8/2.0/2.26 GHz) and 533 MHz FSB (starting 2.26 GHz and above). Pricing divisions emerged: 400 MHz FSB P4s fill mid-tier between Celeron and 533 MHz FSBs; P4 2.26 GHz disappeared from good-value deals by October 2002 `[ps=3]`
- 3.06 GHz HT P4 (introduced November 2002) creates new high-end price category: machines cost $2,200-$3,000; does not suppress pricing of 2.66/2.8 GHz P4 models `[ps=3]`
- Aberdeen observed holiday season price increases from several PC sources, especially Dell; precious few deals from national retailers in December 2002 `[ps=1]`
- Intel Celeron PCs consistently priced under $600; Celeron 1.7 GHz under $450 after P4 1.8 GHz dropped to under $900 in August 2002 `[ps=0]`
- Intel Celeron 1.8 GHz launched June 12 2002; Celeron 2.0 GHz launched August 26 2002; Celeron 2.0 GHz entered PC Deals $600 category in November 2002 `[ps=0]`
- P4 2.6, 2.66, and 2.8 GHz processors released August 26 2002; P4 1.8 and 2.0 GHz dropped $150 to $900 price point within first month; remained at $900 for next two months `[ps=0]`
- P4 2.53 GHz did drop since 2.8 GHz P4 release; 2.66 and 2.8 GHz P4s remained in same price categories for over four months as of January 2003 `[ps=0]`
- Kastner predicts HT processors will NOT eliminate middle-road 533 MHz P4s (e.g. 2.53 GHz) anytime soon, based on FSB bifurcation precedent `[ps=-1]`
