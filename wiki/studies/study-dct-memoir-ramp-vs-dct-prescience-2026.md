# RAMP vs DCT: A Methodology-Level Comparison of My Prescience Track Record (2026 Retrospective)

> A 2026 retrospective memoir comparing Peter Kastner's 2001-2004 prescience track record across two Kastner-authored methodologies: the Maxtor RAMP structured-interview corpus (39 studies, 2-5 year enterprise horizons) and the DCT weekly consumer PC retail tracker (75 studies, days-to-weeks tactical horizons). Written after the Phase 3 backfill of 105 DCT DEFERRED observations, this memoir argues that the methodology differences — not analyst skill differences — drive the gap in study-level prescience scoring, and shows that at the observation level both corpuses post strong records.


_Published 2026, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **memoir**._

- Coding 15 DCT studies as 'not-applicable' for prescience is structural fidelity, not reluctance: tactical weekly notes that resolve within weeks are category errors to grade for multi-year prescience.
- Kastner 2003 DCT commentary treated UWB as the obvious successor to 802.11a/b for in-home consumer video streaming.
- WiMedia UWB lost to 802.11n (2007) and 802.11ac (2013); UWB retreated to short-hop pairing and location services.
- Kastner 2002-2003 DCT commentary treated Mira as the first credible kitchen-screen form factor.
- Microsoft killed Smart Display in 2003; category did not return until iPad and Windows 8 tablets in 2010-2012.
- Both refuted predictions share the failure mode of overestimating how quickly a technically-superior alternative can unseat a good-enough installed base.
