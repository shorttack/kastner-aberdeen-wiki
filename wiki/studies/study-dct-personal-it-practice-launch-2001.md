# Personal Information Technology — Aberdeen Practice Launch

> Program-launch description of Aberdeen Group's Personal Information Technology practice, established by Peter Kastner in 2001. Articulates the nine market paradoxes driving the practice (PC commoditization, digital-device convergence, home-network emergence, 3G wireless, digital TV transition, in-home services needs), catalogs service offerings, technology segments, strategic market questions, key market trends, and the supplier coverage list (Microsoft, Intel, AMD, Compaq, Dell, HP, IBM, Sony, Toshiba, Apple, Gateway, Micron, AOL, Epson, Lexmark, Canon, Nvidia, Cirrus Logic).


_Published 2001, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Aberdeen's newest practice area: Personal Information Technology
- PC commoditization + lowered margins driving share pressure
- PVRs and MP3 players replacing VCRs and cassette players
- Office-life extending into personal time via email etc.
- Digital technology enabling new leisure activities (photo, music, video production)
- Market strategic goal obsolete; add-on PCs per family member is new driver
- Multiple-PC households drive broadband connectivity and home-network demand
- New PCs such as the home server distinct from enterprise servers
- Drive new waves of consumer buying; existing brands don't have assured share
- PCs, Productivity, SOHO, Microprocessors, Consumer OS, Consumer IT (digital appliances/storage/printing/TV-stereo), Digital leisure, Information services
- Custom consulting, market research, education, analysis; Aberdeen Lab product testing
- What will change today's replacement-only home computer market?
- Are microprocessor speeds far outdistancing consumer/SOHO needs?
- When and how will the last-mile broadband problem be solved in advanced countries?
- Will Windows XP drive a new burst of high-end PC growth?
- 802.11b wireless rapidly growing to compete with wired home-networking
- Japan's gaming computers lack traditional OS/app support; can they move up-scale vs Wintel PCs?
- LCD monitors at consumer price point; excellent for desk-space-conscious SOHO
- Digital imaging growing fast enough to materially impact traditional photography-based markets
- Enterprise brand does not automatically translate to consumer sales
- 18 suppliers tracked: Microsoft, Intel, AMD, Compaq, Dell, HP, IBM, Sony, Toshiba, Apple, Gateway, Micron, AOL, Epson, Lexmark, Canon, Nvidia, Cirrus Logic
- Home Networking (Cohen); Messaging (Gardner); Intel Servers (Manter); Digital Content (Hoch); Internet Appliances (Ro)
- In 2001, Kastner initiated the Personal IT practice — focus on PCs, SOHO, and convergence
