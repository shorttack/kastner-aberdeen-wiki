# Q1 2003 Worldwide and U.S. PC Shipments — IDC and Gartner Data

> Compact worksheet summarizing Q1 2003 PC shipment statistics from IDC and Gartner. IDC: +1.5% U.S. / +2.1% WW (third consecutive quarter of growth). Gartner: +7.7% U.S. / +5.5% WW. Dell led at 30.7% U.S. share / 17.3% WW share, 5.989M units, 20% growth, 29% consumer mix. HP at 19.5% U.S. / 15.8% WW, -6% growth, 34% consumer mix. Total WW shipments ~34.6M units. Gartner forecast 6.6% WW IT spending growth for 2003.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._
