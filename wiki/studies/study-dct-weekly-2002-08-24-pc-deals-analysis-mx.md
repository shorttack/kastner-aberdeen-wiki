# PC Deals Analysis: August 24-31, 2002

> Week of August 24-31 2002 PC Deals analysis. P4 2.53 GHz machines appear on radar; 2.26 GHz under $900. Compaq leads with P4 2.0A with monitor under $750, and 2.26 GHz with monitor under $800. P4/Athlon 1.8 GHz disappearing as 2 GHz+ becomes baseline. Best Buy leads retailers. Includes per-price-band winners from $550 to over $1550.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- P4 2.53 GHz enters PC Deals radar week of Aug 24; P4 2.26 GHz under $900; P4/Athlon 1.8 GHz disappearing; 2 GHz and above becomes baseline for machines over $900
- Compaq leads deals: P4 2.0A GHz with monitor under $750; P4 2.26 GHz with monitor under $800
- HP lost market share to Dell last quarter; is pursuing aggressive deals to recover share ahead of Q2 earnings announcement week of Aug 24 2002
- Intel Celeron 1.2-1.4 GHz pushed down to lowest of nine PC Deals price bands (previously common up to $1000 machines) as faster chips move in
- RDRAM machines are hundreds of dollars more than DDR SDRAM equivalents; signals that consumers will soon need to learn about DDR memory; low-end machines upgrading technology while holding prices constant
- Compaq 6000T at Best Buy custom config: P4 2.26 GHz 533 MHz FSB with monitor and printer for under $800 — PC Deals buy of the week; first time Intel's latest 533 MHz FSB technology seen at this price point
- eMachines wins $550 price point; Best Buy has best prices on T1220 and T1440; all machines in this range include printer
- eMachines T1860 (Athlon 1800+ 1.53 GHz) sold out at $750 price point; replaced by T1862 (Celeron 1.8 GHz)
- eMachines T2080 (Athlon XP) wins $1000 category with low price and great specs
- Dell 4500S with P4 2.4 GHz and 19in flat screen monitor wins $1100 category over Compaq Presario 8000Z and 6027US
- $1350 buys P4 2.53 GHz from Compaq Online or Dell; Compaq rated ahead by Kastner methodology but both recommended
- Above $1550 category looks haggard; top machines have fast RDRAM but fastest CPUs with DDR now at lower price points; Aberdeen considering reordering price bands after Labor Day
- Intel expected to announce a new high-end chip soon, which will unleash a further cascade of good deals this fall
- Buyers should buy customized machines this week; technology moving faster through online custom arms of Dell and HP/Compaq through Best Buy; Labor Day circulars expected next Sunday
- Dell Dimension 4500 recommended for $1000-$1500 range; highly configurable; 8200 with RDRAM costs $200 more than comparable DDR 4500 configuration
