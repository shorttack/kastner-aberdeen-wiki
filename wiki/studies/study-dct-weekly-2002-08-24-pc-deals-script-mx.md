# PC Deals Script: August 24-31, 2002

> Radio/broadcast Q&A script (Brian and Peter) for Aberdeen's PC Deals segment, week of August 27 2002. Covers back-to-school market trends: P4 2.53 GHz on radar, 2.26 GHz under $900, Compaq leads with P4 2.0A under $750 / 2.26 GHz under $800, technology shift from 1.8 GHz baseline, RDRAM vs DDR divergence, Intel expected high-end 2.8 GHz chip announcement, Gateway all-in-one debut, and Compaq 6000T buy of the week.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- P4 2.53 GHz enters PC Deals radar (Aug 24 2002); 2.26 GHz under $900; P4/Athlon 1.8 GHz range disappearing; 2 GHz+ is new baseline over $900
- Compaq leads: P4 2.0A GHz with monitor under $750; P4 2.26 GHz with monitor under $800
- HP lost market share to Dell last quarter; out to regain it with outstanding deals ahead of Q2 earnings announcement week of Aug 27
- Intel Celeron 1.2-1.4 GHz pushed to lowest of nine price bands; configurations getting richer in memory and peripherals across the board
- RDRAM machines hundreds of dollars more than DDR equivalents; low end keeps prices flat while upgrading specs; consumers will need to learn about DDR memory
- Intel expected to announce new high-end P4 2.8 GHz chip this week, which will unleash further cascade of lower chip prices and good deals this fall
- Gateway announced all-in-one machine: LCD flat-panel monitor houses P4, ala Apple Macintosh; 17in model at approximately $1900
- Compaq 6000T at Best Buy: P4 2.26 GHz 533 MHz FSB with monitor and printer for under $800; PC Deals buy of the week; first time Intel's latest 533 MHz FSB technology seen at this price
- Dell 4500S P4 2.4 GHz with 19in flat screen monitor wins $1100 category over Compaq Presario 8000Z and 6027US
- $1350 buys P4 2.53 GHz from Compaq Online or Dell; Kastner rates Compaq ahead
