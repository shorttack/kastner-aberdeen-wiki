# PC Deals Weekly Update: October 27, 2002

> The retail ad circulars are back after a two-week hiatus. No notable price cuts emerged this week, although many price increases appeared. Retailers and manufacturers should be gearing up for the holiday season; Compaq custom configurations migrated to hpshopping.com following HP-Compaq merger, and Dell reversed prior week's pricing trend.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- HP has been slow to fully mesh with Compaq; Kastner expects Compaq brand to focus on business/home productivity while HP brand emphasizes performance and multimedia; new line-up due mid-2003 `[ps=5]`
- LCD display sales dollars (but not units) now exceed CRT displays at prices like $249 for a 15-inch LCD `[ps=4]`
- Kastner recommends holiday PC buyers wait until Thanksgiving for best deals; no supply concerns `[ps=3]`
- Option to build-your-own Compaq has disappeared; Kastner rates this 'quite a loss for the consumer' as customized Compaq PCs offered great value `[ps=2]`
- Back after two-week hiatus; no notable price cuts but many price increases this week `[ps=0]`
- athome.compaq.com shut down; redirects to hpshopping.com; Compaq Presario 6000 series now sold via HP Online `[ps=0]`
- Dell Dimension 8250 (2.8 GHz, RDRAM) costs over $300 more this week than last; new-in-October 8250 is more expensive than 8200 predecessor `[ps=0]`
- Dell: $50 off Dimension 2300; free shipping on all Dimension models `[ps=0]`
- Best Buy offering $100-$150 rebate on all computer packages; no interest for 12 months on computer packages `[ps=0]`
- HP advertising up to $250 off Presario PC and monitor bundles via hpshopping.com `[ps=0]`
- $200 off with trade-in of 300 MHz+ Windows desktop; $300 off notebook with 166 MHz trade-in or better; $249 special on 15-inch LCD monitor `[ps=0]`
- OfficeMax HP 513xb: AMD Athlon XP 1700+, 256 MB SDRAM, 40 GB disk, CD-RW, 17-inch monitor for $700; HP 3820 printer add-on for $100 `[ps=0]`
- Sony Online: customize Sony Vaio PC starting at $699; $100 off all models; one cent shipping `[ps=0]`
- No eMachines retail deals this week; neither Best Buy nor Circuit City advertised any eMachines models `[ps=0]`
- HP Media Center PC at Best Buy for $2,000 with 15-inch LCD; same at CompUSA for $1,700 with 17-inch CRT; uses Microsoft Windows XP Media Center extensions `[ps=0]`
