# PC Deals Weekly Update: November 3, 2002

> AMD ruled the retail ads this week with an abundance of Athlon machines at great prices, winning the $600, $750, and $900 price categories. Kastner identifies $1,050 as the sweet spot where additional spend yields significant processor upgrades from Celeron/AMD to Intel P4.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Price fluctuations continue in both retail and manufacturer channels; Dell varies over $100 week to week; PC Deals predicts prices will drop or consumer gets more for same price as holiday season nears `[ps=5]`
- Manufacturers and retailers continue to push extras (printers, digital cameras, scanners) because these sell well while PC sales fall; retail chains corner this space over online manufacturers due to shipping costs and bundled PC deals `[ps=2]`
- AMD Athlon machines win the $600, $750, and $900 price categories this week; Kastner recommends AMD for great basic home PC `[ps=0]`
- $1,050 is the sweet spot: $150 more than $900 buys Intel P4 2.53 GHz without monitor or P4 2.4 GHz with monitor; $1,200 stagnates at P4 2.4 GHz `[ps=0]`
- Dell Dimension 2300 with Celeron 1.8 GHz jumped from $709 to $848; all Dimension 4550 machines decreased by about $50 `[ps=0]`
- Dell offers: free CD-Burner and hard drive upgrades `[ps=0]`
- BestBuy offers free 64 MB nVidia GeForce4 MX440 graphics card with any customized Sony Vaio machine ('Sony Creation Station'); prices competitive with Sony retail models `[ps=0]`
- Circuit City has bundled deal on eMachines T2200 including flat screen monitor `[ps=0]`
- Office Depot: $100 savings on Compaq 8000T customized PCs with Intel P4 processors of 2.6 GHz or higher; confirms custom Compaq PCs still available at retail despite removal from HP-Compaq Online `[ps=0]`
- Gateway 300S offered with $100 off; competitive at $600 price point; 17-inch monitor add-on for $120; upgrades available to P4 1.8 GHz `[ps=0]`
- Sony Online: customize Sony Vaio PC from $699; $100 off all models; one cent shipping; 80 GB hard drive for price of 60 GB on custom orders; $50 off 120 GB or 160 GB drive; free ground shipping on custom models `[ps=0]`
- hpshopping.com page would not open on multiple attempts; accessible via www.shopping.hp.com; good rebates on HP and Compaq bundles `[ps=0]`
