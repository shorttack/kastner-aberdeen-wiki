# PC Deals Special: Pentium 4 with HyperThreading

> Intel's Pentium 4 at 3.06 GHz with Hyper-Threading technology arrived November 14, 2002. Kastner explains HT technology mechanics, assesses its performance benefit (80-90% improvement vs. 50% GHz increase alone), and forecasts that 3.06 GHz HT machines will reach the $1,350 price band by April 1, 2003. Custom-configured HT computers from Dell, HP, and Gateway featured.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Kastner forecasts HT technology will push down the product line as Intel rolls out more speed improvements in 2003 `[ps=5]`
- HT works by providing hardware support for a second executing compute task; single CPU but hardware-assist for fast task switching; reduces cycles spent on OS-level context switching `[ps=3]`
- Kastner's long-term view: P4 with HT will make a great next desktop `[ps=3]`
- Many application mixes show 80-90% performance improvement with HT vs. 3.06 GHz compared to 2.0 GHz P4 (50% GHz boost), particularly machines with lots of fast memory `[ps=1]`
- Intel Pentium 4 at 3.06 GHz with HyperThreading technology launched November 14, 2002 `[ps=0]`
- Entry price over $2,000 for a richly configured HT machine at launch `[ps=0]`
- Kastner forecasts 3.06 GHz HT machines will be in the $1,350 price band by April 1, 2003 `[ps=0]`
- Dell offers custom-configured HT computers (3.06 GHz P4 HT) `[ps=0]`
- HP offers custom-configured HT computers (3.06 GHz P4 HT) `[ps=0]`
- Gateway offers custom-configured HT computers (3.06 GHz P4 HT) `[ps=0]`
