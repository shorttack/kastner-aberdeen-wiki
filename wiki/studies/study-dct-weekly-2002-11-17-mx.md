# PC Deals Weekly Analysis: November 17, 2002

> Weekly analysis for November 17, 2002 covering HyperThreading arrival, buying tips for HT vs. prior-gen machines, Costco vs. Dell Online comparison for Dimension 2300, eMachines return, and pricing from Best Buy, Circuit City, Dell, Gateway, and Sony.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Kastner forecasts HT technology will push down the Intel product line as Intel rolls out more speed improvements in 2003 `[ps=5]`
- Intel Pentium 4 at 3.06 GHz with HyperThreading arrived November 14, 2002; adds trivial number of transistors; performs much more crisply than implied by 200 MHz speed-bump over 2.8 GHz P4 `[ps=4]`
- Kastner forecasts 3.06 GHz HT machines will be in the $1,350 price band by April 1, 2003 `[ps=4]`
- Many application mixes show 80-90% performance improvement with 3.06 GHz HT vs. 2.0 GHz P4 (50% GHz boost); HT allows CPU to spend more time on useful tasks vs. hardware-level task housekeeping `[ps=3]`
- Compaq 6310US wins $600 class despite lacking monitor; superior Athlon processor and DDR memory vs. Celeron/SDRAM competitors `[ps=3]`
- Best Buy build-your-own kiosk: HP 793 with HT processor and house-brand monitor for approximately $1,450; example of aggressive HP pricing versus Dell `[ps=0]`
- Release of new HT processors has not yet dropped prices of 2.8 GHz or lower machines; Kastner advises consumers to wait a couple of weeks for prices to drop; Dell expected to lead with best deals first `[ps=0]`
- Buying Dell Dimension 2300 from Dell Online saves $70 off Costco's price this week; Costco price consistent but Dell Online price is extremely volatile week to week `[ps=0]`
- BestBuy: eMachines T1742 bundled offer reappears this week; may not appear again — Kastner recommends buying it `[ps=0]`
- Office Depot: eMachines T1840 bundled with 19-inch flat screen monitor and color printer for under $700 `[ps=0]`
- CircuitCity.com and BestBuy.com matched prices on multiple PCs; Circuit City offers $100 gift card after mail-in rebate for purchases over $1,000 — edge over BestBuy.com for higher-end machines `[ps=0]`
- Dell offers free shipping and CD-ROM upgrades on all models above Dimension 2300; means CD-RW and DVD-ROM at no extra cost `[ps=0]`
- Gateway 300S upgraded to 2.0 GHz Celeron processor; Gateway continues specials on individual machines `[ps=0]`
- Sony Online: $100 off all models; one cent shipping; free Epson C42UX printer with any Vaio desktop through December 31; customize Vaio from $699; 80 GB drive for price of 60 GB on custom orders `[ps=0]`
