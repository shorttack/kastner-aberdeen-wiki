# PC Deals Weekly Update: December 15, 2002

> eMachines and Sony are the hot retail tickets for the week of December 15, 2002. eMachines wins on low price; Sony benefits from consumer lock-in via Memory Stick media readers. Kastner identifies Compaq 6310US and Gateway 300L as price-value leaders under $600. Dell raises prices aggressively while maintaining market share. Gateway offers an innovative two-for-one deal.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- eMachines and Sony are the hot retail tickets per Kastner's secret shoppers; eMachines wins on low price according to store managers
- PC demand is price elastic; eMachines' low-price strategy demonstrates classic price elasticity in a down market
- Sony benefits from Memory Stick consumer lock-in: consumers buy Sony PCs for media reader compatibility with Sony's proprietary flash memory used in digital cameras and camcorders
- Compaq 6310US and Gateway 300L rated as price-value leaders under $600 this week; 6310US wins despite lacking monitor due to superior Athlon processor and DDR memory vs. Celeron/SDRAM competitors
- Kastner recommends HP add memory media center reading Sony Memory Stick + Secure Digital + Compact Flash + SmartMedia + MultiMedia Card instead of floppy disk to compete with Sony on high-end; HP Pavilion 893 Media Center PC already has such a reader
- Gateway two-for-one deal: buy 700L ($3,400 with dual hard drives, 19-inch LCD, P4 3.06 GHz) and get Celeron-based 300L free (monitor sold separately)
- Dell Dimension 8250 reference machine (P4 2.8 GHz) rose from $1,808 to $2,157 in one week — a 19% price hike; Dell continues to raise prices aggressively this fall while maintaining market share
- Dell deals this week: free shipping all Dimension models; $100 and $200 off 4550 and 8250 respectively; Dimension 2350 dropped into under-$900 class but lags predecessor 2300 in value; 4550 prices up for second week in a row
- hp.com: 3.06 GHz P4 configured for $1,858 with $150 rebate on processors 2.66 GHz or higher; CD-RW upgrade for $10; faster 7200 rpm hard drive included
- BestBuy: eMachines T2085 (AMD Athlon 2400+, 512 MB DDR, DVD/CD-RW, flat screen monitor, Lexmark printer) for $800
- BestBuy: HP Pavilion 523n (Athlon 2200+, HP peripherals) for $850
- BestBuy: HP Pavilion 753n (P4 2.53 GHz, 17-inch HP LCD flat panel monitor, usual peripherals) for $1,400; beats configurations at $1,600
- Gateway Triple Double deal: free doubling of warranty, hard drive size, and memory
- Circuit City: $100 gift card after mail-in rebates; 0% interest financing for a year; Kastner expects more incentives as holiday nears
- Radio Shack retired the attractive Compaq Presario 6331RSH at $750 this week, dropping it from PC Deals' winners circle
