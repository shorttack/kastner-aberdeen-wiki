# PC Deals Weekly Update: December 22, 2002

> Dell partially reduces recent price hikes but remains far from pre-Thanksgiving levels; pitiful number of retail deals the week before Christmas. Sony's Memory Stick lock-in strategy continues to drive strong sales. Kastner advises waiting until December 26 for post-Christmas bargains. Specific deals from Circuit City, Dell, Best Buy, and Staples covered.


_Published 2002, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Kastner predicts excellent post-Christmas bargains for those who wait until December 26; best opportunities Thursday through Saturday `[ps=4]`
- Sony doing well because consumers are falling for the Memory Stick ploy: PCs with media readers compatible with Sony's proprietary flash memory, also fitting digital cameras and camcorders `[ps=3]`
- Many retailers offer best-price guarantees this holiday season; Blockbuster guarantees movie prices; Target same for electronics; reduces need to shop around for popular media items `[ps=3]`
- Dell partially reduced recent price hikes; Dimension 4550 with P4 2.4 GHz is $149 less expensive than last week at just under $1,200; still far from pre-Thanksgiving pricing `[ps=0]`
- Dell: free shipping and memory upgrade; not enough to bring prices to prior levels `[ps=0]`
- Circuit City: $100 gift card on AMD desktop and notebook purchases `[ps=0]`
- Circuit City ended great deal on Sony RXA-842 (Athlon 2400+, 512 MB DDR, 80 GB HDD, DVD/CD-RW) at $750 on Saturday; Kastner notes this is the kind of deal expected right after Christmas `[ps=0]`
- Best Buy: no interest until 2004 on $299+ purchases; $200 off any P4 or AMD Athlon computer with purchase of digital camera, camcorder, or gaming console `[ps=0]`
- Staples: upgrade to Windows XP Pro for $25 with PC purchase `[ps=0]`
- Staples: Verbatim DVD+RW/R and CD-RW drive for $200, about $100 less than the going price for DVD writers `[ps=0]`
- Pitiful number of retail deals the week of December 22; PC Deals expects mid-week flyers to appear advertising beginning of post-Christmas sales `[ps=0]`
