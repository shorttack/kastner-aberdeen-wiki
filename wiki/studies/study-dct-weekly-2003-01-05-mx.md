# PC Deals Weekly Update: January 5, 2003

> Gateway's brand is expanding into peripheral distribution; Kastner detects Intel Celeron attack on AMD's low-end foothold. Dell's 4550 prices spiked $80-$100 in two days. EMachines unveils new T2042 and T1842 models. Sony's retail presence has dropped over the past six weeks. HP and Compaq lack competitive retail deals due to expired rebates.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Last week of December was an online week; best deals at online sites (shopping.hp.com, circuitcity.com) due to lack of PC ads in post-Christmas print advertising and desire to clear warehouse inventory; PC buying should be approached like auto buying — arm yourself with Net research first `[ps=3]`
- Gateway brand expanding into peripheral distribution: Gateway monitors for sale at multiple online stores; Gateway appearing as preferred supplier for Samsung LCD at Yahoo Shopping; taking Gateway into food fight with CDW.com and PCconnection `[ps=2]`
- Kastner identifies Intel Celeron attack as AMD's worst nightmare: $600 class previously an AMD toehold is now back in the hands of Celerons; $900 price category loaded with P4s after post-Thanksgiving AMD wins `[ps=2]`
- Dell Dimension 4550 prices rose $80 to $100 over two days: same 120 GB config was $1,204 on Friday Jan 3, became $1,362 on Jan 5 with even smaller 80 GB drive `[ps=0]`
- Dell Dimension 8250 with 2.8 GHz processor rose $100; 3.06 GHz counterpart rose over $160 `[ps=0]`
- Dell offers: $50, $100, and $150 instant savings on Dimension 2350, 4550, and 8250 respectively; free memory upgrade on all these models; not a good week for best deals — offers do not equal savings `[ps=0]`
- eMachines unveiled new models T2042 and T1842; BestBuy already advertising deals on both; T1842 (lower-end) has CD-ROM only vs. T1840's CD-RW+DVD-ROM; T2042 has slightly faster processor and CD-RW+DVD-ROM `[ps=0]`
- Sony's retail presence dropped over the past six weeks; Sony Online offers free overnight shipping and $100 mail-in rebate; RX850 and RX860 still available but no national retailers sell RX850 online `[ps=0]`
- HP Online: $50 off all PCs but $30 shipping charge; former mail-in rebate expired; Online retailers lack discount on HP/Compaq PCs; not the best time to buy HP-Compaq `[ps=0]`
- Gateway: 300L Special no longer best deal; 300S Value now best in 300 line (slightly higher price but includes CD-RW); 500L Special lost $100 mail-in rebate, now costs $80 more; all other models unchanged `[ps=0]`
- Athlon 2000+ will run rings around Celeron 2.0 despite same GHz number; Celerons lead $600 class this week; AMD only wins $750 class which is under-populated with choices `[ps=-1]`
