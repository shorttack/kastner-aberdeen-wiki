# PC Deals Weekly Update: January 12, 2003

> Weekly deals are back with explosion of options in $600 and $900 price categories. VPR Matrix 3110 is PC Deals' overall winner at $900 with 2.66 GHz P4. eMachines and Compaq fill low-to-mid-range. Dell bounces back from last week's price hikes with over $70 savings on 2350 and over $180 on 4550. Dell Dimension 8250 vs 4550 price discrepancy explored.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Weekly deals are back; explosion of PC options in $600 and $900 price categories; eMachines and Compaq fill ranges with excellent deals; AMD Athlon machines making strong reappearance
- VPR Matrix 3110: 2.66 GHz P4, 512 MB DDR, 80 GB HDD, monitor for under $900 — PC Deals overall winner of the week; only a weekly offer at BestBuy
- Dell bounced back from last week's hikes: over $70 savings on Dimension 2350 (P4 1.8 GHz) vs. last week; over $180 savings on P4 2.0 GHz Dimension 4550 vs. last week
- Dell Dimension 8250 (RDRAM) vs 4550 (DDR) with same 2.4 GHz, 60 GB, 512 MB config: $60 price difference; same config with 2.8 GHz shows $420 price discrepancy — unexplained by RDRAM premium alone
- Dell Online: free shipping; CD-ROM upgrades; hard drive upgrades on all Dimension models; $100 rebate on 8250
- All four eMachines models now in stores and eMachines.com; all four under $750; T2460 for approximately $660 with monitor at BestBuy stores impresses Kastner
- CompUSA: exclusive deals on HP and Compaq machines; especially Compaq 6370US for $1,300
- Gateway: free shipping on all PCs; Home section has better deals and lower prices than At Work/Small Office section; gap in offerings in $1,000-$1,300 range
- Sony Online: free overnight shipping on all Vaio desktops; $100 mail-in rebate; retail presence dropped over past 6 weeks; RX850 and RX860 still available
- HP Online: free shipping; $50 off select models; $100 off customized PCs; best bundled deals at stores like CompUSA
