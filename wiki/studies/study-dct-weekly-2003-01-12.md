# PC Deals Weekly Update: January 12, 2003

> Weekly Kastner PC Deals commentary for 2003-01-12, covering retail PC pricing trends across major U.S. retailers and manufacturers.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- BestBuy is your eMachines source, offering three of the four eMachine's PCs in PC Deals this week. Note that BestBuy is not offering printers in any of their bundles this week, but that does mean another $25 off the price tag of many PCs.
- CompUSA had a lot of deals this week exclusively on HP and Compaq machines. Especially consider Compaq's 6370US for $1,300.
- All four of eMachine's models are in stores or at eMachines.com now. This brand now has four machines out for under $750.
- Dell bounced back from last week's price hikes. Online deals include free shipping, CD-ROM upgrades, and hard drive upgrades on all Dimension models, as well as $100 rebate on the 8250.
