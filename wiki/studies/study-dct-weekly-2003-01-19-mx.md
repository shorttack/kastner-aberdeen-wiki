# PC Deals Weekly Update: January 19, 2003

> Intel has not changed microprocessor prices since November; high-end machines stagnant; Kastner expects Intel price reduction next month. HP, Gateway, ViewSonic, and Alienware now offer Windows XP Media Center PCs. BestBuy's new VPR Matrix FT3110 (P4 2.8 GHz, 15-inch LCD) under $1,400 is impressive. Circuit City no longer sells Sony PCs online. Walmart enters PC Deals coverage.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Alienware Navigator Media Center PC: compact Shuttle-based form-factor; 7.4x7.88x11.82 inch footprint; stereo-component black; fits with media components rack; filled with top-end components; Kastner asks 'Who says the PC can't fit in the living room?' `[ps=3]`
- Intel has not changed microprocessor prices since November; as a result no downward trend in high-end configurations such as 2.8 GHz machines `[ps=0]`
- VPR Matrix FT3110: P4 2.8 GHz, 512 MB DDR, 100 GB HDD, 15-inch LCD flat panel monitor for under $1,400 at BestBuy; previously same VPR Matrix model was non-bundled at higher price point `[ps=0]`
- Circuit City no longer sells any Sony PCs on its website; all Circuit City AMD Athlon bundled deals include $100 gift cards `[ps=0]`
- CompUSA: Compaq 6430US and 6350US bundled offers both under $1,050; 18-month no-interest financing; one cent shipping online `[ps=0]`
- All four eMachines models now in stores; T2460 for approximately $650 with monitor and printer at BestBuy stores `[ps=0]`
- Dell Online: free shipping; CD-ROM upgrades; hard drive upgrades on all Dimension models; $100 rebate; Dimension 2350 prices went down dramatically `[ps=0]`
- Gateway two-fer promo returned: buy 700XL ($3,499) and get free 300S or $399 off a second machine; similar deal on 600XL notebook; national TV promotion; free shipping offer ended — prices back up `[ps=0]`
- Walmart: HP Pavilion 553w-b with 15-inch flat-panel LCD monitor for $998; good deal in $1,000 price category this week but no better than Dell Dimension 2350 deal last week `[ps=0]`
- Sony's retail presence dropped over the past six weeks; no national retailers sell RX850 online; Sony Online offers free overnight shipping and $100 mail-in rebates `[ps=0]`
- HP Online: free shipping; $50 off select models; $100 off customized PCs; best bundled deals at CompUSA `[ps=0]`
- Kastner expects Intel to follow its traditional pattern and reduce prices next month (February 2003); faster HT chip expected to take crown from 3.06 GHz; 2.0A GHz likely to become obsolescent; 2.4 GHz expected to take bottom of P4 line `[ps=-1]`
