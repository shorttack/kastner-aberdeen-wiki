# PC Deals Weekly Update: January 26, 2003

> Contemporaneous Kastner weekly tracking of PC deals at Best Buy, Circuit City, CompUSA, and online retailers in January 2003. Notable coverage of vpr Matrix BestBuy-exclusive machines, HP/Compaq product transitions, eMachines value lineup, Dell price volatility, and Sony retail presence decline.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- VPR Matrix FT9100: P4 HT 3.06 GHz, DDR memory, slightly smaller HDD than $2000+ competitors; online unbundled prices competitive
- vpr Matrix machines won their price categories for past two weeks when offered as bundled weekly flyer deals
- Sony PCV-RZ14G: $1900 at BestBuy; 2.53 GHz P4, 120 GB HDD, 15-inch LCD, DVD writer, CD writer
- Sony PCV-RZ14G bundle includes 15-inch LCD flat panel monitor
- HP Pavilion xx3 (5x3 and 7x3) series reaching end-of-life; in stores since back-to-school August 2002
- HP Pavilion 534: $900 after rebates; Athlon 2400+, 512 MB DDR, DVD/CD-RW, nVidia GeForce 4, 17-inch CRT monitor; 24 months no-interest financing at BestBuy
- Compaq 6300 series reaching end-of-life; Compaq 6350 available at Circuit City as closeout
- HP won back first place from Dell in Q4 2002 using Pavilion 5x3 and 7x3 models
- All four eMachines models available; four machines under $900; T2460 ~$650 with monitor and printer at BestBuy
- Dell Dimension 8250 line price rose over $200 this week despite free shipping and $50-$150 instant rebates
- Dell Dimension 2350 now offers P4 2.4 GHz along with slower P4 and Celeron options
- Dell pricing oscillates by up to $300 week to week since early December 2002; attributed to data-warehouse price optimization
- Gateway free shipping offer back on 500 and 700 series desktops; 700 series decreased in price the most
- Sony Online: free overnight shipping on all Vaio desktops plus $100 mail-in rebates
- Sony's retail presence has dropped over the past six weeks as of 2003-01-26; Sony has cut back models vs. RX model line in October
- HP Online: free shipping, $50 off select models, $100 off customized PCs; best bundled deals at Circuit City or Best Buy
- CompUSA Online no longer offers one-cent shipping; standard shipping is $21.02 for all PCs as of 2003-01-26
- BestBuy.com offers free shipping; BestBuy stores are primary eMachines source
- All Circuit City AMD Athlon bundled deals include $100 gift cards; Circuit City flyer featured only four PCs this week
- VPR Matrix FT9100 described as 'a more affordable way to buy Intel's latest technology' compared to $2000+ competitors
