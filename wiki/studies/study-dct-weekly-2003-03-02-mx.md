# PC Deals Weekly Update: March 2, 2003

> Weekly PC deals tracking for March 2, 2003. Highlights Dell price volatility and a fleeting P4 2.8 GHz deal at $928, rapid decline in LCD flat panel monitor prices (15-inch under $200, 17-inch under $500), HP new all-in-one printer lineup, and per-retailer promotions. CompUSA.com returns to one-cent shipping.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Dell Dimension 4550 with P4 2.8 GHz, 256 MB DDR, 60 GB HDD, DVD, CD-RW, free 17-inch monitor: briefly $928 plus shipping
- Dell has the fastest changing prices of the major online brands; deals appear for a minute or an hour then disappear
- Dell online deals: $50 instant savings on 4550, $150 on 8250 series, free memory upgrades, monitor upgrades (first time observed)
- 15-inch LCD monitors under $200 seen in BestBuy flyers for past few weeks
- Dell or Gateway Online: 15-inch flat panel monitor for only $50 more than a traditional 17-inch monitor
- 17-inch LCD flat panel monitor price: under $500 (often $400) in March 2003, down from ~$700 pre-holidays 2002; some were $1000 with TV options
- Flat panel monitors are equivalent to a CRT monitor one inch larger: 17-inch flat panel equivalent to 18-inch CRT
- HP all-in-one printer new lineup launched 2003-03-02: HP 7140 at $699 MSRP (dual trays, duplex, JetDirect, 802.11 WiFi), 7130 at $499, 7110 at $399, 1210 at $149 (4800 dpi, printer/copier/scanner)
- BestBuy stores: $100 mail-in rebate for all computer packages (bundled deals)
- Circuit City: $100-$200 off all Pentium 4 processor systems; 12 months no interest
- CompUSA.com: one-cent shipping returned; continues selling older models after other retailers remove them (e.g., HP 763n)
- Gateway revamped website; severely cut back home PC models; 300 S Value discontinued; 300 series now starts at $799 (was $399 last week)
- Sony Online: free shipping on all Vaio desktops including customized models; new models introduced at competitive prices
- HP Online: free shipping, $1 CD-RW upgrade, $99 17-inch monitor with customized PC purchase; best bundled deals at CompUSA stores
- eMachines has new models out; BestBuy offers bundled deals on new machines
