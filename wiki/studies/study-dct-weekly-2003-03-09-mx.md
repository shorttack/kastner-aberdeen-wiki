# PC Deals Weekly Update: March 9, 2003

> Compaq leads mid-range and high-end deals for the week. Best deal: Compaq 6000T at BestBuy configuration station for $1,369.99 with P4 HT 3.06 GHz processor. Analysis covers Pentium price compression, AMD Athlon XP resurgence, Dell 4550/8250 promotions, Gateway product line cuts, and Intel supply-chain price-cut forecast.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Compaq 6000T at BestBuy configuration station: $1,369.99; P4 HT 3.06 GHz, 512 MB DDR, 80 GB HDD, DVD/CD-RW, 17-inch monitor, HP printer
- Compaq wins $900, $1050, $1200, and $1400 price categories; HP branding Compaq Presario as 'best value' machines is paying off
- Dell Dimension 2350 with P4 2.0A GHz: $809
- Compaq 6470Nx with P4 2.66 GHz leads $1,050 price category
- Market in stage of Pentium price compression: P4 3.06 GHz HT at $1400; modest price increase buys significantly more components
- AMD Athlon XP reappeared at faster clock rates over past couple of months; price compression opens breathing space for AMD
- Kastner forecasts Intel retail price drops over next month as Intel price cuts work through now-shorter supply chain
- P4 2.4 GHz no longer available in Dell Dimension 4550; P4 2.53 GHz is now bottom of line
- Dell online deals: $1000 off 8250, $50 off 4550, free CD-RW in second bay, first-time speaker upgrades
- BestBuy stores: $100 mail-in rebate on all bundled computer packages; BestBuy.com free shipping and 24 months no financing
- Circuit City: $100-$200 off all P4 systems; 12 months no interest; CircuitCity.com reintroduced free shipping
- CompUSA.com continues selling older models (e.g., HP 763n) after other retailers remove them; can yield closeout deals but requires comparison shopping
- Gateway 300 S Value discontinued; 300 series now starts at $799 instead of $399; website revamped with fewer home PC models
- Sony Online: free shipping on all Vaio desktops including customized; prices risen slightly this week
- HP Online: free shipping, $1 CD-RW upgrade, $99 17-inch monitor with customized PC; best bundled deals at CompUSA
- eMachines has new models; BestBuy carries bundled deals on new machines
