# PC Deals Weekly Update: March 16, 2003

> Intel Centrino wireless notebook platform launches as hot industry topic. Kastner recommends consumers wait for supply to build before expecting deals. Dell SMB site offers Dimension 4550 with P4 2.66 GHz and 17-inch CRT for $888 including shipping — Dell's first winning configuration at $900 price point in months. CompUSA features wireless networking bundle add-on deal and Viewsonic 19-inch LCD monitor at $699.97.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Intel Centrino wireless notebook platform launched week of 2003-03-16; supply constrained at retail
- Kastner assessment: Centrino supply will be hard to come by for a few weeks; consumers should wait for supply to build and more models before expecting PC deals
- Kastner positive on Centrino: good power, battery life, and built-in wireless; consumer versions expected for back-to-school season
- Dell SMB Dimension 4550 with P4 2.66 GHz, 512 MB DDR, nVidia GeForce 4, 17-inch CRT: $888 including shipping
- Dell SMB Dimension 4550 with P4 2.53 GHz, 256 MB DDR: $818
- Dell at $900 price point interpreted as counter-offensive after ceding lower market to HP; Intel price cuts allow Dell to quickly pass savings to consumers
- HP picked up market share after Dell conceded lower half of market; HP held lower price points for months prior to this week
- BestBuy stores: $150 off all P4 computer packages; free shipping on HP configuration station PCs; BestBuy.com free shipping and 18 months no financing
- Circuit City: $100 gift card on all desktop and notebook systems; 12 months no interest; CircuitCity.com reintroduced free shipping
- CompUSA: $100 off AMD Athlon XP bundles; $150 off P4 bundles; 12 months no-interest on systems over $599; featured HP and Compaq machines
- CompUSA: Viewsonic 19-inch LCD monitor at $699.97 — better price than found online at Pricegrabber.com
- CompUSA wireless networking add-on bundle with any desktop or notebook purchase: Belkin 802.11b router+USB adapter, Epson Stylus C42UX printer, 30-pack CD-R, Symantec Internet Security, PC Relocator software, 2 hours technician time
- Dell online deals: $150 instant savings on 4550, free CD-RW second bay, free HDD upgrade to 60 GB, free shipping; P4 2.4 GHz no longer option in Dimension 4550
- Gateway 300 S Value discontinued; 300 series now starts at $799 instead of $399; website revamped
- Sony Online: free shipping on all Vaio desktops including customized; prices risen slightly
- HP Online: free shipping on orders over $250; $1 CD-RW, $99 17-inch monitor with customized PC purchase
