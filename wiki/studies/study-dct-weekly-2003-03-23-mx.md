# PC Deals Weekly Update: March 23, 2003

> PC Deals market analysis for week of March 23, 2003. The $1200-$1400 price categories have thinned out while over-$1600 systems boom, targeting media and gaming users. HP launches second-generation media center desktop (Pavilion 864n) at $1,700. Dell SMB deal advantage fades. Gateway.com permanently adopts free shipping. Sony PCV-RS220 replaces RS100.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- $1200-$1400 price categories previously hot spots now have sparse pickings; systems over $1600 have boomed; over-$1600 machines target media-centric users and gamers
- HP Pavilion 864n second-gen media center desktop: $1,700; P4 2.66 GHz, 512 MB DDR, 160 GB HDD, DVD+RW/CD-RW, Creative Audigy sound, PVR software, nVidia GeForce 4 MX440
- BestBuy bundled deal: HP Media Center 864n with 15-inch LCD monitor and Klipsch speakers for $2,149.97
- BestBuy: $100 off all Celeron and Athlon packages; 24 months no interest with BestBuy credit card
- Circuit City: $100-$300 off all PC packages; 12 months no interest with CircuitCity charge card; CircuitCity.com free shipping
- Dell online deals: $150 off 8250, $50 off 4550, free shipping, free memory upgrade to 256 MB
- Dell's free memory upgrade to 256 MB described as poor deal for 4550 and especially 8250 — consumers at those price points need more than 256 MB
- Dell.com offers free printer, scanner, or digital camera — but accepting adds shipping fees; PC Deals excluded from configurations
- Gateway.com permanently adopts free shipping: 'Shipping always included' label under price of all configured models
- Sony PCV-RS100 discontinued; PCV-RS220 introduced for less than $100 more with faster processor and DVD+RW/+R capabilities
- eMachines.com carries T1842, T2042, T2260, T2460; BestBuy.com carries T4510; CircuitCity.com carries T4480; T4510 and T4480 both under $900
- CompUSA no weekly flyer; CompUSA.com has excellent deals on HP and Compaq pre-configured machines; continues selling older models after other retailers remove them
- PC Deals added several new HP Media Center models to spreadsheet this week
