# PC Deals Weekly Update: April 6, 2003

> PC Deals displays 20 desktops under $900 (9 under $600). eMachines, HP and Compaq dominate lower price points. Key insight: AMD Athlon 2600+ in Compaq 6430NX at $800 outperforms P4 2.53 GHz Compaq 6450NX at $900. Best Buy consistently leads on Sony PC prices. Compaq 8000 series more expensive than HP equivalent at hpshopping.com despite same 3.06 GHz P4 HT specs.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- PC Deals this week lists 20 desktops all under $900; 9 of those under $600
- Sub-$900 PCs no longer just Celeron machines as was case 6 months ago; now P4 2.4+ GHz and AMD Athlon machines populate this range
- Compaq 6430NX at BestBuy.com: Athlon 2600+, 512 MB DDR, 120 GB HDD, DVD-ROM/CD-RW: $800
- Compaq 6450NX at BestBuy.com: P4 2.53 GHz: $900 (slightly less powerful than Athlon 2600+ but $100 more)
- AMD Athlon processors less expensive than comparable Intel P4 processors: consumer pays less or gets better specs at same price
- Best Buy consistently (almost always) offers lowest prices on Sony PCs; leads market on all Sony models this week except PCV-RS220 (Sony.com only)
- Sony PCV-RS100 discontinued; PCV-RS220 replaces it for less than $100 more with faster processor and DVD+RW/+R; PCV-RS210 pre-order at BestBuy.com for $849.99
- CompUSA: HP media center 854n with 15-inch LCD flat panel at $1,700 ($150 off); Compaq s3200nx bundle at $750; Compaq s3300nx bundle at $950
- Dell 4550 and 8250 lines rose dramatically in price despite $50 and $150 off offers and free CD-RW/shipping; Kastner advises waiting for next week
- Compaq 8000 series with 3.06 GHz P4 at hpshopping.com costs over $300 more than equivalent HP 700 series 3.06 GHz P4; explains absence of Compaq in P4 HT price category
- eMachines revamped lineup: T2460, T2042, T1842 discontinued; new models launched at same price points; best deals at BestBuy and Circuit City bundled
- Circuit City: $100-$210 off all desktops and laptops; 12 months no interest; CircuitCity.com free shipping for limited time
- Gateway.com permanently free shipping: 'Shipping always included' label on all configured models
