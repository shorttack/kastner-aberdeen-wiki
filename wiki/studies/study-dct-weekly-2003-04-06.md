# PC Deals Weekly Update: April 6, 2003

> Feeling like your wallet is lighter than it used to be? Don't rule out being able to afford a new PC. This week, PC Deals displays 20 desktops that are all under $900, and 9 of these come in at under $600.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Feeling like your wallet is lighter than it used to be? Don't rule out being able to afford a new PC.
- BestBuy.com still offers free shipping. This week solidified a pattern we've noticed that Best Buy almost consistently offers the lowest prices on Sony PCs.
- This week, save $100-$210 on all desktops and laptops, and pay no interest for 12 months when buying with your Circuit City charge card. Circuitcity.com has reintroduced free shipping for a limited time on all its online items.
- CompUSA is featuring the new Compaq s3200nx and s3300nx machines in bundles at $750 and $950 respectively. The s3200nx is also available this week without a monitor at Circuitcity.com and other online locations.
- EMachines has once again revamped its line of models. If you were thinking about buying a T2460, T2042, or T1842, you're now out of luck because these models are gone from all the usual sources we check.
- Dell's online deals include $50 and $150 off the 4550 and 8250 series, respectively. Free CD-RW upgrade in second bay has returned for all models, as well as free shipping.
- Gateway.com has finally joined the free shipping bandwagon for good. At least that's what we're inferring from its new "Shipping always included" label under the price of any model you configure.
- Sony.com offers a plethora of models right now, although, like eMachines, the best deals usually come from bundled retail offers. The PCV-RS100 has disappeared, but for less than $100 more, the new PCV-RS220 offers a much faster processor and DVD+RW/+R capabilities.
