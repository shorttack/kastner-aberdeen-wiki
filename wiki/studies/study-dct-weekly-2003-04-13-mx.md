# PC Deals Weekly Update: April 13, 2003

> Dell end-of-quarter price cuts make this the best week to buy from Dell: $200 off 4550, nearly $300 off 8250 — deepest discounts in nearly a year of PC Deals tracking. Intel 875P Canterwood chipset launches with P4 3.0 GHz; 865 Springdale and 3.2 GHz P4 expected by July 4th. New Intel 800 MHz bus outperforms existing 533 MHz bus chips. Sony PCV-RZ series customized at Sony.com saves $330 vs. retail.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Dell Dimension 4550: ~$200 price decrease; Dell Dimension 8250: ~$300 price decrease; deepest Dell discounts in nearly a year of PC Deals tracking
- Dell end-of-quarter deals worsen in last couple weeks of fiscal quarter; Kastner advises acting now rather than waiting for next week
- Dell launched new 15-inch and 17-inch LCD flat panel monitors; older and newer options available with 4550 customization; 8250 only gets newer (pricier) model
- Intel 875P Canterwood chipset launched week of 2003-04-13 with P4 3.0 GHz; 800 MHz bus; P4 3.0 GHz with 800 MHz bus outperforms 3.06 GHz with 533 MHz bus
- Intel 865 Springdale chipset and new P4 lineup with 800 MHz bus + HT support arriving later Q2 2003; new king-of-hill 3.2 GHz P4 expected by July 4th
- P4 3.06 GHz with 533 MHz bus will drop in price as 800 MHz bus chips arrive; Dell already dropping prices on chips moving down food chain
- New 800 MHz bus chipsets will bring period of stability to PC market; fewer new introductions; PC makers can lower costs via larger unit volumes; consumers likely to see more stable prices
- Sony PCV-RZ22G at retail vs. customized PCV-RZ series at Sony.com: $330 less at Sony.com with larger 200 GB HDD; can eliminate Giga Pocket PVR option
- Compaq 8000 series with 3.06 GHz P4 at hp.com costs over $300 more than HP 700 series equivalent; reason Compaq absent from P4 HT price category
- HP Online undercuts Best Buy and Circuit City by over $30 on some Compaq models via $50 mail-in rebates and free shipping on orders over $250
- Best Buy stores: $150 off all notebooks by mail; 12 months no interest on PC bundles; free shipping on creation station PCs
- Circuit City: $100 gift card with any PC purchase; CircuitCity.com free shipping on all items
- CompUSA: free HP 3425 printer after rebates with computer+monitor bundle; Sony PCV-RS220 highlighted as very good deal
- eMachines revamped lineup again; T2460/T2042/T1842 gone; new models at same price points; best deals at BestBuy and Circuit City bundled
