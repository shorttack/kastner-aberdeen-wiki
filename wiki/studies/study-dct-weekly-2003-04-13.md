# PC Deals Weekly Update: April 13, 2003

> Spring Cleaning It's spring. Dell is cutting prices. All is status quo?


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Spring Cleaning It's spring. Dell is cutting prices.
- At Best Buy stores this week, save $150 by mail on all notebooks, enjoy no interest for 12 months on PC bundles, and take advantage of free shipping on creation station PCs. BestBuy.com still offers free shipping.
- Receive a $100 gift card with any PC purchase at Circuit City stores. Circuitcity.com has reintroduced free shipping on all its online items.
- Get a free HP 3425 printer after rebates with any computer & monitor bundle. PC Deals included this free printer offer in the "Printer" column for all CompUSA bundled deals.
- Wow! This is the week to purchase a PC from Dell.com.
- EMachines has once again revamped its line of models. If you were thinking about buying a T2460, T2042, or T1842, you're now out of luck because these models are gone from all the usual sources we check.
- Gateway.com has finally joined the free shipping bandwagon for good. At least that's what we're inferring from its new "Shipping always included" label under the price of any model you configure.
- Sony.com offers a plethora of models right now, although, like eMachines, the best deals usually come from bundled retail offers. That being said, if you're interested in a PCV-RZ series Sony PC, then you'll want to customize one at Sony.com.
