# PC Deals Weekly Update: April 20, 2003

> Dell Direct Deals email coupon program introduced: $50 off Dell Home System over $999. Dell wins PC Deals 'Value $600' category for first time in one year with a $559 P4 2.4 GHz system with 17-inch monitor (after $150 rebate). Dell also pushing P4 HT 3.06 GHz with $200 off mail-in rebate on 4550/8250/8300 Dimension lines. BestBuy and Best Buy matched some hpshopping.com Compaq prices this week.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Dell Dimension: 2.4 GHz P4, 256 MB DDR, 30 GB HDD, DVD-ROM, 17-inch monitor at $559 after $150 mail-in rebate — first time Dell wins PC Deals Value $600 category in one year
- Dell's $559 P4 with 17-inch monitor extraordinary — first time in one year of PC Deals research Dell wins Value $600 category; deal expected to last only days
- Dell Direct Deals email coupon program: $50 off any Dell Home System over $999; coupons combine with other offers; short expiration (ours expires April 22)
- Dell pushing P4 HT 3.06 GHz: $200 off mail-in rebate when customizing Dimension 4550, 8250, or new 8300; plus free shipping, memory upgrades, DVD+RW/+R in second bay on non-2350 models
- Dell Dimension 8300 new model introduced; mentioned for first time in PC Deals
- Best Buy stores: $100 rebate on all Intel packages; 12 months no interest; BestBuy.com free shipping
- Circuit City: $100 gift card with any desktop or notebook purchase; 12 months no interest; CircuitCity.com free shipping
- PCV-RZ22G at retail vs. customized PCV-RZ at Sony.com: $330 less at Sony.com with 200 GB HDD; can eliminate Giga Pocket PVR which adds cost at retail
- Compaq 8000 series 3.06 GHz P4 at hpshopping.com costs over $300 more than HP 700 series equivalent; explains Compaq absence from P4 HT price category
- hpshopping.com undercuts Best Buy and Circuit City by over $30 on some Compaq models via $50 mail-in rebates and free shipping over $250; Best Buy matched some Compaq prices this week
- eMachines revamped lineup; T2460/T2042/T1842 gone; new models at same price points; best deals at BestBuy and Circuit City bundled
- Gateway.com permanently free shipping: 'Shipping always included' label
