# PC Deals Weekly Update: May 11, 2003

> Drastic price reductions on high-end Intel P4 processors. Dell 4550 with P4 2.8 GHz and monitor under $1,200. Best Buy HP bundle with P4 HT 3.06 GHz, 17-inch monitor, and printer for $1,280 — triple-checked as exceptional value. Dell Dimension 8300 with new P4 3.0 GHz (faster than 3.06 GHz HT) just under $1,600 with 15-inch LCD. Most manufacturers have not yet dropped 3.06 GHz below $1,600.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Dell 4550 with P4 2.8 GHz and monitor: under $1,200 — drastic price drop
- HP bundle at Best Buy stores: P4 HT 3.06 GHz, 17-inch HP monitor, and printer for $1,280 — triple verified in Sunday newspaper flyer
- HP P4 HT 3.06 GHz system at $1,280 with monitor and printer described as exceptional; Kastner urges immediate purchase
- Dell Dimension 8300 with new P4 3.0 GHz: just under $1,600 with 15-inch LCD monitor
- P4 3.0 GHz with 800 MHz bus is actually faster than P4 3.06 GHz HT with 533 MHz bus despite lower GHz number; most manufacturers have not yet offered 3.0 GHz or dropped 3.06 GHz below $1,600
- Dell Dimension 2350 deals: free CD burner in second bay, free upgrade to 60 GB HDD, free upgrade to 256 MB DDR; all other models get free shipping, HDD upgrades, CD burners
- BestBuy stores: $100 rebate on all computer packages; 12 months no interest; BestBuy.com free shipping and no interest until January 2004
- Circuit City: save up to $310 on Desktop Computer Packages; 12 months no interest; CircuitCity.com free shipping
- CompUSA.com one-cent shipping for limited time; no weekly advertisements in Boston Globe or Atlanta Journal-Constitution
- Compaq 8000 series 3.06 GHz P4 at hpshopping.com costs over $300 more than HP 700 series equivalent; explains Compaq absence from P4 HT price category
- hpshopping.com undercuts Best Buy and Circuit City by over $30 on some Compaq models; $50 rebates; free shipping over $250; Best Buy matched some Compaq prices this week
- eMachines revamped lineup; T2460/T2042/T1842 gone; new models at same price points; best deals at BestBuy and Circuit City bundled
