# PC Deals Weekly Update: May 18, 2003

> PC Deals eliminates separate HT category as P4 HT prices fall to mainstream levels; HT machines now listed alongside standard P4 machines. Kastner provides consumer perspective on PC value: sub-$600 machines meet most consumer needs. eMachines consistently impressive value but bundled deals often price below CPU-alone retail price. CompUSA.com introduces in-store pickup. Gateway includes free Sims software this week. Dell Direct Deals coupon now offers only free shipping — same as current site deal.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- P4 HT prices have fallen to mainstream levels; PC Deals eliminates separate HT category; HT machines now compared side-by-side with standard P4 machines by price
- Majority of US households own a PC; most replacement buyers not using for gaming/movie making; sub-$600 machines meet typical consumer needs; can add DVD burner or flat panel later
- eMachines consistently impressive value; bundled deals at Best Buy and Circuit City stores in past month often priced below retail price of the CPU alone; Kastner recommends waiting for bundled deals
- Sub-$600 PCs cost less than most 17-19-inch flat panel monitors in May 2003
- CompUSA.com introduced in-store pickup for online purchases; BestBuy.com and CircuitCity.com have offered this feature for some time
- CompUSA.com: one-cent shipping for limited time; no weekly advertisements in Boston Globe or Atlanta Journal-Constitution
- Dell Dimension 2350: free CD burner in second bay, free upgrade to 60 GB HDD, free upgrade to 256 MB DDR; all other models: free shipping, HDD upgrades, CD burners in second bay
- BestBuy stores: $100-$150 rebate on all computer packages; 18 months no interest financing; BestBuy.com free shipping and no interest until January 2004
- Circuit City: save up to $350 on Desktop Computer Packages; 12 months no interest; CircuitCity.com free shipping
- Gateway Online: free Sims basic software package this week; permanently free shipping
- Sony Online: free shipping and free Epson C42UX printer after mail-in rebate this week; customizing allows eliminating Giga Pocket PVR
- Compaq 8000 series 3.06 GHz P4 at hpshopping.com costs over $300 more than HP 700 series equivalent; explains Compaq absence from P4 HT category
- hpshopping.com undercuts Best Buy and Circuit City by over $30 on some Compaq models; $50 rebates; free shipping over $250; Best Buy matched some Compaq prices
