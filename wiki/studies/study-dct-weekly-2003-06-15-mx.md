# PC Deals Weekly Update: June 15, 2003

> Post-Father's Day lull analysis: deals dwindling as back-to-school season approaches. Kastner recommends circuitcity.com for clearance HP, Sony, and eMachines models; notes HP's new flat-panel monitors and compact peripherals under $300.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Circuit City's online site has the lowest prices on some HP, Sony, and eMachines models as clearance begins for older models
- HP 505n desktop at $369.99 (clearance)
- $100 mail-in rebate on all computer packages; 18-month no-interest financing on CPU bundles
- Free shipping; no interest until January 2004
- Save up to $350 on select clearance Desktop Computer Packages; no interest for 12 months
- CompUSA.com continues selling older PC models after BestBuy.com and CircuitCity.com have removed them, enabling deals on shelf-clearing models but risking near-parity pricing with newer models
- Free shipping on all models; free hard drive upgrades; $50 off Dimension 4600 line; $100 off 8300 and XPS models
- Dell has new 15" and 17" flat panel monitors; older E151FPb 15" LCD at bargain price
- eMachines revamped its line of models; newest models launched at same price points as predecessors
- Circuit City and Best Buy sell eMachines models not available at eMachines.com; bundled retail deals continue to offer best value
- Gateway.com changes online graphics and layout almost weekly; ready-to-buy configurations often include extras that inflate prices; recommends customizing especially high-end 700 series
- Free shipping and free Epson C42UX printer after mail-in rebate at Sony Online
- Sony PCV-RZ32G desktop at $1299.99; exclusive to Sony Online
- Free shipping on all HP and Compaq PCs; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors purchased with desktops
- HP phasing out models like Compaq 3000 series; expect HP-Compaq current models to disappear from online sites except overstock vendors like CompUSA and PC Connection
- Post-Father's Day lull; with back-to-school season about a month away Kastner predicts fewer outstanding bargains rather than lower prices during the interim
- Flat panel monitors available at under $300; manufacturers capitalizing on space-premium trend with compact monitors as thin as 1.5 inches
