# PC Deals Weekly Update: June 15, 2003

> Not all that much to comment on this week. With Father's Day past, the best deals are dwindling in number. We're not sure whether the normal lull in sales until back-to-school season starts in a month will mean retailers will lower prices or whether there will be fewer deals.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Not all that much to comment on this week. With Father's Day past, the best deals are dwindling in number.
- At Best Buy stores this week, save $100 with Best Buy's mail-in rebate on all computer packages. Also receive no interest financing for 18 months on all these CPU bundles.
- Save up to $350 on select clearance Desktop Computer Packages and enjoy no interest for 12 months. Circuitcity.com has free shipping on all its online items.
- CompUSA.com tends to continue selling "older" PC models after BestBuy.com and CircuitCity.com have removed them. This means you can get better deals on models that retailers want off the shelves to make room for the newer versions.
- Dell's deals include free shipping on all models and free hard drive upgrades. Also look for $50 off instantly on the 4600 line and $100 the 8300 and XPS models.
- EMachines has once again revamped its line of models. EMachine's newest models launched at the same price points as its former brethren.
- Gateway.com seems to change its online graphics and layout almost weekly. While these frequent changes are initially confusing, Gateway does endeavor to make its website as user-friendly as possible.
- Sony.com offers a plethora of models right now, although, like eMachines, the best deals usually come from bundled retail offers. Receive free shipping, and a free Epson C42UX printer after mail-in rebate at Sony Online this week.
- HP Online offers competitive prices on its retail models, although national retailers usually carry well-priced bundled deals. For a few weeks www.hpshopping.com had the best prices on both HP and Compaq PCs.
