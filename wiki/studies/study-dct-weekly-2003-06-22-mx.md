# PC Deals Weekly Update: June 22, 2003

> HP announces m200 Media Center PC series with Intel Hyper-Threading up to 3.2 GHz; Dell Dimension 4600 prices drop over $100; Kastner notes desktop deals drought prediction was premature.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- HP announced m200 Media Center PC series on June 18; starting under $999; retail versions from $1200 available mid-July; online customized desktops available July 9
- HP m200 Media Center series supports full range of Intel HT processors including new 3.2 GHz model
- Kastner recommends HP A250n series as alternative for consumers wanting latest Intel HT technology while awaiting m200 retail availability
- Updating Media Center line is a smart move by HP; current 800n series does not offer consumers the latest technology
- Dell Dimension 4600 line prices decreased by over $100 this week
- Dell offers customization with 3.2 GHz HT processor at $290 premium over 3.0 GHz; Kastner has not added 3.2 GHz HT to PC Deals coverage due to price
- Free memory upgrade on all models; free CD-RW in second bay; $50 mail-in rebate on Dimension 2350; $100 mail-in rebates on all other models; no free shipping this week
- $100 mail-in rebate on all computer packages; 18-month no-interest financing on CPU bundles
- Free shipping; no interest until January 2004
- Save up to $450 on select Desktop and Laptop Computer Packages; no interest for 12 months
- CircuitCity.com had the lowest prices on clearance desktops the prior week but deals have vanished this week; very few best deals on any PCs
- CompUSA.com continues selling older PC models after major retailers remove them; risk of paying near-new-model prices for outdated hardware
- Circuit City and Best Buy sell eMachines models not available at eMachines.com; bundled retail deals offer best value on eMachines desktops
- Gateway prices on customized PCs have not changed over past few weeks; shipping always included
- Gateway not lowering prices as newer technology emerges; stable pricing makes forecasting easy but limits competitiveness
- Free shipping and free Epson C42UX printer after mail-in rebate at Sony Online
- Sony PCV-RZ34G added to RZ line at $1599.99; exclusive to Sony Online
- Free shipping on all HP and Compaq PCs over $250; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors purchased with desktops
- HP phasing out Compaq 3000 series; HP-Compaq current models expected to disappear from online sites except overstock vendors like CompUSA
- Desktop deals drought prediction proved premature; prices remain competitive across multiple vendors suggesting summer buying opportunities
