# PC Deals Weekly Update: July 6, 2003

> eMachines launches new back-to-school desktop lineup; Gateway 700XL upgraded to Pentium 4 HT 3.2 GHz; Dell Dimension 2350 loses competitiveness in sub-$1000 range. Kastner recommends eMachines, HP, or Compaq for sub-$1000 buyers.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- eMachines new back-to-school desktop lineup: T2341 at $399 (unbundled) with CD-RW; T2482 at $499 with 256MB DDR and 80GB; T2615 at $649 with AMD Athlon 2600+, 512MB DDR, 120GB, dual optical; T2625 adds DVD-RW for $100 more
- eMachines T2615 with AMD Athlon 2600+ processor, 512MB DDR, 120GB at $649 unbundled; outstanding price performer in $750 category
- eMachines mission is affordable PCs without mail-in rebate hassle; Kastner endorses this approach noting rebates can take over three months
- Best Buy revamped website; weekly ad shows bundled deals on two eMachines and HP new models; $100 mail-in rebate on all computer packages; 12-month no-interest financing
- BestBuy.com carrying eMachines T2260 at $100 more than new bundled W2260 ($449.97) at Best Buy stores; Kastner warns against purchasing from BestBuy.com until site issues resolved
- Save up to $250 on select Desktop and Laptop Packages; 12-month no-interest financing; weekly ad features two new eMachines models
- CircuitCity.com carries newest HP A200 and A250 line models including A220n and A230n with AMD Athlon processors at attractive prices
- CompUSA.com continues selling older PC models after major retailers remove them; risk of paying near-new-model prices for outdated hardware
- Dell Dimension 2350 with non-HT 2.4 GHz processor jumped to nearly $1000; Dell not competitive in sub-$1000 market; Kastner recommends eMachines, HP, or Compaq instead
- All Dell models offer free monitor upgrades to 15" flat panel; free CD upgrades in second bay on most models; free memory upgrades on 2350, 8300, XPS
- Customizing Dell with 3.2 GHz HT processor continues to yield high prices versus HP in $1600+ category; Dell 3.2 GHz HT not yet added to PC Deals
- Gateway 700XL now comes standard with Pentium 4 HT 3.2 GHz processor at same price; 500X saw slight price drop
- hpshopping.com has clearance Compaq prices: Compaq 6470NX at $759.99; Compaq S3200NX at $507.99; deals likely to disappear soon
- HP expanded A250 line to resemble Compaq S4000 series; 500, 700, and Media Center customization options removed; A250e wins $1200 category; A250y at ~$1700 offers 3.2 GHz Intel at 3.0 GHz price
- New HP A250 PCs compete directly with Compaq; previously Compaq often undercut HP's prices
- Free shipping on HP and Compaq PCs over $250; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors purchased with desktops
- Free shipping at Sony Online this week; customization allows eliminating Giga Pocket Personal Video Recorder not available in retail models
