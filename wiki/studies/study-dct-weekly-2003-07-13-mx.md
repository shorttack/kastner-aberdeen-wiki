# PC Deals Weekly Update: July 13, 2003

> Dell replaces Dimension 2350 with 2400; eMachines and HP new desktops dominate weekly retail flyers; clearance HP-Compaq deals available but consumer caution advised on pricing. Kastner tracks Gateway 700XL HT upgrade and HP A250 line dominance.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Dell replaced Dimension 2350 with newer Dimension 2400; Dimension 2400 with same specs as prior-week 2350 priced at $834
- Dell Dimension 2400 at $834 is a better price than last week's 2350 near $1000, but still does not compete well in that price range
- Free shipping and CD-RW upgrades in second bay on all models; $50 off Dimension 4600 and 4600C; $150 off 8300 and XPS; Dimension 2400 has no rebate
- Customizing Dell with 3.2 GHz HT processor continues to yield high prices versus HP $1600+ category; Dell HT configuration still not added to PC Deals
- Circuit City and Best Buy sell all four eMachines new line-up models plus a few older models; bundled deals from national retailers best value; eMachines.com best for research
- Gateway 700XL maintains same price but now comes standard with Pentium 4 HT 3.2 GHz processor; 500X had slight price drop previous week
- Save $100-$150 with mail-in rebate on select computer packages; 12-month no-interest financing on CPU bundles; revamped website noted
- BestBuy.com carrying many low-end Compaq and HP machines with unverifiable SKUs; Kastner cautious about purchasing until site issues resolved; T2260 still overpriced vs. bundled W2260
- Save up to $200 on select Desktop and Laptop Packages; 12-month no-interest financing; weekly ad features eMachines and HP new models
- CircuitCity.com carries newest HP A200 and A250 lines including A220n and A230n with AMD Athlon processors at attractive prices
- CompUSA.com continues selling older PC models after major retailers remove them; risk of near-new-model pricing on outdated hardware
- hpshopping.com has clearance Compaq prices: 6470NX at $759.99; S3200NX at $507.99; deals likely to disappear soon
- HP A250 line expanded to resemble Compaq S4000 series; 500, 700, Media Center customization options removed; A250e wins $1200 category; A250y at ~$1700 offers 3.2 GHz at 3.0 GHz pricing
- New HP A250 PCs compete directly with Compaq; previously Compaq often undercut HP
- eMachines and HP new desktops dominate Best Buy and Circuit City weekly flyers this week; more HP Media Center PCs expected in coming weeks
- Excellent clearance prices on HP-Compaq desktops but consumer research advised; some outdated models at Best Buy stores priced near original market price
- Free shipping at Sony Online this week; customization allows eliminating Giga Pocket PVR standard in retail models
- Free shipping on HP and Compaq PCs over $250; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors purchased with desktops
