# PC Deals Weekly Update: July 26, 2003

> PC Deals stops tracking old eMachines, HP, and Compaq models; accessory prices noted as excellent including 802.11g routers and HP psc 2110 all-in-one at $100. Dell prices spike upward; HP M200 Media Center PC pricing criticized as over-the-top. eMachines T2605 wins $600 category at CircuitCity.com.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- PC Deals stops tracking old eMachines, HP, and Compaq desktop models because new line-ups compete at same price points; clearance deals still available at hpshopping.com, pcconnection.com, pcdeals.com
- CompUSA advertises wireless 802.11g router running at 54 Mbps for $100
- HP psc 2110 all-in-one printer/scanner/copier at $100; world's smallest all-in-one; same desk footprint as small flatbed scanner; Kastner impressed with color copy capabilities
- Save $100-$150 with mail-in rebate on select computer packages; 12-month no-interest financing; impressed with Creation Station HP and Compaq desktops in flyer
- Free shipping; no interest until January 2004; still carrying low-end Compaq and HP machines with unverifiable SKUs
- Save up to $200 on select Desktop and Laptop Packages; 12-month no-interest financing
- CircuitCity.com carries HP A200, A250, and Media Center 200 lines; A220n and A230n with AMD Athlon at attractive prices
- CircuitCity.com offers eMachines T2605 (Athlon 2600+, 512MB DDR) winning $600 category; competes with $750 category winners
- CompUSA is first to offer HP M290N in bundled deal at $2800; Kastner does not recommend as price performer; HP A250y series at ~$1800 offers nearly equivalent specs for $1000 less
- CompUSA.com continues selling older PC models after major retailers remove them; risk of near-new-model pricing on outdated hardware
- Dell promotions include free shipping, CD-RW upgrades, $50 off 4600/4600C, $100 off 8300, $150 off XPS; but configured prices increased dramatically: 4600 and 8300 jumped over $250 this week
- Dell's promotional offers are not a good indicator of final PC pricing; Kastner suspects Dell will lower prices as back-to-school season draws nearer
- Customizing Dell with 3.2 GHz HT continues to yield high prices versus HP in $1600+ category; not yet added to PC Deals
- Gateway 700XL still at same price with Pentium 4 HT 3.2 GHz standard; 500X slight price drop previous week; shipping always included
- HP fully released M200 Media Center series with DVD+RWs and media software standard; HP A250y at ~$1700 wins most expensive category offering 3.2 GHz at 3.0 GHz prices
- HP M200 Media Center pricing over-the-top; HP A250y series customized plus DVD editing software still costs less than HP Media Center PCs
- Free shipping on HP and Compaq PCs over $250; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors purchased with desktops
- Free shipping at Sony Online; customization allows eliminating Giga Pocket PVR
