# PC Deals Weekly Update: August 3, 2003

> Back-to-school buying frenzy begins; Best Buy and Circuit City lead with bundled deals. Walmart Supercenter reviewed for PC deals, found competitive on Sony, HP, and Compaq pricing but poor monitor selection. CompUSA exclusively carries HP A290n. Dell prices still not competitive.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Back-to-school buying frenzy launched this week; Best Buy and Circuit City have most numerous and best-priced offers
- Circuit City bundled deal: eMachines T2605 at $679.99 with 15" Samsung LCD monitor and Z705 Lexmark printer
- Best Buy bundle deal: HP A250n with 17" HP MX703 monitor and HP 3650 printer at $1039.99
- Walmart Supercenter carries HP desktop with 17" flat panel monitor, Athlon 2800+ processor, and DVD-writer for under $1200; prices on Sony, HP, and Compaq PCs competitive
- Walmart has a nice array of HP printers competitive with Best Buy prices; monitor selection poor, especially flat panels; only one unknown brand available
- Walmart and Best Buy/Circuit City lack strong sales floor support; Walmart has no visible salespeople outside registers; best for informed buyers only
- Compaq pricing on customized desktops at hpshopping.com increased, especially on Compaq monitors; increased prices may be deterring retailers from bundling Compaq
- CompUSA exclusively carries HP A290n desktop; state-of-the-art specs but very high price
- CompUSA.com continues selling older PC models; in-store pickup feature helps save on shipping
- Dell offers CD-RW upgrades in second bay and free hard drive upgrades; $50 off 2400; $150 off 4600; $200 off 8300 and XPS
- Dell prices dropped from prior week's high but still not at competitive level Dell is known for
- Customizing Dell with 3.2 GHz HT continues to yield high prices versus HP in $1600+ category; not yet added to PC Deals
- eMachines T2605 (Athlon 2600+, 512MB DDR) wins $600 category at circuitcity.com; competes with $750 category winners
- Gateway 700XL now comes standard with Pentium 4 HT 3.2 GHz at same price; shipping always included
- HP A250e wins $1200 category; A250y at ~$1700 wins most expensive category with 3.2 GHz Intel at 3.0 GHz pricing
- HP M200 Media Center PC series pricing over-the-top; DVD+RW and media software standard but customizing A250y plus DVD editing software still costs less
- Free shipping on HP and Compaq PCs over $250; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; free DVD-ROM upgrades; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors purchased with desktops
- Free shipping at Sony Online; customization allows eliminating Giga Pocket PVR
