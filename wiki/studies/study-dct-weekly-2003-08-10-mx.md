# PC Deals Weekly Update: August 10, 2003

> Peak back-to-school deals week; ads from Best Buy, Circuit City, Staples, CompUSA, and HP in Boston Globe. Dell and Compaq drop prices noticeably. Dimension 4600 with P4 2.8 GHz HT, 512MB DDR, 120GB falls from $1418 to $1229. Linksys 802.11b WiFi at $89.99 after rebates at Best Buy.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Peak back-to-school deals week; ads from Best Buy, Circuit City, Staples, CompUSA, and HP appeared in Boston Globe; Best Buy and Circuit City led with most numerous and best-priced offers
- Circuit City bundled offer: eMachines T2605 at $679.99 with 15" Samsung LCD monitor and Z705 Lexmark printer
- Best Buy deal: HP A250n with 17" HP MX703 monitor and HP 3650 printer at $1039.99
- Dell Dimension 4600 with P4 2.8 GHz HT, 512MB DDR, 120GB hard drive fell from $1418 last week to $1229 this week
- Compaq S8000T series desktop prices decreased over $200 in some cases this week
- Dell offers free DVD+RW/+R upgrade in second bay and free shipping on all models; $100 off 4600; $150 off 8300
- Dell prices at seasonal low; Kastner recommends buying ASAP
- Customizing Dell with 3.2 GHz HT continues to yield high prices versus HP in $1600+ category; not yet added to PC Deals
- Save $150 with mail-in rebate on select computer packages; 12-month no-interest financing on CPU bundles
- Free shipping; no interest until January 2004
- Save up to $400 on select Desktop Computer Packages; 12-month no-interest financing
- CircuitCity.com carries HP A200, A250, and Media Center 200 lines including A220n and A230n with AMD Athlon at attractive prices
- CompUSA exclusively carries HP A290n desktop; state-of-the-art specs but very high price; in-store pickup available
- Gateway 700XL comes standard with Pentium 4 HT 3.2 GHz at same price; shipping always included
- HP customized desktop showed little to no price change week-over-week; HP A250e wins $1200 category; A250y at ~$1700 wins most expensive category
- HP M200 Media Center pricing over-the-top; A250y plus DVD editing software costs less than HP Media Center PCs
- Linksys 802.11b router plus PC card for laptop at $89.99 after rebates at Best Buy; Kastner notes this is hard to pass up for dorm rooms; little profit margin left in WiFi
- eMachines T2605 (Athlon 2600+, 512MB DDR) wins $600 category; competes with $750 category winners
- Free shipping on HP and Compaq PCs over $250; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; free DVD-ROM upgrades; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors with desktops
