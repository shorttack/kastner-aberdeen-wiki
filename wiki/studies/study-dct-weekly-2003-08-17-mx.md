# PC Deals Weekly Update: August 17, 2003

> Dell prices surge over $200 from prior week's seasonal low; HP-Compaq remain competitive with Compaq prices dropping slightly. Best Buy bundled HP A250y with 2.6 GHz HT, 256MB DDR, 80GB, DVD/CD-RW, 15" LCD, and HP color printer for $900. Sony PCV-RS210 discontinued from PC Deals.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Dell Dimension desktop prices surged over $200 from prior week's seasonal low; Kastner does not recommend purchasing Dell this week especially the Dimension 2400
- Last week was great time to customize at dell.com; this week prices surged over $200; represents Dell's back-to-school seasonal pricing pattern
- Dell offers free hard drive upgrades up to 80GB; free monitor upgrades to 19" CRT or 15" LCD flat panel; $50 off 4600; $100 off 8300
- Customizing Dell with 3.2 GHz HT continues to yield high prices versus HP in $1600+ category; not yet added to PC Deals
- Best Buy stores: customized HP Pavilion A250y desktop with 2.6 GHz HT, 256MB DDR, 80GB, DVD/CD-RW, 15" LCD monitor, HP color printer for $900
- Save $150 with mail-in rebate on select computer packages; 12-month no-interest financing on CPU bundles
- Free shipping; no interest until January 2004; carrying low-end Compaq and HP with unverifiable SKUs
- Save up to $400 on select desktop computer packages; 12-month no-interest financing
- CircuitCity.com carries HP A200, A250, and Media Center 200 lines; A220n and A230n with AMD Athlon at attractive prices
- CompUSA.com continues selling older PC models; in-store pickup feature helps save on shipping
- Compaq prices dropped a few dollars this week at hpshopping.com; HP-Compaq continue to impress with reasonable prices at all desktop levels
- Gateway.com prices rarely change; Gateway usually does not compete as well as vendors that fluctuate prices; shipping always included
- Sony PCV-RS210 no longer exists at any major retail websites; eliminated from PC Deals tracking
- HP A250 line resemble Compaq S4000 series; A250e wins $1200 category; A250y at ~$1700 wins most expensive category with 3.2 GHz at 3.0 GHz pricing
- HP M200 Media Center pricing over-the-top; A250y series plus DVD editing software costs less than HP Media Center PCs
- Free shipping on HP and Compaq PCs over $250; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; free DVD-ROM upgrades; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors with desktops
- Circuit City and Best Buy sell all four eMachines new lineup models plus older models; bundled deals best value; emachines.com best for research
