# PC Deals Weekly Update: August 17, 2003

> Last week was a great time to customize a new desktop at www.dell.com. This week, Dimension desktop prices jolted upwards in some cases over $200. But this doesn't mean that other online retailers that allow customization have increased their prices.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Last week was a great time to customize a new desktop at www.dell.com. This week, Dimension desktop prices jolted upwards in some cases over $200.
- Best Buy has revamped its Web site completely. You will find the link to its weekly ad at the bottom, center of the home page along with the store locator link.
- Save up to $400 on select desktop computer packages and enjoy no interest for 12 months. Circuitcity.com continues its free shipping offer on all of its online items.
- No CompUSA flyer appeared in the Atlanta Journal-Constitution this week. CompUSA.com tends to continue selling "older" PC models after BestBuy.com and CircuitCity.com have removed them.
- Dell currently offers free hard drive upgrades up to 80 GB and free monitor upgrades to a 19" CRT or 15" LCD flat panel. With the exception of the 2400, Dell offers the following mail-in rebates this week: $50 off the 4600 and $100 off the 8300.
- Circuit City and Best Buy sell all four models in eMachines' new lineup and they continue to sell a few older models. Bundled deals from these national retailers supply the best bang for your buck on eMachines desktops.
- The prices on the PCs we customize at Gateway.com rarely change. This means Gateway usually does not compete as well as other vendors that fluctuate their prices.
- Sony.com offers a plethora of models right now although, as with eMachines, the best deals usually come from bundled retail offers. The PCV-RS210 model no longer exists at any major retail websites so we have eliminated it from PC Deals.
