# PC Deals Weekly Update: August 24, 2003

> Gateway lowers 500S, 500X, and 500XL prices by $100 in response to Dell's announced 6%-22% price cuts. Dell Dimension 2400 drops under $900; Dimension 4600 falls over $100; Dimension 8300 rises slightly. Kastner concludes Dell's cuts level inflated prices rather than beat competition.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **dct**._


## Top observations

- Dell announced price cuts ranging from 6% in consumer desktops to 22% for business servers; Dimension 2400 dropped under $900; Dimension 4600 prices fell over $100; Dimension 8300 rose slightly
- Dell price cuts leveled recently inflated prices but do not bring desktop prices to a range that completely out-does the competition
- Dell offers free CD-RW in second bay and free shipping; $150 instant savings on Dimension 4600; $200 instant savings on Dimension 8300
- Customizing Dell with 3.2 GHz HT continues to yield high prices versus HP in $1600+ category; not yet added to PC Deals
- Gateway lowered prices by $100 on 500S, 500X, and 500XL models; 500S and 500X price drops visible in PC Deals this week; shipping always included
- Gateway 500 series $100 price drop suspected to be competitive response to Dell's announced price cuts
- Save $150 with mail-in rebate on select computer packages; 12-month no-interest financing; Creation Station HP and Compaq desktops featured
- Free shipping; no interest until January 2004; carrying low-end Compaq and HP with unverifiable SKUs
- Save up to $400 on select desktop computer packages; 12-month no-interest financing
- CircuitCity.com carries HP A200, A250, and Media Center 200 lines including A220n and A230n with AMD Athlon at attractive prices
- CompUSA.com continues selling older PC models; in-store pickup feature helps save on shipping
- HP A250 line resembles Compaq S4000 series; A250e wins $1200 category; A250y at ~$1700 wins most expensive category with 3.2 GHz at 3.0 GHz pricing
- HP M200 Media Center pricing over-the-top; A250y plus DVD editing software costs less than HP Media Center PCs
- Free shipping on HP and Compaq PCs over $250; $50 mail-in rebate on all Compaq Presario desktops; free DVD-ROM upgrades; $50 mail-in rebate on monitors with desktops
- Sony PCV-RS210 no longer exists at any major retail websites; eliminated from PC Deals tracking
- Circuit City and Best Buy sell all four eMachines new lineup models; bundled retail deals best value; emachines.com best for research
