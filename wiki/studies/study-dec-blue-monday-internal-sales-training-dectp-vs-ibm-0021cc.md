# DEC 'Blue Monday' Internal Sales Training: DECtp Strategy vs IBM and Tandem

> Verbatim transcript of DEC's 'Blue Monday' internal sales training event (Burlington, VT, 1 Aug 1988) on Digital's transaction-processing strategy versus IBM and Tandem. Aberdeen Group's John Logan and Peter Kastner predict DEC will become the TP vendor of choice in the 1990s; Steve Smith of Payne-Webber argues DEC TP will drive demand for bigger systems. Heavy benchmark content: VAX 8830 ≈ IBM 3090-200E at one-fifth the cost; DEC TP revenue estimated at $1B/year, equal to Tandem; TP market $21B/year growing 2x the broader computer market.


_Published 1988, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **transcript**._
