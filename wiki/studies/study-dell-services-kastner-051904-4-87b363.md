# Dell Services — Discussion Materials

> Aberdeen discussion deck prepared for Dell executives analyzing the restructuring of IT professional services. Argues services are beginning a major cost restructuring mirroring US manufacturing 30 years prior, driven by offshore labor arbitrage, BPO/On Demand account control, and ERP-vendor hosted solutions. Proposes a four-stage partner-ecosystem maturity model and asks where Dell sits on that continuum.


_Published 2004, author **Peter S. Kastner (Executive VP, Research, Aberdeen Group)**, type **topic-analysis**._


## Top observations

- Larger providers and ERP vendors re-discovering all-inclusive hosted solutions as means to deliver value, flexibility, transformation and scalable solutions. `[ps=5]`
- Offshore labor arbitrage is a key PSO issue. `[ps=5]`
- Service organizations just beginning a major cost restructuring a la US manufacturing 30 years ago. `[ps=4]`
- Companies seeking lower-cost resources themselves (insourcing). `[ps=4]`
- Appropriate support for global clients now that more clients than ever are global. `[ps=4]`
- Bench optimization is a key PSO issue. `[ps=4]`
- BPO/On Demand are new account control mechanisms that adversely affect many service providers. `[ps=4]`
- Stack wars: customers buy fast, complete, scalable, transforming solutions — not software or hardware alone. `[ps=4]`
- Transform business processes with Internet services. `[ps=4]`
- Threats: commoditization of services, software, and hardware. `[ps=4]`
- Stage 2: Formalize partner program; cull marginal partners; hire in-country pre-sales and post-sales services. `[ps=4]`
- Strategic Partners: design & sell new solutions; extend technologies for new apps; create new markets; focus on client ROI not TCO; complement (not repeat) Dell skill sets; deep industry knowledge. `[ps=4]`
- Buyer dissatisfaction with IT service providers still high. `[ps=3]`
- CXO discomfort with value propositions of IT service providers. `[ps=3]`
- Motivating consulting staff now that careers aren't what they used to be. `[ps=3]`
- HP named as a competitive threat. `[ps=3]`
- Stage 1: Embrace every possible partner in-country; use partners to sell/install/support; on-site service personnel negligible. `[ps=3]`
- Stage 3: Build out service organization significantly; take more control of client implementations; dramatically cull partner count. `[ps=3]`
- Services ecosystem mapped on 'magnitude of effort on customer' vs 'nature of project' (tactical/strategic): Tech Support, Upgrades, Traditional Implementations, BPO/BTO, Hosted Apps, Strategic Consulting. `[ps=3]`
- Account control slipping from technology component providers to large integrators (Microsoft -> IBM). `[ps=2]`
- Integrators seeing no movement in large, long-timeframe work. `[ps=0]`
- Marketing of services is a key PSO issue. `[ps=0]`
- Virtuous cycle: Bench strength + intellectual property + marketing. `[ps=0]`
- Global restructuring of service operations. `[ps=0]`
- Europe and Asia ABM named as threat. `[ps=0]`
