# Data Knowledge: 1998 Practice Summary

> Aberdeen Group's 1998 practice summary for the Data Knowledge and Complex Decision Support practice, covering data warehousing, data marts, OLAP, query/reporting/analysis tools, data mining, and Customer Relationship Management (CRM). The report frames these technologies as components of an enterprise Data Knowledge strategy and assesses their integration with CIS, ERP, and Electronic Commerce applications. Thirteen primary vendors receive detailed abstracts including NCR/Teradata, IBM, Oracle, Cognos, Business Objects, SAS, Arbor/Essbase, and MicroStrategy, plus a second tier of over a dozen additional suppliers.


_Published 1998, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **other-research**._


## Top observations

- $5.2 billion (includes hardware software and systems integration consulting)
- $20.6 billion projected
- From current $1 billion to approximately $2.5 billion in 1999
- $1 million to $1.5 million typical cost
- $50000 to $400000 depending on type
- Approximately $50000 for web server version; $1500 per component for full client and administration
- Approximately $40000 per server
- $250000 to $400000 for high-end scalable systems
- $200000 to $2.5 million depending on size and existing infrastructure
- Implementing data mining is approximately 75% data preparation
- Professional services can represent as much as 60% of CRM contract
- Data warehouses can take well over a year to implement
- Between two weeks and six months depending on type and application
- About six to nine months for implementation
- NT is now considered data mart platform-of-choice; suppliers formerly Unix-focused have tuned for NT; NT has driven down data mart prices
- Microsoft Plato acquisition from Panorama will increase consolidation trend; many suppliers embracing Plato and planning partnerships despite uncertainty about what Plato actually is
- SQL Server 7.0 OLAP Services (1999) did indeed catalyze market consolidation; several pure-play OLAP vendors declined or were acquired
- Start-ups without true differentiation will fall on road to IPO or be acquired by predominant suppliers; consolidation becoming more common
- Arbor Software and Hyperion Software announced intentions to merge into Hyperion Solutions; targeting leadership in custom on-demand and packaged analytic applications
- IBM holds top-ranking position in Data Knowledge marketplace based on combined installed base in hardware software and services; DecisionEdge CRM for finance and insurance
- Reigning monarch in decision support; WorldMark 4700 handles up to 600GB; 5150 for larger; 128-node scalability; prices now compare favorably to Unix competitors
- Cognos has installed more than 700000 seats of Impromptu and PowerPlay; tight resource-sharing linkage between client-server and web versions
- SAS Enterprise Miner has supplanted many third-party suppliers' data mining environments of choice in CRM systems; SEMMA methodology considered leading approach
- Approximately 500 installed sites; selected as tool of choice among information providers; DSS Broadcaster narrows information to web/email/pager subscribers
- Aberdeen believes more integrated CIS-DSS solutions will be introduced in next 12-24 months
