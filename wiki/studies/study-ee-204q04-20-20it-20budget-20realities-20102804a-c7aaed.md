# Strategic Enterprise IT Budget Management Realities: A Benchmark Report

> Research preview for a planned Q4 2004 Aberdeen benchmark study on IT budget management, noting that on average 85% of IT budgets are dedicated to maintaining existing operations. Outlines a planned survey of financial and IT executives to benchmark budget planning, funding sources, and cost-cutting strategies. Lead analyst: Bill Malik.


_Published 2010, author **Bill Malik / Aberdeen Group**, type **research-agenda**._
