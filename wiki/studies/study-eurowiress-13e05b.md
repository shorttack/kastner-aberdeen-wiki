# Cutting EDGE: The 3G Alternative

> November 2000 Executive White Paper analyzing the effect of emerging wireless data technologies (WAP, GPRS, i-mode, EDGE) on European cellular service providers in countries with awarded UMTS/3G licenses (Finland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, UK). Argues that GPRS will be a short-lived bridge technology, contrasts Europe's WAP/GPRS struggles with the commercial success of Japan's i-mode, and projects EDGE as a more durable step toward 3G.


_Published 2000, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- These factors will make the initial uptake of GPRS extremely low, potentially less than 5% of current GSM subscribers in these territories. `[ps=5]`
- Currently more than 40% of DoCoMo’s subscribers use the i-mode system. `[ps=5]`
- Theoretical GPRS networks can transmit data at rates of up to 171 Kbps, yet projections on the actual speeds of live GPRS networks are estimated at 14 Kbps to 28 Kbps — less than 20% of the theoretical speeds eagerly anticipated 12 months ago. `[ps=4]`
- During the 2G-to-3G migration, the cellular market is likely to generate more than $450 billion in license fees and in excess of $300 billion in revenues — a handsome prize with a horrifying entrance fee. `[ps=2]`
- The technology will not have time to develop, as all license holders wrestle with the big picture — i.e., how to perfect the networks that, by the time they go live, will have cost more than $120 billion to build (in the U.K. and Germany). `[ps=2]`
- That lag time wastes a potential six-month advantage over the competitors — and uses approximately 50% of GPRS’s life pre-EDGE. `[ps=2]`
- Twelve months after its launch, WAP has managed to attract fewer than 5 million subscribers worldwide, representing less than 2% of total GSM subscribers. `[ps=0]`
- The U.K. accounts for 2%, and Germany 3% — with less than 30% of subscribers using the service on a regular basis. `[ps=0]`
- WAP phones originally cost approximately $200 more than voice-only competitors. `[ps=0]`
- This delta is likely to reach $350 in GPRS; $450 in EDGE; and, potentially, $800 in 3G telephony. `[ps=0]`
- First packet-switched wireless network • $2 billion revenues in 2000 • $76-per-month average revenue • 9% transaction fee to DoCoMo • 12 million subscribers in 1.5 years • 50% of usage is entertainment • 30% more revenue per data subscriber • 200+ Web sites `[ps=-1]`
