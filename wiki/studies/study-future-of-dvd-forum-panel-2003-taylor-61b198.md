# Future of DVD Panel — DVD Forum 2003 U.S. Conference

> DVD Forum 2003 U.S. Conference panel 'Future of DVD' held Nov 21 2003 in Beverly Hills CA, moderated by Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group. Panelists: Brian Heuckroth (Cirrus Logic) and Matt Jarman (ClearPlay). Documented in Jim Taylor's publications listing (Taylor is author of the 'DVD Demystified' McGraw-Hill book series). The panel occurred just before the Blu-ray vs HD-DVD format war (2006-2008) and during the rise of ClearPlay's parental-filtering DVD technology (which faced lawsuits from Hollywood studios). Kastner's moderator role cements Aberdeen's presence in consumer-electronics industry events alongside his established enterprise-IT coverage.


_Published 2003, author **Jim Taylor (publications listing); Peter Kastner (moderator); panelists Brian Heuckroth and Matt Jarman**, type **conference-panel**._


## Top observations

- Global DVD sell-through peaked 2006 at approximately $23B; Blu-ray launched 2006 failed to replace sales decline `[ps=5]`
- Toshiba discontinued HD DVD 2008-02-19; Warner's Jan 2008 Blu-ray-exclusive announcement precipitated collapse `[ps=5]`
- Netflix Watch Instantly launched Jan 2007; disrupted physical-media rental business and accelerated DVD decline `[ps=5]`
- Family Entertainment and Copyright Act 2005 included the Family Movie Act providing legal safe harbor for third-party content-filtering technology; passed Apr 27 2005 `[ps=5]`
- US DVD+Blu-ray combined physical-media revenue fell to ~$1.5B (2023) from peak ~$16B (2006); category now <5% of video revenue vs streaming `[ps=5]`
