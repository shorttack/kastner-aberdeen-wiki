# GoRemote Is Unifying Secure Remote Access Services

> Aberdeen profile of GoRemote Communications, Inc. (NASDAQ: GRIC), a managed remote access service provider. Analyzes the outworker cost problem, GoRemote's TierOne global network, MobileOffice client, Total Security Protection, and Universal Remote Control management portal. Aberdeen concludes that GoRemote offers the first unified solution for cost-effective management of fixed and mobile outworkers, projecting multimillion-dollar savings for enterprises adopting managed remote access in lieu of do-it-yourself approaches.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- More than 30% on any given day not in home office or behind firewall
- More than $5,000 per year
- 4-5x desktop TCO; up to $8,000/year per worker
- Up to 10x more support calls; cost per call ~2x typical IT help desk call
- More than 150 countries
- More than 35,000
- More than 1.2 billion access minutes per year
- More than 300
- First unified solution for cost-effective management of fixed and mobile outworkers
- Enterprise-oriented; enterprise customer list includes Avnet, Lockheed Martin, Matsushita, P&G, Saint-Gobain, Stanley Works, VeriSign
- Acquired late 2003; added fixed broadband capability for teleworkers and branch offices
- Hundreds of dollars per site per month when replaced with DSL or cable
- DSL or cable broadband preferred for SOHO and branch offices
- Rapid displacement of dial-up by broadband Ethernet; paying $10/coffee-shop stop
- $170 per month
- $375-$900 per month
- Starting at $1 per connect hour
- Starting at $60 per month
- On the order of 60% savings
- Will guarantee service levels and full deployment in less than 90 days
- [UNVERIFIED]
- Single Windows client; VPN + personal firewall + auth + online meetings + contact mgmt + T&E reporting; portal with SSO
- Policy mgmt/enforcement; adaptive location-based protection; automatic remediation; firewall + IDS; VPN endpoint check at each login
- Growing need for interwoven supply chains requiring remote access not just by employees but partners and contractors
- Little risk and potential multimillion-dollar reward in engaging GoRemote; outsourcing remote access is a practical alternative to managed services jumble
