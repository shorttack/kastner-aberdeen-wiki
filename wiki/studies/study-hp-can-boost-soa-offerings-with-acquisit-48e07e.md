# HP Can Boost SOA Offerings with Acquisition of Mercury

> Aberdeen Group market alert analyzing HP's acquisition of Mercury Interactive. Covers strategic rationale for combining HP OpenView with Mercury's IT optimization portfolio, SOA management implications, IT governance, enterprise systems management competitive landscape, and Aberdeen recommendations. Identifies product complementarity and integration challenges; notes competitive positioning against IBM Tivoli, CA, and BMC.


_Published 2006, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Mercury acquired Systinet in January 2006 for SOA management and registry capabilities `[ps=4]`
- Cross-platform product trusted at many enterprises; extends far beyond HP hardware customers `[ps=4]`
- Widely used to test and monitor complicated enterprise applications, infrastructure, and client-server performance `[ps=4]`
- HP less attractive for companies with mixed environments including mainframes; not committed to deeper mainframe penetration `[ps=4]`
- Mainframes heavily involved in enterprise-size ($1B+) SOA plans `[ps=4]`
- Highly likely HP will remain on acquisitive path; will strengthen HP IT system/application management especially in SOA development `[ps=4]`
- Mercury adds SOA management (via Systinet), governance, performance, dev lifecycle; highly complementary not duplicative `[ps=4]`
- SOA affects entire design-code-test-deploy-maintain application lifecycle plus new operational monitoring capabilities `[ps=4]`
- Many large enterprises have more than a year's SOA development experience as of July 2006 `[ps=4]`
- IBM positive growth and financial performance in SOA area `[ps=3]`
- Giant step closer to competing on a level playing field with IBM; distances from CA and BMC `[ps=2]`
- 3,000-person Mercury organization; large workforce turnover could delay deliverables and compromise IP `[ps=2]`
- Customer distraction from Windows Vista/Vista Server introduction will complicate integration cycle `[ps=2]`
- Large enterprises with complex networks, application integration, many IT development projects, multi-site computing `[ps=0]`
- Scheduled over next 18 months from July 2006 `[ps=0]`
- [UNVERIFIED] `[ps=0]`
- Supports Unix, Linux, and Microsoft Windows Server `[ps=0]`
- HP acquiring Mercury Interactive; strategic move to extend OpenView SOA management capabilities
- 90% of all companies will exit 2006 with SOA activities at least underway
- Regulatory compliance (especially SOX 404); policy compliance; alignment of IT assets with business strategies
- Bulk of the market (especially SMB) needs considerable education on IT governance
- Acquisition will bring strength to HP IT system and application management; users should examine proposed product roadmap and deliverable timetable
- Drawing on Mercury's relationship with BEA would make for interesting discussions; HP on acquisitive path toward competing with IBM
