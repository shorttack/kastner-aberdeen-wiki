# HP Can Boost SOA Offerings with Acquisition of Mercury

> Aberdeen Group market alert analyzing HP's acquisition of Mercury Interactive. Covers strategic rationale for combining HP OpenView with Mercury's IT optimization portfolio, SOA management implications, IT governance, enterprise systems management competitive landscape, and Aberdeen recommendations. Identifies product complementarity and integration challenges; notes competitive positioning against IBM Tivoli, CA, and BMC.


_Published 2006, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- HP acquiring Mercury Interactive; strategic move to extend OpenView SOA management capabilities
- Mercury acquired Systinet in January 2006 for SOA management and registry capabilities
- Giant step closer to competing on a level playing field with IBM; distances from CA and BMC
- 90% of all companies will exit 2006 with SOA activities at least underway
- IBM positive growth and financial performance in SOA area
- Cross-platform product trusted at many enterprises; extends far beyond HP hardware customers
- Widely used to test and monitor complicated enterprise applications, infrastructure, and client-server performance
- Large enterprises with complex networks, application integration, many IT development projects, multi-site computing
- Regulatory compliance (especially SOX 404); policy compliance; alignment of IT assets with business strategies
- Bulk of the market (especially SMB) needs considerable education on IT governance
- HP less attractive for companies with mixed environments including mainframes; not committed to deeper mainframe penetration
- Mainframes heavily involved in enterprise-size ($1B+) SOA plans
- 3,000-person Mercury organization; large workforce turnover could delay deliverables and compromise IP
- Scheduled over next 18 months from July 2006
- Customer distraction from Windows Vista/Vista Server introduction will complicate integration cycle
- Highly likely HP will remain on acquisitive path; will strengthen HP IT system/application management especially in SOA development
- [UNVERIFIED]
- Mercury adds SOA management (via Systinet), governance, performance, dev lifecycle; highly complementary not duplicative
- Acquisition will bring strength to HP IT system and application management; users should examine proposed product roadmap and deliverable timetable
- Drawing on Mercury's relationship with BEA would make for interesting discussions; HP on acquisitive path toward competing with IBM
- SOA affects entire design-code-test-deploy-maintain application lifecycle plus new operational monitoring capabilities
- Many large enterprises have more than a year's SOA development experience as of July 2006
- Supports Unix, Linux, and Microsoft Windows Server
