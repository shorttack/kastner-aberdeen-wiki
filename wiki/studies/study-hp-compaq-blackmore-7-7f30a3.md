# Letter to Peter Blackmore (Compaq EVP Sales & Services) — Post-9/11 HP-Compaq Merger Follow-up

> Short business letter from Peter Kastner to Peter Blackmore at Compaq, written in the immediate aftermath of the September 11, 2001 attacks and the HP-Compaq merger announcement. Follows up on a May 2001 Cambridge meeting on customer communications; transmits seven Aberdeen position papers on the HP-Compaq merger integration and proposes field-sales preparation and education program work for Aberdeen. Captures tone and relationship-management style of the Aberdeen era. Aberdeen-internal employer record.


_Published 2001, author **Peter S. Kastner (Aberdeen Group, Chief Research Officer)**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Kastner and Blackmore met in May 2001 in Cambridge at the Compaq analyst's day; discussed effectively communicating with Compaq's large and small customers.
- Blackmore's new responsibility is the infrastructure division of the combined Compaq and HP.
- Letter written 'given the events of this week' — walking fine line between supporting business continuity and being sensitive to grief/loss of innocence post-9/11.
- Aberdeen completed seven position papers on the proposed HP-Compaq merger since announcement; to be published on website next week.
- Aberdeen's seven papers claim to represent a comprehensive review of the major integration challenge points and business opportunities for the two companies.
- 'Now more than ever, the field organization needs a clear message, with room for iterative updates, in order to effectively serve customers and prospects' — during pre-merger and post-merger stages.
- Aberdeen proposes project work in position-paper foundation and field-sales education program areas.
- Kastner to follow up with Kay Hart in the near future to explore how to proceed.
