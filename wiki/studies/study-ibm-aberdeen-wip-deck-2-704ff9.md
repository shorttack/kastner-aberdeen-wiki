# IBM-Aberdeen: Storage Change Brings Opportunities (Sales Team Workshop)

> Aberdeen Group sales team workshop deck prepared for IBM on Storage Pools and Information Lifecycle Management (ILM). Introduces Aberdeen's DSET C-level framework (Drivers, Strategies, Enablers, Technology) and demonstrates how research agenda sponsorship, value assessment tools, and installed base assessments accelerate IBM storage sales through Aberdeen's ~150,000 registered user research community. Flag: Aberdeen-Confidential / internal IBM enablement.


_Published 2004, author **Aberdeen Group (Peter S. Kastner et al.)**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Business driver: decrease development time/cost and improve quality in outsourced CPG design/manufacturing → IT implications: document management, 24x7 operations, security, networking → Aberdeen Q2-2004 study: Product Development Strategies in CPG. `[ps=4]`
- Business driver: billions wasted in public sector procurement/supply management → IT implications: e-sourcing & procurement, spend analytics, Supply & T&E OLTP → Aberdeen study: Supply Management in the Public Sector. `[ps=4]`
- Business driver: compliance-driven supply management enables automated invoicing/payment → IT implications: mission-critical EDI, internal/external data exchange, complex data management, exception-based processing → Aberdeen study: Invoice Reconciliation and Payment. `[ps=4]`
- Business driver: higher capital costs driving inventory/asset management → IT implications: new data capture & storage, location-based data structures, data federation → Aberdeen study: RFID-enabled Asset Management. `[ps=4]`
- Q2-2004 IT Research Agenda: (1) Value Chain Opportunities — IT implications of five procurement/fulfillment reports; (2) Internet-Based Disruptions Benchmark; (3) Strategic IT Budget Management Realities. `[ps=4]`
- Research delivery is more than print: value assessment tools educate and explain, simulate research-backed proof points, simulate enterprise parameters, and deliver first-level business case. IT spend is based on much more than TCO for strategic projects. `[ps=4]`
- Aberdeen's Approach to Acceleration for Storage Pools combines Thought Leadership Papers + ILM Business Case Tool + Pools of Storage Simulation Tool + Sales Executive Workshops + Partner training + Qualified Leads from Aberdeen Research Agenda + Aberdeen.com leads + Case Studies + Installed Base Assessments + Web Seminars — across Create Demand → Educate → Accelerate Sales → Expand Customers. `[ps=3]`
- Aberdeen's business model: drive results through independent research via Agenda Sponsorships, Benchmarking Research, AOCS, Prospect Symposiums, Value Assessment Tools, Competitive Analysis Frameworks, Win/Loss Analysis, Growth/GTM Strategy Services. `[ps=0]`
- Aberdeen reach: ~150,000 registered users of research, distributed across Customer-Facing Executives (15.6%), SCM Execs (16.6%), Enterprise Applications (21.3%), Data/Knowledge Mgmt (11.6%), E-Business Execs (13.9%), Security & Risk (8.7%), Technology & Platform Mgmt (12.4%). `[ps=0]`
- DSET C-Level Focus: Drivers (external forces — economic, political, regulatory, technology, customer preferences, competitive) → Strategies (product/service, target markets, financial, GTM) → Enablers (skilled people, brand, positioning, products/services, partners, financing) → Technology (development platform, applications, network connectivity, UI, training, partner interfaces, data management) `[ps=0]`
- Storage Pools Tool distills: survey of Pools, ILM & Buying Intentions + Storage Trends Research + Value Chain Impact + Graphical Design & Simulation, combining two research reports. `[ps=0]`
- Aberdeen offices 2004: Boston, Palo Alto, Ft. Collins. `[ps=0]`
