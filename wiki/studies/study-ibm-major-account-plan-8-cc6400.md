# Major Account Plan: IBM (excluding Global Services) — Aberdeen Internal Q3 2001

> Aberdeen internal IBM major-account plan covering Q3 2001 status, 18-month goals, and recommended approach. Documents $31K YTD revenue vs $491K prior year; IBM locked-down PO status for two quarters; sales exec Cindy Murray returning from maternity leave. Targets include eLiza, pervasive computing, WebSphere, and leveraging relationship with IBM senior VP Linda Sanford. Flag: Aberdeen-internal confidentiality.


_Published 2001, author **Peter S. Kastner, Cindy Murray, Ed Black (Aberdeen Group)**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Through six months: $31,000 Aberdeen revenue from IBM versus $491,000 for full year 2000.
- IBM has been locked down to Aberdeen for new purchase orders for two quarters.
- Sales executive Cindy Murray was on maternity leave during this period.
- Major account process is surfacing more opportunities into new areas, including a six-figure proposal to IBM's primary market research department.
- Aberdeen has suffered from IBM's apparent historic experience that Aberdeen was a white-paper company at a time when white-papers are an IBM expendable budget item.
- Despite lack of success, Aberdeen should continue to invest in marketing to IBM — IBM is a leader or player in virtually all Aberdeen practice areas and is an industry survivor; success could leverage to a dozen or more pods.
- Goal: IBM revenue into every Aberdeen practice area (pod).
- Mix shift from white-papers to ETI content/primary research and Core retainers and consulting.
- Grow Aberdeen-IBM revenues to a $750K run rate by Q4-2002.
- IBM's revenue run-rate outcomes for the 2001 major account plan are internal proprietary data not publicly available. IBM's total revenue for 2001 was approximately $85.9 billion, declining from $88.4 billion in 2000 due to the dot-com recession. Specific account-level outcomes cannot be verified.
- Be known, respected and quoted by IBM general managers.
- Book $150,000 MAS and content deal (close proposal).
- Book $100,000 in Core projects (half identified today).
- Find out what Aberdeen has to do to get business back in Server group.
- Capabilities briefing to IBM Software (Tivoli & WebSphere).
- 2002 quarterly revenue targets: $125K / $150K / $175K / $200K (Q1-Q4).
- 2002 revenue mix target: 20% ETI, 35% amplification, 20% retainers, 25% consulting.
- Goal: get a plan from every practice area manager and execute.
- Q3/4-2001: Aberdeen corporate capabilities presentation to IBM.
- Use Linda Sanford lever to rest of IBM executive team; keep her engaged (example: Sept 5 voicemail by Peter offering to brief her on Aberdeen HP-Compaq analysis).
- Use 20-25% of Cindy's time on IBM through Q1, then measure effectiveness.
- Use Peter where appropriate in briefings, projects and sales.
- Try to approach IBM on multiple projects tied to a single IBM initiative: eLiza, pervasive computing, or WebSphere infrastructure.
- Attend all IBM analyst conferences and analyst briefings.
- Brief Linda Sanford quarterly plus ad hoc event conference calls (i.e., HP acquires Compaq).
