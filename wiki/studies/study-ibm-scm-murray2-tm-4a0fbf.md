# IBM-Aberdeen Meeting: Building Value Chain Leaders

> Aberdeen Group consulting pitch deck to IBM proposing a research, benchmark, and workshop alignment program focused on Supply Chain Management (SCM), Procurement, and Strategic Consulting. Identifies IBM's On Demand validation, market visibility, and intellectual capital needs; proposes a three-phase approach combining Aberdeen Online Conference Series (AOCS), benchmark studies, installed base assessments, and joint workshops to grow IBM's top-line revenue and document business-value impact. Flag: Aberdeen-internal / client-confidential.


_Published 2003, author **Jamie Bedard, Peter Kastner, Tim Minahan, Chris Jones, Tom Ryan, John Farrell (Aberdeen Group)**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Alignment challenge: need for detailed global research and regionalized frameworks — beyond standard US-centric analyst content. `[ps=4]`
- Alignment challenge: how to have direct relationship with IBM BCS and fulfill analyst-relations obligations — a structural tension in IBM's multi-BU organization. `[ps=4]`
- Aberdeen challenge to IBM: 'How much is real? How much is aspiration? Where should we start first?' — candid push for prioritization. `[ps=4]`
- Proposed Q1-Q2 Supply Management public research: Procurement Outsourcing Benchmark (Q1), Spend Category Management Strategies (Contract Labor Q1, Travel Q2), Strategic Sourcing in Retail (Q2), Best Practices in Contract Management (Q2). `[ps=4]`
- Proposed Strategic Consulting & SCM research: On Demand Business Value Roadmap; RFID Benchmark Study; RFID Tech Assessment; Next-Gen Order Fulfillment Strategies Benchmark. `[ps=4]`
- IBM's On Demand needs validation: what is application of On Demand in SCM/Supply Management? How do we demonstrate On Demand value to prospects? `[ps=3]`
- Issue requiring further alignment: 'Simply endorse me as great — old world pay for praise' — Aberdeen differentiates from pay-for-play analyst model. `[ps=2]`
- Aberdeen positioning: 'The Trusted Advisor to the Global 5,000 and Their Solution Providers for Technology-Driven Business Value, Insight, and Impact.' `[ps=2]`
- Aberdeen's charter with IBM: (1) understand IBM's businesses; (2) identify value in growing top-line revenue, augmenting BD, delivering business-value/technology frameworks, and documenting client impact. `[ps=0]`
- IBM quote: 'Our strategic consulting competence and domain expertise is undervalued in the market' — addressed via thought leadership and demand generation. `[ps=0]`
- IBM seeks increased market recognition for its proven value — Aberdeen proposes market-visibility programs. `[ps=0]`
- Key IBM-Aberdeen alignment areas: business value first; G5000 customer overlap; research agenda synchrony; market visibility; actionable frameworks. `[ps=0]`
- Aberdeen practice alignments across Strategic Consulting, Procurement, and SCM — each with Challenges, Needs, and Alignment layers. `[ps=0]`
- Aberdeen suggested approach: three phases combining AOCS, Benchmark Study, Installed Base, On Demand Assessment, Private IBM Webinar, Value Assessment Workshop, DSETs, Maturity Grids, Business Modeling Tools. `[ps=0]`
- Deliverables split: Public (AOCS, Benchmark Study) vs IBM-Specific (Installed Base, On Demand Assessment, Private IBM Webinar, Value Assessment Workshop). `[ps=0]`
- Proposed joint workshop: 'On Demand Supply Management: Accelerating and Achieving Value in Supply Relationships' in conjunction with ISM Conference in May. `[ps=0]`
- IBM benefits from relationship: Quarterly Research Sponsorship; Lead and Demand Creation; Unlimited Analyst Access; Custom/Proprietary IBM-Branded Research; Customer Proof-Point Panelists in Webinar Series; Joint Workshop to Drive Spend Management Market Traction. `[ps=0]`
- Aberdeen offices circa 2003: Boston, Palo Alto, Ft. Collins. `[ps=0]`
