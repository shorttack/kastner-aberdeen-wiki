> Aberdeen Group formal research-sponsorship proposal to IBM covering Supply Management and Supply Chain/Fulfillment research agendas for 2004. Packages 10 research program sponsorships ($250K) + 10 customer case studies ($250K) + joint On Demand Supply Management workshop ($25K) with discount pricing to $125K total. Introduces Enterprise Research Alliances (ERA) with Penton Media, iSource, IndustryWeek and cites ~2,000-3,200 downloads per report. Flag: Aberdeen-Confidential / IBM-commercial-proposal.


_Published 2004, author **Aberdeen Group (Peter S. Kastner et al.)**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- IBM Goals: (1) drive deal flow via improved/qualified leads; (2) increase deal size via fact-based value impact proof points; (3) create pull-through for additional services; (4) detail fact-based differentiation for Procurement services; (5) provide field teams with actionable research and frameworks. `[ps=4]`
- Supply Chain/Fulfillment research agenda: Next Generation Order Fulfillment Benchmark (Q2), Postponement Strategies (Q2), Warehouse Management (Q2), Outsourced Manufacturing Strategies Benchmark (Q3), Transportation Management (Q3), Performance Management in Fulfillment Benchmark (Q3). `[ps=4]`
- Aberdeen Lifecycle Research Services cycle: Create Awareness (Customer Success Case Studies, Center of Excellence Sponsor, Speaking Engagements, Installed Base Research, Benchmarking, valuechainaccess, AOCS, Prospect Symposiums) → Create Demand → Accelerate Sales → Expand Customers. `[ps=4]`
- ERA definition: sponsored collaborative business-oriented research in partnership with widely-recognized industry media outlets having high decision-maker profile; used to capture leads, educate customers/partners/investors, and position vendors for market recognition. `[ps=4]`
- Aberdeen tagline: 'The trusted advisor to the Global 5000 and their solution providers for technology-driven business value, insight, and impact.' `[ps=1]`
- Supply Management Q1-Q2 research agenda: Procurement Outsourcing Benchmark (Q1), Spend Category Management — Contract Labor (Q1) and Travel (Q2), Strategic Sourcing in Retail (Q2), Best Practices in Contract Management (Q2). `[ps=0]`
- Joint workshop proposed: 'On Demand Supply Management: Accelerating and Achieving Value in Supply Relationships' in conjunction with ISM Conference in May. `[ps=0]`
- Aberdeen-IBM pricing: 10 program sponsorships @ $250K + 10 case studies @ $250K + joint On Demand Supply Management workshop @ $25K, bundled at $125K total to IBM (discount off $525K list). `[ps=0]`
- Penton Media partnership: Spending Analysis Benchmarking Report 'Dissecting a Corporate Epidemic' — go-live Jan 15, 2003; 2,845 downloads. `[ps=0]`
- iSource Business partnership: Supplier Performance Measurement Benchmark Report — go-live Jan 4, 2003; 3,213 downloads. `[ps=0]`
- IndustryWeek partnership: Service Parts Management Benchmark Report — go-live Sep 2003; 2,212 downloads to date. `[ps=0]`
- Aberdeen delivered 12 Supply-Chain-Related ERA Reports in 2003; average readership 1,000-2,000 in first 6 weeks. `[ps=0]`
- AOCS Best Practices in e-Sourcing metrics: 315 pre-event registrants, 149 attendees, 187 post-event downloads; audience split 43.2% Users / 27.4% Influencers. `[ps=0]`
- Testimonial — Brian Desmond, Sr. Marketing Director: 'Aberdeen's lead-generation programs are of high value. We can map two closed deals this year directly to these programs.' `[ps=0]`
- Fortune 100 Service Provider: of ~2,000 Aberdeen leads, ~20% solid; of those, 17% A-leads actively evaluating, 33% B-leads exploratory. `[ps=0]`
- Glen Kazerman, SVP Sales & Marketing: of one Aberdeen report sponsored in a year, 25% of leads entered pipeline and at least 3 closed deals directly attributed. `[ps=0]`
