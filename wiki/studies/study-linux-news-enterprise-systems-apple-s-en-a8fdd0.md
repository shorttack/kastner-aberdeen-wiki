# Apple's Enterprise IT Battle Plan, Part One

> LinuxInsider/MacNewsWorld feature on Apple's enterprise IT ambitions post-Xserve, framed through Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group. Kastner argues Apple needs 'a specialized SWAT team out of Cupertino' to make business cases to skeptical enterprises, and flags Apple's weak indirect channel as the key obstacle. Article covers Xserve, Xgrid grid-computing, education/scientific footholds, and Apple's pricing strategy.


_Published 2004, author **Keith Regan (reporter, MacNewsWorld/LinuxInsider); Peter Kastner (quoted source)**, type **press-article**._


## Top observations

- Indirect channel partners not strong enough to win large enterprise deals `[ps=4]`
- No client access licenses and lower list prices vs. Windows/Unix server competitors `[ps=3]`
- Mainly university use today; worth watching as grid computing matures `[ps=2]`
- Discontinued January 2011; Xgrid deprecated in OS X 10.7 Lion (2011) `[ps=0]`
- Needs a Cupertino 'SWAT team' to build enterprise business cases
