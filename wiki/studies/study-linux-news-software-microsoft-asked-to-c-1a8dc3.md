# Microsoft Asked To Change Misleading Linux Ad

> LinuxInsider coverage of a UK Advertising Standards Authority action requiring Microsoft to change a misleading Linux comparison ad. Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group frames the European regulatory context, noting growing government adoption of Linux and that while Microsoft has 'a lot of friends' in Europe it is increasingly under scrutiny. Article ties ad-compliance ruling to broader open-source political momentum in the EU.


_Published 2004, author **Elizabeth Millard (reporter, LinuxInsider); Peter Kastner (quoted source)**, type **press-article**._


## Top observations

- Microsoft is under growing scrutiny in Europe, evident in Linux-ad compliance action
- Microsoft has 'a lot of friends' in Europe; dominant in many regions
- Growing use of Linux by European government bodies as a 2004 trend
- Munich LiMux (2004-2017), French Gendarmerie GendBuntu (~70k seats), various EU OSS procurement mandates validated growth; Munich later reversed to Windows (2017-2020) validating Kastner's hedge
