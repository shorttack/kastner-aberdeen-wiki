# E2open Broadens Supply Chain Capabilities, Expands Discrete Manufacturing Industries Served

> A Market Alert analyzing E2open's expansion of its on-demand supply chain and supplier relationship management platform beyond electronics manufacturing into other discrete manufacturing segments, along with new Technology Performance Guarantees. It details E2open's eight newly pre-configured business process templates (order management, inventory management, logistics visibility, etc.) and provides recommendations for which manufacturers should or should not consider the vendor.


_Published 2005, author **Beth Enslow / Aberdeen Group**, type **market-alert**._
