# Procuri-CMSI Deal Signals Market Shift and Convergence

> A Market Alert analyzing Procuri's acquisition of contract management vendor CMSI, describing how the deal reflects convergence of sourcing, procurement, and contract management markets driven by spend compliance, regulatory compliance (e.g., Sarbanes-Oxley), and risk mitigation trends. It covers implementation timelines, competitive responses (Ariba, Emptoris, Oracle), and recommendations for Procuri and CMSI customers and prospects.


_Published 2005, author **Tim A. Minahan / Aberdeen Group**, type **market-alert**._


## Top observations

- Aberdeen benchmarks found that 60% of e-sourcing users have been unable to fully implement cost savings negotiated online. `[ps=4]`
- Companies typically implement only 70% to 80% of negotiated savings. `[ps=4]`
- **Market convergence** : More than 80% of large enterprises (>$1 billion in revenues) have tested online reverse auction and/or e-RFx solutions, and nearly half are now evaluating extended e-sourcing platforms, including contract management capabilities. `[ps=4]`
- One challenge: 40% of CMSI’s customers have installed the CMSI application on-site. `[ps=0]`
