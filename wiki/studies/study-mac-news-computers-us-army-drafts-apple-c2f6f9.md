# US Army Drafts Apple Xserve for Supercomputer

> MacNewsWorld coverage of the U.S. Army's MACH 5 supercomputer built on Apple Xserve G5 (via COLSA), projected at ~25 teraflops and ranked second after DOE systems at the time. Peter Kastner, executive vice president and chief research officer at Aberdeen Group, calls the deal a 'wakeup call' and declares Apple 'not a one-shot wonder, but a force to be reckoned with' in supercomputing after Virginia Tech's earlier System X deployment.


_Published 2004, author **Blane Warrene (reporter, MacNewsWorld); Peter Kastner (quoted source)**, type **press-article**._


## Top observations

- ~25 teraflops, projected #2 behind DOE systems at time of article
- 'Apple not a one-shot wonder, but a force to be reckoned with' post-Virginia-Tech System X
- Government IT provider, ~800 employees
- Virginia Tech System X (Big Mac) — first high-profile Apple cluster
- Apple discontinued Xserve in January 2011 without further major HPC wins; Apple effectively exited supercomputing by late 2000s
