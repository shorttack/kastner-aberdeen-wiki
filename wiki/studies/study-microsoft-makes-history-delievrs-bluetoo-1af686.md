> Microsoft press release announcing its Wireless Optical Desktop for Bluetooth, among the first Bluetooth-branded mainstream PC peripherals from a major vendor. Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group characterizes the launch as the catalyst that finally moves Bluetooth from pre-launch hype into mainstream consumer peripherals.


_Published 2002, author **Microsoft Corporation (press release)**, type **press-release**._


## Top observations

- Kastner endorsement implies Bluetooth PC peripherals will go mainstream following this launch. `[ps=4]`
- By 2010 Bluetooth peripherals were standard; by 2020 the standard was ubiquitous across phones, laptops, wearables, cars. `[ps=4]`
- Positioned around mobile phones integrating with PC via Bluetooth; enables laptop users to leave handset in pocket/briefcase. `[ps=3]`
- 'It is like the Bluetooth gun went off, but everyone stumbled across the start line.' Microsoft Wireless Optical Desktop for Bluetooth is the key catalyst making the Bluetooth marketplace happen. `[ps=2]`
- Microsoft Wireless Optical Desktop for Bluetooth unveiled as first major-brand Bluetooth keyboard+mouse bundle with USB transceiver. `[ps=0]`
