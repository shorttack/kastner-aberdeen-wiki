# Aberdeen Group Mutual Confidentiality Agreement Template

> This is Aberdeen Group's standard Mutual Confidentiality Agreement template, governed by Massachusetts law. It establishes mutual obligations for confidentiality of proprietary information shared during potential business transactions, including a 24-month non-solicitation of employees clause, provisions for return/destruction of confidential information, and standard exceptions for publicly available information. The document is blank with placeholder fields for parties and dates.


_Published 2000, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Commonwealth of Massachusetts internal laws; no conflicts of laws principles
- 24 months from date of agreement; mutual prohibition on employing/retaining/soliciting employees
- All documentation, tangible/intangible discoveries, concepts, software, designs, drawings, specifications, techniques, models, methodologies, diagrams, flowcharts, procedures, know-how
- Upon request: return all Confidential Information; no copies retained; documents based on CI must be destroyed and certified in writing
- Money damages insufficient remedy for breach; both parties entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief; no bond requirement
- Public information; information available from non-confidential source; information in possession prior to disclosure
- Receiving party must provide prompt notice of legal request; disclosing party may seek protective order; minimal disclosure if compelled
