# NT Professional Services: 1998 Practice Summary

> Aberdeen Group's 1998 practice summary on NT Professional Services analyzing the market for consulting and systems integration services surrounding Windows NT deployments. The report quantifies the NT professional services market at $3 billion in 1996 growing to $20 billion in 1997 and projects growth to $110 billion by 2001. It profiles fourteen service providers across four categories — OEMs (Amdahl, Compaq, Digital, Data General, HP, IBM, NCR, Tandem, Unisys), ISVs (Microsoft, SAP), IPSPs (Coopers & Lybrand, CSC, Deloitte, EDS), and Resellers (Entex, MicroAge).


_Published 1998, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **other-research**._


## Top observations

- $3 billion in NT Professional Services on $750 million NT platform revenue ($4 services per $1 platform) `[ps=4]`
- ~$110 billion by 2001 based on 8x platform cost multiplier; platform revenues growing at astounding rate `[ps=4]`
- HP has only 20% PSO revenue from NT; expects 50% by year-end 1999; lacks MCSE workforce; perplexingly slow to respond `[ps=4]`
- SAP sees clear 1997 migration trend toward Windows/NT environments on front-end; SQL Server back-ends increasing alongside Oracle `[ps=4]`
- Microsoft Consulting Services not entering market to be leader but to encourage enterprise NT usage; knowledge shared back to channel `[ps=4]`
- C&L merging with Price Waterhouse to form largest accounting/consulting firm; ERP engagements driving NT services demand `[ps=4]`
- NT has 5 critical weaknesses: scalability `[ps=3]`
- Pre-Digital Compaq rated limited in high-end NT; only 9000 services staff; weak vs. IBM and HP for midrange enterprise `[ps=3]`
- If Digital acquisition goes through Compaq becomes major player in NT upscale universe; neutralizes HP in 11 of 15 categories `[ps=3]`
- DG engineers committed to high-end NT deployment; CLARiiON storage; 27% of $1.53B revenue from services `[ps=3]`
- EDS second-largest independent services provider; $15.2B revenue; outsourcing and data management specialty; global `[ps=3]`
- Four categories: 1) OEMs 2) ISVs 3) Independent Professional Services Providers (IPSPs/SIs) 4) Resellers `[ps=3]`
- Amdahl is NT/mainframe integration specialist not just mainframe maker; EnVista line with ESCON channel for mainframe connectivity `[ps=2]`
- NCR smartly positioned for High Availability Transaction Processing NT market; largest installed NT site running Exchange `[ps=2]`
- Unisys learned from missed PC/Unix opportunities; strong NT-legacy mainframe integration especially in banking/finance verticals `[ps=2]`
- Digital built largest Microsoft-certified NT professional services org (~2000 MCSEs); first Microsoft Prime Integrator `[ps=0]`
- CTP solid client/server NT provider; fixed-time/fixed-price model; $407M revenue; key client Sun Microsystems `[ps=0]`
- Tandem CCP/NT provides 3-year lifecycle program for highly available NT; planning/implementation/training/operations/migration `[ps=0]`
- Reached almost $20 billion; $12 services per $1 NT platform; driven by move to application servers
- Verify actual 2001 IT services market size; likely substantially below $110B projection.
- NT 5.0 introduction will drive strong near-term demand for design and deployment professional services
- IBM offers NT plus IBM alternative apps (AS/400 RS/6000 mainframe); resonates with enterprise IS managers wary of Microsoft dependence
