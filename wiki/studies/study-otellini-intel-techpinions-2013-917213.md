# On the Impact of Paul Otellini's CEO Years at Intel (Tech.pinions, Published Version)

> Published Tech.pinions column by Peter Kastner assessing the 40-year Intel career and 8-year CEO tenure (2005-2013) of Paul Otellini, organized across four dimensions: technology (tick-tock model, multi-core, High-K metal gate, 3D tri-gate, performance-per-watt), growth (BRIC geographies, mobile, software via McAfee and Wind River, intelligent systems), competition (AMD retake, ARM mobile threat), and finance (revenue up 60% to ~$53B; dividend initiation). Notes the missed-iPhone cost to Intel and the new emerging devices division.


_Published 2013, author **Peter Kastner**, type **topic-analysis**._


## Top observations

- Launch 'later this quarter' of Haswell (4th-gen Core) `[ps=5]`
- Intel acquired Wind River Systems 2009 `[ps=5]`
- Intel acquired McAfee 2010 `[ps=5]`
- Intel exited smartphone modem business 2019; sold to Apple $1B `[ps=5]`
- Intel abandoned tick-tock model in 2016; 10nm delayed multi-year `[ps=5]`
- Marketing shift from NA/Europe to BRIC (Brazil, Russia, India, China) `[ps=4]`
- Odd years = new architecture; even years = new smaller process node `[ps=3]`
- Multi-core PC born 2006 in Core 2 Duo `[ps=3]`
- Intel #46 on Fortune 100 `[ps=0]`
- 1 million Pentium 4's per week in 2004 `[ps=0]`
- $34.2B `[ps=0]`
- ~$53B estimated `[ps=0]`
- Intel set up new emerging devices division 'last month' `[ps=0]`
- 40 years at Intel; MBA 1974; 8-year CEO tenure 2005-2013 `[ps=0]`
- 50% reduction in wafer production time `[ps=0]`
- Over 1 million processors per day (2013)
- AMD 52% US retail share; Intel fell to #2
- Forthcoming 14nm process 2014
- Intel acquired Infineon wireless unit 2010
- Embedded systems TAM 'well north of $20B'
- Atom + Haswell + 14nm + Infineon will allow Intel to 'stand toe-to-toe' with Qualcomm, nVidia, Samsung in mobile
- 'The company missed the coming iPhone and lost out on a lot of market potential'
