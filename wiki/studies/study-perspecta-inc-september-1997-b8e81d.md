# Perspecta Inc. — September 1997

> Aberdeen Group profiles Perspecta Inc., a San Francisco startup that developed OLAP-I (OLAP for Information) — a server-based technology applying OLAP-style analytics to non-numeric content. The SmartContent system dynamically reorganizes information in real-time based on user behavior. Aberdeen endorsed Perspecta as a strong candidate for applications requiring more effective information navigation interfaces.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group (Matt Nerney)**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Perspecta survived until at least 2002 (added XML support) but never achieved mainstream commercial adoption; company eventually dissolved `[ps=5]`
- Analyzes and defines complex relationships between disparate information sources; dynamically reorganizes content in real-time based on user behavior `[ps=4]`
- Server-based technology analogous to OLAP but applied to non-numeric information; unique in market `[ps=3]`
- Aberdeen implies Perspecta is a viable market participant with a distinctive product worthy of enterprise consideration `[ps=2]`
- Perspecta should be on the short list for applications benefiting from more effective interfaces `[ps=0]`
- Elevates user from two-dimensional information environment; extends digital metaphor to new level via dynamic content reorganization `[ps=0]`
- Pursuing Java and Internet/intranet/extranet-enabled markets needing more effective access formats
- Companies amassing digital data creating information warehouses that are dispersed and hard to manage — industry-wide problem
