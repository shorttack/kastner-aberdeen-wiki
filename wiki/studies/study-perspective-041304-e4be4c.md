# Intel's Itanium: Ready and Desirable for Mainframe-Class Workloads

> This Aberdeen Perspective piece evaluates whether Intel Itanium 2-based platforms can handle mainframe-class workloads and compares them to IBM zSeries mainframes. Drawing on Q1-2004 interviews and a survey of 98 mainframe users, the paper concludes that Itanium platforms are not only capable but in many cases desirable: users report superior performance/scalability, lower TCO, greater flexibility, and comparable robustness. Key findings include that ~40% of mainframe users are open to shifting to Itanium-based platforms; Xeon handles migrated mainframe workloads already in production; COBOL/FORTRAN/DB2 migration is surprisingly straightforward; and CICS/DL1/assembler migration requires significant effort. Aberdeen recommends 'surround, offload, or migrate' strategies over full 'replace' initiatives.


## Top observations

- ~40% of mainframe users
- 40% looking to offload or surround mainframes
- Itanium 2 superior to Xeon; comparable or superior to mainframe
- Xeon-based mainframe solutions already in production 1+ years
- Straightforward; no code changes required
- Significant but doable
- IBM increasingly discounting mainframe prices
- Mainframe hardware + software licensing perceived as more expensive than open systems
- Largest payback: CPU-intensive workloads (batch, data warehousing, analytics)
- More scalable, more cost-effective, more flexible, smaller footprint, more programmer-productive
- AMD Opteron seen as less real-world-tested
- Linux on mainframe less manageable and less performant
- Surround / Offload / Replace / Migrate
- Itanium will increasingly host former mainframe workloads

- Replace strategy has cultural resistance and long implementation time
- 98 qualified mainframe users surveyed
- Itanium ecosystem matured; nearly all major EBAs support Itanium with customers in production
- Most success stories just reaching production in Q1-2004
- DB2 on Intel multiprocessor more cost-effective than mainframe VM-based allocation
