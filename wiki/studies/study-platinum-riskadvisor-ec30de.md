# PLATINUM RiskAdvisor: Insurance Data Warehousing For Intelligent Risk Management

> Aberdeen Group profiles PLATINUM technology inc.'s RiskAdvisor — a packaged data warehouse application for the insurance industry built on an RDBMS star schema (RDM) with 20+ client-side modules covering premium and loss functions. The study argues that custom-built insurance data warehouses take too long and cost too much, and that RiskAdvisor's pre-built industry-specific data model dramatically accelerates deployment. Early user testimony confirms 90-120 day implementation timelines at large insurers.


_Published 1997, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Executive Level Summaries: back-end triggers/alerts with drill-down via direct access buttons for senior executives `[ps=5]`
- Founded 1987; public company NASDAQ:PLAT since 1991; growth strategy of acquiring and refining software `[ps=5]`
- Computer Associates acquired PLATINUM technology for $3.5B in 1999 — largest software industry deal at that time; PLATINUM FY1998 revenue was $968M `[ps=5]`
- Packaged vertical data warehouse application for insurance industry targeting largest insurers with $100M-$30B annual net premiums written `[ps=4]`
- Highly refined RDBMS star schema (RDM) organizing insurer data by master policy and claim records with premium/claim financial transactions `[ps=4]`
- Metadata layer provides point-and-click adjustment to system changes; automatically updates desktop modules when server-side changes occur; reduces ongoing maintenance `[ps=4]`
- Operational Flow: organizational structure drill-down from parent to individual companies and lines of business into modules `[ps=4]`
- RiskAdvisor embodies what should become the prevailing trend in data warehousing: deep industry-specific content delivered via strong technology backbone to different user classes `[ps=4]`
- RDBMS star schema pattern for analytical data warehouses remains the dominant design paradigm in 2026 (Kimball dimensional modeling); directly validates Aberdeen's architectural observation `[ps=4]`
- Largest insurers typically multinational, geographically dispersed; $50-100M risk per commercial customer failure; requires analysis tools for premiums/claims/investments/brokers/agents/loss-reserves `[ps=4]`
- Managed nearly 25 years of historical risk management data in RiskAdvisor `[ps=3]`
- $100 million annual net premiums written (minimum target) `[ps=2]`
- PLATINUM planned to verticalize industries including finance / healthcare / telecommunications following RiskAdvisor insurance model `[ps=2]`
- Aberdeen encourages enterprises to introduce RiskAdvisor as point of comparison when evaluating insurance data warehousing; PLATINUM well established in insurance `[ps=2]`
- Aberdeen predicts packaged industry-specific data warehousing will become the prevailing trend vs. custom build-your-own approach `[ps=2]`
- 20+ client-side modules covering most premium- and loss-related tasks `[ps=0]`
- 500 consultants `[ps=0]`
- $30 billion annual net premiums written (maximum target) `[ps=0]`
- Between $50M and $100M risk if commercial customer not effectively serviced `[ps=0]`
- 90 to 120 days typical for comprehensive RiskAdvisor installation to begin returning enterprise-enhancing results `[ps=0]`
- Large insurer ($3.5B+ net premiums) praised rapid installation; learned what information to include/exclude; system instantly received at senior executive level; now bringing subsidiaries into RiskAdvisor `[ps=0]`
- RiskAdvisor desktop modules based on PLATINUM Forest & Trees decision-support software; Windows 3.1+; approx 300 different views of information `[ps=0]`
- Free Flow: menu-driven open access to all RiskAdvisor modules broken down by business function for designated users `[ps=0]`
- IBM DB2, Informix, Microsoft SQL Server, Oracle, Red Brick, Sybase; also DB2 for OS/2 and AIX, Lotus Notes, HP Allbase/Image, dBase, Paradox `[ps=0]`
- PLATINUM RiskAdvisor and related products gradually discontinued under Computer Associates ownership; CA absorbed PLATINUM's infrastructure and mainframe tools; BI/data warehouse products were not continued as strategic investments `[ps=0]`
