## Top observations

- Aberdeen defines four basic pools of storage (Figure 1): online, midline, nearline, and offline. `[ps=5]`
- About 25% of the cost of FC/SCSI for moderate random access on data that seldom or never changes. `[ps=4]`
- Much more than 70% of the respondents have less than 25% of their data in a midline or nearline disk storage pool. `[ps=4]`
- Aberdeen research shows examples of midline storage in the $9.00 per gigabyte range. _The opportunity for significant savings by migrating fixed content data to new midline storage while freeing existing FC/SCSI storage for active content growth is obvious._ `[ps=4]`
- Thus, most enterprise storage, as every IT executive knows, is FC/SCSI disk arrays at roughly $30 a gigabyte. `[ps=2]`
- As shown in Figure 2, each factor was rated independently on a 10-point scale ranging from not important (1) to extremely important (10). `[ps=0]`
- Respondents were asked the importance of a set of factors that they expect from midline and nearline disk, assuming a 50% lower cost per gigabyte (Figure 3). `[ps=0]`
- Today, the focus should be on targeted solutions (Figure 4). `[ps=0]`
