> Peter S. Kastner (Aberdeen Group) discusses Portal Software of Cupertino, founded 1985, and its Infranet platform — the first billing system architected for real-time, service-oriented Internet commerce. Kastner frames the Internet as a 'dog-eat-dog competitive world' where 30-45-day billing cycles no longer work, and describes Portal's customers (Juno, with 6M free-email accounts) needing real-time usage-based billing for in-game upsells, video-conf bandwidth, and fraud cutoff. Characterized as the 'second surgery' — companies rebuilding billing after first-generation Internet sites failed.
