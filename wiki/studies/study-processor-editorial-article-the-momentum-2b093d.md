# The Momentum Is Gathering At Cisco: Developing SME Products Is A Growing Priority

> Processor Magazine editorial (2004-07-16) by Elizabeth Millard covering Cisco's April 2004 SMB Class Solutions Initiative, IPCC Express contact-center bundles, and Cisco Security Agent Starter Bundle. Peter Kastner (Aberdeen analyst) notes Cisco has the clout to become a major SME player but is still smarting from the May 2004 leak of proprietary Cisco IOS router source code, which he predicts will force Cisco to redouble router-security investment — ultimately a positive for SMEs getting more secure products.


_Published 2004, author **Elizabeth Millard (Processor Magazine / Sandhills Publishing)**, type **news-article**._


## Top observations

- Unspecified amount of Cisco IOS proprietary source code was stolen and posted publicly on the Internet May 2004. `[ps=5]`
- Cisco acquired cloud-managed networking vendor Meraki November 2012 for $1.2B, becoming the SMB / mid-market platform Kastner anticipated. `[ps=5]`
- Following IOS code leak, Cisco expanded PSIRT advisory cadence and released IOS Software Modularity / IOS XR further hardening router software — consistent with Kastner prediction. `[ps=4]`
- Cisco will redouble its effort to make sure its routers are secure. The company will also look more closely at other products and get closer to making bulletproof technology that can stand up to hackers. `[ps=3]`
- IPCC Express Standard/Enhanced versions: CRM Communications Connector for click-to-dial and screen pop; skills-based routing and priority queuing (Enhanced). `[ps=0]`
- Cisco Security Agent Starter Bundle bundles CiscoWorks VPN/Security Management Solution Basic 2.2, Management Center for CSA 4.01, one CSA for servers, 10 CSAs for desktops. `[ps=0]`
- Cisco certainly has the clout to make a difference and reach its goals as an SME provider.
- Cisco SMB Class Solutions Initiative announced April 2004; 36-month IPC leasing/service bundle starting at $5,000/year; includes wireless, VPN, routers, switches, service.
