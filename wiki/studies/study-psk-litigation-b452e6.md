# Peter S. Kastner -- Litigation Biography and Expert Witness Credentials

> This document presents Peter S. Kastner's biography as an IT expert witness, including his 30+ year career background and a detailed account of five litigation engagements spanning 1989–1997. Cases involve complex computer system disputes including mainframe design, software development breach of contract, bid protests for distributed government systems, trademark infringement, and ERP implementation failure. The document establishes Kastner's credentials for future expert witness retentions.


_Published 1998, author **Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Joined Aberdeen as VP in 1988; currently EVP; 30+ years IT experience
- Marketing executive at Digital Equipment Corporation 1987-1988; commercial market competitive analysis
- Marketing executive at Stratus Computer (prior to DEC)
- Over 8 years as consultant/program manager at Arthur D. Little Inc.; dozens of complex technology development projects
- 3 years as programmer/analyst at Wang Laboratories subsidiary; internal accounting systems; application software factory
- Kastner supported plaintiff counsel Blanc Gilburne; reviewed CCI's RISC-Unix technical documentation; proposed as expert witness in commercial computer performance; case settled with substantial damages for plaintiff
- Kastner supported defense counsel Fulbright & Jaworski; software development breach of contract; Aberdeen assessed technical/project management aspects of IBM AS/400 and 3090 mainframe application development case
- Kastner supported defense counsel Carlton Fields for Unisys; Unisys protested EDS/IBM bid for distributed human resources system; served as plaintiff expert witness; while plaintiff not sustained Florida cancelled $110M EDS contract afterward
- Florida cancelled $110M EDS contract for non-performance; precisely the technical areas Kastner predicted were inadequate
- Kastner supported defense counsel Fish & Richardson for defendant Crossroads Software; trademark infringement claim on 'crossroads' name; judge denied preliminary injunction citing Kastner's affidavit; case settled out of court
- Kastner retained by Montgomery McCracken for Andersen Consulting as defendant's fact expert witness; IE accused Andersen of failing $17M ERP project (order management, inventory, warehouse, financial accounting); Kastner produced 129-page expert witness report; case settled day before trial
- Key issue: whether Andersen should have recommended replacing IE's existing HP 3000 computer system; technology maturity and fitness for purpose in mid-1990s context
- Author or co-author of three book-length computer technology market research studies; numerous white papers; widely quoted in trade press; TV appearances; guest lecturer at MIT and Federal University Rio de Janeiro
- OLTP, decision support, client/server architectures, distributed commercial systems development, electronic commerce, commercial benchmark reviewing/analysis; pioneer in industry-standard commercial performance benchmarks
- Aberdeen performs custom projects; 50-person research organization; principals quoted extensively in publications; frequent conference/seminar speakers
