# An Overview of Internet Application Development

> A presentation delivered by Peter S. Kastner of Aberdeen Group at the Blyth Software User Group in Dallas, June 1996, providing an overview of Internet application development for business practitioners. The document covers what the Internet is, current Internet development tools, Internet growth and opportunity, and the future of business on the Internet. This file also contains title slides and agendas from several other Kastner speeches circa 1994-1996.


_Published 1996, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **white-paper**._


## Top observations

- Internet transitioning from research network to commercial business platform; growth and opportunity are primary themes
- Business on the Internet tomorrow identified as major opportunity area
- Confirmed — Internet became primary business platform; e-commerce, cloud computing, SaaS transformed all industries
- Current Internet tools are a primary area of assessment for business developers; Blyth Software audience evaluating how to build on Internet
- Blyth Software user group Dallas explicitly evaluating Internet application development with Aberdeen analyst guidance
- Client-server computing and internet convergence identified as major business computing trend
- Customer-driven information systems identified as key trend requiring IT adaptation
- Aberdeen Group presented at Computer Associates events in Madrid and New York circa 1994
- Aberdeen Group presented on Enterprise Applications for Next Century to CA Senior Management Forum 1995
- Kastner expresses skepticism: 'So You Think Objects Are A Panacea? I have a bridge to sell you in Brooklyn'
- CASE tools referenced as a prior technology that was overpromised to management but underdelivered
- Distributed Systems Management: Chaos or Cosmos — presentation questioning whether distributed systems can be managed effectively
- Internet growth and opportunity cited alongside 'fear' — balanced view of Internet's business potential and risks in 1996
- Vision 2000 May 1996: Emerging Technologies: Assessing Strategic Benefits — forward-looking assessment presentation
- Aberdeen Group listed www.aberdeen.com on presentation slides by 1996
