# Peter S. Kastner -- EVP/CRO Biography, Publications List, and Career History

> This document presents Peter S. Kastner's professional biography as Aberdeen Group's Executive Vice President and Chief Research Officer, detailing his career from 1967 through 1999, including roles at Cornell University, PHI Computer Services (Wang subsidiary), Arthur D. Little, Prime Computer, Stratus Computer, Digital Equipment Corporation, and Aberdeen Group. It also records his ten-year publications list (1988-1998) of over 60 Aberdeen publications and lists speaking engagements on behalf of major IT companies.


_Published 1999, author **Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- Executive Vice President and Chief Research Officer; responsible for ~40 analysts; officer and board member
- Joined Aberdeen as Vice President in 1988
- Marketing Executive at Digital Equipment Corporation 1987-1988; commercial market competitive analysis for enterprise marketing campaign
- Market Development Manager 1981-1987; alternate channels, sales support, trade shows, advertising, partnering, press/analyst relations, customer visits
- Market Development Manager 1979-1981; government, finance, manufacturing markets; business planning, sales training, vertical market seminars
- Group Program Manager/Program Manager/Consultant 1971-1979; dozens of complex technology development projects spanning manufacturing, banking, health care, government; US and overseas
- Senior Programmer/Analyst at PHI Computer Services (Wang subsidiary) 1969-1971; commercial applications and systems development; developed payroll/personnel systems, banking applications, application development tools
- Independent consultant at Cornell University 1967-1969; contract application development of commercial, scientific, academic applications
- Aberdeen's 1990 research study: Transaction Processing into the 1990s: The Hardware Suppliers
- 1991 study: Evaluating Options in Microprocessor Multiprocessing
- 1992 study on clustering technology
- Over 60 publications authored during Aberdeen tenure; contributed research to and edited several hundred
- Auditor of TPC benchmarks; now industry advisor to Transaction Processing Council; frequently quoted in trade press
- Spoke on behalf of Apple Computer, AT&T, Computer Associates/CA Ingres, CEO Institute, Digital Equipment, Hewlett-Packard, IBM, Informix, Intel, NCR, Oracle, Sequent, Software AG, Stratus Computer, Tandem Computers
- One of computer industry's leading analysts of OLTP, decision support, client/server, distributed development, e-commerce, commercial benchmark auditing
- Plaintiff support (Blanc Gilburne); RISC-Unix technical documentation; proposed expert witness; settled substantial damages for plaintiff
- Defense support (Fulbright & Jaworski); AS/400 and 3090 mainframe expertise; breach of contract
- Plaintiff expert witness (Carlton Fields for Unisys); distributed IBM mainframe systems; $110M EDS contract later cancelled
- Approximately forty analysts under Kastner's leadership
- Career began 1967 at Cornell University (Langmuir Computer Laboratories); independent consulting 1967-1969
