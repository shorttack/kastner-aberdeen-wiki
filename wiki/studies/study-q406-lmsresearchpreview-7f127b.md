# Labor Management Systems: Strategic Integration of Functions Drives Advanced LMS Agenda

> This research preview previews an Aberdeen study on Labor Management Systems (LMS), covering planning/scheduling, timekeeping, and attendance functions across manufacturing, transportation, banking, retail, and service industries. It outlines efficiency and accounting problems firms face (e.g., significant unaccounted paid time, distorted labor cost allocation) and describes the planned research approach to identify strategic and tactical LMS optimization opportunities.


_Published 2006, author **David Foster / Aberdeen Group**, type **research-agenda**._
