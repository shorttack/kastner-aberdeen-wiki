# Real-Time Customer Interactions Through Precision Marketing

> This research preview describes Aberdeen's planned benchmark on precision marketing techniques such as real-time customer interactions, predictive analytics, segmented campaigns, multi-channel marketing, and closed-loop processes. It explains the survey-based research approach to identify how Best-in-Class companies differ in strategy, execution, and results when using customer intelligence to drive profitable growth.
