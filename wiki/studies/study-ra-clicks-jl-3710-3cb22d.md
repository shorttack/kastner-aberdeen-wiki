## Top observations

- However, a growing number of companies (including 12% of Best-in-Class) are migrating to On Demand solutions that offer monthly rates for use of online eCommerce applications. `[ps=5]`
- Businesses will need to differentiate themselves on the uniqueness of their products, competitive price and the quality of their service operations, as indicated by 47% of Best-in-Class who identify gaining or maintaining market leadership by delivering innovative tools and solutions as a key driver to their success. `[ps=5]`
- Today, 42% of all retailers face pressures to improve data integration processes by centralizing databases and sharing information throughout their enterprises. `[ps=5]`
- 94% of Best-in-Class will use personalization next year to bring in profits. `[ps=5]`
- In contrast, nearly 23% of all merchants are considering dropping their existing platforms and working with a new eCommerce software provider or On Demand solutions provider (although Best-in-Class is leading the On Demand charge). `[ps=5]`
- Technology selection is based on a number of factors, but Best-in-Class B2C companies prioritize product functionality, product architecture and flexibility as the top drivers when selecting technology partners (Figure 6). `[ps=5]`
- Best in class merchants are concerned with functionality, architecture and flexibility when purchasing online technologies. `[ps=5]`
- Best-in-Class retailers are also feeling pressure from customers to improve and upgrade their sites. `[ps=5]`
- Nearly half of the Best-in-Class are combating these pressures by both listening to what their customers are saying and watching what they do. `[ps=5]`
- Previous Aberdeen Reports including _Data Clogs Mid-Market Retail Channels: No Money for a Plumber?,_ have illustrated that 58% of mid-market retailers claimed that they currently collect CRM data but are not using it effectively, and in many cases not at all. `[ps=5]`
- Ninety-three percent of all BIC retailers plan on using personalization in some form or another (Figure 7). `[ps=5]`
- Loyalty programs must differentiate a merchant’s online offering from their competitors. 51% of all firms will use these programs to draw in new and existing customers. `[ps=5]`
- In each of these categories, survey results show that firms exhibiting Best-in-Class behaviors and characteristics also enjoy Best-in-Class customer satisfaction and financial performance: `[ps=5]`
- Yet, 59% of BIC firms make this data actionable by strategically up-selling and crossselling products using personalized collaborative filtering, also known as affinity selling. `[ps=5]`
- Aberdeen research shows that ubiquitous tools like site search and security/privacy encryption are prerequisites for a strong eCommerce experience, yet do little for those trying to catch up to Best-in-Class online merchants in terms of sales lift (Figure 9). `[ps=5]`
- Rather, tools that are designed to capture revenue by providing better information (e.g., interactive imaging), targeted messaging (e.g., alerting) and community driven feedback (Web 2.0 technologies) have resulted in the greatest lift in sales from those trying to catch up to Best-in-Class. `[ps=5]`
- Many online organizations fall short of Best-in-Class status because their ability to make operational changes is constrained by a need to demonstrate ROI. `[ps=5]`
- All retailers are concerned with ROI yet 29% of companies concede to the fact that they don’t measure ROI as much as they should. `[ps=4]`
- Although a significant 22% of the market is currently shopping for new eCommerce platforms, the majority are sticking with their existing platforms and making incremental improvements in the form of online tools designed to capture customers and revenue. `[ps=4]`
- But things are looking up; this holiday season, nearly 90% of consumers will research products T online and about 30% of them will actually transact online **[1]** . `[ps=4]`
- As a result, 59% of Best-in-Class list keeping pace with competitors as a top driver for their online initiatives. `[ps=4]`
- 59% of Best-in-Class retailers are investing in incremental upgrades and improvements to improve their existing eCommerce platforms. `[ps=4]`
- 65% of Best-in-Class online merchants are deploying tools to improve the customer experience and in turn the bottom line. `[ps=4]`
- When thinking about the future and how to achieve revenue goals, 58% of firms are planning to make incremental improvements to their existing web platforms (Figure 4). `[ps=4]`
- Just over 36% of survey respondents have been selling online for five years or more. `[ps=4]`
