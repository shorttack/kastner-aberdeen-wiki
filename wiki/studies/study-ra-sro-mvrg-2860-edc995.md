# The Field Service Scheduling & Routing Optimization Solution Selection Report

> This study analyzes The Field Service Scheduling & Routing Optimization Solution Selection Report using Aberdeen Group research material and prepared extraction artifacts.


_Published 2006, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **topic-analysis**._


## Top observations

- 83% of leading companies indicate that field service optimization is core to improving their competitive positions. `[ps=4]`
- Recent Aberdeen research indicates that companies that re-optimize their service schedules on a real-time or hourly basis have achieved such performance milestones as a 30% jump in work orders completed per day per technician and 26% increase in wrench time (Figure 1). `[ps=4]`
- 90% of large companies have current or planned field service optimization initiatives in place, and the majority of small and mid-size companies are or will be optimizing field service in the near future. `[ps=4]`
- While large companies are leading the way — with 90% of survey respondents currently or planning to optimize field service — the majority of midsize and small companies are or will be optimizing field service in the near future (Figure 2). `[ps=4]`
- As it happens, Aberdeen research indicates that 92% of best-in-class companies are aligning technician attributes with service order demand on a real-time basis (Figure 3). `[ps=4]`
- Aberdeen research has shown that the best service organizations benchmark the competitiveness of their capabilities and performance against industry peers and best-in-class performers. `[ps=4]`
- improved work orders completed per day, per technician by 20%, service contract compliance 25%, and increased wrench time 18% (Figure 5). `[ps=4]`
- According to recent survey data, 58% of respondents do not forecast service demand, and 63% do not forecast service supply or they rely on spreadsheets for this purpose. `[ps=3]`
- Aberdeen research shows that firms exhibiting best-in-class field service characteristics and a successful track record with optimization solutions also enjoy best-in-class service and financial performance (Figure 4). `[ps=3]`
- Case-in-point: For the US$11 billion utilities giant, **British Gas (Centrica),** costly latencies in the service chain were putting a significant drain on profit margins and negatively impacting productivity, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction. `[ps=3]`
- 74% of best-in-class companies optimize their field service operations to increase overall profitability. `[ps=0]`
