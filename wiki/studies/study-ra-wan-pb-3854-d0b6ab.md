- 94% of respondents expect total WAN bandwidth requirements to grow within the next 12 months `[ps=4]`
- Best-in-Class measure more service attributes on a daily or real-time basis than any other class of respondent `[ps=4]`
- demand as forecasted by the respondents is quite robust: 94% of respondents expect total bandwidth requirements within the WAN to grow within the next 12 months while only 6% expect it to remain the same. `[ps=4]`
- And, in terms of 12 month purchase intent as shown in figure 2 below, the most widely deployed services are forecasted with nominal growth, while the emerging services such as MPLS and Ethernet WAN services are forecasted at more robust levels (21% and 12% respectively). `[ps=4]`
- Given the much higher emphasis of Best-in-Class organizations on Class of Service and Voice and Data Convergence, it is clear that managing the dynamics of multiple traffic types – voice requires low jitter (variability in packet transmission) and low latency (delay) while data traffic is far more tolerant of these – is a higher priority for Best-in-Class organizations. `[ps=4]`
- Through inefficient processes to manage and process telecom expenses causing late payment penalties, high frequency of service provider billing errors, incomplete comparative benchmarking of sourcing, and inadequate visibility into spend, many organizations are clearly missing opportunities to reduce telecom expenses by as much as 10% of their spend. `[ps=4]`
- Working with the escalation procedures of service providers who monitor their own service networks to proactively report in real-time the disruptions, service outages and recovery responses is the most frequently used method for 75% of the Best-in-Class. `[ps=4]`
- The presence of a 7x24 Network Operations Center to monitor and respond to the operational dynamics of the complete organizational communications and applications infrastructure is a strong complement to the service provider escalation process and is used by 69% of the Best-in-Class, but only 41% of the other respondents. `[ps=4]`
- The Best-in-Class measure more attributes on a daily or in real-time basis `[ps=4]`
- The Best-in-Class WAN measurements are on a site-by-site basis `[ps=4]`
