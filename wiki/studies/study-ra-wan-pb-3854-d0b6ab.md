## Top observations

- 94% of respondents expect total WAN bandwidth requirements to grow within the next 12 months `[ps=5]`
- Best-in-Class measure more service attributes on a daily or real-time basis than any other class of respondent `[ps=5]`
- 94% of organizations expect to increase bandwidth, 6% expect it to stay the same, none expect bandwidth requirements to decline `[ps=5]`
- demand as forecasted by the respondents is quite robust: 94% of respondents expect total bandwidth requirements within the WAN to grow within the next 12 months while only 6% expect it to remain the same. `[ps=5]`
- Converging voice & data is a key capability for 88% of Best-in-Class, 65% of Others `[ps=5]`
- urther to figure 3 below, the convergence of voice and data onto the WAN has been a major capability for the better part of the past decade. `[ps=5]`
- Given the much higher emphasis of Best-in-Class organizations on Class of Service and Voice and Data Convergence, it is clear that managing the dynamics of multiple traffic types – voice requires low jitter (variability in packet transmission) and low latency (delay) while data traffic is far more tolerant of these – is a higher priority for Best-in-Class organizations. `[ps=5]`
- Through inefficient processes to manage and process telecom expenses causing late payment penalties, high frequency of service provider billing errors, incomplete comparative benchmarking of sourcing, and inadequate visibility into spend, many organizations are clearly missing opportunities to reduce telecom expenses by as much as 10% of their spend. `[ps=5]`
- In contrast, the Best-in-Class view WAN services as a necessary business infrastructure and therefore expect value for dollar paid through other features of the offering. `[ps=5]`
- Clearly, methods for integrating the device into the existing management and operational methods and tools are key for the Best-in-Class. `[ps=5]`
- The Best-in-Class measure more attributes on a daily or in real-time basis `[ps=5]`

- The process and organizational discipline necessary to measure audio quality, availability, jitter and latency performance, and WAN caC pacity performance are among the top actions of the Best-in-Class. `[ps=5]`
- 21% of organizations plan to deploy MPLS services; 12% plan to deploy Ethernet WAN services in the next 12 months `[ps=4]`
- 2.7 times more of the Best-in-Class rated their ability to measure ROI of the WAN as high or very high `[ps=4]`

- Organizations are already in various stages of deployments, pilots and testing as demonstrated in the number of services deployed in support of the business: 75% of respondents had at least three WAN services in place or planned, 37% had at least five services deployed or planned and 12% had only one. `[ps=4]`
