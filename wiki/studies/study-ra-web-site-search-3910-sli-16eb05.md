## Top observations

- Web site search is a minimum competitive requirement for online commerce as indicated by the 96% of eCommerce web sites that currently have or will have a search tool in place in the next twenty-four months. `[ps=5]`
- • Analytics derived from search are valuable. 72% of Best-in-Class use this data to improve SEO practices and 64% use analytics data to influence marketing campaigns. `[ps=5]`
- • Search tools convert shoppers to buyers at conversion rates of 5% or higher for 60% of companies that track this metric. `[ps=4]`
- Although search is a ubiquitous technology, producing accurate and relevant results remains a top pressure for 78% of survey respondents who seek to deliver search tools to enhance the customer experience. `[ps=4]`
- Best-in-Class companies follow this up by offering collaborative filtering in the form of related searches or similar products purchased by others to influence a buyer’s purchasing decision. `[ps=4]`
- Additionally, learning more about customers through analytics and personalizing the results for unique customers or customer segments ranked as top strategies for Best-in-Class merchants. `[ps=4]`
- 52% of Best-in-Class use metrics derived from search extensively throughout their enterprises. `[ps=4]`
- To combat these challenges, 50% of Best-in-Class companies use a faceted search `[ps=4]`
- While 41% of average and leading merchants are learning from collective behavior, predictive merchandising is currently used by 26% of Best-in-Class merchants. `[ps=4]`
- When asked about processes used to merchandise to customers, Aberdeen found that Best-in-Class companies predominately use search analytics to learn about customer be- `[ps=4]`
- Best-in-Class companies use search technologies that foster a customer centric online experience such as tolerance for mis-spelled words, thesaurus/synonyms and natural language processing. `[ps=4]`
- These vendors are helping 91% of leading online retailers improve customer conversion rates, 60% show increased average order value and 100% of leading retailers report year over year increases in customer satisfaction as a result of their web site search applications. `[ps=3]`
- These features are currently used by 52% of Best-in-Class and can be modified easily based on product availability or seasonality to generate additional revenue. `[ps=3]`
- 51% of Laggards dedicate very little time if any at all, to managing search applications. 17% of Best-in-Class have a full time employee dedicated to this task. `[ps=3]`
- Best-in-Class companies are twice as likely to utilize search data and knowledge throughout their enterprise. `[ps=3]`
- Twice as many non-Best-in-Class companies use customer feedback to drive operational changes. `[ps=2]`
- In all cases except natural language processing, at least half of Best-in-Class companies currently deploy these technologies while at least 80% of all companies plan to deploy these technologies within the next 12 to 24 months. `[ps=2]`
- Nearly 60% of customers visiting these same web sites utilize the search tool on their visits. `[ps=0]`
