# Good CFOs Count Contracts More Than Beans

> This September 2006 Research Brief argues that effective contract management is now central to the CFO's agenda, covering both sales contracts (revenue) and supply contracts (cost/risk). It discusses how contract rules, negotiated terms, and full-term deliverable management help enterprises improve revenue recognition and forecasting reliability, manage compliance and supply risk, and capture financial rewards.


_Published 2006, author **Vishal Patel / Aberdeen Group**, type **benchmark**._


## Top observations

- Contract cycle times for Best in Class enterprises are _**more than three times faster**_ (8 days vs. 26 days) than their peers. `[ps=4]`
- Also according to Aberdeen research, Best in Class supply management organizations with effective contract management programs have a _**28.6% performance advantage**_ over their peers. `[ps=4]`
