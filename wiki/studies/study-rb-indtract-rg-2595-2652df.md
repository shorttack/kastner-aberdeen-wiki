# Can Post-Sales Service Drive Profitable Business for All OEMs?

> This January 2006 Research Brief summarizes findings from Aberdeen's Industry Traction of Strategic Service Management benchmark, finding 61% of surveyed companies treat field service as a strategic operation with revenue and profit goals, and that medical equipment, high-tech, and industrial manufacturing industries lead in post-sales service process improvement. It identifies factors driving industry adoption of strategic service management, including asset complexity, product introduction frequency, and field service force size.


_Published 2006, author **Mark Vigoroso / Aberdeen Group**, type **benchmark**._
