# True Source: The Dark Horse in Spend & Supplier Management Race?

> This October 2005 Research Brief evaluates True Source, a spend data management, contract management, and supplier management vendor, based on Aberdeen's meetings, product demonstrations, and customer interviews. It describes True Source's Spend Intelligence Solution (SIS) product family, built on data cleansing/analysis rather than transactional e-RFx or auction capabilities, and positions the company as a potential dark horse in the Global Supply Management market.
