# The Identity and Authentication Management Benchmark: Know Your Friends and Partners

> This research preview/sponsor prospectus outlines Aberdeen's planned benchmark on identity and authentication management, addressing how organizations verify identity and manage trust as network boundaries blur with SOA, mobile, and outsourcing trends. It covers privacy considerations for protecting customer, partner, and employee information, and describes sponsor benefits rather than presenting completed research findings.


_Published 2005, author **Mounil Patel / Aberdeen Group**, type **research-agenda**._
