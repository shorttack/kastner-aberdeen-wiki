# Managing Security Like a Business: The Security Information Management (SIM) and Reporting Benchmark

> This is a research preview describing Aberdeen Group's planned survey-based benchmark study on security information management (SIM) and reporting tools. It outlines how organizations use SIM tools and advanced reporting to increase efficiency in systems maintenance, measure trends and policy compliance, and prioritize risk management spending. The preview describes the research approach (broad-based survey plus end-user and vendor interviews) and target audience, but does not present findings since the study itself had not yet been conducted.


_Published 2005, author **Mounil Patel / Aberdeen Group**, type **research-agenda**._
