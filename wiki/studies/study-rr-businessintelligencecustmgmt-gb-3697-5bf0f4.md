## Top observations

- Best-in-Class retailers lead the way when it comes to managing customer product interaction: 70% of these organizations track new product adoption; 100% track promotion participation; and 91% of these retailers track product preferences/affinity. `[ps=5]`
- The net result is that Best-in-class retailers have a sharper understanding of customer/product behaviors. `[ps=5]`
- Other top uses of BI in customer management included sales analysis, identification and targeting specific households as potential customers, and prediction of spending patterns (Figure 1). `[ps=5]`
- Best-in-Class retailers are on the cutting edge of quantifying customer behaviors: 73% of these organizations measure customer conversion, and 100% of Best-in-Class retailers measure retention and acquisition. `[ps=5]`
- • Best-in-Class retailers also lead the way when it comes to managing a customer’s product interaction: 70% of these organizations track new product adoption, 100% track promotion participation, and 91% of these retailers track product preferences/affinity. `[ps=5]`
- Lack of data management centralization was the top challenge associated with business intelligence in customer management (according to 49% of respondents), and data cleansing projects to improve data quality was the top response to existing challenges (according to 61% of respondents). `[ps=5]`

- In addition, in all three metrics, Best-in-Class were much more likely then either of the previous two groups to use such metrics in their business process. `[ps=5]`
- In the original Aberdeen Group business intelligence benchmark report, it was reported that 58% of average performers, and 70% of Best-in-Class performers noted that their BI endeavors were driven by top management, including C-level executives. `[ps=5]`
- Indeed, Best-in-Class C-level executives may be using Corporate Performance Dashboards, in addition to and augmented by operational BI, to monitor customer activity. `[ps=5]`
- This type of challenge was consistent among retailers of all types, such as laggard, industry average, and Best-in-Class respondents. `[ps=5]`
- 100% of BIC organizations measure customer retention. `[ps=5]`
