# Information Security: 1998 Practice Summary

> Aberdeen Group's 1998 overview of the enterprise information security market, sizing it at $4.285 billion in 1997 with a projected 24% CAGR to $10 billion by 2000. The report maps the security landscape into access-control and risk-management categories, identifies fragmentation and non-interoperability as the dominant pain points, and forecasts consolidation, the rise of VPNs, and the commoditization of standalone firewalls. Supplier profiles cover 14 vendors including Check Point, Axent, Network Associates, IBM, and VeriSign.


_Published 1998, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **other-research**._


## Top observations

- $4,285 million
- 24% compound annual growth rate
- $10,005 million
- Identity credentials and authentication systems — $1.928 billion
- Anti-virus and applet controls — $890 million
- Firewall and network access controls — $429 million
- Information access controls — $343 million
- commoditizing; fragmenting to high-end and low-end; embedding into routers
- Prediction proved accurate; Check Point maintained high-end while low-end commoditized.
- replacing expensive leased lines; ROI in 1-3 months
- will go mainstream by 1999 with automated rules-engines
- declining rate; moving to gateway; unit volumes shrinking
- access-control solutions vs. risk-management solutions
- IS decision makers do NOT expect Microsoft
- want consolidation; no single supplier dense enough yet
- key business executives commonly involved alongside IS executive
- Web computing requires security unlike client-server
- larger suppliers with capital acquiring innovative smaller suppliers
- IS will spend more on professional services for security
- few will emerge before 2000 enabling integrated I&A and IAC
- financial services and telecom bypassing Big 5 consulting for in-house and specialist contractors
- leading firewall and VPN vendor
- leading PKI and digital certificate services provider
- web-based I&A will drive spending through 2000 as enterprise integrates customers and partners
