# AberdeenGroup SnapShot: Snap Appliance Inc.

> This Aberdeen Group SnapShot profiles Snap Appliance Inc., the self-described number-one volume NAS provider, which was reborn as a private company in 2002 after being spun out of Quantum. The profile details Snap's product line spanning workgroup to enterprise NAS using inexpensive ATA disks, and explicitly maps Snap's coverage of Aberdeen's midline storage categories including staging, fixed content, consolidation, replication, and PC backup. The document positions Snap as a key validation of Aberdeen's midline disk storage thesis.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **case-analysis**._


## Top observations

- Number one, volume leading provider of NAS solutions from workgroup to enterprise
- Over 130,000 NAS units installed as of October 2003
- First Snap Servers launched by Meridian Data in 1998; Quantum acquired company in 2000
- Spun out of Quantum in 2002 as private company; original IPO plans shelved
- Private company with strong product line and 130k+ installed base; positioned for growth in NAS market
- Acquired by Overland Storage in 2008; Overland later merged with Sphere 3D in 2014. Company did not survive as independent entity.
- Sells through manufacturers, distributors, and resellers in North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific
- Snap storage appliances use inexpensive ATA disks in variety of redundancy configurations — key cost advantage
- Snap creatively covered all mid-line storage categories envisaged by Aberdeen
- (1) SCSI-to-tape staging, (2) fixed content long-term storage, (3) light/moderate applications with storage consolidation, (4) data replication, (5) PC client backup
- IT professionals increasingly vocal about urgent need: keep up with storage growth, low-cost alternatives, simple products
- SMB companies and enterprises with minimal or no IT staffing — heterogeneous environments
- World's best selling NAS server as of 2003; capable and portable file server
- Snap Server 2200: larger storage capacity with RAID 1 data protection
- Snap Server 4100: entry-level RAID 5 in slim rack-mountable chassis
- Built around 4 ATA-100 disks; 320 GB to 1 TB total; dual gigabit-Ethernet; 1U rack; expandable to 3 TB with Snap Disk 10 arrays
- 1.44 TB to 3.0 TB across 12 disks in 3U rack; ADS and NIS security protocol support for Unix/Windows datacenters
- Linux-based OS for departmental and enterprise Snap Servers; snapshots and Windows ADS support; same ease of use as workgroup SnapOS
- Unlimited user license of DataKeeper desktop backup software for Windows clients included with every Snap Server
- Snap's NAS + ATA product line validated as winning combination for midline storage tier
- NAS became ubiquitous in enterprise storage by 2008-2012 exactly as predicted. Every major vendor built out NAS product lines.
- Founded 1994 per company facts sidebar
- Aberdeen research cited: Mid-Line Disk Storage Emerging As Significant Cost-Saving Opportunity (October 2003)
- Software partners: APC, Computer Associates, Legato, PowerQuest, SyncSort, Veritas, Sun Microsystems
- Aberdeen framework positions midline ATA/NAS as significant cost-saving opportunity vs FC/SCSI for non-OLTP workloads
