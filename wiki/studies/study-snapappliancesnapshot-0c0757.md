# Snap Appliance Inc.

> This is an Aberdeen Group vendor snapshot profiling Snap Appliance Inc., the volume leader in network-attached storage (NAS) solutions with more than 130,000 units installed. It traces the company's history from Meridian Data's 1998 launch of Snap Servers through acquisition by Quantum and its 2002 rebirth as a private company, and details its Snap Server product line (1100 through 14000), software partnerships, and leadership team as of 2003.
