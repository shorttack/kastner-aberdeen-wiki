# Enterprise Integration Technology: Aberdeen Group's Market Trends & Research for 2006

> 24-slide Aberdeen Group webinar presentation deck (SOA_AON_webinar_022206a). Covers enterprise integration market challenges/opportunities, 2005 SOA benchmark findings, and Aberdeen's full 2006 Enterprise Integration Research Agenda (Q1-Q4). Key 2005 findings: 92% of companies at or below 25% SOA adoption; over half predict 50%+ SOA-based software in 5 years. Best-in-Class SOA adopters spend 29.6% of IT budget on innovation vs. 18.5% average; software maintenance costs 12.4% vs. 27.3% average. Research agenda spans NAP, SOA app development, edge computing, data center integration, real-time BI, and knowledge worker productivity.


_Published 2006, author **Peter S. Kastner, Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- 92% at or below 25% SOA adoption rate in 2005
- Over half of respondents predict 50%+ of software will be SOA-based in five years
- 29.6% of IT budget on innovation (vs. 18.5% average); +11pp advantage
- 12.4% of IT budget on software maintenance (vs. 27.3% average); -15pp advantage
- Development of new business capabilities or products: 50% of respondents
- Management of IT complexity: 44% of respondents
- Re-usage of applications via Web Services: 43% of respondents
- Alignment with the business: 37% of respondents
- IT cost of delivering/building new applications: 64% of respondents tracking
- 54% of respondents tracking IT budget as percent of revenues
- Changes within IT organization required to mirror service-delivery aspects of SOA
- Network traffic shifting from binary data packets to XML-based content routing and web services messaging due to SOA
- Lines between network and software architecture blurring due to SOA and XML traffic
- Changing technology landscape as SOA development integrates with legacy applications and development tools
- RFID and POS devices driving volume and complexity at SOA edge; edge vs. core computing tradeoffs
- Integrating Real-Time Technology and Business Management Benchmark
- Q2 2006: Integrating the Data Center: New Architectures and Opportunities
- Q4 2006: Enterprise Integration Strategies and Best Practices for the Mid-Market
- 137,000+ research members; 100+ primary research studies in past 12 months; 500+ derivative reports; 17,100 business/technology executives benchmarked
- 900 of G1000 companies in Aberdeen's research community; 465 of Software 500 are Aberdeen clients
- Speed of IT implementations: 27% of respondents
- Managing IT integration costs: 24% of respondents
- Companies Are Bullish on SOA Adoption, But the Market is Immature
- SOA, EAI, ETL, B2BI, EII, XML, CORBA named as core enterprise integration technologies
- SOA enables IT to be more productive under ongoing budget pressures
