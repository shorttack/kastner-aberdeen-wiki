# SOA and Web Services Testing: How Different Can It Be?

> 26-page Aberdeen Group benchmark report examining testing and quality assurance challenges for SOA and web services deployments. Unit and functional testing insufficient; integration, regression, business process, performance, and security testing all required. Survey of 240 end-users categorizes companies into Best-in-Class (top 20%), Industry Average (middle 50%), and Laggard (bottom 30%) using Aberdeen Competitive Framework and PACE model. Best-in-Class companies redesigned QA processes, use automated testing, involve business users throughout lifecycle, and track quality across entire project—not just at end.


_Published 2007, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **employer-record**._


## Top observations

- N=240 end-users
- 94% of Best-in-Class reported increase in software quality
- 61% of Best-in-Class saw reduction in production defects
- 57% of Best-in-Class reported decrease in mean time to repair defects
- 71% of Best-in-Class saw increase in code test coverage
- 70% of Best-in-Class said maintainability improved
- 57% of Best-in-Class vs 35% of others use automated testing tools
- 81% of Best-in-Class manage quality throughout lifecycle vs 63% of others
- 26% Best-in-Class vs 19% others involve business users in quality
- 55-56% of all companies cite reducing time to deliver as top QA driver
- 60% Best-in-Class vs 52% others cite reducing defects as QA driver
- 28% BIC vs 43% others cite risk reduction as QA driver
- 20% Best-in-Class redesigned QA completely vs 7% others
- 62% Best-in-Class vs 54% others test SOA/web services differently from traditional software
- 45% of Best-in-Class use requirements tracking tools vs 35% of Avg/Laggard
- 81% of BIC manage requirements throughout software lifecycle vs 72% Avg, 54% Laggard
- 38% BIC vs 28% Avg vs 21% Laggard use automation for requirements tracking
- 48% BIC use design-time governance to foster reusability vs 33% others
- Only 39% of Laggard organizations conduct regression testing
- Companies with formal QA often budget 2-3x development time for QA testing
- iSOFT rebuilt LORENZO product from scratch on SOA when older platform reached extendibility limits
- Best-in-Class are twice as likely to use automated requirements-tracking tools vs Laggard
- 70% of Best-in-Class measure quality throughout project lifecycle not just in testing
- 47% of Best-in-Class use design-time governance to promote reuse vs 33% average
- Expand quality focus: take end-to-end perspective and test interoperability across entire business process
