# Akamai Technologies

> This is an Aberdeen Group vendor spotlight (dated December 31, 2002) profiling Akamai Technologies (Nasdaq: AKAM), a publicly traded content and application delivery network provider with 550 employees. It covers the company's financial results (a $202.6 million loss on $145 million revenue for FY2002), technology partnerships (BEA Systems, IBM, Microsoft, Oracle, Vignette), leadership team, and its EdgeSuite family of content/application delivery products and pricing.
