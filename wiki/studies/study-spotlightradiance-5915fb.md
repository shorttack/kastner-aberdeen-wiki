# Radiance Technologies

> This is an Aberdeen Group vendor spotlight profiling Radiance Technologies, a privately held managed data-delivery software company founded in 2000 with venture funding from Sutter Hill Ventures, Vanguard Ventures, and ChevronTexaco. It covers the company's TrueDelivery System for moving large product-related data files across global networks, target verticals (automotive, aerospace/defense, oil and gas, consumer electronics), and offices in California and India.
