# Stratacache, Inc.

> This is an Aberdeen Group vendor spotlight profiling Stratacache, Inc., a privately held network and application acceleration appliance maker founded in 1999 with roughly 100 employees. It covers the company's five solution segments (content caching, content distribution, video delivery, traffic management, bandwidth optimization), 2002 sales figures (25,000 units to 2,400 accounts), and technology partnerships (Websense, Trend Micro, Foundry Networks, F5, Packeteer, Hughes, Telesat).
