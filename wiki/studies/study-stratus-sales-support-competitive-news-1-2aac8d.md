> Stratus Sales Support Competitive News, Volume 6 Number 3, May 1987. Editor Peter Kastner (Manager, Sales Support Programs, Marlboro x2192) publishes a detailed competitive analysis of Tandem's April 20 and May 4, 1987 announcements: the new low-end CLX and LXN 'network extender' products, smaller VLX configurations (2- and 3-CPU models at $585K entry), and price cuts across the EXT/TXP line. Kastner also notes Tandem's quiet restatement of ET-1 performance numbers under NonStop SQL (2, 4.5, 6.5 tps/CPU for NS-II/EXT10, TXP/EXT25, VLX) with relaxed 2-second 90th-percentile response time. Contributor Raphael Frommer authored the detailed CLX/LXN technical analysis. The newsletter quotes Omri Serlin (Fault Tolerant Systems Newsletter No. 55, March 16 1987). Signature Kastner quote: 'Business has only two basic functions — marketing and innovation.'


_Published 1987, author **Peter S. Kastner, Editor (Manager, Sales Support Programs, Stratus); Raphael Frommer, contributor**, type **newsletter**._


## Top observations

- Tandem's trend toward proven comparator-based self-checking design is NOT a departure from their software approach to hardware fault tolerance; checkpointing still critical `[ps=5]`
- Stratus ET-1 measurements to date have been based on file system, not ORACLE SQL environment — apples-to-apples comparison complicated `[ps=3]`
- Business has only two basic functions — marketing and innovation `[ps=3]`
- 2-CPU CLX 620: 5 NonStop SQL ET-1 tps; $85,000 unit (4Q87); $59,500 in qty 25-39 `[ps=0]`
- 4-CPU CLX 640: 10 tps; $161,000 unit; $112,700 in qty 25-39 (1Q88) `[ps=0]`
- 6-CPU CLX 660: 15 tps; $240,000 unit; $168,000 in qty 25-39 (2Q88) `[ps=0]`
- Uniprocessor non-fault-tolerant CLX 610: 2.5 tps; $57,000 unit; $39,900 in qty 25-39 `[ps=0]`
- $23,700 unit (2MB, 80MB disk, 10-port comm); $18,012 in qty 25 under Networking Discount Plan `[ps=0]`
- NonStop SQL-based transaction-protected ratings are 2, 4.5, 6.5 tps/CPU for NS-II/EXT10, TXP/EXT25, VLX with 2-second 90%-ile response time (relaxed from sub-second) `[ps=0]`
- Official reason given is that this permits comparison across entire product line including low end `[ps=0]`
- Time will tell how well Tandem can market this customized UNIX product against other less costly UNIX offerings `[ps=0]`
- $8,000 per CPU for GUARDIAN XL (GUARDIAN 90, ENCOMPASS, NonStop SQL, TRANSFER, PS MAIL, EXPAND) `[ps=0]`
- Tandem has added new low-end and high-end products, cut prices, and cut performance to go with a new SQL database product
- New 2- and 3-CPU VLX models; minimum VLX now $585K (down from ~$1M 4-CPU)
- LXN is an Altos 3068-based product resold by Tandem — 'unprecedented reselling of another company's product'
- In spite of Tandem's numerous announcements, Stratus maintains a strong competitive position
