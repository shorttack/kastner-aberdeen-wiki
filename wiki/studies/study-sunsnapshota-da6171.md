# Sun Microsystems, Incorporated

> This is an Aberdeen Group vendor snapshot profiling Sun Microsystems, Incorporated, a major technology vendor with about 34,800 employees and $12,496 million in fiscal year 2002 revenue. It covers Sun's security and identity product portfolio (Sun ONE Identity/Certificate/Portal/Directory Server, Trusted Solaris, SunScreen, Sun Crypto Accelerator, and related services), an extensive partner list, and leadership team including CEO Scott McNealy.
