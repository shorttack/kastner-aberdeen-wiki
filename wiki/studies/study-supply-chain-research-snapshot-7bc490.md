> This Aberdeen Group document is a 2007 research calendar/agenda outlining planned supply chain and logistics studies across three focus areas: Supply Chain Planning, Logistics and Supply Chain Execution, and Global Trade and Supply Chain Finance. It previews upcoming reports (e.g., automotive/aerospace supply chain strategies, SaaS TCO benefits, B2B collaboration benchmarks, process industry supply chains, demand management) with challenge/focus/outcome summaries, and announces Aberdeen's first annual Global Supply Chain Summit.
