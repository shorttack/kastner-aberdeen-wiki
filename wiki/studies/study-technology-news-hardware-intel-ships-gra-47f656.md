# Intel Ships Grantsdale and Alderwood Chipsets

> TechNewsWorld article (Jun 22 2004, Jay Lyman) on Intel's release of the 915 G/P (Grantsdale) and 925 X (Alderwood) desktop chipsets — bundled with new Pentium 4 LGA775 processors, supporting high-definition video, 7.1 surround audio, DDR2 memory, PCI Express, RAID 0/1, Wi-Fi, and hardware security for the forthcoming Windows XP SP2. Intel's Louis Burns calls this 'a significant evolution.' Aberdeen chief research officer Peter Kastner delivers the canonical analyst frame: 'The new 915 G/P and 925 X chipsets represent the biggest change in input-output capability in a decade.' Kastner says the primary benefit will be support for media-center-type computers, positively benefiting consumers and corporate users. Gartner's Martin Reynolds: 'A major cycle rather than a minor cycle… this is a big cycle and provides the platform headroom for new processors.' Reynolds forecasts the new chipsets will be the basis of most PCs for the next two years.


_Published 2004, author **Jay Lyman, TechNewsWorld**, type **news-article**._


## Top observations

- Windows Media Center PCs (Kastner's primary Grantsdale use-case prediction) peaked 2005-2010 with Windows XP MCE and Vista/7 Media Center editions, then declined: Microsoft discontinued Media Center in Windows 10 (2015); displaced by streaming services (Netflix/Hulu/Amazon Prime Video) and smart TVs. `[ps=5]`
- The new 915 G/P and 925 X chipsets represent the biggest change in input-output capability in a decade. All of this adds up to significant changes that will positively benefit consumers and corporate users. `[ps=4]`
- Gartner's Martin Reynolds: This is a major cycle rather than a minor cycle. This is a big cycle and it provides the platform headroom for new processors. It will be the basis of most PCs for the next two years; will represent the mainstream platform in a year's time. `[ps=4]`
- Enterprise users will appreciate fast access and mirroring capabilities from built-in RAID technologies while consumers will benefit from better audio, graphics, gaming performance. `[ps=3]`
- Intel 915 G/P (Grantsdale) and 925 X (Alderwood) chipsets; LGA775 Pentium 4 with Hyper-Threading; high-definition video support; 7.1 surround audio; DDR2 memory; PCI Express; RAID 0/1; Wi-Fi; hardware security for Windows XP SP2; graphics media accelerator with wide-screen LCD and HD support. `[ps=0]`
- The primary benefit of the new chipsets will be support for media-center-type computers that play music, video and more in a way that previous computers could not.
- PCI Express (introduced in mainstream PCs via Grantsdale Jun 2004) became the universal PC expansion bus: replaced PCI and AGP by ~2008; today at PCIe Gen5/6 standard in PCs, servers, laptops. Kastner's 'biggest I/O change in a decade' framing validated.
