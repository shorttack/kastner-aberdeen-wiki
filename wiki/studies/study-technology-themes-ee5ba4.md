# IT Technology Themes and Trends

> Aberdeen Group analysis of 10 major technology themes shaping enterprise IT: IT deflation and server pricing pressure, storage market transformation via ILM, empowered consumers driving retail IT, best-of-breed consolidation, ROI-driven IT purchasing, IT over-buying and rationalization, consumer power reshaping supply chains, real-time consumer tracking, wireless laptop adoption barriers, enterprise management in web services environments, and Sarbanes-Oxley/Basel II compliance costs.


_Published 2003, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **topic-analysis**._


## Top observations

- ~30% per year
- Significant; fewer processors per server reducing per-processor license revenue
- Skyrocketing due to compliance, replication, PC data proliferation
- Early/emerging; new thinking about tiered storage pools
- Increasing in retail automation, CRM, analytics
- ~1/3 consider themselves IT-savvy; another 1/3 at least competent
- CIOs seeing integration costs favoring single-vendor suites
- Executive committee now controls budget; wants high-ROI projects implementable in weeks not months
- Large firms own dozens of redundant/overlapping software products; multi-million dollar unused purchases
- RFID tags, automated replenishment, enhanced parcel tracking driven by modern consumer
- Real-time click tracking replacing survey/Nielsen panel methods
- ~30% of enterprise computers are laptops; few takers for dramatic increase
- Security fears, high support cost, high initial cost cited by executives
- Extended value chains vastly complicating enterprise management; partner autonomy conflicts
- Several hundred thousand to several million dollars per year per affected business
- Commodity prices and cradle-to-grave services incompatible business models
- Consumers expect purchase via store, phone, web, PDA; mass customization on demand
- Uncertain; hotspot investments hard to profit without corporate laptop adoption growth

- Some firms may revert to private status due to compliance costs
