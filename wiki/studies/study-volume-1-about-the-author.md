# About the Author

> This biographical summary introduces Peter S. Kastner as a fifty-year participant in the information technology industry, tracing his path from a Cape Cod restaurant bookkeeping department through MIT, Cornell, and a succession of influential roles at Philip Hankins, Arthur D. Little, Prime Computer, Stratus Computer, Digital Equipment Corporation, and Aberdeen Group. It highlights his role in founding Aberdeen Group, building its research practice to 150 analysts, co-founding the Transaction Processing Performance Council, and maintaining an active expert witness practice in technology disputes.


_Published 2026, author **Peter S. Kastner**, type **memoir**._


## Top observations

- Fifty years as a participant at each defining inflection point of the IT industry
- Grew up in Chatham, Massachusetts
- Family operated one of Cape Cod's landmark restaurants in Chatham, Massachusetts
- Sorted 10,000 pieces of paper a night in the bookkeeping department
- Machines should handle the routine work, so that people could do something better with their time
- Trained as a computer operator at MIT in 1966
- ELIZA was being built in the same building where Kastner trained at MIT in 1966
- Project MAC was laying the foundations of modern timesharing in the same MIT building where Kastner trained
- Taught himself to program on IBM mainframes overnight on his own operator account at MIT
- $360 per hour for IBM mainframe computer time at MIT in 1966
- Returned to Cornell University, School of Hotel Administration, after MIT training
- Operated Cornell's IBM System 360/65 while continuing undergraduate education
- Kastner wrote some of Cornell University's first commercial software applications
- Professional career began at Philip Hankins Inc. in Arlington, Massachusetts
- Described as a 'hot house' of elite talent
- Alumni went on to shape Wang Laboratories, application packages, the Apollo program's software heritage, and earliest enterprise computing
- Philip Hankins alumni went on to shape Wang Laboratories
- Philip Hankins alumni contributed to the Apollo program's software heritage
- Eight years of management consulting at Arthur D. Little Systems in Cambridge, MA
- Chase Manhattan Bank was a consulting client of Kastner at Arthur D. Little Systems
- Kastner consulted for a federal university hospital in Rio de Janeiro at Arthur D. Little Systems
- Kastner consulted for municipal police departments in Florida and Virginia at Arthur D. Little Systems
- Kastner consulted for a paper mill in Louisiana at Arthur D. Little Systems
- Kastner consulted for a publisher in crisis at Arthur D. Little Systems
- Consulted for a utility running remote meter reading over power lines at five baud
