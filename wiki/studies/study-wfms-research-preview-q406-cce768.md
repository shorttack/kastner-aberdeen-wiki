# Workforce Management Systems: Strategic Integration of Functions Drives Advanced WFMS Agenda

> A Q4 2006 Aberdeen Group research preview outlining an upcoming benchmark study on workforce management systems (WFMS), covering planning/scheduling and timekeeping/attendance functions across manufacturing, transportation, banking, retail, and service industries. It frames efficiency, accounting, and strategic problems in current WFMS deployments, lists vendors to be covered (ADP, Ceridian, Kronos, Oracle, SAP, Workbrain, and others), and describes the survey-based research approach and target audience.


_Published 2006, author **David Foster / Aberdeen Group**, type **research-agenda**._


## Top observations

- What we don’t know yet, and what this study is designed to reveal, is the extent to which such additional functionality is being used or planned for by corporations with both types of work and what impacts such multi-purpose workforce management applications are having in generating Best in Class results. `[ps=0]`
