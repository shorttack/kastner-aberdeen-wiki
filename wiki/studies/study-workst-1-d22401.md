> Aberdeen Group's 1998 practice summary covering standalone Unix and Windows NT technical workstations used for CAD/MCAD/CAE/DCC/animation/scientific visualization and decision-support. The document profiles market leaders (HP Sun IBM SGI for Unix; HP Compaq Dell Digital IBM Intergraph for NT) and provides key findings including NT growing at 30%+ annually, Unix maintaining high-end dominance for 4-5 more years, and the emerging use of workstations as data visualization decision-support tools. HP is identified as the overall combined-platform market leader for 1997.


_Published 1998, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **other-research**._


## Top observations

- 2000 at earliest; ensures Unix dominance at high end for next 3 years `[ps=5]`
- Flat-to-decreased growth over next several years as NT-on-Intel gains in processor and graphics performance `[ps=4]`
- $2,000 (low-end) to ~$20,000; median $5,000-$7,500 `[ps=4]`
- NT benchmarked equal to midrange Unix at one-third the acquisition cost `[ps=4]`
- #3 unit sales; quality AIX and IBM Global Services positive; PowerPC performance issues `[ps=4]`
- Declining market share; financial and management turmoil; undercut by Sun HP and NT-on-Intel `[ps=4]`
- #2 NT position achieved in only 16 months; strategy: right price for low-end and mid-range enterprise `[ps=4]`
- #5 total NT unit volume; strong 3D graphics but loss of share due to lack of mid-level product focus `[ps=4]`
- Majority through indirect channels (HP Sun IBM Compaq SGI Intergraph); direct model (Dell Gateway Micron) gaining `[ps=4]`
- NT 4.0 Workstation compliant with minor issues; made fully compliant via Service Pack 3 and free QFE fixes `[ps=4]`
- #6 NT position in 1997 after March launch; significant progress on ISV relationships and qualified apps `[ps=3]`
- Market-leading ISV for CAD/MCAD; applications capture for PTC determines platform purchasing decisions `[ps=3]`
- Greater than 30% growth rate; continuing at ~30% well into next millennium `[ps=1]`
- #1 Unix workstation seller by greater than 2:1 margin over HP (#2 Unix) `[ps=0]`
- #3 NT within only 5 months of first product launch (Workstation 400) `[ps=0]`
- #1 NT workstation market by edging out Compaq; VISUALIZE rendering on Kayak differentiator `[ps=0]`
- Overall technical workstation market leader 1997 (combined Unix+NT units)
- #4 NT; Alpha maintained 10-50% performance lead over Intel for floating-point NT workstations
- Major new market emerging; Unix has early edge on statistical visualization; NT has long-term advantage via desktop app breadth
- Platform performance; applications capture (qualified ISVs); service and support; connectivity
