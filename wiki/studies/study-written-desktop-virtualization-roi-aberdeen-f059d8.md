## Top observations

- Twenty-three percent (23%) of small companies and 14% of mid-sized organizations report that they have no plans to deploy desktop virtualization at all. `[ps=3]`
- Likewise, 80% of large companies have already deployed desktop virtualization in at least some part of their company. `[ps=2]`
- Thirty-one percent (31%) of small companies report that they will begin a desktop virtualization program this year with 24% of mid-sized organizations reporting they will soon begin deployment of a solution already in the planning phase. `[ps=2]`
- They also report that when all current programs are complete they will have virtualized 61% of available desktops, or about twice as many as are virtualized today. `[ps=2]`
- A 12% savings is not very large in absolute terms but will become larger as more than the 31% of current desktops get virtualized. `[ps=2]`

- Ten percent (10%) of respondents reported that they have already virtualized over 90% of their desktops. `[ps=0]`
