## Top observations

- On any given day, more than 30% of U.S. knowledge workers are not in the home office and not behind the enterprise firewall’s security. `[ps=4]`
- Heavy data users are likely to spend more than $5,000 a year and can have a laptop TCO that is four to five times that of a home office desktop — at up to $8,000 a year per worker. `[ps=3]`
- They are displacing dial-up service with broadband Ethernet, but are paying for it through their expense reports at a rate of $10 per coffee or airport stop times several stops a day. `[ps=3]`
- As every stop can involve a different broadband service provider, and all-you-candrink service is $50 a month _per provider_ , it is hard to fault mobile knowledge workers who currently pay for service a la carte — that is, until the CFO spots the millions in hidden a la carte costs rolling up to the enterprise level. `[ps=3]`
- GoRemote’s communications cost-displacement formula for medium-sized and large enterprises is simple: home/remote broadband, dial-up, fixed broadband, and wireless for $1 per hour (typical users) or fixed-cost flat-rate plans starting at $60 per month (heavy users). `[ps=3]`
- Factor in the higher breakdown levels of laptops, do the multiplication, and it is not hard to see why outworkers — especially mobile ones — can have a laptop TCO that is four to five times that of an office desktop and up to $8,000 a year per worker. `[ps=2]`
- Yet all the overlapping services are still incomplete, as any laptop traveler with wireless has found — users have to pay by the $10 drink or else buy multiple $50 per month contracts to patch together something approaching national coverage. `[ps=2]`
