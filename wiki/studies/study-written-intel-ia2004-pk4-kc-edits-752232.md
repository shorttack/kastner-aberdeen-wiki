# Planning for Industry-Standard Computing Opportunities

> A 2003 Aberdeen Group executive white paper, produced in collaboration with Intel Corporation, on emerging industry-standard (Intel-architecture) server platforms. It discusses memory, systems management, and serial I/O advancements expected to reduce total cost of ownership, and reviews Intel's growing line of enterprise chipsets (E7501, E7505, E7205, E8870) supporting Xeon and Itanium 2 processors.
