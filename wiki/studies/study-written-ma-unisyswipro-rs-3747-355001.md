# Can Unisys-Wipro Alliance Cover All Outsourcing Bases?

> A December 18, 2006 Aberdeen Group market-event analysis of an alliance between Unisys Corp. and Wipro Technologies aimed at combining offshore cost savings with on-site customer care in IT outsourcing. Drawing on Aberdeen's 'Outsourcing Application Development and Maintenance' benchmark survey, it compares the top outsourcing challenges cited by Unisys versus Wipro customers, such as communication/cultural gaps and integration/testing effort.


_Published 2006, author **Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._
