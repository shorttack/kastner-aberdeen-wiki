# SARS May Impact Global Electronics Industry

> A March 28, 2003 Aberdeen Group 'Hot Topic' assessing how the SARS outbreak in China's Guangdong Province could disrupt global semiconductor and electronics manufacturing, given the region's dominant share of chip production and consumption. It documents quarantine and travel disruptions in Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Singapore affecting companies like Motorola, Sony, and Compal, and flags risk to firms such as Broadcom, Cirrus Logic, Conexant, and National Semiconductor that rely on regional applications-engineering support.


_Published 2003, author **Russ Craig / Aberdeen Group**, type **market-study**._


## Top observations

- Asia Pacific consumed 37% or $52B of 2002 semiconductor production. `[ps=0]`
