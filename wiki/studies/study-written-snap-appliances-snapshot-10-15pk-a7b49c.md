# Snap Appliance Inc.

> A 2003 Aberdeen Group vendor snapshot on Snap Appliance Inc., the leading provider of workgroup-to-enterprise network-attached storage (NAS), with an installed base of over 130,000 units. It covers the company's history (originally Meridian Data, acquired by Quantum in 2000, later spun out as a private company in 2002), its go-to-market channels, software partnerships, and leadership team.


_Published 2001, author **Aberdeen Group**, type **vendor-snapshot**._


## Top observations

- Every Snap Server 4500 can support up to two Snap Disk 10 expansion arrays for easy to manage, best-in-class storage expansion of up to 3TB of raw storage capacity. `[ps=2]`
