# Snap Appliance Inc.

> A 2003 Aberdeen Group vendor snapshot (templated format) on Snap Appliance Inc., a leading provider of network-attached storage (NAS) with more than 130,000 units installed. It recounts the company's origin as Meridian Data (founded 1994), its acquisition by Quantum in 1999, and its 2002 rebirth as a private company, along with its ATA-disk-based midline storage positioning and leadership team.
