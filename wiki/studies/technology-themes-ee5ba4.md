## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — Server price deflation rate: ~30% per year
- **2003** — Software licensing risk from lower processor counts: Significant; fewer processors per server reducing per-processor license revenue
- **2003** — Storage budget trajectory: Skyrocketing due to compliance, replication, PC data proliferation
- **2003** — ILM concept adoption: Early/emerging; new thinking about tiered storage pools
- **2003** — Retail IT spending direction: Increasing in retail automation, CRM, analytics
- **2003** — IT-savvy US population: ~1/3 consider themselves IT-savvy; another 1/3 at least competent
- **2003** — Best-of-breed vs integrated suite: CIOs seeing integration costs favoring single-vendor suites
- **2003** — IT purchasing decision dynamics: Executive committee now controls budget; wants high-ROI projects implementable in weeks not months
- **2003** — IT over-buying problem scope: Large firms own dozens of redundant/overlapping software products; multi-million dollar unused purchases
- **2003** — Consumer-driven supply chain innovations: RFID tags, automated replenishment, enhanced parcel tracking driven by modern consumer
- **2003** — Consumer research transformation: Real-time click tracking replacing survey/Nielsen panel methods
- **2003** — Corporate laptop penetration: ~30% of enterprise computers are laptops; few takers for dramatic increase
- **2003** — WiFi enterprise adoption barriers: Security fears, high support cost, high initial cost cited by executives
- **2003** — Value chain management risk: Extended value chains vastly complicating enterprise management; partner autonomy conflicts
- **2003** — Sarbanes-Oxley compliance cost: Several hundred thousand to several million dollars per year per affected business
- **2003** — IT deflation impact on commodity services: Commodity prices and cradle-to-grave services incompatible business models
- **2003** — Consumer multi-channel purchase expectations: Consumers expect purchase via store, phone, web, PDA; mass customization on demand
- **2003** — WiFi hotspot profitability outlook: Uncertain; hotspot investments hard to profit without corporate laptop adoption growth
