# The Business Value in IT Outsourcing

> Aberdeen research brief announcing a benchmark study on IT outsourcing business value, to be published late June 2006. Identifies five key drivers for outsourcing (core competency focus, cost savings, resource constraints, time-to-market, culture wars); describes a maturity model for outsourcing outcomes; classifies enterprises into three tiers: misaligned (~30%), sub-optimal (50-67%), and optimal (5-10%) outsourcing engagements. Author is Peter Kastner, VP Enterprise Integration Research.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2006** — Primary IT outsourcing drivers (ranked): 1. Core competency focus; 2. Cost savings; 3. IT resource constraints; 4. Time-to-market; 5. Culture wars
- **2006** — Enterprises without serious make-or-buy outsourcing decision: Many enterprises have never done a serious make-or-buy outsourcing decision on any IT segment
- **2006** — Estimated misaligned outsourcing engagements: Up to 30% of IT outsourcing engagements
- **2006** — Estimated sub-optimal outsourcing contracts: 50-67% of IT outsourcing contracts running at sub-optimal levels
- **2006** — Estimated optimal outsourcing engagements: 5-10% of multi-million dollar IT outsourcing agreements driving all anticipated business value
- **2006** — Best-in-class ROI measurement frequency (IT maturity): 56% measure ROI after every application change
- **2006** — Average companies ROI measurement frequency: 25% measure after each upgrade; 25% sometimes; 50% never
- **2006** — Laggard companies ROI measurement frequency: 0% after each upgrade; 10% sometimes; 20%+ never
- **2006** — Aberdeen IT survey scope: Over 2,000 companies surveyed on IT in past two years
- **2006** — SLA misalignment root causes: Poor scope understanding; inaccurate internal metrics; business changes over time away from contract terms
- **2006** — Universal outsourcing lesson: Cannot name a single company that outsourced IT without learning painful lessons
- **2006** — IT outsourcing critical success dimensions: SLAs, Governance, Preparation, Transition, Operations, Change Management, Pricing, Conflict Resolution, Metrics
- **2006** — Common IT outsourcing buyer complaint types: Misjudging ability to manage outsourcer; poor SLA metrics; confusing low cost with value; inflexible contract language
- **2006** — IT outsourcing benchmark scope: Global 5000 companies across all industries, all sizes
- **2006** — IT outsourcing sub-optimal rate prediction: 50-67% of contracts sub-optimal; only 5-10% fully optimal

- **2006** — Business process outsourcing scope: Ranges from simple app outsourcing (e-mail) to full BPO of major processes (general ledger, accounts payable)
