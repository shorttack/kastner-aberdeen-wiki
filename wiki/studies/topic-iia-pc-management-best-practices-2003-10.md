# PC Management Best Practices: A Study of the Total Cost of Operation, Risk, Security, and Audit

> IIA Research Foundation rough-draft report (Oct 7, 2003) authored by Mark Salamasick (University of Texas at Dallas) and Charles Le Grand (IIA AVP Technology Practices). Based on 221 GAIN flash-survey responses plus the July 2003 IIA Technology Audit Forum where Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group was keynote speaker. Organized around the COSO Internal Control Integrated Framework and SAC model; covers business risk and insurance, Sarbanes-Oxley/HIPAA/GLBA compliance, information security and appropriate use, business continuity, software management, and PC TCO. Cites Kastner's research extensively on PC fleet TCO ($8k-$9k per seat), 20-25% cost reduction through best practices, 70-80% of TCO in operations, 40 million desktops running outdated OSes, and standardized images.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — survey-n: 221 responses as of 2003-07-16
- **2003** — forum-date: 2003-07-24 through 2003-07-25
- **2003** — tco-per-seat-usd: 8000-9000 TCO per system typical enterprise
- **2003** — tco-reduction-pct: 20-25% cost reduction from PC best practices
- **2003** — tco-operational-share: 70-80% of PC TCO in operations; only 20-30% in acquisition/deployment
- **2003** — cost-per-user-savings: $4000 per user annual savings (~50% reduction) from automated PC mgmt tools
- **2003** — outdated-os-installed-base: 40 million+ desktops running outdated operating systems
- **2003** — eol-year: Microsoft ending Windows 98 support in 2003
- **2003** — eol-year: Microsoft ending Windows NT 4.0 support in 2003
- **2003** — security-policy-coverage: 93% respondents have overall security policy for PCs
- **2003** — tight-control-rate: only 33% considered policies/practices tightly controlled
- **2003** — virus-best-practice-rate: less than 30% believed organization employed virus-protection best practices
- **2003** — wireless-cost-analysis-rate: only 21% had performed wireless cost analysis
- **2003** — wireless-policy-rate: only 30% addressed wireless in PC policy/procedures
- **2003** — auto-patch-rate: only 58% automatically deliver software patches
- **2003** — ad-hoc-patch-rate: 85% deliver patches as needed rather than on fixed schedule
- **2003** — risk-assessment-pc-coverage: over 80% include PC in overall enterprise risk assessment
- **2003** — pc-risk-differentiation: approximately 50% differentiate PC risk by applications/processes
- **2003** — refresh-cycle: more than 50% replace PCs 'as needed' or on 4+ year cycle
- **2003** — os-upgrade-criteria: 32% replace OS 'as needed'; 54% have no OS upgrade criteria
- **2003** — backup-central-rate: 40% back up PC data in central location
- **2003** — full-image-backup-rate: 47% have full image backed up for PC recovery
- **2003** — standards-enforcement-rate: 92% have controls to enforce corporate standards; only 8% tightly enforced
- **2003** — incident-count-h1: 76,404 security incidents reported first half 2003 (vs 82,094 total 2002)
- **2002** — incident-yoy-growth: 56% increase 2001 to 2002
