# Here Come the Hot Boxes: Unix/RDBMS TPC-A Performance Analysis (1992)

> A brief 1992 Aberdeen Group section analyzing the emergence of Unix/RDBMS servers as leaders in TPC-A transaction processing price-performance benchmarks. Bull's DPX/2 and Sun's Sparcserver took TPC-A leadership positions pushing best price-performance below $10K/TPS-A, and Aberdeen explains why Unix/RDBMS vendors are increasingly favoring TPC-A over TPC-B for competitive benchmarking.

**Author:** Peter S. Kastner / Aberdeen Group · **Date:** 1992 · **Type:** market-study
**Importance:** medium — *Historically significant early Aberdeen analysis documenting the Unix/RDBMS competitive emergence in TPC benchmarking; records the transition of market leadership in 1992 transaction processing benchmarks.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Aberdeen's prediction that Unix/RDBMS vendors would increasingly use TPC-A was accurate; TPC-A became the dominant commercial benchmark until succeeded by TPC-C in the mid-1990s.*
