# Microsoft Spends a Bunch on Patents — But Is It Worth It?

> USA Today's Kevin Maney calculates Microsoft spent ~$9.5M per patent in 2003 — far more than IBM ($2.5M), HP, or Sun. Maney questions whether $5B+ annual R&D yields breakthrough products. Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group defends Microsoft Research as underappreciated. The piece foregrounds the disconnect between research output and commercial mega-hits beyond Windows and Office.

**Author:** Kevin Maney · **Date:** 2004-04-21 · **Type:** news-column
**Importance:** medium — *Surfaces the early-2000s critique that Microsoft's R&D dollars under-converted to new platform hits — a thesis that aged well as Xbox, MSN, and Tablet PC under-performed until the cloud/AI era.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Maney's skepticism about Microsoft converting R&D into mega-hits proved correct for the 2004-2014 decade; only Azure (2010) and later AI/Copilot reversed the pattern.*
