# Small Is Huge in PCs These Days

> Reuters reports the rise of small-form-factor PCs designed for the living room. Gartner forecasts shipment volume rising from 1.2M units (2003) to 32.1M (2008). Peter Kastner of Aberdeen Group describes 'the next form factor battles being fought in the living room' as size and decor begin to matter. Gateway's 901 Family Room Media Center exemplified the trend at $1,000-2,000.

**Author:** Kenneth Li (Reuters) · **Date:** 2004-04-07 · **Type:** news-article
**Importance:** medium — *Captures the early Media-Center-PC/HTPC era that prefigured streaming-device displacement of the PC-as-living-room hub.*
**Prescience:** medium — *Kastner's living-room form-factor framing was correct, but the winning hardware was not the small PC — it was the streaming stick/console. Gartner's 32.1M forecast was broadly hit but small-form PCs cannibalized desktops rather than created new TAM.*
