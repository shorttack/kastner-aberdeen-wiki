# Dell's Warranty Policies

> Warranty Week analyzes Dell's warranty policies. Peter S. Kastner, executive vice president and chief research officer for the Consumer Digital Technology Practice at Aberdeen Group, recommends enterprise PC buyers seek minimum three-year basic warranties with next-business-day on-site repair. Aberdeen's PC reference model specifies a 2.4 GHz Pentium 4, 256 MB RAM, 40 GB drive. Dell's Dimension/Inspiron consumer lines were cut to one-year coverage; OptiPlex/Latitude business lines retain three-year terms — a $129-$199 differential.

## Key observations (top 25)

- **2003** — Three-year NBD warranty minimum: Enterprise PC buyers should look for a minimum three-year basic warranty with provision for next-business-day on-site repairs
- **2003** — Vendor-leveling warranty methodology: We price all vendors with three-year, next business day service as a means of leveling the configuration playing field
- **2003** — Aberdeen reference PC config: 2.4 GHz Pentium 4, 256 MB RAM, 40 GB HDD, CD-ROM, 15- or 17-inch monitor, Ethernet
- **2003** — OptiPlex GX260 reference fit: Dell OptiPlex GX260 fits Aberdeen reference model precisely
- **2003** — Dimension warranty cut to 1-year: Dell cut Dimension and Inspiron consumer line warranties to one year (from prior three-year baseline)
- **2003** — Dimension extended-warranty pricing: $129 for three-year extended warranty on Dimension desktops; $199 on Inspiron laptops
- **2003** — Latitude/OptiPlex warranty retention: OptiPlex desktop and Latitude notebook lines retain three-year warranties with NBD on-site service
- **2003** — Dell FY2003 revenue: Revenue climbed 13.6% to $35.4 billion in fiscal year ended January 2003
- **2003** — Dell FY2003 EPS: EPS increased from $0.46 to $0.80
- **2003** — Dell gross margin trend: Gross margin rose from 17.7% (FY02) to 17.9% (FY03)
- **2008** — Three-year NBD warranty norm endurance: Three-year-NBD warranty becomes enterprise-procurement default
