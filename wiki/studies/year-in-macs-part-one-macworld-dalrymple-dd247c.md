# 2004 In Review: Apple — Part One (Year of the iPod, No 3-Peat on G5)

> Macworld year-in-review recapping Apple's 2004: iPod division split from Mac, 2 million iPods sold in Q4 FY04 contributing to $276M annual profit, Power Mac G5 maxed at 2.5GHz (no 3GHz despite Jobs' mid-2003 promise), PowerBook lines upgraded to 1.5GHz with standard AirPort Extreme and Bluetooth. Peter Kastner (Aberdeen) explains the G5 90nm-transition wall as industry-wide, commending IBM's execution.

**Author:** Jim Dalrymple · **Date:** 2004-12-30 · **Type:** news-article
**Importance:** medium — *Contemporaneous mainstream-press retrospective capturing Apple's iPod-driven financial inflection and the PowerPC 90nm thermal wall that drove the 2005 Intel transition decision.*
**Prescience:** high — *Kastner's framing of the 90nm wall as industry-wide (not IBM-specific) correctly anticipated that Apple's roadmap problem was unsolvable within PowerPC — validated six months later when Jobs announced the Intel transition at WWDC 2005.*
