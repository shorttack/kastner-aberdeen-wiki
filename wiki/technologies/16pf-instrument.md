# 16 Personality Factors (16PF) Instrument

> Psychometric assessment tool developed by Raymond Cattell; used to generate this report


## Top observations

- Rather extraverted; tends to be sociable and good at making and maintaining contacts — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Well adjusted individual; finds life generally satisfying; achieves things considered most important — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Keeps feelings under control when coming to balanced judgement; neither fiercely independent nor strongly conformist — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Fairly good natured and inclined to be cooperative — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Prefers to be direct, open and forthright; more likely to be trusted than less outspoken people; may not always be acceptable to others unless suitably tactful — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Gets warmly involved with working groups; natural and spontaneous; takes life as it comes — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- May not be very good at coolly and objectively analysing people's motives; being natural and spontaneous limits objective people-analysis — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Dominant individual; apt to be stern and authoritarian; assertive and independent-minded when opposed — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Rather talkative and lively; tends to be an active communicator; somewhat adventurous and sociable — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Prefers to work and make decisions with other people; seeks social approval and group support; sometimes at cost of conforming with others' wishes — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Likes to take people at face value; not suspicious but not over-trusting — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Resilient person; possesses mature, unanxious confidence in ability to deal with any kind of situation — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Gives impression of being relaxed, composed and generally satisfied; may seem laid-back and lacking in motivation to others — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Extremely relaxed style could lead to low performance unless kept under pressure to achieve results — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Emotionally mature; copes well with frustrating circumstances; adjusts to realities of a situation; does not allow feelings to interfere with seeing things as they are — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Tends to lack will-power and self-control — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Attends to most moral standards and rules but prepared to ignore a regulation when it no longer seems to serve its purpose — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Happier thinking about practical and concrete matters rather than abstract problems — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Fascinated by ideas, theories and beliefs; rather interested in new ideas and changes; occasionally inclined to experiment — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
- Often absorbed in work; tends to be unconventional; generally unfussy; likes to manage own affairs if possible — [[study-psk-personality-profile-2000-8be0a6]]
