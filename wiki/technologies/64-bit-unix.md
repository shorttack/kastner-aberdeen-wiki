# 64-bit Unix Operating Environment

> HP promised 64-bit systems in Q2 1996; IBM not until 1998; all major suppliers except IBM expected to move to 64-bit in 1996; NT not expected to follow for at least 4 more years

- Start or continue 64-bit Unix transition — [[study-aberdeen-1996-risc-unix-market]]
- DEC Alpha was already 64-bit (since 1992). HP shipped PA-8000 64-bit processors in 1996 for HP-UX. Sun shipped UltraSPARC-II with Solaris 7 64-bit in 1998. IBM AIX 4.2 (1996) added 64-bit addressing. All major RISC/Unix vendors (except IBM being already in AS/400 64-bit path) transitioned to 64-bit within 12-18 months as predicted. — [[study-aberdeen-1996-risc-unix-market]]
