# Agile/Extreme Programming

> Kastner notes agile modestly improved IT project success rates; Standish CHAOS Report findings confirmed partial improvement.


## Top observations

- Agile and Extreme Programming promote development reengineering rather than just control — [[study-2003-aberdeen-group-free-research-aberdeen-insights-1-968e18]]
- Roughly 30% succeed as defined; 50% succeed partially; 20% fail completely. Rates have improved modestly with agile, cloud, SaaS but persist over 30 years. — [[study-volume-1-ch09-expert-witness-technology-on-trial-1992-2015]]
