# AMD Athlon 64 (desktop, ClawHammer)

> AMD first desktop 64-bit CPU (Sep 2003, K8 microarchitecture); brought AMD64 ISA to consumer desktops ahead of Intel EM64T


## Top observations

- The Aug 2003 PC Deals commentary kept recommending Athlon XP as the value play through August 2003 without anticipating the 64-bit transition. My current view: AMD was way early on 64-bit — OS support was years away. It was not a compelling buying factor at time of purchase and maybe even at end of product life for an August 2003 purchase. — [[study-dct-memoir-athlon-64-prescience-2003]]
- AMD Athlon 64 desktop processor (ClawHammer) launched September 23, 2003 — [[study-dct-memoir-athlon-64-prescience-2003]]
- For an August 2003 consumer buyer: no shipping 64-bit Windows; no mainstream 64-bit consumer applications; no memory configurations exceeding the 32-bit 4GB limit at consumer price points; typical desktop lifecycle 3-4 years (replacement 2006-2007) — 64-bit was not a compelling purchase factor at time of purchase — [[study-dct-memoir-athlon-64-prescience-2003]]
- By 2006-2007 (end of typical Aug 2003 PC lifecycle): Vista x64 just launched with weak driver support; most consumer software still 32-bit; 2-4 GB RAM the common consumer ceiling — 64-bit still not a compelling factor at end of life for the Aug 2003 buyer — [[study-dct-memoir-athlon-64-prescience-2003]]
- AMD is selling a lot of its Athlon 64 chips because they are good 32-bit performers. AMD customers are more concerned about the late delivery of Unreal Tournament 2004 than they are waiting like Godot for Windows XP 64. — [[study-technology-news-future-tech-microsoft-la-625f49]]
- It's not so much 64-bit as it is AMD's next-generation chip — which is better, faster and more efficient. — [[study-technology-news-future-tech-microsoft-la-625f49]]
- Apple's G5 might in fact have been the fastest PC on the day it was made available; AMD's Athlon is a very competitive chip priced at a heady level for AMD. — [[study-technology-news-hardware-amd-beats-apple-5399ec]]
- PC World tests: Athlon 64 beat Apple Power Mac G5 and Intel Pentium 4 on Word, Premiere 6, Quake III frames-per-second, and (Opteron dual) Photoshop 7.0.1. — [[study-technology-news-hardware-amd-beats-apple-5399ec]]
- The Athlon 64 has managed increased market traction because it is a lot of performance at a good value. — [[study-technology-news-hardware-amd-makes-athlo-214a36]]
- AMD priced Mobile Athlon 64 2800+ at US$241 and 2700+ at $209 in 1,000-unit quantities, immediately available worldwide. Acer Ferrari and Chinese OEM Amoi Electronics named as first notebook launch partners. — [[study-technology-news-hardware-amd-makes-athlo-214a36]]
- AMD's Mobile Athlon 64, which is aimed at the same notebook market, is another respin of AMD's long-term effort to increase laptop market share. — [[study-technology-news-hardware-intel-unveils-l-1f420a]]
- AMD Mobile Athlon 64: industry's only Windows-compatible 64-bit mobile processor for mainstream notebooks; 60+ manufacturers and system builders supporting, including HP, eMachines, Fujitsu. — [[study-technology-news-hardware-intel-unveils-l-1f420a]]
