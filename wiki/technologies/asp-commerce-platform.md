# ASP E-Commerce Platform

> Application Service Provider commerce platform; precursor to SaaS e-commerce


## Top observations

- Market wants rapid time-to-market, low investment 'commerce platform'; ASP model preferred for low capital expenditure and scalability — [[study-2001-fletcher-spaght-3bb872]]
- Customers want ASPs with established hosting track record; performance risks drive preference for proven vendors; Digital River had 700+ stores as proof — [[study-2001-fletcher-spaght-3bb872]]
- Digital River; Escalate; OrderFusion; CommerceOne; multi-point solution providers with order management and fulfillment management — [[study-2001-fletcher-spaght-3bb872]]
- Doug Witt FedEx: 'I very much like that Omnigy is offered on an ASP basis… I like ASP because of the low up front cost and the fact that it is a scalable system' — [[study-2001-fletcher-spaght-3bb872]]
- Chris Fletcher, Aberdeen Group: 'If Omnigy is saying that a single customer record is added value, that is exaggerated. Many other companies have this [capability].' — [[study-2001-fletcher-spaght-3bb872]]
- Market wants broad functionality especially in back-end; not necessarily tied to full-service providers; front-end (CRM, shopping cart) complex piece — [[study-2001-fletcher-spaght-3bb872]]
