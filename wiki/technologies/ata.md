# ATA (Advanced Technology Attachment)

> The technology base for mid-line storage; used in PCs for years; reliability risen enough for low duty-cycle enterprise use


## Top observations

- ATA disk up to 300 GB per disk vs 142/72/36 GB per FC/SCSI disk — 2x to 8x capacity advantage `[ps=5]` — [[study-04-mid-line-storage-v2-whitepaper-7db9bc]]
- ATA disks cost approximately half the price per gigabyte compared to FC/SCSI disks `[ps=5]` — [[study-04-mid-line-storage-v2-whitepaper-7db9bc]]
- Mid-line storage is a 'quiet' revolution — enterprises still learning best use of the new tier but widespread adoption predicted `[ps=5]` — [[study-04-mid-line-storage-v2-whitepaper-7db9bc]]
- Mid-line storage emerging in spite of storage buyer inertia with support of major disk drive manufacturers storage systems suppliers and early adopters `[ps=4]` — [[study-04-mid-line-storage-v2-whitepaper-7db9bc]]
