# Performance Benchmarking Methodology

> Aberdeen and TPC benchmarking frameworks used to evaluate technology performance in litigation; Kastner’s TPC credentials recognized by courts.


## Top observations

- Kastner was a founding member of the Transaction Processing Performance Council (TPC) — [[study-volume-1-about-the-author]]
- TPC brought audited transparency to database performance claims and established standards still in use today — [[study-volume-1-about-the-author]]
- Aberdeen's benchmark research methodology, TCO modeling frameworks, and Best Practices databases defined the gold standard for enterprise technology research through the 1990s and early 2000s — [[study-volume-1-about-the-author]]
- Aberdeen’s performance benchmarking methodology was a core tool applied in litigation to evaluate whether technology performed to contracted specifications. — [[study-volume-1-ch09-expert-witness-technology-on-trial-1992-2015]]
