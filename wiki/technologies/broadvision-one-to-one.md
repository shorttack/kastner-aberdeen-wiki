# BroadVision One-to-One Application System

> CORBA-based, object-oriented high-end dynamic web application platform; supported personalized content, CRM, knowledge management, e-commerce. Still exists as BroadVision Commerce Agility Suite in evolved form.


## Top observations

- BroadVision stock peaked at $26B market cap in 2000; crashed in dot-com bust; nearly delisted; Salesforce.com (founded 1999) captured CRM market; BroadVision became marginal player; acquired by ESW Capital (private equity) May 2020 `[ps=5]` — [[study-1997-broadvision-dynamic-web-application-5f10bb]]
