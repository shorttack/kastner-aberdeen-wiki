# Customer Interaction Software (CIS)

> Aberdeen's umbrella term for front-office business applications; the category was subsequently renamed CRM (Customer Relationship Management) by Siebel, Gartner, and the analyst community; CIS as a term is now obsolete though the underlying capabilities persist in modern CRM platforms.


## Top observations

- Reactive customer service; handling complaints, returns, inquiries; key CIS function — [[study-1997-cis-report-ba825e]]
- Lead tracking, opportunity management, account management, forecasting; most mature CIS category — [[study-1997-cis-report-ba825e]]
- Outbound calling, campaign management, lead qualification via telephone channels — [[study-1997-cis-report-ba825e]]
- Complex product quoting and configuration; enables accurate pricing for multi-option products — [[study-1997-cis-report-ba825e]]
- IT service desk for employee support; tracking incidents and resolutions — [[study-1997-cis-report-ba825e]]
- Multi-hundred million dollar market; report priced at $895 for 246-page analysis — [[study-1997-cis-report-ba825e]]
- Over 60 CIS software suppliers profiled; 7 systems integrators; 3 systems suppliers — [[study-1997-cis-report-ba825e]]
- CIS clearly demonstrated ability to raise revenues; user organizations using CIS pulling ahead of rivals in customer satisfaction, retention, new business development, and profitability — [[study-1997-cis-report-ba825e]]
