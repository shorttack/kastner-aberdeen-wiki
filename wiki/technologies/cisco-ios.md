# Cisco IOS (Internetwork Operating System)

> The de facto standard internetworking operating system; described by Aberdeen as 'unifying thread' providing cohesion to disparate networks; four cornerstones: scalability, WAN optimization, management/security, reliable adaptive routing; IOS remained dominant through 2000s; evolved into IOS XE, IOS XR for modern platforms; still the basis for Cisco's networking OS.


## Top observations

- Cisco IOS described as de facto standard in internetworking; unifying thread across disparate networks; embedded in hardware, server software, client OS — [[study-1997-cisco-s-core-products-business-inte-bd8697]]
- Cisco IOS remained the de facto internetworking standard through the 2000s; evolved into IOS XE and IOS XR for modern routing platforms; still the basis of Cisco's networking OS as of 2026 — [[study-1997-cisco-s-core-products-business-inte-bd8697]]
