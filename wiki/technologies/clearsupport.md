# ClearSupport

> External customer service/support; call/problem tracking, workflow, Diagnosis Engine, service contract verification


## Top observations

- CIS industry experiencing growth nearing 50% per year; leading firms exceeding this — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Revenues increasing over 100% on annualized basis; 1H 1996 revenues exceeded $20.1M; Aberdeen expects more than double in 2H 1996 — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Aberdeen research indicates Clarify has achieved 99% uptime with its customer base — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Over 200 customers; 43,000 individuals using Clarify solutions — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- 20% of Clarify's revenues invested in R&D — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Response time paramount; rapid deployment; global scale for thousands of users — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Must adapt to company's unique policies/processes; end-user modifiable without IS staff; upgrades preserve customizations — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Supplier must understand best practices; each application must stand on its own right — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Three-level integration: (1) cross-CIS app data sharing, (2) enterprise back-office (SAP, Oracle), (3) cross-organizational boundaries — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Remote/mobile access, Internet availability, security, data replication and synchronization for mobile users and customers — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Integrated best-of-breed CIS suite; refuse implementations unlikely to succeed; TAM assigned to every customer; linchpin account strategy in IT/telecom verticals — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Aberdeen expects Clarify will maintain its rapid growth, particularly in IT and telecommunications markets; 'Clarify is a CIS winner and will continue to be so in the future' — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Clarify acquired by Nortel Networks in 1999 for $2.1B in stock; validating Aberdeen's 'CIS winner' assessment — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Nortel sold Clarify to Amdocs for $200M in 2001 — a fraction of the $2.1B paid; CRM market shifted toward Siebel and Salesforce — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Market quickly consolidating from hundreds of software suppliers to a few leaders with resources for Fortune 1000 requirements — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- CIS/CRM market consolidated rapidly: Siebel Systems dominated by 2000, followed by SAP CRM, Oracle Siebel, and eventually Salesforce.com (2000 IPO) — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Named linchpin accounts: Microsoft, Cisco, HP (IT), MCI, Sprint (telecom), Bay Networks (networking); these customers' partners naturally adopt Clarify — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
- Aberdeen believes Clarify's vision of connecting helpdesks across company boundaries is on-track with market realities; endorsed via CSC and DMTF participation — [[study-aberdeen-1996-clarify-inc]]
