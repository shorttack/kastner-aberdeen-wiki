# Commerce eXchange 2.0

> Enterprise internet commerce platform; components: transaction engine (Financial eXchange), application portfolio (Catalog, Digital Delivery, Chat, Note), Oasis distributed architecture, Workplace management tools; EDI integration driver; CORBA and DCOM neutral via abstraction layer; clients included Broderbund, MicroWarehouse, Multiple Zones, Comdisco, Scholastic.


## Top observations

- Major commercial site deployable in weeks to months vs. in-house projects spanning a year or more `[ps=5]` — [[study-1997-commerce-exchange-pvp-250db8]]
- Aberdeen strongly endorses Commerce eXchange; recommends it for corporations and entrepreneurs opening internet sales channels; positions it as having production-quality credentials with multiple successful deployments `[ps=2]` — [[study-1997-commerce-exchange-pvp-250db8]]
- Sells 20,000+ computer products and distributes Microsoft software titles using Commerce eXchange `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-commerce-exchange-pvp-250db8]]
- Comprehensive enterprise internet retail solution with no technology strings attached; abstraction layer protects against single technology lock-in; supports CORBA, DCOM, multiple databases, EDI — [[study-1997-commerce-exchange-pvp-250db8]]
- InterWorld Technology Ventures went public then pivoted to B2B; merged with DynamicWeb Enterprises (April 2000) creating eB2B Commerce, Inc.; eB2B filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy (October 2004); Commerce eXchange platform dissolved — [[study-1997-commerce-exchange-pvp-250db8]]
