# Commercial Messaging

> Aberdeen Group term for store-and-forward middleware category; market below $100M in 1995; foundational to modern message-oriented middleware


## Top observations

- <$100 million — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- underutilized; IS not yet taking effective advantage — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- growing mass of networking-limited legacy applications; costly retrofit or early rearchitecting required — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- no one-stop shop; market not yet developed for full bundled solution from single supplier — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- openness; broad scope of applicability; customizability; ability to evolve as market matures — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- multithreading; multiple queues; effective caching and memory management; pointer-passing vs. copying; administrative reconfiguration — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- APIs for wide range of tasks; coding and testing support; design tools for Queue Manager placement on enterprise internetwork — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- potentially on verge of major increase in strategic importance to enterprise IS — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- users will eventually see Commercial Messaging as applicable to most or all legacy and new applications — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- staged: (1) skeleton on directory nodes, (2) 2/3/4-tier LAN deployment, (3) WAN extension for targets of opportunity — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- Commercial Messaging + enterprise-wide directory/name service + enterprise-wide systems management — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- verified; enterprise integration and API modernization debt became multi-billion dollar challenge — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- Aberdeen predicts that companies not adopting Commercial Messaging will accumulate a growing mass of legacy networking-limited applications requiring costly retrofits — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
- Confirmed: enterprises that delayed messaging adoption faced costly SOA/ESB modernization projects in 2000s; message broker architectures became multi-billion dollar market (IBM WebSphere MQ, TIBCO, webMethods) — [[study-aberdeen-1995-commercial-messaging]]
