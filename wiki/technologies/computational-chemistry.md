# Computational Chemistry

> Umbrella domain: molecular modeling, quantum chemistry, polymer simulation, drug design.


## Top observations

- Computational chemistry payoff in chemical industry measured in hundreds of millions to billions of dollars if discovery time reduced even modestly `[ps=5]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Some CAD/CAM-like packages aimed at less-than-PhD practitioners will be on the market in 1990-91 `[ps=5]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- ab initio, semiempirical, molecular mechanics - 10,000-to-1 computational intensity range `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Casale calls IBM a real sleeper gearing up for a lengthy assault on technical markets including computational chemistry `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- From 1983 to 1987 Digital Equipment overwhelmingly dominated; VAXes in 80 percent of some 300 molecular modeling installations `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Drug design has been principal focus of all computational chemistry companies; projected payoff period typically a decade `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Successful future software differentiation must include proprietary chemistry or data, quantifiable leadtime advantage, or superior support; vendors relying on hardware markups can expect a profit squeeze `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Aberdeen believes the correct outlook is much closer to the finite element analysis model (double-digit millions revenue, protracted losses then high profits, slow scale-up, high tech hurdles) than the CAD/CAM model (single-digit billions, faster profits, many suppliers) `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- System integrators similar to those in FEA and CAD/CAM will mechanize routine design problems, increasing user count by at least 5x `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Computational chemistry users have little allegiance to hardware suppliers that have failed to keep pace - far more so than commercial markets `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Hardware war fought along three lines: (1) Point solutions vs systems; (2) Topology and integration; (3) Decision and control (senior mgmt/MIS vs technical scientists) `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Computer industry will continue its 40-year pattern of computing momentum shifting noisily among shared large systems, single-user smaller systems, and departmental systems `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Hardware drives a two-step cyclic computational chemistry process: new hardware permits software application advances, which generate demand for more powerful hardware `[ps=4]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Revenue mix among local-compute servers, central-site computers, and workstations to vary substantially year-to-year; each segment capturing 15-40 percent of overall hardware sales in any given year `[ps=3]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- 35-50 year-old Ph.D. chemist; organic or physical chemistry background; FORTRAN plus statistics plus quantum mechanics skill set `[ps=3]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Distinct risk that IBM will not stay the course because of inability to keep up competitively with technical product offerings - shorter life cycles and corporate nimbleness usually absent `[ps=3]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Computational chemistry quantum-effects work will extend semiconductor technology 10-15 years beyond current limits if successful `[ps=3]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Adhesion, optoelectronics, new alloys, films/coatings, fibers, high-T superconductors, cold fusion `[ps=3]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- Among directly competing suppliers, price/performance varies by as much as 3:1 (or more when total cost of ownership considered) `[ps=3]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
- After a period of respectable but unspectacular growth accompanied by hardware and software vendor shakeouts, field should become highly profitable for those with deep enough pockets to stay the course `[ps=3]` — [[study-conflicting-trends-computational-chemistry-fe5c31]]
