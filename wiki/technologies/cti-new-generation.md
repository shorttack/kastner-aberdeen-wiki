# New CTI (Computer Telephony Integration)

> Aberdeen coined 'new CTI'; category superseded by UCaaS, VoIP, and smartphones


## Top observations

- Aberdeen predicts broad and deep market for Telechips 'new CTI' technology across Global 1000 enterprises — [[study-aberdeen-1996-telechips-access-pc-telephony-interaction]]
- Telechips Corporation did not achieve mass market scale; company dissolved; CTI appliance category displaced by smartphones and UCaaS platforms — [[study-aberdeen-1996-telechips-access-pc-telephony-interaction]]
- Most computer suppliers treat telephony as just another application; telco suppliers cannot develop software in timely/cost-effective manner — [[study-aberdeen-1996-telechips-access-pc-telephony-interaction]]
