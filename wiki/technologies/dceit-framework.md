# Digital Consumer Electronic and Information Technology (DCEIT) Framework

> Aberdeen's six-category taxonomy for analyzing PC-CE convergence


## Top observations

- Two converging worlds (CE and PC) meet in a broad spectrum of possibilities; six player categories: broadband, point-product CE, game, PC/peripheral, display, software, new entrants; semiconductors as arms merchants `[ps=4]` — [[study-dct-dceit-practice-v6-2002]]
- Target is mass consumer market (not geek early adopters); no singular solution; information interface need not be PC; single system management/content catalog accessible remotely; multiple content/computing centers in home; consumer choice over redundancy; industry standards with acceptable proprietary integration; plug-and-play imperative; upgradeability imperative `[ps=4]` — [[study-dct-dceit-practice-v6-2002]]
- This v6 deck (Sep 2002) is the final iteration of a practice-overview presentation evolved through DCT-Board-Presentation (Jul 2002, board-level summary), DCT-practice-jul-26 (Jul 26 2002, full deck with market sizing: US CE market ~$94B 2001, forecast $118B 2005 @ 6% CAGR, home networking 58% CAGR, home gateways 73% CAGR), and DCEIT-Practice (Aug-Sep 2002, DCEIT rebrand).
