# DDR2 SDRAM (DDR2-400)

> JEDEC Double Data Rate 2 synchronous DRAM standard; 400-1066 MT/s; 1.8V; mainstream 2005-2008; replaced by DDR3 2007+


## Top observations

- DDR2-400 draws half the power of DDR400, 40% less than DDR333 — critical given 1U rack and blade form-factor trend. IT buyers can pick slow DDR266 or fast DDR2-400 at same power. — [[study-planningforemergingindustry-6-40decb]]
- Cost and density advantages of polymer memory still do not address the larger issues for electronic memory — heat and leakage — that will be tackled in new DDR2 memory due out next year (2004). — [[study-technology-news-future-tech-plastic-disc-4a4a71]]
- DDR2 SDRAM became mainstream in 2005-2006 desktop and server memory — exactly the year Kastner forecast — and dominated until DDR3 (2007+) and DDR4 (2014+). — [[study-technology-news-future-tech-plastic-disc-4a4a71]]
