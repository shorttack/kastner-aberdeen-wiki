# DDR2 SDRAM memory

> Enables >400 MHz DRAM operation; higher bandwidth, lower power than DDR1; not pin-compatible with DDR1. Platforms expected DDR2-based for 2-3 year cycle.


## Top observations

- Enables >400 MHz DRAM operation; >800 MHz front-side buses require faster memory; DDR1 will not scale. `[ps=5]` — [[study-dell-precision-workstations-3-581e89]]
- Platforms will be DDR2-based for the next 2-3 year cycle. `[ps=5]` — [[study-dell-precision-workstations-3-581e89]]
- DDR2 memory was introduced in server and workstation platforms in 2004-2005 with the Nocona/Lindenhurst platform. However, DDR2 did not achieve particularly long longevity as a platform; DDR3 began displacing it by 2007-2008. The platform served approximately 3-4 years in mainstream use. `[ps=4]` — [[study-dell-precision-workstations-3-581e89]]
