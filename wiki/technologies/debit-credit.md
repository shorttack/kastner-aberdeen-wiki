# DebitCredit Benchmark

> DEC's proprietary predecessor to TPC Benchmark A; used to validate analytical model; results published CMG 1989; model validated against multiple VAX configurations running DebitCredit


## Top observations

- "The best way to get valid performance measures is to use a widely recognized, easily understood, and easily duplicated benchmark in which time, cost, throughput, and recovery are clearly specified." — [[study-dectp-press-conference-transcript-and-benchmark-charts-plaza-5e5836]]
- "95% of these transactions must be completed in one second or less. Cost per transaction is calculated by a formula which divides the five-year cost of hardware, software, and maintenance exclusive of staff by the number of transactions per second." — [[study-dectp-press-conference-transcript-and-benchmark-charts-plaza-5e5836]]
- "IBM systems were benchmarked by Digital or by third parties, and Tandem's numbers are based on data reported by them from their own debit credit runs." — [[study-dectp-press-conference-transcript-and-benchmark-charts-plaza-5e5836]]
- "We dropped the response percentage from 95 to 90% and the response time from 1 to 1.3 seconds and relaxed some of the recovery requirements." — [[study-dectp-press-conference-transcript-and-benchmark-charts-plaza-5e5836]]
