# DEC Alpha/OSF1

> 5th DEC Unix attempt in 5 years; Aberdeen found ISVs still considered it beta; no TPC-A results available at June 1993; DEC itself told Aberdeen OpenVMS was primary OS


## Top observations

- Still beta — not ready for production; ISVs and early adopters confirmed; no TPC-A results available `[ps=2]` — [[study-1993-hercules-sap-28344a]]
- Production yields mostly 133-200 MHz; unable to produce 200 MHz in quantities; first-generation chip with no proven next-gen `[ps=-1]` — [[study-1993-hercules-sap-28344a]]
- 550 applications stated by ISVs as planned ports as of March 1993; multiuser support not a priority without demand — [[study-1993-hercules-sap-28344a]]
