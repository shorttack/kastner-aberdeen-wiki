# DECtp (Digital Transaction Processing)

> Umbrella brand for DEC's integrated TP hardware and software product suite; announced 1988; includes ACMS, DECintact, DECdtm, DECdta, Rdb/VMS, DECforms, VAXft; Kastner led marketing work for this announcement


## Summary




## Top observations

- Partially verified: DEC was a serious OLTP benchmark and architecture participant, but enterprise database/application economics consolidated around other vendors. `[ps=5]` — [[study-2026-kastner-dec-longitudinal-22d177]]
- DECtp was right that OLTP wouldn't stay confined to special-purpose machines and that benchmarks would matter, but DEC fought a platform war just as platform economics shifted beneath it. `[ps=5]` — [[study-2026-kastner-fault-tolerant-wars]]
- DEC's OLTP/TPC material shows a serious enterprise transaction-processing challenge rather than a purely engineering-centered minicomputer story. `[ps=4]` — [[study-2026-kastner-dec-longitudinal-22d177]]
- Many older TP systems centralized and tied to particular vendor; as expansion continues beyond geographic boundaries, centralized single-vendor TP systems less able to offer needed flexibility `[ps=4]` — [[study-dtj-v03-01-tp-and-fault-tolerant-1991-cf078c]]
- 64-bit field previously dominated by DEC with Sun as newer entrant `[ps=3]` — [[study-2026-kastner-dec-longitudinal-22d177]]
- Kastner recalls the Plaza Hotel launch, July 1988, was built around Debit-Credit benchmark evidence, because benchmark discipline was the only way to escape vendor specmanship. `[ps=3]` — [[study-2026-kastner-fault-tolerant-wars]]
- ACMS uses 4GL task definition language (divide and conquer); DECintact uses 3GL library calls; persistent difference will be different application programming interfaces `[ps=3]` — [[study-dtj-v03-01-tp-and-fault-tolerant-1991-cf078c]]
- Set of common features shared by both monitors growing with latest releases; external convergence fostered by internal convergence sharing underlying code; investment in either monitor protected `[ps=3]` — [[study-dtj-v03-01-tp-and-fault-tolerant-1991-cf078c]]
- DECtp was described internally as 'most important launch after VAX', reflecting significance of OLTP positioning. `[ps=3]` — [[study-volume-1-appendix-career-timeline]]
- DECtp campaign was a success: sales into OLTP accounts improved; TPC was founded; mainframe cost-performance advantage was documented, quantified, and published. `[ps=3]` — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
- Digital is a leader in transaction processing with enterprise-wide networking and VAXcluster family; DECtp announced as major product initiative `[ps=2]` — [[study-dec-mgmt-memo-v7n5-state-of-company-1988-6a9954]]
- "VAX 8830 for 1.8 million." Hughes: "price performance at half the cost of a comparable IBM system." `[ps=2]` — [[study-dectp-press-conference-transcript-and-benchmark-charts-plaza-5e5836]]
- Digital's strategy is to be a leader in transaction processing `[ps=2]` — [[study-dtj-v03-01-tp-and-fault-tolerant-1991-cf078c]]
