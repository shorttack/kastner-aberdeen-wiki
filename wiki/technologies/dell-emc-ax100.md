# Dell|EMC AX100

> Entry-level SATA SAN; up to 3 TB; wizard-based setup; dual controllers; mirrored cache; Brocade SAN switch optional. Validated midline concept at workgroup level.


## Top observations

- Up to 3 TB on SATA drives — [[study-2004-05dell-storage-profile-editpk-05170-3c00b1]]
- Dell conceived AX100 to meet customers' needs for easier-to-use Fibre Channel system at lower price than CX line — [[study-dell-storage-profile-2004]]
- AX100 scales up to 3 TB on SATA drives; wizard-based setup; dual controllers; mirrored cache — [[study-dell-storage-profile-2004]]
- Large companies with many remote sites and small organizations deterred by SAN complexity and cost; AX100 addresses this — [[study-dell-storage-profile-2004]]
- SATA-based entry-level SANs became standard at workgroup/branch level by 2008. AX100 concept validated and expanded by EMC's later CX4 and VNXe product lines. — [[study-dell-storage-profile-2004]]
