# e-Learning Platforms

> Core subject of study; now a multi-hundred-billion dollar global industry


## Top observations

- e-Learning enables personalized curricula vs generalized classroom training `[ps=5]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- e-Learning enables rapid deployment and continuous course updates vs slow classroom refresh `[ps=5]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Well over $10 billion worldwide (software + services + content) `[ps=4]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- More than 70% of corporations offer employees computer- or web-based training options `[ps=4]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Virtual learning delivery eliminates travel expenses and leaves students in place `[ps=4]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Modular e-learning provides only necessary information in smaller blocks vs just-in-case classroom `[ps=4]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Businesses running on Internet time require training delivery that matches speed of change `[ps=4]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- COVID-19 pandemic (2020) caused overnight shift of all enterprise training to virtual/online; e-learning became mandatory `[ps=4]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- e-Learning will have approximately one-third of total training market by 2002 `[ps=3]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- 60% of higher education institutions offer online learning courses `[ps=3]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Travel costs consume up to 40% of average corporate training budget `[ps=3]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Classroom training and TBT will reach approximately equal share of training opportunities by 2002 `[ps=2]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- e-Learning market grew far beyond $10B forecast; global corporate e-learning market exceeded $200B by 2019 `[ps=2]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Near zero in revenues `[ps=0]` — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Online learning penetration in corporate/government/higher education will reach ~90% within a few years — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
- Enterprises' competitive economic advantage is related to education and knowledge — [[study-1999-e-learning-in-the-enterprise-psk-d0fc8a]]
