# Enterprise Information Integration (EII)

> Leaves data sources in place; real-time ETL; cross-data-source querying without copying; creates global metadata repository; distinct from EAI and ETL; market <$200M in 2003


## Top observations

- Slight web site disadvantage can cause 10-20% of installed base to walk away even in early e-commerce era `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- For every $1 spent on EII software licenses, $1.25 to $1.50 spent on professional services for implementation `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- EII visibility grown dramatically over last year, especially due to IBM's announcement of Information Integrator products `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- EII typically lacks persistence technology; combination of EII and ODS is better than either separately for data-store size and performance; EII/mid-tier ODS enables 'have it your way' architecture `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- IT buyers should assess existing information aggregation tools, supplement where necessary, create long-range plan to use EII as ongoing effort to amass metadata about all enterprise data sources `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- Suppliers should implement full-fledged information aggregation tool set combining data migration, replication/ETL, data warehouse, ODS, and EII tools with guidance on when to use each `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- EII today is project-specific, seeking to simplify developer interfaces; portals, web services making cross-database queries as initial EII implementations `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- EII will form part of larger information aggregation market delivering tools for strategic information management over next 2 years `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- EII projected to grow to $250M+ market within 2 years if major hardware/database suppliers successfully enter market `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- EII to become key technology in $7.5 billion market as incorporated in portal, EAI, BI, and BPI solutions `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- Database administration costs now dominate TCO of applications below 500-user level and continue to increase in importance for all sizes `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- Business benefit of EII increases exponentially with successive integration of each related application and database `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- Distinct EII market exists, beginning 'high-end user per-project' stage; driven by need to rationalize multiple database access to cut admin costs and leverage cross-database proprietary information `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- Six enabling technologies for EII in 2003: application servers, Java, XML, database administration technologies, enterprise portals, and Web services `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- Partial deployment of intranet/extranet/internet applications without EII increases difficulties of implementing EII and increases costs of failing to implement EII `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- EII enables three high-priority e-business initiatives: CRM, e-business integration across supply chain, and web-based employee productivity applications `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- EII can serve as focal point for gathering unprecedented amount of metadata about enterprise proprietary information; potential for cross-data-source administration `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- Most M&A activity over next 2 years will center on larger infrastructure solution providers acquiring newcomer EII suppliers `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- IBM and BEA quickest to respond to EII opportunity; Oracle and Microsoft have not shown full appreciation of EII potential `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
- Progress Corp: ObjectStore XML database (mid-tier ODS), PeerDirect InnerEdge/OuterEdge replication (ETL/migration), SonicMQ XML messaging, OpenEdge embedded infrastructure — combination covers multiple EII-related categories `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-eii-2003-ea3f1a]]
