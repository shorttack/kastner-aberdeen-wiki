# Electronic Commerce (EDI-style Document transmission)

> Agreement Section 6: electronic transmission of legally binding purchase/sale Documents via digital signature or User ID.


## Top observations

- Section 6 authorizes legally binding electronic transmission of purchase/sale Documents between parties using digital signature or Buyer-specified User ID; documents deemed received upon arrival at the receiving party's mailbox/Internet address. — [[study-sun-master-contract-10-129daa]]
