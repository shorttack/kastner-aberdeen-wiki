# Enterprise Training and Change Management Platforms

> Aberdeen identified growing market for customized, on-demand training delivery beyond classroom instruction; foreshadowed LMS and e-learning explosion.


## Top observations

- Education (communication, change management, process training, skills training) consistently named as critical enabler for technology adoption `[ps=5]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- Educational investments cut because: difficult to communicate effect of smaller education budget on project outcome `[ps=5]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- Without training: best case = underutilization; worst case = outright rejection of new system `[ps=4]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- Education consistently short-shifted due to cost containment, competitive pressure, and day-to-day operating demands `[ps=4]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- Respondents demand training tailored to unique project requirements: 'I don't want a least-common-denominator one-size-fits-all approach' `[ps=4]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- Prefer local training provider but want consistent global/national/regional delivery capability `[ps=4]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- Decision-makers require trainers with both educational credentials AND implementation/operational expertise `[ps=3]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- HP commissioned Aberdeen interviews with IT executives undergoing major technology transitions, Sep 1996 `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- HP engaged Aberdeen to produce research validating HP's training and education services offerings for enterprise technology transitions `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- One-on-one interviews with key project leaders and decision-makers, September 1996; qualitative primary research `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
