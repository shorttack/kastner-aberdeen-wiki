> Core subject of study; mainframe paradox — adding MIPS but losing money; 7% installed base decline per year

- More MIPS shipped in 1992 than any prior year — but least profitable year — [[study-nti-9-mainframe-role-1993-77fc40]]
- Declining 7% per year as datacenters consolidate — [[study-nti-9-mainframe-role-1993-77fc40]]
- 30% vs 45% for PC-clone manufacturers — [[study-nti-9-mainframe-role-1993-77fc40]]
- IBM $30B; Microsoft $25B; Intel $25B; HP $20B — [[study-nti-9-mainframe-role-1993-77fc40]]
- 18-36 months for major new applications; 6-9 months for trivial report changes — [[study-nti-9-mainframe-role-1993-77fc40]]
- Approximately 50% of all capital spending in many enterprises — [[study-nti-9-mainframe-role-1993-77fc40]]

- Annual fees increasing with no corresponding productivity gain; installed base declining creates destructive cycle — [[study-nti-9-mainframe-role-1993-77fc40]]
- Computer Associates pricing models exemplify trend to harvest mainframe business — [[study-nti-9-mainframe-role-1993-77fc40]]
