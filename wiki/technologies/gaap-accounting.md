# Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP)

> Casale analyzes the limits of GAAP in detecting earnings quality issues; notes quarterly interim statements are 'neither audited nor carry the same weight as annual statements.' Quarterly statements technically 'interim statements' per GAAP.


## Top observations

- Auditors (CPA firm) are not paid to tell management how to smooth earnings; quarterly review less stringent than annual audit; stopped-clock shipment timing would require exhaustive investigation to detect. `[ps=5]` — [[study-casale-evp-4-9bd4e6]]
- Quarterly financial statements are technically 'interim statements': neither audited nor carry same weight as annual statements. Many annual expenses (benefits, annual report costs) occur in peaks but are spread over quarters per matching principle. `[ps=4]` — [[study-casale-evp-4-9bd4e6]]
