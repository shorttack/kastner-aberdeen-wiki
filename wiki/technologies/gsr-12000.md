# Cisco 12000 Series Gigabit Switch Router (GSR)

> Cisco's next-generation router designed for ISPs and internet backbone; first deployed 1997; multigigabit crossbar fabric sustaining 80 Gbps; interfaces OC-3/OC-12/OC-48, Gigabit Ethernet, Packet over SONET, ATM; Aberdeen described as 'appropriate and necessary'; sold 1,000 units by November 1998; became a major commercial success; still referenced in carrier networks.


## Top observations

- Multigigabit crossbar switching fabric sustaining 80 Gbps; high port density; interfaces OC-3, OC-12, OC-48; Gigabit Ethernet, Packet over SONET, ATM; fully distributed forwarding; topology-driven route-table switching — [[study-1997-cisco-s-core-products-business-inte-bd8697]]
- Aberdeen views GSR addition as both appropriate and necessary for ISPs and large enterprise; will complement the 7500 router addressing different needs and markets — [[study-1997-cisco-s-core-products-business-inte-bd8697]]
- Cisco sold 1,000 GSR 12000 units within first year (by November 1998); became major commercial success; backbone routers for major ISPs and carriers; still referenced in carrier networks — [[study-1997-cisco-s-core-products-business-inte-bd8697]]
