# Snap Appliance GuardianOS

> Linux-based unified NAS/iSCSI platform for Snap Servers; v3.1 added iSCSI/NDMP/server-to-server replication (Dec 2004)


## Top observations

- Together with GuardianOS v3.1, Snap has a solid software offering for its departmental and enterprise servers that can continue to scale as the company expands its hardware product line. — [[study-snap-appliance-launches-new-release-of-i-373ab7]]
- GuardianOS v3.1 delivers a 50%+ performance improvement for iSCSI over GuardianOS v3.0, bringing iSCSI throughput more in line with file-sharing NAS throughput on Snap Servers. — [[study-snap-appliance-launches-new-release-of-i-373ab7]]
- GuardianOS v3.1 powers all Snap Appliance departmental and enterprise Snap Servers ranging from 320 GB to 30 TB of storage capacity. — [[study-snap-appliance-launches-new-release-of-i-373ab7]]
