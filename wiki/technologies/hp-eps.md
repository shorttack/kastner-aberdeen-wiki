# HP Enterprise Parallel Server (EPS)

> EPS21 (K-Class SMP nodes) and EPS30 (T-Class SMP nodes) introduced May 15 1996; PA-RISC based; eventually superseded by Itanium and then x86


## Top observations

- HPP (Highly Parallel Processing): SMP nodes connected via fibre channel switch; evolutionary extension of SMP/clustering; gradual upgrade path for existing K/T-Class systems — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- 17,826 tpmC at $396/tpmC (48 processors, T-Class SMP nodes, Oracle 7.3); second highest published result as of July 1996 — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- H2 1998: support for 768 processors (64 SMP nodes x 12 processors); interconnect target 10 km by 3Q97 — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- Aberdeen predicts EPS will become datacenter system-of-choice for very large application requirements as HP builds aggressive EPS sales/marketing campaign — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- HP EPS/PA-RISC platform did not become dominant datacenter standard; HP shifted to Itanium (Integrity servers) around 2001-2002; x86/Linux ultimately dominated high-end commercial computing; HP-UX market share declined steadily — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- Aberdeen: most IS decision makers interviewed do not know EPS exists; HP needs more aggressive sales/marketing campaign urgently — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- EPS customers exceeded expectations for 'headroom' problems; smooth migration from HP SMP/cluster; workload balancing described as 'a real joy'; investment protection major business benefit — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- EPS inherent hardware redundancy + HP MC/ServiceGuard: users can achieve same or higher availability than state-of-art clustering while maintaining high performance — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- Each K-Class or T-Class SMP node: multi-processor, high-performance compute unit; T-Class supports up to 14-way SMP — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- Hub architecture allows direct node-to-node messaging without intermediate hops; key differentiator from pure MPP interconnects — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
- MC/System Environment tools: single-system view, single-point management, systems admin, config, performance monitoring, load balancing bundled with EPS clusters — [[study-aberdeen-1996-hp-enterprise-parallel-server-scalable-performance]]
