# HP OpenView Network Node Manager (NNM) 5.0

> HP NNM 5.0 for NT; earlier versions dated to early 1990s; version 6.4 discontinued December 2007; version 7.5x discontinued May 2010; succeeded by HP NNMi then OpenText NNM


## Top observations

- HP reached success then lost momentum; competitors filled vacuum; complacency and lack of investment in product R&D caused NNM to fall behind in user-friendliness / efficiency / breadth `[ps=4]` — [[study-powerful-network-management-4cb125]]
- HP leads network management market in both market share and mindshare; large installed base of hardware and software; worldwide presence; strong marketing `[ps=3]` — [[study-powerful-network-management-4cb125]]
- HP NNM requires full package licensed and deployed at each location to be monitored; processes all event data unfiltered; leads to wasted bandwidth and system delays `[ps=3]` — [[study-powerful-network-management-4cb125]]
- HP NNM web browser is static text-only HTML format; requires third-party supplier separately; text-based table reports time-consuming to decipher `[ps=3]` — [[study-powerful-network-management-4cb125]]
- HP discontinued NNM version 6.4/6.41 effective December 31 2007; final version was 7.5x discontinued May 2010; product line sold to MicroFocus then OpenText `[ps=3]` — [[study-powerful-network-management-4cb125]]
- Aberdeen predicts NNM destined to fall further behind as Tivoli moves forward unless HP redirects commitment to customer needs `[ps=2]` — [[study-powerful-network-management-4cb125]]
- TME 10 NetView supports: Unix AIX / Digital UX / Solaris / NT on Alpha and Intel `[ps=0]` — [[study-powerful-network-management-4cb125]]
- HP NNM supports: Solaris / HP-UX / NT Server environments `[ps=0]` — [[study-powerful-network-management-4cb125]]
