# IBM 3090 Mainframe

> IBM's flagship mainframe; 3090-400 (4-processor) used as System Under Test and 3090-200 as load driver in the Westwood benchmark; exposed SNA Tax.


## Top observations

- Kastner served as TPC benchmark audit client for IBM; finds needle in haystack in IBM performance data — [[study-2026-kastner-ibm-longitudinal]]
- Plaintiff expert witness (Carlton Fields for Unisys); distributed IBM mainframe systems; $110M EDS contract later cancelled — [[study-psk-niopubs-3d103b]]
- DEC conducted a secret (blind) benchmark vs. IBM 3090 under Kastner's marketing leadership; IBM responded with DB2 v2. — [[study-volume-1-appendix-career-timeline]]
- DECtp blind benchmark vs. IBM 3090 conducted 1988; 'SNA Tax' overhead quantified. — [[study-volume-1-appendix-career-timeline]]
- June 1988: DEC announces benchmark results. IBM responds with DB2 v2 release in October 1988. — [[study-volume-1-appendix-career-timeline]]
- Objective: measure IBM's highest possible throughput on a 3090 running DB2, then compare head-to-head against a DEC VAX under identical constraints. — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
- IBM's own systems analysts brought in under contract; told in writing IBM was to provide all reasonable services and tuning to maximize throughput; unaware DEC was the client. — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
- IBM 3090-400 as System Under Test; IBM 3090-200 as load driver — two of IBM's largest mainframes in the same room, running tests at night and on weekends. — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
- IBM's DB2 Version 2 announcement (claiming faster performance) was described as crystallizing the need for independent standards: 'the era of proprietary benchmarks had to end.' — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
- IBM analysts knew a benchmark was being run and had written instructions to maximize throughput, but had no idea DEC was the client behind the curtain. — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
