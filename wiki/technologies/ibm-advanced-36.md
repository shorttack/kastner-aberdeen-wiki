# IBM Advanced/36

> Introduced October 1994; priced $9,800-$21,000; ran System/36 SSP applications; withdrew from marketing by 2000; Advanced/36 Machine support removed in OS/400 V4R5


## Top observations

- Priority #1 for System/36 owners; Advanced 36 rated Best, AS/400 Acceptable, PC-LAN Unacceptable `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Priority #3; Advanced 36 rated Best (1-day migration via TDL), AS/400 Acceptable, PC-LAN Unacceptable (cannot reuse any S/36 skills or data) `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Priority #4; Advanced 36 rated Best (same apps, same users, max 3-day cutover), AS/400 Acceptable, PC-LAN Unacceptable (all new processes) `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Priority #5; AS/400 rated Best (multi-billion dollar IBM division), Advanced 36 Acceptable (IBM direct support), PC-LAN Unacceptable (local dealers, hourly billing, no accountability) `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Advanced 36 is clearly the best choice for the vast majority of current System/36 owners; all former System/36 owners interviewed who chose Advanced 36 were extremely happy `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- $9,800 to $21,000 depending on model and additional peripherals; all existing System/36 applications run without modification `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Maintenance reduced from ~$330/month for larger System/36s to ~$95/month for even larger Advanced 36s (including internal storage) `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Advanced 36 runs batch processing jobs 8-10 times faster than System/36 — described as System/36 on steroids `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Advanced 36 detects potential problems before they occur and automatically reports to system manager requesting IBM service call scheduling `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Priority #2; Advanced 36 rated Best, AS/400 Acceptable, PC-LAN Unacceptable — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- Aberdeen recommends migrating to Advanced 36 within 12 months then planning for eventual move to OS/400 for Year 2000 compliant applications — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
- IBM withdrew Advanced 36 from marketing by 2000; Advanced/36 Machine support removed in OS/400 V4R5; System/36 owners who migrated to Advanced 36 then migrated to AS/400 as predicted; prediction proved accurate — [[study-aberdeen-1996-evaluating-system36-migration-strategies]]
