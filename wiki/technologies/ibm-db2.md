## Top observations

- IBM's database and middleware software stack (DB2, Tivoli, WebSphere, MQ, Rational) would be a durable revenue engine independent of hardware `[ps=4]` — [[study-2026-kastner-ibm-longitudinal]]
- IBM was a dominant TPC benchmark participant and Kastner used TPC results extensively in competitive analysis `[ps=4]` — [[study-2026-kastner-ibm-longitudinal]]
- Kastner: IBM DB2 for TP still maturing; many customers still on IMS/CICS-VSAM `[ps=4]` — [[study-dec-blue-monday-internal-sales-training-dectp-vs-ibm-0021cc]]
- IBM gained Unix ISV market share in 1994, usually at expense of HP `[ps=4]` — [[study-jungle-6-7af8b7]]
- DB2 for AIX 2 functionally much improved; Data Propagator for mainframe-RS6000; CICS/Ensina/Tuxedo OLTP; HACMP and SP2 scalability `[ps=4]` — [[study-jungle-6-7af8b7]]
- IBM/EDS strategy leaned on 'no one ever got fired for buying IBM' reputation; promised mainframe power and DB2 maturity could handle volume `[ps=4]` — [[study-unisys-florida-hrs-expert-witness-tallah-5b4524]]
- FAILED: response times initially in minutes; system crashed during food stamp distribution; CPU at 95-100% capacity; national food stamp error records set `[ps=4]` — [[study-unisys-florida-hrs-expert-witness-tallah-5b4524]]
- IBM/EDS strategy: mainframe + DB2 can handle volume at lower perceived risk than Unix/RDBMS `[ps=3]` — [[study-2026-kastner-ibm-longitudinal]]
- New-found competitiveness in DB2: parallel/scalable PQS and SP2; triggers/stored procs; strong proprietary replication `[ps=3]` — [[study-jungle-1-5-675c58]]
- Good async replication in Data Propagator; no peer-peer replication `[ps=3]` — [[study-jungle-6-7af8b7]]
- Worldwide support and service ... the SAP R/3 story; enterprises committing to DB2 across IBM platforms `[ps=3]` — [[study-jungle-6-7af8b7]]
- In October 1988, IBM rushed out a press announcement claiming DB2 now ran much faster than DEC's measurements; DB2 Version 2 announcement was an acknowledgment DEC had found real weaknesses. `[ps=3]` — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
- Follower in RDBMS functionality and performance vs Oracle, Sybase, Informix; IS decision makers value best-in-class `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-ibm-transformation-business-machines-information-service]]
- DB2 had been tuned almost exclusively for query workloads; Edgar Codd and Christopher Date designed the relational model for data retrieval; transactional throughput was 'an architectural afterthought.' `[ps=2]` — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
- Edgar Codd and Christopher Date are credited as the IBM researchers who invented the relational database model; designed for elegant retrieval, not transactional throughput. `[ps=2]` — [[study-volume-1-ch06-dec-mainframes-last-stand-1987-1988]]
