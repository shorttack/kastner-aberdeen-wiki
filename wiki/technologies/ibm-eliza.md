# IBM Project eLiza (autonomic computing)

> Self-managing, self-healing architecture initiative; products-not-just-concept; heterogeneous management across IBM and non-IBM environments; precursor to autonomic computing branding.


## Summary




## Top observations

- MIT in 1966 was alive with early AI research energy — LISP, ELIZA, Project MAC, researchers working on foundations of machine learning and knowledge representation. `[ps=5]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- ELIZA (MIT, 1966, Joseph Weizenbaum): first chatbot; demonstrated pattern-matching rules could simulate conversation convincingly. No actual language understanding; worked by reflecting user statements as questions. `[ps=5]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- ELIZA fooled users who knew it was a program; Turing Test implications were described as 'unsettling.' `[ps=5]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- eLiza self-management is transparent to developers — third-party software will not require modification using a whole new API set. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Knowledge-worker exception handling follows: discover exception, query cause, make decision, transact to change, report/collaborate; eLiza self-management should perform all steps subject to preset policies. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Preset-policy pager modes: (1) problem fixed, (2) approve default procedure, (3) choose among actions, or (4) no notification needed — contrasted with low-bar 'wireless page that something is wrong'. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Message 1: Best Practices Automation — leveraging IT and domain expertise of IBM and others to address skills, expertise, flexibility, technology, and heterogeneity for more effective business processes. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Message 2: Simplification — rooting out complexity wherever found by limiting and directing human interaction to high-level tasks requiring human decision-making. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Key part of process is defining what and when eLiza is; eLiza is ultimately things as well as a concept; does not exist today; first phase must come into being. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Sub-text to eLiza theme: 'this is not business as usual' — CEO/CIO-level pitch matched by dramatic proof points (e.g., reducing management keystrokes so one operator can manage 10x more servers). `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Avoid positioning eLiza as 'great technology' (even though it is) — focus on business outcomes. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- eLiza message to developers should emphasize masking of complexity — eLiza is not about mastering a thousand more APIs; it acts as a 'super operating system' providing a reliable foundation. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- eLiza is about both e-Business and other businesses; assertion that e-Business is the harder general case may be true but will get lost in translation. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- IBM needs to articulate proof points for specific areas of eLiza with strongest leadership positions; proof points are key to selling CxOs on ROI. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Make sure 'eLiza is heterogeneous' message is not confused with 'eLiza is based on standards' — heterogeneous has great pull with executives; standards are ho-hum. `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Umbrella messaging opportunity: Pervasive Computing + Real-time Computing demands Managed Computing (i.e., eLiza). `[ps=4]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Try to approach IBM on multiple projects tied to a single IBM initiative: eLiza, pervasive computing, or WebSphere infrastructure. `[ps=3]` — [[study-ibm-major-account-plan-8-cc6400]]
- eLiza is NOT equivalent to or a synonym for e-servers; eLiza is a server management strategy, not a server strategy. `[ps=3]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- eLiza is products, not just a concept or an internal IBM engineering rallying point. `[ps=3]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Set clear parameters around eLiza: specific quantifiable 'stretch' goals (e.g., 90% reduction in management costs), milestones, and proof points; lay out in IBM statement-of-direction style. `[ps=3]` — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
