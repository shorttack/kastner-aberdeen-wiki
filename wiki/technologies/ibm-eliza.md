# IBM Project eLiza (autonomic computing)

> Self-managing, self-healing architecture initiative; products-not-just-concept; heterogeneous management across IBM and non-IBM environments; precursor to autonomic computing branding.


## Top observations

- Positioning and messaging eLiza initiative to IT management, line of business, and developer communities; $1B IT management initiative per success-stories deck — [[study-aberdeen-corporate-collateral-2001-v2-b4b001]]
- Try to approach IBM on multiple projects tied to a single IBM initiative: eLiza, pervasive computing, or WebSphere infrastructure. — [[study-ibm-major-account-plan-8-cc6400]]
- eLiza is NOT equivalent to or a synonym for e-servers; eLiza is a server management strategy, not a server strategy. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- eLiza is not IBM server-specific; it will manage customer environments including solutions from IBM and others (IBM best/first supported, but not only). — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- eLiza self-management is transparent to developers — third-party software will not require modification using a whole new API set. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- eLiza is products, not just a concept or an internal IBM engineering rallying point. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Knowledge-worker exception handling follows: discover exception, query cause, make decision, transact to change, report/collaborate; eLiza self-management should perform all steps subject to preset policies. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Preset-policy pager modes: (1) problem fixed, (2) approve default procedure, (3) choose among actions, or (4) no notification needed — contrasted with low-bar 'wireless page that something is wrong'. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Message 1: Best Practices Automation — leveraging IT and domain expertise of IBM and others to address skills, expertise, flexibility, technology, and heterogeneity for more effective business processes. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Message 2: Simplification — rooting out complexity wherever found by limiting and directing human interaction to high-level tasks requiring human decision-making. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Message 3: Lower Cost — reducing overall costs, improving expense predictability, control, and accounting. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Set clear parameters around eLiza: specific quantifiable 'stretch' goals (e.g., 90% reduction in management costs), milestones, and proof points; lay out in IBM statement-of-direction style. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Compelling need to brand eLiza — clear identity at non-technical level, logo (e.g., 'eLiza-enabled'); cited Budweiser iguanas as precedent. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Key part of process is defining what and when eLiza is; eLiza is ultimately things as well as a concept; does not exist today; first phase must come into being. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Sub-text to eLiza theme: 'this is not business as usual' — CEO/CIO-level pitch matched by dramatic proof points (e.g., reducing management keystrokes so one operator can manage 10x more servers). — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Avoid positioning eLiza as 'great technology' (even though it is) — focus on business outcomes. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- eLiza message to developers should emphasize masking of complexity — eLiza is not about mastering a thousand more APIs; it acts as a 'super operating system' providing a reliable foundation. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- eLiza is about both e-Business and other businesses; assertion that e-Business is the harder general case may be true but will get lost in translation. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- IBM needs to articulate proof points for specific areas of eLiza with strongest leadership positions; proof points are key to selling CxOs on ROI. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
- Make sure 'eLiza is heterogeneous' message is not confused with 'eLiza is based on standards' — heterogeneous has great pull with executives; standards are ho-hum. — [[study-ibm-messaging-workshop-report-final-9-ef7fbc]]
