# IBM/Apple PowerPC G5 (PowerPC 970)

> Apple Power Mac / Xserve G5 CPU; reached 2.7GHz before Apple transitioned to Intel 2006


## Top observations

- G5 heat/yield problems forced Apple-Intel transition (announced 2005, completed Aug 2006); last PowerPC Mac discontinued 2006 — [[study-macdailynews-analyst-apple-will-have-pro-54defe]]
- IBM gets an 'A' for the second quarter for being able to crank the chip up from 2GHz to 2.5GHz — that's a 25 per cent increase. — [[study-power-macs-won-t-hit-3ghz-this-year-anal-73ad0f]]
- I question IBM's ability to meet Apple's increased G5 processor demand moving forward given the company's availability challenges to date. — [[study-power-macs-won-t-hit-3ghz-this-year-anal-73ad0f]]
- WWDC June 2005: Steve Jobs announces Apple's two-year transition from PowerPC to Intel x86; first Intel Macs shipped January 2006. G5 never reached 3GHz in shipping product. — [[study-power-macs-won-t-hit-3ghz-this-year-anal-73ad0f]]
