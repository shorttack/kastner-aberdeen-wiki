# InfiniBand Architecture (IBA)

> High-speed low-latency data center I/O interconnect; became standard in HPC and AI training clusters; owned by NVIDIA since 2020

- Intel cancelled plans to supply silicon for InfiniBand interface adapters — [[study-2002-does-intel-s-decision-not-to-manufactur-iniband-si-0bd64b]]
- Scheduled for production launch late 2002 — [[study-2002-does-intel-s-decision-not-to-manufactur-iniband-si-0bd64b]]
- Many industry observers believe Intel's decision will cause InfiniBand to stall — [[study-2002-does-intel-s-decision-not-to-manufactur-iniband-si-0bd64b]]
- Intel's decision is good for Intel — [[study-2002-does-intel-s-decision-not-to-manufactur-iniband-si-0bd64b]]

- InfiniBand initiative will not be damaged by Intel's exit — [[study-2002-does-intel-s-decision-not-to-manufactur-iniband-si-0bd64b]]

- InfiniBand market estimated at $18.28 billion in 2024 projected to grow at 40% CAGR — [[study-2002-does-intel-s-decision-not-to-manufactur-iniband-si-0bd64b]]
- Important next-generation data-center I/O initiative supported by IT industry broadly — [[study-2002-does-intel-s-decision-not-to-manufactur-iniband-si-0bd64b]]
- 2.5 Gbps to 10 Gbps today; expected to reach up to 30 Gbps as it evolves — [[study-infiniband-and-beyond-computerworld-supp-200261]]
- More than 150 companies worldwide — [[study-infiniband-and-beyond-computerworld-supp-200261]]
- 'In many instances, [InfiniBand] will replace TCP/IP as the high-speed, server-to-server interconnect technology.' — Kastner — [[study-infiniband-and-beyond-computerworld-supp-200261]]
- 'InfiniBand works at very high speeds with very low latency and is a very efficient and transparent protocol.' Plus: parallel connections with low CPU use, enabling huge I/O bandwidth; low-latency blade-to-blade messaging for clustering. — [[study-infiniband-and-beyond-computerworld-supp-200261]]
- ISPs, application service providers, and large web sites with multi-tiered architecture will be initial targets for InfiniBand's functionality — [[study-infiniband-and-beyond-computerworld-supp-200261]]
- Mixed — did NOT broadly displace TCP/IP in enterprise server-to-server. Substantively validated in HPC (top-500 supercomputers majority-InfiniBand by 2010s) and AI-training clusters (NVIDIA acquired Mellanox 2019 for $6.9B; InfiniBand HDR/NDR the dominant fabric for GPU training 2022-2026). Enterprise storage adopted RoCE/iWARP-over-Ethernet as alternative. — [[study-infiniband-and-beyond-computerworld-supp-200261]]
