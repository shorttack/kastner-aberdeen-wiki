# Intel Next-Generation Multi-core Platforms/Microarchitecture

> Mega-briefing topic — setup for Core microarchitecture (2006).


## Top observations

- Intel scheduled a dedicated 'Mega-Briefing on Intel's Next Generation Multi-core Platforms and Microarchitecture' for Tuesday morning following Otellini's opening keynote — signaling that multi-core was the central IDF Fall 2005 narrative. — [[study-2005-fall-idf-schedule-b5e6c4]]
- Spring IDF 2005 scheduled a dedicated Multicore Briefing (Tue 10:45-11:45 AM), six months before Fall IDF's multi-core mega-briefing — showing Intel's consistent multi-core drumbeat throughout 2005. — [[study-intel-idf-2005-spring-delegate-confirmat-0de810]]
- Intel delivered multicore processors in 2005 as predicted. Intel Pentium D (dual-core desktop) shipped May 26, 2005. Paxville MP (dual-core server Xeon) shipped November 1, 2005. Core Duo (Yonah, mobile) shipped January 2006. Multicore was in production by end-2005. — [[study-intel-idf-2005-spring-delegate-confirmat-0de810]]
