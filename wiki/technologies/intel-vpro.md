# Intel vPro manageability platform

> Intel business-PC platform combining CPU, chipset, and networking with AMT (Active Management Technology) for remote OOB management


## Top observations

- No matter where in the world the device is, the corporate laptops that workers take home or on business trips are all now manageable, fixable, and diagnosable. As long as you can get to a network, vPro and related technology can help to solve the problem. `[ps=4]` — [[study-small-business-intel-targets-smbs-with-r-2cd3ec]]
- On the high end, vPro has pushed the cost to less than $2,000. The not-quite-so-efficient companies have whacked out $1,000 per employee per year — a huge savings that can go right to the bottom line. `[ps=3]` — [[study-small-business-intel-targets-smbs-with-r-2cd3ec]]
- On the low end, vPro has cut PC TCO to under $500 per PC per year, not much more than the device itself. `[ps=2]` — [[study-small-business-intel-targets-smbs-with-r-2cd3ec]]
- Day 2 lab tours: (1) Microprocessor Validation Lab — CPU post-silicon validation, platforms, debug tools; (2) vPro Technology Business Client & Enterprise Platform Integration Lab; (3) DataCenter Tour. `[ps=0]` — [[study-peter-kastner-10-bde195]]
- Five years ago, major service companies told Kastner their TCO outcomes were in the range of $700 to $3,000 per PC per year. `[ps=0]` — [[study-small-business-intel-targets-smbs-with-r-2cd3ec]]
- Industry analyst Peter Kastner discusses impact of Intel vPro technology including giving an overview of total cost of ownership benefits `[ps=0]` — [[study-youtube-kastner-intel-vpro-tco-2008-cb1733]]
