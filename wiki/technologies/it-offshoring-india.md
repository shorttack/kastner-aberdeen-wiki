# IT offshoring to India

> Indian IT-services delivery (Wipro, Infosys, TCS, HCL) displacing onshore US/EU IT services; massive cost arbitrage mid-2000s


## Top observations

- Two or three years down the road, rapid wage inflation in India is likely to erode the economic benefits that are so enticing today. — [[study-seven-ways-to-prepare-for-the-economic-u-0a7d35]]
- By 2006-2008 Indian IT-services wage inflation routinely ran 10-15% annually at Infosys/Wipro/TCS, compressing margins and forcing expansion to Philippines, Eastern Europe, and Tier-2 Indian cities — directly validating Kastner's 2004 two-to-three-year prediction. — [[study-seven-ways-to-prepare-for-the-economic-u-0a7d35]]
- That's a horse of a completely different color, and a potential economic threat to the livelihood of a lot of Americans. — [[study-slow-growth-for-it-spending-in-2003-it-t-231f47]]
- US IT employment data 2003-2015 show substantial shift of application development and back-office IT work to India/Philippines; Kastner's 'economic threat' framing was broadly borne out in narrow IT-services occupations though overall US tech employment grew. — [[study-slow-growth-for-it-spending-in-2003-it-t-231f47]]
