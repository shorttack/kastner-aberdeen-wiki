# IT Outsourcing (ITO)

> Core subject. Range from simple application outsourcing (e.g. email) to full business process offshoring (e.g. general ledger, accounts payable).


## Top observations

- Focusing on core competencies: decision that IT excellence is best achieved by an outsider — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Saving money: most expected benefit; palming off dysfunctional IT on naïve outsourcer is a pipe dream — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- IT resource constraints: lack of talent, costly talent, staff turnover, wrong skill-sets — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Time-to-market concern in highly competitive industries where IT agility is critical — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Culture wars: LOB management fed up with IT intransigence; outsourcing as 'blow it up' nuclear option — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Up to 30% of IT outsourcing engagements seriously misaligned; customers may not realize it — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- 50-67% of IT outsourcing contracts running at sub-optimal levels — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Only 5-10% of multi-million dollar IT outsourcing agreements drive all anticipated business value — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Aberdeen tracking IT planning and outsourcing issues quantitatively since 2004 — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Many enterprises have never done a serious make-or-buy outsourcing decision on any IT segment — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Cannot name a single company that has outsourced IT and not learned painful lessons — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
- Report will benchmark all-size companies in Global 5000 across all industries — [[study-business-value-outsourcing-57ce57]]
