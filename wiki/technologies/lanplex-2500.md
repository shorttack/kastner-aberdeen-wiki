# LANplex 2500

> 16-port 10 Mbps Ethernet + 2 FDDI ports. Modular workgroup switch. ISE-chip ASIC. Introduced December 1994.


## Top observations

- Feature-rich and aggressively priced; most capable offering vs. Cisco and ALANTEC — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- 565,000 pps (packets per second) — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- 6 to 9 months ahead on performance and features — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- LANplex 2500 'came through in these environments with flying colors' — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- Device must be fully flash PROM and fully downloadable from central site; no forklift upgrades acceptable — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- Ethernet, FDDI, 565K pps (3Com) vs. IP/IPX routing with AT in Q3'95 (Cisco) vs. superior but not as complete as Cisco (Bay/ALANTEC) — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- 3Com: IP routing available; Cisco: IP/IPX routing with AT in Q3'95; Bay: will have key routing protocols like ALANTEC — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- 3Com: FDDI today + Fast Ethernet and ATM; Cisco: ambitious timeframe; Bay/ALANTEC: all players have indicated support — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- 3Com: redundant power and hot-swap modules; Cisco and 3Com appear stronger on reliability — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- Up to 16 switched 10 Mbps Ethernet ports and 2 switched FDDI ports — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- Breaks FDDI packets (max 4500 bytes) into Ethernet frames (max 1500 bytes) for FDDI-to-Ethernet translation — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- Switched Ethernet client connections to FDDI-based servers on campus backbone; replaces expensive router-based FDDI connections — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- Isolated server traffic on separate backbone using second FDDI port; departmental backbone with local servers — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- Large number of early 2500 customers had existing LANplex 6000 installations or were evaluating 6000 — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- LANplex 2500 has met and fully exceeded its promise as excellent workgroup solution and flagship revenue performer for 3Com Switching Division — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- 3Com must deliver Fast Ethernet and ATM uplink promise to maintain leadership; density improvements also expected — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
- Competitive lead eroded quickly: Cisco and Bay Networks closed gap by 1997; 3Com acquired by HP in 2010 after struggles in enterprise networking — [[study-aberdeen-1995-3com-lanplex-2500]]
