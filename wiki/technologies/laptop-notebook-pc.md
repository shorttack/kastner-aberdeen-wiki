# Laptop / notebook PC

> Portable clamshell PCs; thin-and-light category emerged 2003-2005; overtook desktops in unit sales ~2008


## Top observations

- As more consumers buy notebooks as desktop replacements, it makes sense for AMD to replace its mobility processors with 64-bit Athlons. The laptop share of the total PC market is rising steadily, prompting a response from chip and PC makers. `[ps=4]` — [[study-technology-news-hardware-amd-makes-athlo-214a36]]
- 2003 proved that laptop sales are price elastic in that $800 to $1,000 laptops are flying off the shelves. `[ps=4]` — [[study-technology-news-hardware-intel-unveils-l-1f420a]]
- Sub-$1,000 laptop share grew from ~25% in 2003 to ~60% by 2010 per Gartner/IDC — strongly validating Kastner's 2004 price-elasticity thesis. Netbooks 2007-2011, sub-$500 Chromebooks 2011+, budget Windows laptops became the bulk of PC unit sales. `[ps=4]` — [[study-technology-news-hardware-intel-unveils-l-1f420a]]
- Worldwide PC shipments: notebooks passed desktops in unit volume in 2008 per IDC/Gartner — validating Kastner's rising-laptop-share thesis. US retail notebook sales crossed over desktops already in 2005. — [[study-technology-news-hardware-amd-makes-athlo-214a36]]
- Both companies are rightly attacking the mobile PC space, which is the fastest-growing portion of the industry. — [[study-technology-news-hardware-intel-unveils-l-1f420a]]
