# Large Language Models (LLMs)

> GPT series, Claude, Gemini. Trained on internet-scale text. Emergent capabilities at scale. First AI systems that handle ambiguity, context, and implicit knowledge qualitatively differently than predecessors.


## Top observations

- A narrow AI system automates one task. A broad AI system augments one worker across many tasks. The economic calculus is different in kind, not just in degree. `[ps=5]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- Organizations that built large, well-governed data assets in the 1990s-2000s are the same organizations most effectively deploying LLMs and AI inference today. No one planned it that way. `[ps=4]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- The thread from ELIZA to GPT-4 is six decades of compounding progress. Each generation solved problems the previous could not while introducing new limitations the next would address. `[ps=4]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- Broad trajectory of AI from narrow tools toward general-purpose cognitive capability correctly predicted. `[ps=4]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- LLMs are the first AI systems capable enough across wide range of knowledge-work tasks to be genuinely useful as general-purpose cognitive tools — not just narrow automation. `[ps=4]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- Breadth of LLMs changes the economics: augments one worker across many tasks rather than automating one task. Different in kind, not just degree. `[ps=4]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- LLMs demonstrate emergent capabilities — skills not explicitly trained — at sufficient scale. First AI systems to handle ambiguity, context, and implicit knowledge qualitatively differently from predecessors. `[ps=4]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- This time is different: previous 'different' claims referred to narrow AI. LLM breadth across many knowledge-work tasks is qualitatively new. `[ps=4]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
- What makes current AI generation genuinely different: combination of language fluency with broad knowledge and reasoning capability. Previous AI systems were narrow; LLMs are broad general-purpose cognitive tools. — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
