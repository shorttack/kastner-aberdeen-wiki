# Living-room / home-theater PC

> Category Kastner flagged; evolved into streaming-box era


## Top observations

- 'The next form factor battles are beginning to be fought in the living room' — Peter Kastner, Aberdeen Group, via Reuters — [[study-pc-style-living-room-macobserver-seward-97ed83]]
- 'Why put a PC where it has to compete with other things for your attention? ... adding a computer only makes the relatively simple process of watching TV far more complicated' — Seward — [[study-pc-style-living-room-macobserver-seward-97ed83]]
- Reuters/Kastner article predicted PCs would be designed to sit in the living room with less bulk and more style — [[study-pc-style-living-room-macobserver-seward-97ed83]]
- By 2015 the living-room-content-delivery battle was won by streaming appliances (Apple TV 2007, Roku 2008, Chromecast 2013, Fire TV 2014) — not general-purpose PCs. Windows Media Center was discontinued in Windows 10 (2015). Kastner's broader 'form factor battles in living room' was correct, but the form factor that won was the TV-attached appliance, not the living-room PC. — [[study-pc-style-living-room-macobserver-seward-97ed83]]
