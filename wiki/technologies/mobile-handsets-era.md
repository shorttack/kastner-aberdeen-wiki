# Mobile handsets / feature-phones / smartphones

> Consumer mobile phone industry; Motorola DynaTAC, StarTAC, RAZR; later iPhone/Android era


## Top observations

- Motorola launched the RAZR V3 in 2004 under Zander's leadership; the RAZR became one of the best-selling cellphones in history (~130 million units by 2008), briefly validating Kastner's reinvention endorsement. `[ps=5]` — [[study-technology-news-boardroom-former-sun-exe-bc336f]]
- Zander resigned as Motorola CEO on Jan 3 2008 after a $4.3B 2007 handset-division loss, missed smartphone-transition window, and ongoing activist pressure from Carl Icahn — ending the Kastner-endorsed reinvention era short of long-term success. `[ps=4]` — [[study-technology-news-boardroom-former-sun-exe-bc336f]]
- Zander's computer industry background is important at a time when digital technology is creating new opportunities for consumer electronics. Zander is a take-charge guy in a company that's been searching for new levels of zeal and enthusiasm. `[ps=3]` — [[study-technology-news-boardroom-former-sun-exe-bc336f]]
- Edward Zander brings a wealth of high-tech experience to a high-tech company. Motorola has laid out plans to reinvent itself, and it will be Zander's job to drive those changes. `[ps=0]` — [[study-technology-news-boardroom-former-sun-exe-bc336f]]
