# Mitel NeVaDa (Networked Voice and Data)

> Integrated broadband enterprise backbone; voice/data/video on single OC-3 fiber; collapsed backbone star topology

- Ethernet, Token Ring, FDDI, and 100 Base-T in switched or shared media modes — [[study-aberdeen-1996-mitels-nevadanetworked-voice-data-speeding-toward]]
