# Network Operating System (NOS)

> General category; memo is framed around Aberdeen's NOS practice as worked example


## Top observations

- Implementation and maintenance of NOSes has gotten so complex it often requires outside assistance; especially true for NT Server as enterprise NOS `[ps=5]` — [[study-1999pr~2-cd0049]]
- Most mid/large organizations have multi-tier hardware/software with multiple NOSes; key IS question is interoperability strategy not NOS selection `[ps=4]` — [[study-1999pr~2-cd0049]]
- NOSes proving more effective than ever as internet arrives; Internet requires directory/admin/security services that NOS is best positioned to provide `[ps=3]` — [[study-1999pr~2-cd0049]]
- NOS suppliers answering user server-consolidation goals by delivering broader directory services and cross-platform administrative solutions `[ps=3]` — [[study-1999pr~2-cd0049]]
- All three major NOS vendors (Microsoft/Novell/IBM) showed sales increases over past two years despite NT Server dominance; market expanding overall `[ps=3]` — [[study-1999pr~2-cd0049]]
