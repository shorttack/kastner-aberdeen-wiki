# Sequent NUMA-Q

> Non-Uniform Memory Access architecture for scalable enterprise servers; concept now standard in all modern multi-socket x86 servers.


## Top observations

- Aimed at world's toughest commercial computing problems: OLTP, complex DSS, and messaging — [[study-1996-sequent-38f0b1]]
- Engineering elegance delivering scalability with minimal overhead and no application software changes — [[study-1996-sequent-38f0b1]]
- Will be a benchmark for high-end commercial computing leadership into the 21st century — [[study-1996-sequent-38f0b1]]
- NUMA-Q will extend Intel-standard to well beyond even traditional mainframes while preserving software investments — [[study-1996-sequent-38f0b1]]
- Sequent acquired by IBM for approximately $810M; NUMA technology integrated into IBM x-series servers — [[study-1996-sequent-38f0b1]]
- AMD Opteron and Intel Xeon adopted NUMA architecture as standard for multi-socket servers — [[study-1996-sequent-38f0b1]]
