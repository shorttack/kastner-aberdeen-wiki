# On-Demand / Online Learning Systems

> Respondents demanded 'on-demand, on-line, real-time, continuous access' training — directly describing what became e-learning platforms.


## Top observations

- Traditional classroom training demand supplanted by universal appeal for on-demand, on-line, real-time, continuous access learning — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- Classroom training seen as: too long, not specific enough, overwhelming detail unlikely to be retained — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- Aberdeen implicitly predicts on-demand, customized, online training will supplant classroom instruction for enterprise technology transitions — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
- E-learning market grew to $35B+ by 2011; corporate LMS market (Cornerstone, SuccessFactors, Saba) became mainstream; LinkedIn Learning, Coursera launched; on-demand fully replaced classroom as primary modality — [[study-1997-managing-the-people-dimension-of-ma-1d6b17]]
