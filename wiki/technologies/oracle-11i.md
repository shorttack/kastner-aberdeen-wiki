# Oracle e-Business Suite 11i

> Web-based ERP/CRM/SCM suite; QA problems in early releases; ~50% of Oracle clients on 10x release (older) at time of study.


## Top observations

- Installed base satisfaction and upgrade pathways over new product announcements — [[study-2002-a-kinder-gentler-larry-ellison-oracle-presents-new-cb69fb]]
- Largely absent — [[study-2002-a-kinder-gentler-larry-ellison-oracle-presents-new-cb69fb]]
- Upgrading installed base is core of new license revenue opportunity — [[study-2002-a-kinder-gentler-larry-ellison-oracle-presents-new-cb69fb]]
- Predicted to be the central upgrade vehicle for existing Oracle customers — [[study-2002-a-kinder-gentler-larry-ellison-oracle-presents-new-cb69fb]]
- Premier Support ended December 2013; sustaining support extended through December 2014 — [[study-2002-a-kinder-gentler-larry-ellison-oracle-presents-new-cb69fb]]
- Oracle Apps World 2002 messaging shifted from new-product launch to 'let's get you guys to use what we already have' — focused on upgrading the installed base to 11i. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Approximately 50% of Oracle client base (~1,500 customers) still on a 10x release of the applications suite. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Initial releases of Oracle 11i had quality assurance (QA) problems that further dampened upgrade enthusiasm among existing customers. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
- Aberdeen predicts businesses won't be rushed to upgrade due to economy, IT investment slowdown, and magnitude of effort involved. — [[study-oracle-appsworld-insight-edit-4-813c2e]]
