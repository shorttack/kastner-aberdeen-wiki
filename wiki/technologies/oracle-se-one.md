# Oracle Standard Edition One

> SMB-targeted Oracle DB variant; retired in favor of SE2 (2014)


## Top observations

- Oracle discontinued Standard Edition One and merged it into Standard Edition 2 in 2014 — SMB database strategy continued under different branding. `[ps=3]` — [[study-oracle-and-dell-take-aim-at-midsize-busi-770ea6]]
- Oracle granted Dell the exclusive right (through 2004 year-end) to deliver preinstalled Oracle Standard Edition One on PowerEdge 2600/2650 running Red Hat Linux or Windows; pricing starts at $4,108 for 5/10/25/unlimited user variations. `[ps=0]` — [[study-oracle-and-dell-take-aim-at-midsize-busi-770ea6]]
- The pre-installation arrangement between Dell and Oracle will be particularly useful to small and midsize companies; although it could take only 15 minutes for these companies to have the Oracle database installed at a later time, it could be an expensive 15 minutes if they have to hire a service provider. — [[study-oracle-and-dell-take-aim-at-midsize-busi-770ea6]]
