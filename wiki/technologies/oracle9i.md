# Oracle9i

> RDBMS at center of the 2001 Compaq Tru64 recommendation and the 2004 Dell RAC win


## Top observations

- Kastner read SAP's database dependency on Oracle as a convenience to optimize; SAP read the same dependency as a strategic vulnerability to eliminate — he underweighted how badly SAP wanted independence. `[ps=5]` — [[study-2026-kastner-sap-r3-hana-longitudinal]]
- The 2001 Compaq Tru64/Oracle9i recommendation — that SAP should embrace and not resist the Compaq/Oracle partnership — sat exactly backwards to what SAP actually did; scored low, the most instructive miss in the archive. `[ps=4]` — [[study-2026-kastner-sap-r3-hana-longitudinal]]
- Author testimony (not in archive): Kastner pitched Plattner that Aberdeen had 'actionable intelligence and a plan for SAP to rapidly gain market share against Oracle,' then revealed Oracle's employee turnover had reached 35% — Plattner's demeanor turned congenial and the meeting ran over time. `[ps=0]` — [[study-2026-kastner-sap-r3-hana-longitudinal]]
