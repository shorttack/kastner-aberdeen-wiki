# PCI-X (incl. PCI-X 2.0)

> Extended PCI with higher bandwidth; superseded by PCI Express (PCIe); cited as inadequate for long-term data center needs.


## Top observations

- three PCI-X I/O slots for server-grade peripherals — [[study-dct-apple-powermac-g5-2003-06]]
- PCI-X and PCI-X 2.0 are inadequate and will not satisfy long-term data center connectivity needs — [[study-infiniband-data-center-wp-2002]]
- PCI Express (PCIe) — not IBA — became the universal server I/O bus; IBA coexisted as an external fabric rather than replacing internal I/O — [[study-infiniband-data-center-wp-2002]]
- 1.06 GB/second at 133 MHz — [[study-intel-ia2004-pk4-kc-edits-de9c37]]
- 532 MB/second (parallel PCI) — [[study-intel-infiband-wp--edit-psk-5-22f-a2c551]]
- PCI-X doubles PCI to 1.06 GB/s at 133MHz. PCI-X 2.0 proposes 266MHz (2.1 GB/s peak) and 533MHz (4.2 GB/s peak). PCI-X 266 not in production before 2004; PCI-X 533 still in spec development. — [[study-planningforemergingindustry-6-40decb]]
