# Public-Private Switched Network (PPSN)

> Aberdeen's coined concept for emerging market where LAN equipment manufacturers would compete in carrier Central Office markets and vice versa; the PPSN concept partially materialized as MPLS and Metro Ethernet but was dominated by Cisco/Lucent, not Cabletron. The conceptual framework anticipated carrier-edge convergence.


## Top observations

- Successful companies will deliver integrated product lines scaling from CPE bandwidth requirements up to gigabit carrier speeds — [[study-1997-cabletron--discovering-the-beacon-u-2d44d5]]
- Network management platform spanning CPE and Central Office environments; integrated management view — [[study-1997-cabletron--discovering-the-beacon-u-2d44d5]]
- Rapid reaction to changes in still-emerging PPSN market — [[study-1997-cabletron--discovering-the-beacon-u-2d44d5]]
- Empowering strategic vision providing courage to make necessary investments and face potential failures — [[study-1997-cabletron--discovering-the-beacon-u-2d44d5]]
