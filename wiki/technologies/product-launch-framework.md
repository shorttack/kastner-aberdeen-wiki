# Cross-Functional Matrix Product Launch Framework

> Canonical 12-task / 7-method launch framework codified by the questionnaire.


## Top observations

- All 5 companies described a global, standardized launch process across hardware/software/services product groups. — [[study-sun-launch-best-practices-report-draft-8-9741fb]]
- 2 companies said variations are insignificant; 3 acknowledged individual product groups had some autonomy with meaningful product-to-product variation. — [[study-sun-launch-best-practices-report-draft-8-9741fb]]
- Launch execution always involves a matrix-managed cross-functional team led by a launch team owner, integrating product dev, test, procurement, manufacturing, marketing, sales channel, fulfillment, training, support, finance, AR, PR. — [[study-sun-launch-best-practices-report-draft-8-9741fb]]
- 2 of 5 companies had separate launch organizations reporting into VP Marketing (Product and Solutions Launch; Product Release Strategy). 3 had no common launch organization. — [[study-sun-launch-best-practices-report-draft-8-9741fb]]
- While a rigorous published framework is defined for the organization, it is rarely executed precisely in an identical fashion — difficult to differentiate between process as defined and as implemented. — [[study-sun-launch-best-practices-report-draft-8-9741fb]]
- As timeframes tighten and pressures for new products increase, there is increasing variation from standard process and increasing autonomy of individual product groups/business units. — [[study-sun-launch-best-practices-report-draft-8-9741fb]]
- In companies without a separate launch org, process-change authority is vague — senior executives of product groups approve changes but no clear owner. — [[study-sun-launch-best-practices-report-draft-8-9741fb]]
- Aberdeen framed participation as voluntary with confidentiality assurance ('your response will not be made public'). — [[study-sun-launch-questionnaire-9-a2c35d]]
- Questionnaire probes: (a) common launch process/methodology across product lines; (b) variations for hardware/software/services; (c) common front-end leading to tailored direct/channel launches; (d) separate launch organization vs marketing/ops/product management. — [[study-sun-launch-questionnaire-9-a2c35d]]
- 12 task categories for lead time measurement: beta testing end, product configurator ready, ERP/order management, globalization/internationalization, launch readiness review, release to manufacturing, pricing US signoff, pricing country/region, product literature, press/analyst materials, training materials, web site. — [[study-sun-launch-questionnaire-9-a2c35d]]
- 7 delivery methods prompted: hardcopy paper; web e-learning; web site; classroom; electronic documents (.pdf); intranet database; collaboration software. — [[study-sun-launch-questionnaire-9-a2c35d]]
- 7 material categories probed for delivery: direct sales training; indirect sales/channel training; announcement sales kit; international (languages); product literature; FAQs and Q&A; press materials. — [[study-sun-launch-questionnaire-9-a2c35d]]
