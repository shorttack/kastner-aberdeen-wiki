# Rebate fulfillment-house model

> Third-party processor handling inbound mail, data entry, and check issuance for manufacturer/retailer rebates


## Top observations

- Three parties — the product manufacturer, the retailer, and the rebate fulfillment house — are usually involved in each redemption, with each party relying on the others to ensure smooth processing. — [[study-research-reveals-that-60-percent-of-reba-c887df]]
- Sponsor-to-fulfillment-house payment cycles take 15 to 90 days after the fulfillment company invoice date; paper-check payment adds several more days for check clearance before consumer checks can be issued. — [[study-research-reveals-that-60-percent-of-reba-c887df]]
- A few fulfillment houses (anonymous) have reported to the Federal Trade Commission that sponsoring manufacturers sometimes do not pay rebate-fund invoices at all. — [[study-research-reveals-that-60-percent-of-reba-c887df]]
- Traditional rebate-fulfillment houses consolidated or pivoted to broader promotions/loyalty services (e.g., Parago -> Blackhawk Engagement Solutions -> Blackhawk Network) as mail-in rebates shrank. — [[study-research-reveals-that-60-percent-of-reba-c887df]]
