# RISC processor architecture (SPARC, PA-RISC, POWER, MIPS, Alpha)

> Family of reduced-instruction-set CPU architectures; displaced by x86 in most commercial server markets by 2010


## Top observations

- By approximately 2010 Intel x86/x64 decisively displaced RISC (Sun SPARC, HP PA-RISC, DEC Alpha) in most commercial server markets; IBM POWER retained niche high-performance-computing/AIX installed base; ARM later re-entered the server market. `[ps=5]` — [[study-server-deal-turns-rivals-into-allies-seq-bdc3af]]
- RISC: 100 instructions, merchant, 75 MIPS by 1991 vs CISC 300 instructions, captive, 40 MIPS `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-trends-90s-logan-1991-648c68]]
