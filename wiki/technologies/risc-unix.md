# RISC-Unix Computer (CCI Planned)

> Subject of study; commercial multiuser systems for OLTP, decision support, batch processing


## Top observations

- Y2K drove significant server infrastructure investment 1998-2000. IBM pSeries Unix revenue grew 28% in 2000, 49% in Q4 2000 (IBM Annual Report 2000). However much Y2K spending was split across RISC/Unix, Windows NT, and emerging Linux x86 rather than purely benefiting RISC/Unix. The opportunity materialized but was shared across platforms. `[ps=5]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-risc-unix-market]]
