# Sagister

> Datacenter UNIX platform combining Escala hardware, AIX, HACMP, ISM/OpenMaster; 3 configs: M1/M2/M3


## Top observations

- Bull acquired by Atos SE in October 2014 for €620M; Sagister product discontinued well before acquisition as UNIX midrange market consolidated around IBM, HP, Sun `[ps=5]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- The broader need for mainframe-discipline UNIX management proved correct; met by ITIL frameworks, HP OpenView, IBM Tivoli, and eventually cloud-native ops practices `[ps=5]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Datacenter must provide new-generation client-server applications for organizational efficiency `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Datacenter must maintain mainframe disciplines: 7x24x365 availability, backup, security, reliability, low costs `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- HP MC/ServiceGuard may be better at clustering but HP has not undertaken ambitious mainframe-class operational functionality; relies on partners via OpenView `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Digital TruCluster with Oracle 7.3 may provide highest throughput of any UNIX platform including Sagister; but Digital has not developed datacenter operational capabilities `[ps=4]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Packaging proven best-of-class technology components (not inventing proprietary tech) with deep mainframe-discipline integration; positioning as 'UNIX Multiframe' `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Two-fold challenge: (1) convincing experienced UNIX users of operational cost savings; (2) convincing mainframe users that UNIX operational problems are real `[ps=3]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Operations Base lowers datacenter support costs with less than 7% systems overhead increase; greater than 50% ROI vs alternative UNIX with add-ons `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Sagister's design philosophy is on target; future MVS interoperability will make it viable for non-Bull enterprises `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- IS professionals consistently state UNIX is a step down from GCOS/MVS/VMS in manageability, reliability, availability, security; Sagister fills this gap `[ps=2]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Aberdeen assesses Sagister as 'must-have' product for running production UNIX applications in enterprise datacenters `[ps=1]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- CA-Unicenter provides similar operations functionality but lacks event management, HA functions, common graphical command icons, and Function Sets `[ps=1]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Bull has over 15 years of experience implementing UNIX systems in customer operations `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- Approximately 200 individuals assigned to the Sagister project `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
- 1995 revenues FF 26,656M; net profit FF 306M — first profit since 1988 `[ps=0]` — [[study-aberdeen-1996-bulls-sagister-datacenterdisciplined-unix-businesscritical-applications]]
