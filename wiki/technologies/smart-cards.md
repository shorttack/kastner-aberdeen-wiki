# Smart Cards (chip-based payment/identity cards)

> Aberdeen predicted smart cards would bridge internet and physical retail; chip cards now universal (EMV standard) but used differently than Aberdeen envisioned.


## Top observations

- Smart cards will allow financial institutions to bridge internet storefronts and brick-and-mortar stores; enable cross-pollination of promotions `[ps=2]` — [[study-1997-broadvision-dynamic-web-application-5f10bb]]
- Smart card (EMV chip) became universal by 2015 for payment security but did not bridge online/offline personalization as Aberdeen predicted; that role was filled by loyalty programs, cookies, and mobile apps — [[study-1997-broadvision-dynamic-web-application-5f10bb]]
