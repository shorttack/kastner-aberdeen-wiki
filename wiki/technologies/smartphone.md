# Smartphone

> Kastner's biggest admitted miss: underestimated how thoroughly the smartphone would transform enterprise IT. Mobile-first architecture implications larger than recognized in 2005.

- Consumerization of IT correctly predicted. `[ps=5]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]

- A billion people carrying always-connected computers changed what enterprise applications needed to do, not just how they needed to look. Mobile-first, not just mobile-compatible, had larger architecture implications. `[ps=5]` — [[study-volume-1-ch10-the-long-view-1966-2026]]
