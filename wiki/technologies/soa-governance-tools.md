# SOA Governance Software

> Automated SOA management and governance; >80% of Best-in-Class use third-party software for this; about 25% of Global 10000 employing design-time governance


## Top observations

- Between 1/3 and 1/2 of 950 companies surveyed in 2006 had serious difficulties deploying SOA applications into stable production — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- 33% of Best-in-Class have more than 2 years experience with SOA technology — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- 68% of Best-in-Class achieving positive ROI on SOA investments vs 23% overall (77% have yet to see payback) — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- More than 80% of Best-in-Class have implemented automated solution for SOA operations and governance, typically third-party software — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- 90% of Global 10,000 organizations have embarked on SOA journeys — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- 44% cite establishment of operational security, governance, and management as top SOA stumbling block — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- 39% cite security issues as different from older IT as stumbling block — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- 34% cite data management of SOA services as problematic — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- About 25% of Global 10,000 organizations employing design-time governance — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- 45% of survey and 46% of supply chain organizations cite developing new business capabilities as top SOA governance driver — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- More than 50% of Best-in-Class have at least 12 months production experience with deployed SOA apps vs 30% of overall survey — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- 33% of Best-in-Class have more than 24 months SOA experience vs 12% of overall — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- Re-usage of applications via Web Services: BIC 47%, Average 43%, Laggard 36% — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- Speed of IT implementations: Laggard 43%, Average 30%, BIC 32% — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]

- Banking/brokerage/insurance most focused on compliance governance; manufacturing on operational complexity — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- Organizations dawdling with web services and no firm SOA plan are falling behind peers — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- Design in SOA management and governance; manageability, reuse policies, security most economically designed in not bolted on — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- None of survey participants will ignore SOA management and governance investments; BIC have it now, Industry Average is planning — [[study-soa-governance-626545]]
- Establishment of operational security, governance, and management: cited by 45% of survey base as top challenge — [[study-soa-lifecycle-perspective-1ff953]]
