# SOA Management and Governance

> Operations + design-time governance category


## Top observations

- Between a third and half of the 950 companies surveyed in 2006 are having serious difficulties getting SOA-enabled applications into stable deployment — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- Predominant reason: inexperience exacerbated by inadequate tools for automated management and governance of growing plethora and complexity of web services and applications under SOA — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- Best-in-Class (top 20%) most likely (33%) to have more than two years experience with SOA technology — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- 68% of Best-in-Class achieving positive ROI on SOA investments and seeing lower application development costs; 77% of overall survey has yet to see SOA payback — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- Close to half of Best-in-Class have set minimizing application software lifecycle costs as a measurable management objective — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- The inefficiencies caused by a lack of SOA management and governance lower IT performance, raise current operating costs, and collar IT with long-term software maintenance costs that will endure for decades — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- Organizations that are dawdling with web services and with no firm enterprise plan for an SOA are falling behind. Get focused training to bring not just specialists but the whole IT organization up to snuff. SOA is not a fad. It will be with us for decades. — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- Aberdeen Group examined SOA management strategies of more than 200 companies between November 2006 and January 2007, supplemented by telephone interviews — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- Geography distribution: North America 57%; Central/South America 4%; Asia/Pacific 16%; EMEA 24% — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- Industry: 52% manufacturing/supply-chain (53% of which manufacturing); services (banking, brokerage, insurance, healthcare) 40%; public sector 8% — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
- Company size: 46% large enterprises (>$1B revenue, 27% >$5B); 38% midsize ($50M-$1B); 28% small businesses (<$50M) — [[study-aberdeen-ra-soa-management-governance-20-ff6a2c]]
