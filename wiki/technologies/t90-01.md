# Ingres version 5 / version 6

> v5 for decision support; v6 first designed for high-performance OLTP. Aberdeen recommends v6.3 for Bull.


## Top observations

- Ingres users extremely satisfied; many Sybase customers would prefer Ingres; no Ingres users reported reliability problems — [[study-1990-bull-rdbms-1990-and-2024-metadata-fe61bf]]
- Both Ingres v6 and Sybase v4 applications are just now going into production or within next 3 months; high-performance OLTP not yet proved at scale — [[study-1990-bull-rdbms-1990-and-2024-metadata-fe61bf]]
- Aberdeen predicts Ingres 6.3 as reliable, stable preferred supplier for Bull; Ingres customers show higher loyalty than Sybase — [[study-1990-bull-rdbms-1990-and-2024-metadata-fe61bf]]
- Ingres sold to ASK Group (1990), CA (1994), spun out (2005), became Actian (2011); acquired by HCL for $330M (2018); Ingres 12.0 active in 2024 — [[study-1990-bull-rdbms-1990-and-2024-metadata-fe61bf]]
- Ingres users believe deficiencies in version 5 corrected in version 6; v6 gained tremendous additional customer satisfaction and loyalty — [[study-1990-bull-rdbms-1990-and-2024-metadata-fe61bf]]
