# Macintosh (Apple Computer)

> Subject of study; enterprise perception: toy computer, lacks applications, connectivity myths.

- Apple's market share in this decade unlikely to exceed 15% of business PC market; large MIS shops putting up largest resistance `[ps=5]` — [[study-1991-apple-c-s-e9ffd7]]
- macOS desktop market share ~16% in 2025 (all PCs); Apple enterprise presence grew significantly post-iPhone but fragmented MIS pattern persisted `[ps=4]` — [[study-1991-apple-c-s-e9ffd7]]
- At 10%-20% of sites, Apple is established supplier with healthy installed base; other 80% have virtually zero Apple visibility `[ps=4]` — [[study-1991-apple-c-s-e9ffd7]]
- Aberdeen concerned Apple planning fewer than 25 frontware pilots in 1991; Fortune 1000 needs 1991 wake-up call `[ps=2]` — [[study-1991-apple-c-s-e9ffd7]]
