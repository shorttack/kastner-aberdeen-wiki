# HP Vectra Corporate PC

> HP's flagship enterprise PC line; first DMI-compliant PCs (1992); discontinued 2002 after HP-Compaq merger; replaced by HP Evo


## Top observations

- HP merged with Compaq in 2002 for $25 billion; Vectra line discontinued; replaced by HP Evo (based on Compaq platform); DMI competition moot `[ps=5]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP was the first major supplier to introduce DMI-compliant PCs starting in 1992 `[ps=1]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP Vectra Corporate PC features more than 350 total DMI attributes `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- Vectra PC platform prices declined significantly in 1996 due to CPU and memory price reductions `[ps=0]` — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- Average cost to manage a single networked PC is 2x to 3x its original purchase price — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP added approximately 300 DMI manageable attributes plus 65 unique HP DMI attribute extensions to Vectra PC line — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP will position its DMI-based desktop management environment as a major differentiator to surpass Compaq in enterprise PC sales — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
