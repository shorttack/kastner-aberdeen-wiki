# HP TopTOOLS

> DMI-compliant desktop management suite; included AssetTOOLS, ConfigTOOLS, FlashTOOL, SafeTOOLS, ProtectTOOLS; discontinued with Vectra line


## Top observations

- HP expects to reduce desktop management costs by at least 20-25% (approximately $3,000 per PC) in the near term — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP Vectra BIOS flash capability allows IS managers to extend PC life by 2 years beyond typical 3-year major technology cycles — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- HP TopTOOLS is the only product capable of upgrading BIOS of multiple PCs simultaneously from a central console — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
- TopTOOLS includes AssetTOOLS (inventory), ConfigTOOLS (configuration/troubleshoot), FlashTOOL (remote BIOS upgrade), SafeTOOLS (health monitoring), ProtectTOOLS (security) — [[study-1997-desktop-and-systems-management-the--169a9b]]
