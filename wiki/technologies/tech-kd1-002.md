# Data Mining (Retail)

> Core analytical capability the RDS product implemented; became commoditized through SAS Oracle IBM and open-source tools


## Top observations

- Retail data mining market poised for growth as retailers seek to understand and predict customer behavior `[ps=5]` — [[study-1997-knowledge-discovery-one's-retail-di-f6b834]]
- Data mining software market grew from ~$50M in 1996 to ~$800M by 2000; retail analytics became a major enterprise software segment `[ps=4]` — [[study-1997-knowledge-discovery-one's-retail-di-f6b834]]
- Data mining tools have not become proficient at delivering results to all retail professionals charged with daily decision making `[ps=4]` — [[study-1997-knowledge-discovery-one's-retail-di-f6b834]]
- Comprehensive retail solutions have strong data analysis but lack drill-down detail capability; data mining has depth but requires substantial ongoing labor `[ps=3]` — [[study-1997-knowledge-discovery-one's-retail-di-f6b834]]
