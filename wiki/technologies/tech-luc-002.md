# Voice over IP (VoIP)

> Core enabling technology; routing voice and fax over IP networks; became standard for all voice communications by 2010s


## Top observations

- PC-based client requirement has alienated internet telephony from mass markets; incomplete solutions from gateway vendors — [[study-1997-lucent-delivers-internet-telephony--888598]]
- Internet telephony promises significantly lower costs for long distance and international voice and fax traffic — [[study-1997-lucent-delivers-internet-telephony--888598]]
- Skype (2003) and Vonage (2004) demonstrated massive cost reductions for long distance; international VoIP calls fell to near-zero by mid-2000s; Aberdeen's prediction was fully validated — [[study-1997-lucent-delivers-internet-telephony--888598]]
- ISPs cable TV MSOs utilities RBOCs CAPs CLECs and IXCs all cited as potential internet telephony service providers — [[study-1997-lucent-delivers-internet-telephony--888598]]
- All major service providers (AT&T Verizon BT Deutsche Telekom) eventually deployed IP-based voice switching; circuit-switched PSTN being phased out globally; Aberdeen's service provider VoIP prediction fully validated — [[study-1997-lucent-delivers-internet-telephony--888598]]
- Many internet telephony technologies give poor quality of service and spotty reliability; Lucent solution delivers near toll-grade sound quality — [[study-1997-lucent-delivers-internet-telephony--888598]]
