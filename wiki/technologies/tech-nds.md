# Novell Directory Services (NDS)

> NDS/eDirectory still maintained by Micro Focus/OpenText; never achieved broad cross-platform adoption. LDAP became dominant.


## Top observations

- excellent cross-platform interoperability for Novell+NT+Unix; hierarchical management view — [[study-aberdeen-1995-novell-predicament]]
- little evidence NDS will achieve widespread acceptance outside Novell installed base — [[study-aberdeen-1995-novell-predicament]]
- Must make NDS best on NT bar none; open new markets to dominate; embrace NT not fight it — [[study-aberdeen-1995-novell-predicament]]
- NDS infrastructure services marketed to AT&T, Deutsche Telekom, NTT, Telstra, Unisource — [[study-aberdeen-1995-novell-predicament]]
- Either Novell fully integrates NDS with NT or Microsoft delivers OLE Directory interoperating with NDS — [[study-aberdeen-1995-novell-predicament]]
- NDS targeted at multimedia/telecom, home, retail POS, healthcare—beyond corporate LAN — [[study-aberdeen-1995-novell-predicament]]
