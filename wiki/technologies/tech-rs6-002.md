# RS64 (Apache) Processor

> IBM's first 64-bit PowerPC-AS commercial processor. Designed for transaction processing. RS64-II (262 MHz) and RS64-III (450 MHz) followed. POWER4 (2001) merged PowerPC and PowerPC-AS instruction sets.
