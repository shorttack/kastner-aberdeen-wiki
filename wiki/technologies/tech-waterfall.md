# Traditional Waterfall Development Methodology

> Planning-heavy waterfall methods criticized in study; still used in regulated industries but displaced by Agile for most software.


## Top observations

- fixing mistake after release costs 10-100x the cost of identifying during planning — [[study-aberdeen-1995-upgrading-isv-methodology-enterprise-application-development]]
- Enterprise IS prioritizes cost, compatibility, functionality, quality; ISVs prioritize time-to-launch, functionality, personnel, quality (cost not primary) — [[study-aberdeen-1995-upgrading-isv-methodology-enterprise-application-development]]
- Waterfall largely displaced by Agile/SCRUM for software development; remains in regulated industries only — [[study-aberdeen-1995-upgrading-isv-methodology-enterprise-application-development]]
- Mainframe app suppliers failed transition to Unix/GUI; DOS app suppliers failed Windows 3 transition—inability to change fast enough — [[study-aberdeen-1995-upgrading-isv-methodology-enterprise-application-development]]
