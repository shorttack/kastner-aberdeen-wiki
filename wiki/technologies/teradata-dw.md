# Teradata Data Warehouse

> Massively parallel processing data warehouse; spun off from NCR 2007


## Top observations

- Teradata spun off from NCR as independent company in 2007; became publicly traded on NYSE `[ps=5]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Teradata remains active as independent company in 2025; shifted to cloud (Teradata Vantage) but faces competition from Snowflake and Databricks `[ps=5]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Walmart operated the world's largest Teradata system — Safeway benchmarked its DW ambitions against Walmart and Kroger `[ps=4]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Teradata platform expected to remain the leading purpose-built data warehouse for large-scale retail analytics `[ps=3]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- $1.226 billion Teradata division revenue `[ps=0]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Approximately 700 Teradata systems installed worldwide; ~100 new systems per year `[ps=0]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- 40-node Teradata system; grown from original 28 nodes (1997) to 34 (2000) to 40 (2002) `[ps=0]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- $16.7 million total Teradata hardware investment ($10.2M original + $6.5M expansions) `[ps=0]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
- Safeway's Teradata implementation was among the more sophisticated in grocery retail — 25+ applications across 3 tiers `[ps=0]` — [[study-safeway-damages-report-draft-b502f4]]
