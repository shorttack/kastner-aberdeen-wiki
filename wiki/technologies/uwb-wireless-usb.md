# Ultra-Wideband Wireless USB

> UWB/Wireless USB Briefing — technology largely failed to reach market.


## Top observations

- Dedicated UWB / Wireless USB Briefing at W Hotel on Wednesday suggests Intel was bullish on Wireless USB at Spring IDF 2005. — [[study-intel-idf-2005-spring-delegate-confirmat-0de810]]
- UWB (Ultra-Wideband) failed to achieve mass market adoption as predicted. The WiMedia Alliance IEEE 802.15.3a standardization process stalled; the task group disbanded January 2006. Wireless USB based on WiMedia saw limited consumer adoption. WiMedia Alliance dissolved in 2009. Wireless USB never achieved mass adoption, replaced by USB 3.0 and Wi-Fi. — [[study-intel-idf-2005-spring-delegate-confirmat-0de810]]
