# Wireless Encryption Protocol (WEP)

> Early Wi-Fi security protocol referenced in XP wireless setup; deprecated due to severe vulnerabilities; replaced by WPA/WPA2


## Top observations

- WEP used as wireless security standard in tested SOHO network configuration — [[study-2001-upgrading-to-windows-xp-a-long-and-winding-road-ps-81e090]]
- WEP officially deprecated by IEEE 802.11i in 2004; replaced by WPA and WPA2 due to fundamental cryptographic weaknesses — [[study-2001-upgrading-to-windows-xp-a-long-and-winding-road-ps-81e090]]
- WEP insufficient for enterprise WLAN security; dedicated solutions required — [[study-2002-wireless-lan-security-an-adolescent-market-88de03]]
- Wi-Fi Alliance deprecated WEP in 2004; replaced by WPA/WPA2 — [[study-2002-wireless-lan-security-an-adolescent-market-88de03]]
