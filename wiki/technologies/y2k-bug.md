# Y2K / Year 2000 Two-Digit Year Bug

> Two-digit year field vulnerability; Kastner fixed an early instance in Marine Midland Bank's bond portfolio system in July 1969, 30 years before the global Y2K crisis.


## Top observations

- Two-digit year field: 30-year bond issued in 1970 maturing in 2000 would be recorded as '00', read as 1900. Kastner expanded field to fix it. — [[study-volume-1-ch02-physics-of-the-machine-1969-1972]]
- "No one wrote a press release about it. We were just doing the job." Fix came 30 years before the global Y2K panic. — [[study-volume-1-ch02-physics-of-the-machine-1969-1972]]
- Systems with two-digit year fields stored year 2000 as '00', interpreted as 1900—a design choice consistent with 1960s storage economics. — [[study-volume-1-ch02-physics-of-the-machine-1969-1972]]
- Aberdeen Group views the Year 2000 problem as much about competitive advantage as it is about technology — [[study-y2k-computerworld-aberdeen-psk-irving-19-5bcd20]]
