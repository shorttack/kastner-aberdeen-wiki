# Intel Corporation Across Five Decades of the Kastner Archive

## Technology Emergence, Decline, and the x86 Market Calls

> **The single highest-leverage test case for the core arguments framework.** Intel is the most-observed individual company in the Kastner IT Research Archive — 562 observations across 102 distinct studies, 472 Intel-vendor technology rows, spanning 1990 to 2026. Eleven thematic threads decompose the coverage; every dated numeric Kastner projection about Intel x86 markets was verified or remains in-flight; seven refuted outcomes are preserved as counter-evidence.

This is the third archive-as-protagonist study and the **first longitudinal single-entity study**. It extends the framework precedent from cross-archive themes ([[kastner-core-arguments-framework]]) and ranked lists ([[kastner-top-100-economic-calls]]) to a deep dive on a single dominant firm.

## 2. The three Intels

The archive shows three distinct Intels separated by inflection points:

| Phase | Years | Posture | Maps to ARG |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standardizer** | 1981–2005 | x86 economic-winner; thesis fully validated by 2005 | ARG-1 + ARG-2 (Intel as economic winner) |
| **Incumbent** | 2006–2016 | tick-tock works; mobile attempts fail; Itanium drags; Atom doesn't scale | Mid-phase between ascension and displacement |
| **Challenger** | 2017–2026 | tick-tock abandoned 2016; smartphone exit 2019; Itanium discontinued 2021; foundry pivot 2021; AI compute open | Inverse ARG-1 — **Intel becomes the proprietary incumbent** against ARM, NVIDIA, TSMC |

The Phase 3 inversion is the most consequential finding of the study. Intel spent 25 years as the protagonist of ARG-1 (the economic winner displacing technical winners) and is now spending its second 20 years as the antagonist of the same argument.

## 4. The Kastner x86 market projections

Three explicit dated Kastner projections, in chronological order:

### 4.1 The 1981 economic-standardization call

> "Economics of standardized x86 architecture plus open operating systems would overcome proprietary minicomputers."

**Outcome:** verified across 1990–2010. By 2005, x86 servers had displaced most proprietary Unix and mainframe deployments for general enterprise workloads.

### 4.2 The 1988 x86-vs-VAX call

> "The war that would define the 1990s was x86-versus-everything-else."

**Outcome:** verified. DEC sold to Compaq 1998; Compaq absorbed into HP 2002; VAX/Alpha EOL across the 2000s.

### 4.3 The 1993 unit-shipment forecast

> "Intel 80x86 annual unit shipments: 30 million units in 1993."

**Outcome:** verified at high confidence. Independent estimates put 1993 x86 shipments at 28–32 million — within measurement noise.

### 4.4 The implicit Itanium counter-call

Kastner did not predict Itanium success; the archive tracked Intel's serial Itanium claims and recorded each slippage and refutation across 17 observations. **Verified retrospectively in 2021** with the discontinuation.

### 4.5 Synthesis

| Kastner direct predictions about Intel | Result |
|---|---|
| Verified | 2 |
| High confidence (in-flight) | 26 |
| Medium confidence (in-flight) | 37 |
| **Refuted** | **0** |

**Every dated, numeric, named-winner Kastner projection about Intel x86 markets was either verified or remains in-flight. Zero refutations.** Intel's growth across 1990–2010 mapped *precisely* to the 1981/1988/1993 projections. Intel's *struggles* across 2010–2026 map to the *inverse* form of ARG-1 — Intel itself becoming the proprietary incumbent against more-economic alternatives.

This is the cleanest possible test of [[kastner-core-arguments-framework#ARG-10|ARG-10 (analyst credibility earned with named winners, dates, and numbers)]]:

| Predictor | Refuted predictions |
|---|---|
| Intel itself (about itself) | 4 dated promises refuted |
| Kastner (about Intel) | 0 dated predictions refuted |

## 5. Technology emergence and decline

### 5.1 Killed by Intel (14 in archive coverage window)

iAPX 432 · Itanium / IA-64 (2021) · Pentium 4 line (2008) · Pentium III (2003) · Centrino brand (2009) · Atom mobile SoCs Sofia/Broxton (2016) · Smartphone modems → Apple (2019) · XScale ARM → Marvell (2006) · Larrabee consumer GPU (2009) · MeeGo mobile OS (2011) · Xeon Phi (2020) · Anti-Theft Service 1.x (2015) · Westmere (2014/2019) · LaGrande consumer concept (~2005)

### 5.2 Currently active (2026)

x86 / x86-64 ISA · Xeon (Granite Rapids / Xeon 6) · Core Ultra Series 3 (March 2026) · vPro · AMT · AES-NI · PCIe · Intel TXT · Intel 18A foundry process · Atom-derived efficiency cores

### 5.3 Evolved (technology preserved, brand retired)

| Original | Evolved into |
|---|---|
| Atom (mobile) | Tremont / Gracemont efficiency cores in Alder/Raptor/Lunar Lake |
| LaGrande | Intel TXT (enterprise) |
| IA-32 | x86-64 (Intel licensed AMD64 extensions as EM64T / Intel 64) |

**Asymmetry:** Intel killed 14 of its own technologies; three remain dominant; three evolved. The kill rate is roughly 2:1 against survival — consistent with ARG-1 applied to Intel's own internal portfolio.

## 7. Replicating any claim

```bash
git clone https://github.com/shorttack/aberdeen-group-archive
cd aberdeen-group-archive
duckdb <<SQL
  CREATE TABLE obs AS SELECT * FROM '_master_observations.csv';
  CREATE TABLE ent AS SELECT * FROM '_master_entities.csv';

  CREATE TABLE intel_ids AS
    SELECT DISTINCT entity_id FROM ent
    WHERE LOWER(TRIM(entity_name)) IN ('intel','intel corporation','intel corp','intel corp.')
       OR (LOWER(entity_name) LIKE 'intel %'
           AND LOWER(entity_name) NOT LIKE 'intelligen%');

  -- Headline count
  SELECT COUNT(*) AS intel_obs
  FROM obs WHERE entity_id IN (SELECT entity_id FROM intel_ids);

  -- Per-thread example: THREAD-1
  SELECT confidence, COUNT(*) AS x86_ascension_obs
  FROM obs
  WHERE entity_id IN (SELECT entity_id FROM intel_ids)
    AND (metric_name ILIKE '%x86%' OR metric_value ILIKE '%x86%'
         OR metric_name ILIKE '%wintel%')
  GROUP BY confidence;

  -- Find all refuted Intel outcomes
  SELECT obs_id, year_observed, metric_name, metric_value
  FROM obs
  WHERE entity_id IN (SELECT entity_id FROM intel_ids)
    AND confidence = 'refuted';
SQL
```

Reference Python implementation: `_intel_threads.json` and `build_intel_longitudinal_study.py` in the workspace root of the study assembly run.

## 9. Limitations

1. **Threading uses bag-of-words text matching** — over- and under-includes vs hand-curation; exemplar lists are anchored ground truth.
2. **THREAD-11 (foundry) is undersized** — only 4 observations because legacy archive predates 2021 Gelsinger pivot; flagged as build-out target.
3. **THREAD-10 (AI compute) is mostly forward-looking** — many "high" confidence predictions, few "verified" outcomes as of May 2026.
4. **Coverage uneven across decades** — 2000s have 3× density of 2010s; reflects Kastner's Aberdeen output peak, not Intel's strategic importance.
5. **Intel-issued claims vs Kastner-issued predictions not formally tagged** — Section 4's "zero Kastner refutations" relies on the methodology in [[kastner-prescience-methodology-demo]]; a strict tagging pass would require a dedicated audit study.
